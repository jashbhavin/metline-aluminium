6061 Aluminium Flat Bar

6061 flat bar is the general-purpose structural rectangle in aluminium: strong in T6 temper, weldable, corrosion resistant and easy to machine. It is used for frames, brackets, base plates, rails, busbars and machined parts across engineering, transport and construction. Overview

Why 6061 is the default choice for aluminium flat bar

If a drawing simply says "aluminium flat bar" and the part carries load, 6061-T6 is usually what the designer had in mind. It combines useful strength with weldability, corrosion resistance and good machining in one alloy.

6061 is a heat-treatable aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloy with a small copper and chromium addition. Solution heat treatment followed by artificial ageing produces fine Mg2Si precipitates, giving T6 a typical yield strength around 275 MPa, about twice that of 6063-T5 and well above any common non-heat-treatable alloy. It resists atmospheric and fresh-water corrosion well, and it takes clear and coloured anodising.

Flat bar is made in two ways. Extruded bar (T6 or T6511) is the most common: the profile is pressed through a flat die, quenched at the press or in a furnace, stretched and aged. It covers the widest size range at the lowest cost. Cold-finished or rolled bar (T6 or T651) is drawn or rolled to closer tolerances and sharper corners, and is chosen when the bar is used with little or no machining. Wide, thick flats can also be sawn from 6061-T651 plate.

What 6061 flat bar offers

  • Good strength-to-weight ratio for structural and machined parts
  • Weldable by TIG and MIG, unlike 2024 and 7075
  • Good resistance to weather and fresh water without coating
  • Takes anodising, powder coating and paint well
  • Wide size range and short supply chains, because it is so widely stocked

Tempers

Which temper of 6061 flat bar to order

6061-T6

Solution-treated and artificially aged. The standard temper for extruded and rolled flat bar used as supplied or lightly machined.

6061-T6511

Extruded, stretched for stress relief, aged, with minor straightening allowed. Stays flatter when slotted or machined on one face.

6061-T651

Stress-relieved by stretching; used for cold-finished bar and for flats sawn from plate. Best for heavy machining.

6061-T4 / O

Softer tempers for bending and forming, after which parts can be aged to T6-level strength.

For flats that will be machined on one side only, such as base plates or slotted rails, specify a stress-relieved temper (T6511 or T651). Unrelieved T6 bar can bow when metal is removed from one face, because quenching leaves compressive stress at the surface.

Tolerances & inspection

Tolerances and inspection

Extruded flat bar is supplied to the tolerances in ANSI H35.2 (ASTM B221) or EN 755-5, which cover thickness, width, corner radius, flatness, straightness and twist. Cold-finished bar to ASTM B211 or EN 754-5 holds closer limits. If a face must be flat to a tight figure without machining, say so at enquiry stage: we will propose cold-finished bar, a stress-relieved temper or a flat sawn from plate.

Working with 6061

Machining, welding, bending and finishing

Machining

Good in T6 and T651, with continuous chips that break better with chip-breaker inserts. Use sharp carbide tools, high speeds and coolant.

Welding

TIG and MIG with ER4043 (less crack-sensitive) or ER5356 (stronger, better colour match after anodising). The heat-affected zone falls to roughly T4-level strength, so size welds accordingly.

Bending

T6 flat bar bends best the easy way (across the thickness) over a radius of about 1.5 – 3 × thickness, depending on thickness. For tight bends, order T4 or anneal locally.

Finishing

Anodises well for wear and appearance; powder-coats and paints after conversion coating.

Applications

Where 6061 flat bar is used

  • Structures and frames: brackets, gussets, braces, base plates and supports
  • Transport: trailer and truck body parts, rail and bus fittings, marine hardware above the waterline
  • Machinery: guards, rails, spacers, jig plates and machined components
  • Electrical: busbars and earthing strips where strength matters as well as conductivity
  • Construction: handrail posts, stair parts, balustrade fixings and architectural trim
  • Fabrication and DIY: general workshop stock for welded and bolted assemblies

FAQ

6061 aluminium flat bar: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6061-T6 and T6511 flat bar?

Both have the same strength. T6511 has been stretched to relieve quenching stress, so it moves less when machined on one face. T6 is fine when the bar is used as supplied.

Can 6061 aluminum flat bar be welded?

Yes. TIG and MIG with ER4043 or ER5356 filler work well. Expect the area next to the weld to lose some strength unless the part is heat-treated again.

Can 6061-T6 flat bar be bent?

Yes, over a generous radius, typically 1.5 – 3 × thickness for bends across the thickness. Tight bends crack; order T4 or anneal the bend area for those.

Is 6061 flat bar suitable for outdoor use?

Yes. It resists weathering well and is often used uncoated. Anodise or paint it for coastal or polluted environments, or where appearance must last.

How much does 6061 flat bar weigh?

Multiply thickness by width in millimetres and by 0.0027 to get kg per metre. A 10 × 50 mm bar weighs 1.35 kg/m.

Is 6061 or 6082 better for flat bar?

They are near equivalents. 6082 is slightly stronger and common in Europe; 6061 anodises a little better and is standard in North America and Asia.
