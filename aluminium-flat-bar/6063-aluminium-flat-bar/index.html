6063 Aluminium Flat Bar

6063 aluminium flat bar is an extruded rectangular bar in the alloy known as "architectural aluminium": moderate strength, very smooth surface, excellent anodising response and high thermal and electrical conductivity. It is the right choice for visible trims, frames, heat sinks and electrical flats, and the wrong choice for heavily loaded structural parts. Metline supplies 6063 flat bar in T5 and T6 tempers, cut to length and certified, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is 6063 aluminium flat bar?

6063 is a lean Al-Mg-Si alloy with less magnesium and silicon than 6061 and almost no copper. That chemistry makes it the easiest of all structural alloys to extrude: it flows through a die at high speed, fills sharp corners and leaves a surface so smooth that it can be anodised without polishing.

A flat bar is the simplest extruded shape, a solid rectangle with square or slightly radiused edges. In 6063 it is produced by pushing a preheated billet through a flat die, cooling it at the press exit (air or water) and then ageing it. Because the alloy can be quenched on the press, it reaches the T5 condition without a separate solution heat-treatment furnace, which keeps cost low and dimensional consistency high.

The trade-off is strength. A 6063-T6 flat bar is roughly 25 % weaker than 6061-T6 in tensile strength and about 20 % weaker in yield. For trims, decorative strips, door and window components, ladder treads, heat-spreading plates, electrical earthing and busbar flats, and light frames, that strength is ample, and the better finish and conductivity are worth more than extra megapascals.

When 6063 flat bar is the right choice

  • The bar will be visible and anodised, powder coated or brushed
  • Heat or electricity has to move through the part (heat sinks, earthing, light busbar)
  • Loads are moderate and the design is stiffness-limited rather than strength-limited
  • You want sharp, clean edges and tight thickness tolerance at a low price per kilogram

Tempers

Which 6063 temper should you order?

6063-T5

Cooled from the extrusion temperature and artificially aged. The standard temper for architectural and decorative flat bar: good finish, consistent dimensions and the lowest cost.

6063-T6

Solution heat-treated (or press-quenched with enough cooling to count as solution treatment) and aged. About 30 % stronger than T5; ordered where the bar carries load, such as ladder rungs, handrails and frame members.

6063-T52

Stress-relieved by compression after quenching, then aged. Used where the bar will be machined or cut into short pieces and must stay straight.

6063-T4

Quenched and naturally aged. Soft enough for bending and forming; can be aged to T6 after fabrication.

6063-O

Annealed for maximum ductility: tight bends, coiling and forming operations.

6063-T66 / T64

European and automotive variants with modified strength or formability. Specify the exact standard if your drawing calls for them.

Tolerances & quality

Tolerances, straightness and surface inspection

Extruded flat bar tolerances depend on width and thickness. For ASTM orders they follow ANSI H35.2 (the tolerance tables referenced in ASTM B221); for EN orders they follow EN 755-5 for rectangular bar. Straightness, twist and flatness are covered in the same tables.

Quality checks

Dimensions
Width and thickness measured at both ends of sampled bars; edge radius checked against drawing
Straightness and twist
Checked on a surface table to the order standard
Surface
Visible faces inspected for die lines, pick-up and scratches; important for anodised work
Mechanical
Tensile and hardness results per lot on the mill certificate
Certification
EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate with chemistry and mechanical results

If the bar will be anodised, tell us which faces are visible. Extrusion die lines run lengthwise and are normally acceptable on non-visible faces, but critical decorative faces may need to be specified as "architectural" or "anodising quality" so the extrusion and handling are controlled accordingly.

Working with 6063

Machining, welding, bending and finishing

Anodising

The best anodising alloy among the structural grades. Clear anodising gives a uniform silver; colour and bronze finishes match well from lot to lot. Low copper and iron are the reason.

Welding

Welds easily by MIG and TIG with ER4043 or ER5356 filler. The heat-affected zone drops close to the O or T4 level, so welded frames are designed to reduced allowable stresses.

Bending

T5 and T6 bars bend cold around moderate radii, easiest the "easy way" (about the thickness axis). For tight bends, order T4 or O and age afterwards.

Machining

Fair. 6063 is softer and gummier than 6061, so use sharp, polished carbide tools with high rake and good coolant. For heavily machined parts choose 6061 or 6082.

Powder coating

Accepts powder and liquid paints well after chromate or chrome-free conversion pretreatment.

Corrosion

Very good in atmospheric and mildly corrosive conditions, including coastal air when anodised.

Applications

Where 6063 flat bar is used

  • Architecture: window and door components, glazing beads, cladding trims, balustrade flats, shopfitting
  • Thermal management: heat spreaders and simple heat sink bases where conductivity matters more than strength
  • Electrical: earthing flats, light busbar and connectors (for main busbar, 6101 is the purpose-made grade)
  • Furniture and interiors: frames, edge trims, handles and display systems
  • Transport interiors: rails, luggage racks, trim strips in buses and rail coaches
  • Access equipment: ladder components, steps, light platforms
  • Signage and lighting: LED mounting strips and frames

FAQ

6063 aluminium (aluminum) flat bar: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6063-T5 and 6063-T6 flat bar?

T5 is cooled straight from the extrusion press and then aged; T6 is fully solution treated and aged. T6 is roughly 30 % stronger (typical yield about 214 MPa against 145 MPa), while T5 is cheaper and perfectly adequate for trims and frames.

Is 6063 flat bar strong enough for structural use?

For light structures such as frames, handrails and ladder parts, yes, in T6. For heavily loaded or bolted structural members, 6061-T6 or 6082-T6 give about 25 – 40 % more strength for similar cost.

Can 6063 flat bar be used as a busbar?

It conducts well (about 53 – 55 % IACS) and is often used for earthing flats and light busbar. For main power distribution busbar with a specified conductivity, 6101 is the dedicated grade.

How much does 6063 flat bar weigh?

Multiply width × thickness in millimetres by 0.0027 to get kg per metre. A 50 × 6 mm bar weighs 0.81 kg/m and a 100 × 10 mm bar 2.70 kg/m.

Does 6063 flat bar anodise well?

Yes; it is the reference alloy for decorative anodising because its low copper and iron give a clear, even colour. Tell us which faces are visible so they are protected in handling.

Do you supply 6063 flat bar outside India?

Yes. Metline ships 6063 flat bar worldwide from Mumbai with mill certificates and export packing, on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
