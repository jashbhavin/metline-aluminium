Aluminium Round Tube

Aluminium round tube is a hollow circular section described by outside diameter (OD) and wall thickness, made by extrusion, by cold drawing or by seam welding of strip. The route matters as much as the grade: structural porthole tube, seamless tube and welded tube suit different pressures, tolerances and budgets. Metline supplies round tube in all common grades and routes, cut to length and certified, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is aluminium round tube?

Round tube is the most efficient aluminium section for torsion, internal pressure and bending in any direction, which is why it appears in everything from bicycle frames and handrails to heat exchangers, hydraulic lines and aircraft structures. It is specified by OD and wall thickness (or OD and ID for thick tube), unlike pipe, which is sized by nominal pipe size and schedule.

Aluminium tube is lighter than steel tube of the same size by about two thirds, does not rust, and can be anodised or powder coated. The trade-off is a lower modulus (about 69 GPa against 200 GPa for steel), so for the same stiffness an aluminium tube is usually made larger in diameter rather than thicker in wall.

If you are sizing pipe for fluid service by schedule (SCH 40, SCH 80), see the aluminium pipe page. This page covers tube sized by actual OD and wall.

Tolerances & inspection

Tolerances, ovality and testing

Round tube tolerances cover mean OD, OD at any point (which controls ovality), wall thickness, straightness and length. Extruded tube follows EN 755-7/-8 or ASTM B221/B241 tables; drawn tube to EN 754-7 or ASTM B210/B483 is several times tighter, which matters for hydraulic cylinders, telescopic assemblies and push-fit joints.

Inspection options

Dimensional
OD, ID, wall and ovality by micrometer and bore gauge; eccentricity checked on seamless tube
Eddy current
Through-coil eddy current testing to detect seam defects and pits on thin tube
Pressure
Hydrostatic or pneumatic leak tests for heat-exchanger and fluid tube
Mechanical
Tensile, flattening and expansion (flare) tests per the order standard
Certification
EN 10204 3.1 certificate with chemistry, mechanical results and test records
Working with tube

Bending, joining and finishing round tube

Bending

Mandrel bending gives the tightest radii without wrinkling. Annealed (O) or T4 tube bends much tighter than T6; bend in T4 and age afterwards for strong formed parts.

Welding

6061, 6063, 6082, 3003 and 5052 weld well by TIG or MIG. The heat-affected zone of 6xxx tube loses part of its T6 strength.

Mechanical joints

Swaging, flaring, crimping and push-in connectors suit drawn tube with tight OD tolerance.

Brazing

3003 tube is widely brazed in heat exchangers with 4xxx filler and flux or vacuum processes.

Anodising and coating

6063 gives the best decorative anodised finish; 6061 and 6082 anodise well for protective coatings.

Machining

Thin-wall tube needs soft jaws or expanding mandrels to avoid ovality during turning.

Applications

Where aluminium round tube is used

  • Structures and frames: trusses, stage rigging, scaffolding, greenhouses, solar mounting
  • Transport: bicycle and motorcycle frames, roof racks, truck and trailer components, aircraft tube
  • Heat transfer: condenser, radiator and evaporator tube, refrigerant lines
  • Fluid power: pneumatic and hydraulic cylinder barrels, fuel and lubrication lines
  • Architecture: handrails, balustrades, sunshades, curtain-wall elements
  • Consumer goods: furniture, tent poles, ladders, sports equipment, medical aids

FAQ

Aluminium (aluminum) round tube: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between aluminium tube and pipe?

Tube is specified by its actual outside diameter and wall thickness and is used mainly for structures and mechanical parts. Pipe is specified by nominal pipe size and schedule (for example 2 in SCH 40) and is used mainly for fluid systems.

Is extruded aluminium tube seamless?

Not always. Most structural tube is made on porthole dies, which leave longitudinal extrusion welds. Seamless tube is extruded over a mandrel from a hollow or pierced billet, or drawn from such tube. Specify seamless when the tube carries pressure or is safety-critical.

Which aluminium tube grade is best for bending?

3003, 5052 and 6063 bend most easily. For a strong bent part, bend 6061 in O or T4 temper and then age it to T6, or use mandrel bending with generous radii in T6.

How do I calculate the weight of aluminium tube?

Weight (kg/m) = 0.0084823 × (OD − wall) × wall, with dimensions in millimetres. A 50 × 3 mm tube weighs about 1.20 kg per metre.

Can aluminium round tube be anodised?

Yes. 6063 gives the brightest, most uniform decorative finish; 6061 and 6082 are widely anodised for protection and hard coating. Tell us the finish when ordering so we can select the right grade and surface quality.

Do you ship aluminium tube worldwide?

Yes. Round tube is supplied in mill or cut lengths with mill certificates and export packing, by sea or air.
