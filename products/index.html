Alloy series

Aluminium alloy series at a glance

The first digit of a wrought alloy tells you its main alloying element, and with it the broad character of the metal.

SeriesMain elementHeat treatableCharacter
1xxxNone (99%+ aluminium)NoHighest conductivity and corrosion resistance, low strength
2xxxCopperYesHigh strength and fatigue resistance, lower corrosion resistance
3xxxManganeseNoModerate strength, very good formability
4xxxSiliconSomeLower melting point; used for brazing and welding filler
5xxxMagnesiumNoGood strength, excellent marine corrosion resistance, weldable
6xxxMagnesium and siliconYesVersatile, extrudable, weldable, good corrosion resistance
7xxxZincYesHighest strength; aerospace and high-load parts
8xxxOther (iron, lithium)VariesFoil stock and specialist alloys

FAQ

Product questions

Which aluminium alloy should I choose?

Start with the environment and the load. 5xxx alloys suit marine and welded structures, 6xxx alloys are the versatile default for structures and machining, and 2xxx or 7xxx alloys are used where strength matters most. Send us your application and we will recommend a grade.

Do you supply small quantities?

Tell us the quantity you need and we will confirm what can be supplied and the most economical way to do it.

Do you export?

Yes. We are headquartered in Mumbai, close to the Nhava Sheva (JNPT) port, and supply customers across India and overseas.

Ready to discuss your requirement?

Send us the alloy, form, size and quantity. Our Mumbai team will reply with availability, lead time and a competitive quote.
