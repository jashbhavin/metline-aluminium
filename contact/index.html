Before you write

What to include in your enquiry

  1. Product and alloyFor example 6061-T6 plate or 6063-T5 profile.
  2. DimensionsThickness, width, length, diameter or a drawing.
  3. QuantityPieces or total weight, and whether delivery can be split.
  4. SpecificationThe standard you buy to and any tolerances.
  5. DocumentsMill test certificates or inspection you need.
  6. DeliveryCity or port, and the date you need the material.
