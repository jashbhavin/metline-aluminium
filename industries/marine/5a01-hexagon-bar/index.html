5A01, 5A03, 5A05 & 5A06 Marine Aluminium Hexagon Bar

The 5A-series alloys are the Chinese-standard aluminium-magnesium grades used on ships and equipment designed to GB, CCS or GOST practice. As hexagon bar they are machined into nuts, unions, couplings and threaded fittings that match the 5A-series plate around them. Overview

What the 5A-series alloys are

In the Chinese designation system an "A" in the second position marks a national alloy that is not an Aluminum Association registration. The 5A-series are aluminium-magnesium alloys with a long history in Chinese and Soviet-designed shipbuilding, aerospace and pressure equipment, and three of them closely match the Russian AMg grades.

They step up in magnesium and strength: 5A03 (about 3.5 % Mg) is a moderate-strength, easily welded alloy; 5A05 (about 5 %) and 5A06 (about 6 %) are progressively stronger; and 5A01 (6 – 7 % Mg with chromium and zirconium) is a high-strength marine development. All are non-heat-treatable, resist seawater well and weld with Al-Mg fillers.

Hexagon bar in these grades matters to buyers maintaining or building vessels to GB drawings, where substituting an AA alloy would need design approval. It is used for spanner-flat fittings: nuts and lock nuts for aluminium structures, pipe unions, hose connectors, bulkhead glands and threaded bosses.

Typical reasons to specify 5A-series hex bar

  • The drawing or ship specification is written to GB or GOST standards
  • Fittings must match 5A05 or 5A06 hull, tank or pipework material
  • High strength without heat treatment (5A06, 5A01)
  • Good seawater resistance and weldability across the family

Like 5083 and 5456, they should be kept out of long-term service above about 65 °C, where β-phase can form on grain boundaries and cause intergranular or stress-corrosion cracking. 5A01's chromium and zirconium additions are intended to improve its resistance, but the temperature limit should still be respected.

Fabrication

Machining, threading and welding

Machining

Higher-Mg grades (5A05, 5A06, 5A01) machine more cleanly than 5A03, which is soft and gummy. Use sharp polished carbide, positive rake and coolant.

Threads

Roll threads where possible and use anti-seize; aluminium-on-aluminium threads gall if dry.

Welding

Use high-Mg fillers: 5B06-type or ER5183/ER5556 for 5A05, 5A06 and 5A01; ER5356 or ER5183 for 5A03.

Isolation

Separate from stainless and copper alloys with insulating washers and sealant.

Heat

Keep long-term temperature below about 65 °C for 5A05, 5A06 and 5A01.

Finishing

Sulphuric anodising for corrosion protection and wear; hard anodise for frequently assembled threads.

Applications

Uses of 5A-series hex bar

  • Fasteners: nuts, lock nuts and special bolts for 5A05 / 5A06 structures
  • Pipework: unions, adaptors, bulkhead fittings and hose tails
  • Deck equipment: threaded sockets, stanchion bases and fitting bodies
  • Cable and pipe supports: threaded spacers and standoffs
  • Equipment and machinery: fittings on pumps, valves and pressure equipment to GB drawings

A typical case is repair stock for a vessel built to GB standards: the hull is 5A05 or 5A06 plate, and replacement pipe unions and bulkhead glands are turned from 5A06-H112 hex bar so that they match the original alloy and certificates.

FAQ

5A-series marine aluminium (aluminum) hex bar: FAQs

What does the "A" in 5A06 mean?

It marks a Chinese national alloy registered in GB/T 3190 rather than an international Aluminum Association alloy. 5A06 was formerly called LF6 and matches the Russian AMg6.

What is the international equivalent of 5A05 and 5A06?

5A05 corresponds closely to Russian AMg5 and 5A06 to AMg6. There is no exact AA equivalent; 5456 and 5083 are the nearest in behaviour but differ in composition.

How strong is 5A06 hex bar?

GB/T 3191 lists minimums of 315 MPa tensile, 155 MPa proof and 15 % elongation for O and H112 bar in the standard size range.

Is 5A01 the same as 5A06?

No. 5A01 has slightly more magnesium (6.0 – 7.0 %) plus chromium and zirconium additions, and is a higher-strength marine alloy. Some published tables wrongly show 5A01 with 6 – 7 % manganese; it is magnesium.

Can 5A-series hex bar replace 5083 fittings?

Only with design approval. 5A05 or 5A06 often has equal or greater strength, but the composition and standards differ, and class approval follows the society's rules.

Do you supply 5A-series bar with certificates?

Yes. Metline supplies 5A01, 5A03, 5A05 and 5A06 hex bar with EN 10204 3.1 certificates stating GB/T 3191 results, cut to length and export-packed.
