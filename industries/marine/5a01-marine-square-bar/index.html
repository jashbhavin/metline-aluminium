Overview

Square bar for GB-standard aluminium vessels

Square bar is the solid building block of welded aluminium structure: engine seatings, chocks under deck machinery, keel bars, hatch frames and lifting-point doublers. When the hull is in a 5A-series alloy, square bar in the same grade keeps strength, corrosion behaviour and welding procedure consistent.

Ships and craft designed to Chinese standards commonly use 5A05 and 5A06 plate for hulls, tanks and superstructures, with 5A01 for high-strength shell plating and 5A03 for lightly loaded parts. Square bar in each grade follows the same pattern: 5A06 or 5A01 for heavily loaded seatings and foundations, 5A05 for general welded structure and 5A03 where easy forming and welding matter more than strength.

Because none of these alloys is heat-treated, the bar keeps close to its supplied strength after welding. That gives it an advantage over 6xxx square bar such as 6061 or 6082, which loses up to half its proof strength next to a weld.

Typical selection

  • Machinery seatings and crane bases: 5A06 or 5A01
  • General welded framing and chocks: 5A05
  • Lightly loaded parts and formed frames: 5A03
  • Same grade as the surrounding plate wherever possible

Square bar is ordered to GB/T 3191; tolerances follow the GB/T tables or, by agreement, EN 755-4. IACS UR W25 does not list 5A-series alloys, so vessels classed outside Chinese or Russian rules need prior agreement with the society. For those, 5083 or 5383 square bar is often the simpler choice.

All four grades except 5A03 carry more than about 4.5 % magnesium. Like 5083 and 5456, they should be kept out of long-term service above about 65 °C, where β-phase can form on grain boundaries and cause intergranular or stress-corrosion cracking. 5A01's chromium and zirconium additions are intended to improve its resistance, but the temperature limit should still be respected.

Fabrication

Fabrication and installation

Welding

High-Mg filler (5B06-type, ER5183 or ER5556) for 5A05, 5A06 and 5A01; ER5356 or ER5183 for 5A03. Clean oxide immediately before welding.

Seatings

Machine the top face flat after welding if engine alignment demands it; allow machining stock in the bar size.

Tapped holes

Use helical-coil inserts for bolts that are removed in service.

Isolation

Insulate steel engine feet and stainless fasteners with pads, sleeves and washers.

Forming

Bend O-temper bar cold over a former; avoid warm forming of the high-Mg grades.

Heat

Keep 5A05, 5A06 and 5A01 seatings away from long-term heat above about 65 °C.

Applications

Applications on vessels and equipment

  • Machinery seatings: main and auxiliary engine, generator and pump foundations
  • Deck machinery bases: winch, capstan, crane and davit chocks
  • Keel and stem bars: solid bars along keel lines and stems of small craft
  • Frames and coamings: hatch, door and window frames
  • Tank structure: stiffeners and flange blocks on 5A05 / 5A06 tanks
  • Offshore and coastal: pontoon, gangway and aquaculture structures

In a typical GB-standard patrol craft, the shell is 5A01 or 5A06 plate, longitudinal frames are extruded profiles, and each engine bed is a pair of 5A06-H112 square bars welded to the floors and machined flat after welding.

  1. SpecifyAlloy and temper, side length and length or cut pieces, quantity and the governing standard with its edition (GB/T 3191 and GB/T 3190, with edition year). If the vessel is classed, name the society and whether a class certificate is required.
  2. ConfirmWe confirm the standard and tolerance class can be met, which tests are included (tensile, chemistry, corrosion tests where the standard calls for them) and the delivery terms: EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP.
  3. InspectMaterial is checked for dimensions, straightness, twist and surface condition before packing; third-party or class surveyor inspection can be arranged on request.

FAQ

5A-series marine aluminium (aluminum) square bar: FAQs

Which 5A grade should I use for an engine seating?

5A06, or 5A01 where it matches the hull plate. Both have the highest strength in the family and keep it after welding. Keep seatings away from long-term heat above about 65 °C.

What is the strength of 5A05 square bar?

GB/T 3191 lists minimums of 265 MPa tensile, 120 MPa proof and 15 % elongation for O and H112 bar in the standard size range.

Can 5A-series square bar be used on an IACS-classed vessel?

Only with prior agreement from the society, because UR W25 does not list these alloys. 5083 or 5383 square bar is the usual alternative.

How much does 5A06 square bar weigh?

Side² × 2.64 ÷ 1000 kg per metre. A 50 mm bar weighs about 6.6 kg per metre.

Why not use 6082 square bar instead?

6082-T6 is stronger before welding but loses roughly half its proof strength in the heat-affected zone and needs coating in seawater. 5A-series bar keeps its strength across welds.

Do you cut square bar to a list?

Yes. Metline supplies cut pieces with EN 10204 3.1 certificates against GB/T 3191, export-packed for delivery worldwide.
