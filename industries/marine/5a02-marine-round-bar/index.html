5A02 Marine Grade Aluminium Round Bar

5A02 round bar is extruded from a 2.5 % magnesium aluminium alloy that resists seawater, welds without losing a heat-treated temper and machines into shafts, pins, bushes and fittings. It is supplied in the soft O and as-extruded H112 tempers to GB/T 3191, with AMg2, 5251 and 5052 as its nearest international grades. Overview

5A02 round bar in marine service

Round bar is the most versatile form of a marine alloy. It is turned into shafts and pins, bored into bushes and sleeves, cut into blanks for flanges and bosses, and drawn down into wire for rivets. In a salt-laden environment the alloy choice matters as much as the dimensions, because a part that pits or cracks around a fastener hole soon becomes a maintenance problem.

5A02 belongs to the aluminium-magnesium (5xxx) series. Its 2.0 – 2.8 % magnesium content gives moderate strength and a robust passive film in seawater, while copper is held to 0.10 % or less. Because the alloy is strengthened by its magnesium and by cold work rather than by heat treatment, there is no quench-and-age temper to lose when a bar is welded to a bracket or plate. The heat-affected zone ends up close to the annealed condition that O-temper bar already has.

The grade is designated in China's GB/T 3190 and was formerly known as LF2. Shipyards and equipment makers working to GB and GOST specifications use it widely, and it is common on fishing vessels, ferries, patrol craft and inland waterway boats built to those codes. Elsewhere the same role is filled by 5052 or 5251 rod, and 5A02 is a sensible substitute when the drawing allows it.

Why buyers choose 5A02 rod for marine parts

  • Very good resistance to seawater, salt fog and marine atmospheres
  • Weldable with standard 5xxx filler and no heat-treated temper to lose
  • Ductile enough for cold heading, swaging and flaring in O temper
  • Below 3 % magnesium, so no sensitisation concern in warm engine spaces

Compared with 5052 (Mg 2.2 – 2.8 %, Cr 0.15 – 0.35 %), 5A02 allows slightly lower magnesium and permits manganese in place of chromium. The effect on properties is small, but it is the reason the two grades are described as close equivalents rather than identical.

Fabrication

Turning, welding and finishing 5A02 rod

Turning

Use sharp, polished tools with a large positive rake and a chip-breaker; run high cutting speeds with coolant to stop built-up edge. Expect a better finish from H112 than from fully soft O bar.

Drilling and tapping

Spiral-flute drills with polished flutes clear chips best. Form taps and roll threads give stronger, galling-resistant threads.

Welding

TIG or MIG with ER5356 or ER5183. Clean oxide and grease immediately before welding; preheat is not normally needed.

Cold heading and swaging

O-temper 5A02 heads and swages well, which is why rivets, studs and terminal fittings are made from it.

Surface protection

Clear anodising or a marine primer adds life in the splash zone; avoid copper-containing antifouling paints in direct contact.

Assembly

Isolate from stainless steel, bronze and steel with nylon or PTFE washers and a barrier compound to prevent galvanic attack.

Applications

Typical uses of 5A02 marine round bar

  • Rivets and studs: drawn into wire and headed for riveting aluminium superstructures and fittings
  • Pins and shafts: hinge pins for hatches, doors and davits where loads are moderate
  • Bushes and sleeves: bored-out sleeves and spacers for railings and stanchions
  • Pipe fittings: turned connectors and flanges for fuel, water and ventilation lines
  • Handrail and ladder hardware: end caps, brackets and joint pieces welded to tube
  • Equipment on buoys and pontoons: mounting bosses and housings exposed to splash and spray

FAQ

5A02 marine aluminium (aluminum) round bar: FAQs

Is 5A02 the same as 5052?

Not exactly. Both contain about 2.5 % magnesium, but 5052 uses chromium as its grain-refining addition while 5A02 allows manganese or chromium. Properties and corrosion behaviour are very close, so many engineers accept one for the other after checking the drawing.

What diameters of 5A02 round bar are available?

Extruded bar is normally available from about 8 mm up to roughly 320 mm diameter, in lengths up to about 6 m, with cut pieces on request.

Why does 5A02 bar have a maximum tensile strength in the standard?

The O and H112 conditions are meant to be soft and ductile for heading, swaging and forming. A tensile ceiling ensures the bar has not been accidentally cold-worked beyond what those processes can tolerate.

Can 5A02 round bar be used underwater?

Yes, it resists seawater well. Keep it electrically isolated from more noble metals such as stainless steel and bronze, and protect submerged assemblies with sacrificial anodes.

How heavy is a 100 mm 5A02 round bar?

About 21.0 kg per metre, so a 3 m length weighs roughly 63 kg.

Do you supply a mill certificate?

Yes. Each lot ships with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate showing chemistry and mechanical test results; class-society or third-party witness testing can be arranged when your order calls for it.
