6082 Marine Aluminium Square Bar

6082-T6 is the strongest of the common 6xxx extrusion alloys, with a minimum proof strength of 260 MPa for mid-range bar sizes, and the standard structural aluminium for European and class-approved ship extrusions. As square bar it is used for stiffeners, frames, machinery seats and machined fittings that carry real load. Metline supplies 6082 square bar in O, T4 and T6, cut to length with EN 10204 3.1 certificates, worldwide.

Overview

Why 6082 is the marine structural bar of choice

6082 replaced the older 6351 and HE30 grades as Europe's main structural aluminium alloy, and it is the extrusion grade most often used alongside 5083 plate in aluminium ships, offshore modules and gangways. Its relatively high silicon (0.7 – 1.3 %) and magnesium (0.6 – 1.2 %) give the most Mg2Si strengthening in the 6xxx family, and its manganese addition (0.4 – 1.0 %) keeps the grain structure fine, which supports toughness and strength in thick sections.

Copper is held to 0.10 % or less, so 6082 resists marine atmospheres better than the copper-bearing 6061, although like all 6xxx alloys it should be anodised or painted where seawater contact is frequent. Class societies list 6082 in T5 and T6 for ship extrusions, and Eurocode 9 (EN 1999) gives design values for welded and unwelded members.

Square bar in 6082-T6 suits stiffeners and frames, keel and machinery seats, hatch coamings, brackets and pads, and all kinds of machined fittings. Strength depends on size: the EN 755-2 minimum is 295 / 250 MPa up to 20 mm, rises to 310 / 260 MPa from 20 to 150 mm, and drops to 280 / 240 MPa between 150 and 200 mm because thick bar cools more slowly during quenching.

Before you order 6082 square bar

  • Confirm the side length and therefore the strength band you are designing to
  • Choose T6 for structure and machining, T4 or O for bending followed by ageing
  • Specify any class-society certification required for structural members
  • Allow for weld-zone softening in the structural design

Fabrication

Welding and fabrication

Welding

MIG/TIG with ER5356 (marine standard) or ER5183 for higher fillet strength; ER4043 on crack-prone joints. Weld-zone proof strength falls to roughly half the T6 value; Eurocode 9 gives the reduction factors.

Machining

Excellent in T6: short chips and a good surface; hold-down stresses are lower in T6511 bar.

Bending

T6 bends only to generous radii; bend in O or T4 and age to T6 afterwards.

Finishing

Protective anodising is effective; decorative anodising is slightly less uniform than 6060 or 6063.

Classification

Class requirements for 6082 structural bar

When 6082 square bar forms part of the hull structure or of class-relevant equipment, the classification society's material rules apply. In broad terms they require the material to come from a works approved by the society, to be tested per batch for tensile properties, and to be delivered with a certificate endorsed or issued under the surveyor's supervision. Requirements differ between DNV, ABS, Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas and other societies, so state the society and the rule edition on your order.

ItemWhat is usually required
Alloy and temper6082 in T5 or T6 (as listed in the society's aluminium alloy tables)
TestingTensile test per batch; chemistry per cast; additional tests where the rules call for them
MarkingAlloy, temper, cast or batch number and the surveyor's stamp where applicable
CertificateInspection certificate type 3.1 or 3.2 to EN 10204, as the rules specify
WeldingApproved welding procedures and qualified welders; welded design values from the rules

For non-class items such as outfitting, a standard EN 10204 3.1 certificate to EN 755-2 is normally sufficient.

FAQ

6082 marine aluminium (aluminum) square bar: FAQs

Is 6082 good for marine applications?

Yes. It is the main class-recognised extrusion alloy for ship structure, with low copper for good atmospheric corrosion resistance. Protect it where seawater contact is frequent.

What is the strength of 6082-T6 square bar?

EN 755-2 minimums are 310 MPa tensile and 260 MPa proof for 20 – 150 mm, 295 / 250 MPa up to 20 mm and 280 / 240 MPa for 150 – 200 mm.

Is 6082 stronger than 6061?

Slightly, in extruded bar: 260 MPa versus 240 MPa minimum proof strength in T6 for mid-range sizes.

How much does a 60 mm 6082 square bar weigh?

About 9.72 kg per metre.

What was 6082 called in the UK?

HE30 (or H30) under the former BS 1474 standard.
