6060 Marine Aluminium Square Bar

6060 square bar is a low-alloy, heat-treatable aluminium extrusion with an exceptionally clean surface and the best anodising response of the structural 6xxx grades. Its moderate strength (about 150 MPa minimum proof in T6) suits outfitting, trim, frames and light fittings on boats rather than primary structure. Metline supplies 6060 square bar in T5 and T6, mill finish or anodised, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What 6060 square bar is good for at sea

6060 is the lowest-alloyed member of the common 6xxx extrusion family. Low silicon, magnesium and iron let it flow through a die quickly and leave a smooth, uniform surface, which is why it is the European workhorse for window frames, interior systems and decorative extrusions. On boats the same qualities make it a good choice for visible, lightly loaded parts that will be anodised.

As a solid square bar it is used for trim, cabinet and furniture frames, ladder treads, rubbing strips, light brackets, grab rail stock and the many small machined parts of interior outfitting. The alloy is fully heat-treatable, so it can be supplied in T4 for bending and then aged, in T5 straight from a press quench, or in T6 for the highest strength.

The limitation is strength. A minimum proof strength of 150 MPa in T6 is about 60 % of 6082-T6, so 6060 is not the right bar for load-carrying structure. When in doubt, step up to 6005A or 6082.

Strengths and limits of 6060

  • Best decorative and protective anodising of the 6xxx structural alloys
  • Low copper (≤ 0.10 %) and good corrosion resistance in marine air
  • High thermal conductivity, about 200 W/m·K in T6
  • Moderate strength only; not a primary structural grade

6060 is sometimes confused with 6063. 6063 has more magnesium (0.45 – 0.90 %) and slightly higher strength; 6060 is leaner and extrudes faster. Both are excellent for anodising, and both are widely used in marine outfitting.

Fabrication

Working with 6060

Anodising

Gives a bright, even film in clear, bronze or black. For marine use, specify at least 20 µm sealed film.

Machining

Softer than 6061 or 6082; use sharp, polished tools and high speeds to avoid smearing.

Bending

Bend in T4 and artificially age afterwards for best results.

Welding

Weldable with ER5356 or ER4043; strength near the weld falls to about T4 level.

Surface finishing

Specifying an anodised finish on 6060 bar

Anodising is the main reason buyers choose 6060, so it pays to specify it properly. The film thickness, sealing quality and pre-treatment decide how long the surface stays bright in salt air. A thin decorative film that looks fine indoors can chalk and pit within a few seasons on an open deck.

ExposureRecommended filmNotes
Interior, dry cabins10 – 15 µm, sealedDecorative; any colour
Interior, wet areas and engine room15 – 20 µm, sealedResists condensation and cleaning chemicals
Exterior, deck and superstructure20 – 25 µm, sealedStandard marine-grade film; quality labels often class this as 20 or 25 µm
Wear surfaces (treads, slides)Hard anodising, 25 – 50 µmHigher abrasion resistance, darker colour

Ask for a seal-quality test (for example an admittance or dye-spot test) on exterior parts, and keep finished bars wrapped until installation. Rinse anodised fittings with fresh water periodically; salt deposits left to dry are the main cause of staining. Avoid strongly alkaline or acidic cleaners, which attack the anodic film.

Applications

Marine uses

  • Interior frames for furniture, lockers and panels
  • Trim and edge strips, rubbing strips and cover bars
  • Ladder treads and steps for light duty
  • Window and door hardware in deckhouses
  • Heat-sink blocks for marine electronics, using its high conductivity

FAQ

6060 marine aluminium (aluminum) square bar: FAQs

Is 6060 strong enough for boat structure?

Not for primary structure. With 150 MPa minimum proof strength in T6, it suits trim, frames and light fittings; use 6005A or 6082 for loaded parts.

Is 6060 the same as 6063?

No. 6063 has more magnesium and is slightly stronger; 6060 extrudes faster. Both anodise very well.

Does 6060 corrode in salt air?

It has good resistance in marine atmospheres, especially when anodised. Avoid constant seawater immersion and contact with stainless steel or bronze without isolation.

How much does a 30 mm 6060 square bar weigh?

About 2.43 kg per metre.
