2024 Drawn Seamless Aluminium Tube for Aircraft

2024-T3 drawn seamless tube is the high-strength aluminium tube of aircraft hydraulic and structural systems: about 480 MPa typical tensile strength, good fatigue performance and a fine drawn surface. It is ordered to AMS 4086 for hydraulic lines and AMS 4088 for general tubing. Metline supplies 2024 tube in O, T3 and T4, in imperial and metric sizes, certified to AMS, ASTM and federal specifications, worldwide.

Overview

What is 2024 aircraft drawn tube?

Drawn seamless tube starts as an extruded hollow, which is then cold drawn through a die over a mandrel or plug in several passes. Drawing sets precise outside diameter and wall thickness, improves the surface and, for 2024-T3, adds the cold work that gives the temper its strength.

2024-T3 means the tube is solution heat-treated, cold worked by drawing or straightening, and naturally aged. It offers higher strength than 6061-T6 tube with better fatigue resistance, which suits aircraft hydraulic lines, where pressure pulses load the tube in fatigue, and structural tubes such as struts and control rods. Where the tube must be bent or flared heavily, it is formed in O temper and heat-treated afterwards, or 6061-T6 or 5052 tube is chosen for lower-pressure lines.

Because 2024 has lower corrosion resistance than 6xxx alloys, tube is normally anodised or chemically conversion-coated and painted after fabrication, and installed with attention to dissimilar-metal contact at fittings.

Typical selection logic

  • High-pressure hydraulic lines: 2024-T3 to AMS 4086 (or titanium and stainless on newer aircraft)
  • General structural tube: 2024-T3 to AMS 4088 or WW-T-700/3
  • Formed parts: order O temper, form, then heat-treat
  • Low-pressure, easily formed lines: 6061-T6, 5052-O or 3003

Inspection

Inspection and documentation

  • Tensile tests per lot and hardness or conductivity checks
  • Eddy-current inspection of hydraulic tube where the specification requires
  • Flattening and flaring tests on samples
  • OD, wall, ovality and straightness measurement
  • Inside and outside surface inspection for scratches, die marks and pits
  • EN 10204 3.1 certificate with chemistry, properties and lot traceability
Fabrication

Bending, flaring and protection

Bending

T3 tube bends on mandrel benders with adequate radius (commonly 3 × OD or more). Tighter bends are made in O and the tube is heat-treated afterwards.

Flaring and fittings

Flareless (swaged or bite-type) fittings are common on 2024 hydraulic tube; follow the fitting maker's tube-hardness limits.

Welding

Not fusion-welded; joints use mechanical fittings.

Protection

Anodise or conversion-coat; paint where exposed. Isolate from steel and copper fittings.

Applications

Applications

  • Aircraft hydraulic pressure and return lines
  • Fuel, oil and pneumatic lines on legacy aircraft
  • Control rods, push-pull tubes and torque tubes
  • Struts, braces and seat structures
  • Racing and high-performance vehicle structures

FAQ

2024 aircraft aluminium (aluminum) tube: FAQ

What is the difference between AMS 4086 and AMS 4088?

Both cover 2024-T3 drawn seamless tube. AMS 4086 is specific to hydraulic tube with extra requirements suited to pressure lines; AMS 4088 covers general drawn tube.

Can 2024-T3 tube be bent?

Yes, with mandrel bending and adequate radius. For tight bends, form in O temper and heat-treat afterwards.

Is 2024 tube corrosion resistant?

Only moderately. It should be anodised or conversion-coated and painted, and isolated from dissimilar metals.

Is 2024 stronger than 6061 tube?

Yes. Typical tensile strength of 2024-T3 is about 483 MPa against 310 MPa for 6061-T6, with better fatigue strength.

Do you supply imperial hydraulic sizes?

Yes, for example 1/4 in × 0.028 in, 3/8 in × 0.035 in and 1/2 in × 0.035 in, as well as metric sizes.
