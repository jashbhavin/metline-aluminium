3A21 Aerospace Aluminium Sheet & Plate

3A21 is an aluminium-manganese alloy, the GB counterpart of 3003 and the Russian AMts, used in aircraft and launch vehicles for welded tanks, fuel and oil lines, ducting and formed secondary panels. It is non-heat-treatable, so it is supplied in O, H14, H24, H18 and H112, not in T tempers. Metline supplies 3A21 sheet and plate with mill certificates, worldwide.

Overview

What 3A21 is used for in aerospace

3A21 is one of the older national designations that place a letter in the second position. It is a manganese-strengthened alloy often called "rust-proof aluminium", and it behaves almost exactly like 3003 and EN AW-3003.

Its value in aerospace is a combination of three things: it forms very easily, it welds without hot cracking, and it resists corrosion by fuels, oils, water and the atmosphere. Those properties made 3A21, and its Russian twin AMts, a standard material for welded fuel and oxidiser-system tanks, pipelines, ducting and formed panels in aircraft and launch vehicles designed to GB and GOST documentation. Many of those designs are still produced and maintained, so 3A21 continues to appear on drawings and purchase orders worldwide.

Strength is modest. Annealed 3A21 has a tensile strength of about 100 – 130 MPa, and half-hard H14 about 145 – 180 MPa. That is enough for tanks, lines and formed covers, but not for primary structure, which uses heat-treatable 2xxx and 7xxx alloys such as 2A12 (≈ 2024) and 7A04.

Correction to older data sheets

  • 3A21 is an Al-Mn alloy and cannot be solution heat-treated or aged
  • T3, T4 and T6 tempers do not exist for 3A21; listings that offer them are wrong
  • Strength is set by cold work: O, H14, H24, H18; thick plate is supplied as-rolled H112
  • AMS 4028 and AMS 4029 are 2014-alloy sheet specifications and do not apply to 3A21

Tempers

3A21 tempers and what each is for

O (annealed)

Maximum ductility for deep-drawn tank ends, spun domes, bellows-type parts and tube bending. The usual choice for welded tanks and pipe work.

H14

Half hard. Holds shape in flat and lightly formed panels, covers and brackets; still bends well in thin gauges.

H24

Same strength band as H14 but reached by over-hardening and partial annealing, giving more elongation. Better when parts are bent after blanking.

H18

Full hard. Flat parts, fins and feedstock that will not be formed much.

H112

As-rolled plate above about 6 mm, for machined flanges, bosses and thick welded components.

Not available

T3, T4, T6 and other T tempers. If a drawing calls for them with 3A21, the drawing needs clarifying before material is ordered.

Working with 3A21

Welding, forming and finishing 3A21

Welding

Excellent by TIG and MIG, and historically by gas welding. Al-Mn or Al-Si fillers (for example ER4043) are usual. Tanks and lines are welded in O temper, so the weld zone loses little strength.

Forming

O temper draws, spins and flares without cracking. H14 bends to tight radii in thin sheet; use H24 when bending after cutting.

Brazing

Good brazeability, which suits heat-exchanger and cooling-plate work.

Machining

Soft and gummy in O; machine harder tempers with sharp, polished, high-rake tools.

Surface treatment

Accepts chemical conversion coatings, primer and paint; anodising gives a protective but slightly grey film.

Corrosion

Close to commercially pure aluminium; suitable for fuel, oil and water systems with correct isolation from copper and steel.

Applications

Typical 3A21 aerospace applications

  • Tanks: welded fuel, oil, hydraulic-reservoir and water tanks; tank ends and domes spun or drawn from O sheet
  • Lines and ducting: fuel, oil and air lines, ventilation and cooling ducts (with 3A21 tube from the same alloy family)
  • Formed covers: fairings, access covers, closeouts and shrouds that carry no primary load
  • Cooling hardware: heat-exchanger fins, cold plates and radiator parts
  • Interiors: cabin panels, equipment housings and trays
  • Ground equipment: fuel-bowser tanks, enclosures and covers

Wings, tail surfaces, flight-control parts and other primary structure are not 3A21 applications, whatever older marketing text may claim.

FAQ

3A21 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) sheet: buyer questions

Can 3A21 be supplied in T4 or T6 temper?

No. 3A21 is an Al-Mn alloy and is not heat-treatable, so T tempers do not apply. It is supplied in O, H14, H24, H18 or H112, with strength set by cold work.

Is 3A21 the same as 3003?

It is a close equivalent. 3A21 allows 1.0 – 1.6 % manganese and has no copper minimum, while 3003 requires 0.05 – 0.20 % copper. For aerospace parts, substitute only with the design authority's approval.

What is the Russian equivalent of 3A21?

AMts (АМц), covered by GOST 4784. Drawings from CIS design offices often use that designation.

Which temper should I use for welded tanks?

O temper is normal for tanks and lines: it forms easily and the weld zone loses little strength because the parent sheet is already annealed.

Is 3A21 strong enough for aircraft structure?

Not for primary structure. With 100 – 180 MPa tensile strength depending on temper, it suits tanks, lines, ducts and formed covers. Load-carrying parts use 2xxx or 7xxx alloys.

Do you supply 3A21 internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 3A21 sheet and plate from Mumbai to customers worldwide, with mill certificates and export packing.
