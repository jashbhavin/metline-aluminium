2024 Aerospace Aluminium Rivet Wire

2024 wire is the high-strength aluminium-copper-magnesium wire used to make "DD" solid rivets, pins and special fasteners for aircraft structure. After heat treatment it gives about 280 MPa shear strength, among the highest of the standard aluminium rivet alloys. Metline supplies 2024 rivet and cold-heading wire in coils and straight lengths, in O, T3, T4, T351 and T851, certified to ASTM and AMS specifications, worldwide.

Overview

Why 2024 wire is used for aircraft rivets

Solid rivets are still the most common permanent fastener in aluminium airframes. Each rivet alloy is identified by a letter code and a head marking: 1100 (A), 2117 (AD), 2017 (D), 2024 (DD), 5056 (B) and 7050 (E). 2024 rivets are the strongest of the traditional group and are used where joints carry high shear loads.

The catch is how 2024 behaves after heat treatment. Freshly solution-treated 2024 is soft enough to drive, but it begins to harden at room temperature within minutes. 2024 and 2017 rivets are therefore known as "icebox rivets": they are solution treated, quenched and then held in a freezer to delay natural ageing, and must be driven within a short working time after removal, typically 10 – 20 minutes. Once driven, they age to full strength over several days. Many modern designs replace them with 7050 "E" rivets or 2117 rivets that can be driven as received, but DD rivets remain on many existing aircraft and repair schemes.

The wire itself must head cleanly. That means tight diameter tolerance, a smooth surface free of seams and laps, consistent chemistry and a uniform grain structure, because any defect opens up as a crack when the head is upset to about 1.5 times the shank diameter.

What makes good rivet wire

  • Diameter held to close tolerance along the whole coil
  • Seam-free surface that passes upset and heading tests
  • Uniform response to solution treatment and natural ageing
  • Traceable chemistry and properties for every lot

Tempers

Wire tempers and when to use them

O (annealed)

Softest condition for heavy cold heading. Rivets and blanks headed in O are solution treated and aged afterwards.

T4

Solution treated and naturally aged: the standard condition for 2024 rivet wire and for rivets supplied heat-treated.

T3

Solution treated, cold worked (drawn) and naturally aged; tighter diameter control and slightly higher strength for screw-machine and pin stock.

T351

Stretched to relieve stress; for straight lengths machined into pins and bushings.

T851

Stretched and artificially aged for higher yield strength and better stress-corrosion resistance; used for pins and machined parts that run warm.

Driven rivets

Icebox rivets driven after quenching age at room temperature; the installed condition is commonly referred to as T31.

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) rivet wire: FAQ

Why are 2024 rivets called icebox rivets?

After solution treatment they harden quickly at room temperature. Keeping them frozen delays ageing so they can still be driven; once removed they must be used within a short time, typically 10 – 20 minutes.

What is the head marking of a 2024 rivet?

DD rivets carry two raised dashes on the head.

Which temper should I order for rivet manufacture?

Usually T4 or O, depending on whether the rivets are headed before or after heat treatment. Agree the temper with your heading process.

Is 2024 wire still used for new aircraft?

Less than before; many designs use 7050 E rivets or 2117 AD rivets instead. 2024 remains in use for existing designs, repairs and special fasteners.

What is the shear strength of 2024 rivets?

Typical shear strength of 2024-T4 material is about 283 MPa (41 ksi). Use published allowables for design.
