1050 Aerospace Aluminium Wire

1050 wire is 99.5 % pure aluminium drawn to round, square or flat section. It is chosen in aircraft and ground equipment where a part must bend, head or conduct well rather than carry load: soft rivets, lock and tie wire, bonding and earthing parts, mesh and formed clips. Metline supplies 1050 and 1050A wire in annealed and strain-hardened tempers with mill certificates, worldwide.

Overview

What 1050 wire does in an aircraft programme

1050 is the 99.5 % grade of the 1xxx series. With no deliberate alloying additions, it is the softest and most ductile wire most aerospace buyers will ever specify, and also one of the most conductive.

Strength in 1050 comes only from cold work. Each pass through a drawing die hardens the wire, and an anneal returns it to the fully soft O temper. That gives the buyer a simple choice: order O for wire that will be coiled, wrapped, upset or flattened in a later operation, or a hard temper (H14 to H18) when the wire must hold its shape, run straight through a feeder, or resist handling damage.

In aerospace the grade sits well away from primary structure. Typical duties are secondary and support items where ductility, corrosion resistance and conductivity matter: formed clips, cable ties and lacing, wire mesh for screens and filters, earthing and bonding hardware, gasket and seal reinforcement, and cold-headed blanks for low-load fasteners. On aircraft ground support equipment, 1050 wire turns up in guards, tie-downs and electrical fittings.

When 1050 is the right wire

  • The part is formed, coiled or headed and must not crack
  • Electrical or thermal conductivity is part of the function
  • Loads are low and corrosion resistance matters more than strength
  • A soft wire is needed that will not damage the parts it touches

The H2x tempers are worth knowing about. They are hardened beyond the target and then partially annealed back, which gives the same strength as the matching H1x temper with noticeably more elongation. For wire that is bent sharply after delivery, H24 is often a better choice than H14.

Working with 1050

Forming, joining and finishing 1050 wire

Cold heading

O and H12 heads cleanly with large upset ratios. Use clean dies and a light lubricant; pick-up on tooling, not cracking, is the usual problem.

Bending and coiling

O temper bends flat on itself. Hard tempers spring back more and need a tighter mandrel; H24 is a good compromise.

Welding and brazing

Excellent weldability by TIG and MIG with 1100 or 4043 filler, and good brazeability. Joints lose any strain hardening in the heat-affected zone.

Electrical joints

Remove the oxide before crimping or bolting and use compatible terminals to avoid galvanic attack where copper is involved.

Anodising

Gives a clear, bright anodic film; 1050 is often chosen for decorative or reflective parts for this reason.

Corrosion

Very good resistance to atmosphere, fresh water and many chemicals. Isolate from carbon fibre and copper alloys in wet conditions.

FAQ

1050 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) wire: questions buyers ask

What is the difference between 1050 and 1050A wire?

Both are 99.5 % aluminium. 1050 is the Aluminum Association grade; 1050A is the European EN AW grade, with slightly different limits on zinc and titanium and no vanadium limit. For most wire applications they are interchangeable, but the certificate must match the grade on your drawing.

Which temper of 1050 wire should I order for cold heading or bending?

O or H12 for cold heading and severe forming. For wire that is bent after delivery but must stay reasonably stiff, H24 gives H14-level strength with more elongation.

Can 1050 wire be used for structural rivets?

No. 1050 has a tensile strength of roughly 65 – 95 MPa when annealed, which suits only non-structural fastening. Structural aircraft rivets use 2117, 2017, 2024 or 7050 wire.

Is 1050 wire covered by ASTM B211?

The commercially pure grade in ASTM B211 is 1100. 1050A wire is normally supplied to EN 1301 with EN 573-3 chemistry, or to a customer specification.

How conductive is 1050 aluminium wire?

Annealed 1050 reaches roughly 61 – 62 % IACS. If you need a guaranteed conductivity minimum, specify 1350 electrical-grade wire instead.

Do you supply 1050 wire outside India?

Yes. Metline ships 1050 and 1050A wire worldwide from Mumbai, with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and export packing.
