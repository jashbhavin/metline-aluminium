5251 Aerospace Aluminium Tube

5251 tube is drawn from a 2 % magnesium aluminium alloy that bends, flares and welds easily while resisting corrosion, which is why British aircraft standards adopted it for low-pressure fluid lines as BS L56. Metline supplies 5251 seamless drawn tube in O, H22, H32 and H14 tempers, in straight lengths or coils, certified to BS L56 or EN 754, to buyers worldwide.

Overview

Why 5251 is used for aircraft tubing

Aircraft fluid systems need tube that can be bent to tight radii, flared or beaded at the ends, joined by welding or fittings, and left in service for decades without corrosion. 5251 meets those needs at moderate pressure, and it has been the standard British choice for that work through the L56 specification.

Typical uses are low- and medium-pressure fuel, oil, vent, drain and coolant lines, pneumatic and instrument lines, breather pipes and small air ducts. Higher-pressure hydraulic lines normally use 6061-T6, 5052 or titanium and stainless tube, so 5251 is chosen where formability and corrosion resistance matter more than burst pressure.

L56 tube is drawn and hydraulically tested, so every length has been pressure-proved before delivery. Annealed tube is easiest to bend on the aircraft; H22 and H32 give extra stiffness for straight runs and supports.

Working with 5251 tube

Bending, flaring and joining

Bending

O tube bends to small radii with a mandrel or by hand bender. Harder tempers need larger radii; check ovality after bending against the line drawing.

Flaring and beading

O and H22 accept 37° and 74° flares and beads for hose clamps. Inspect flares for splits on the first articles.

Welding and brazing

TIG welds well with ER5356 or ER5183. 5251 is not normally torch brazed because magnesium makes flux brazing difficult.

Cleaning

Lines for fuel and oxygen-compatible systems need cleaning and capping to the system specification; state this on the order.

FAQ

5251 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) tube: FAQs

What is BS L56 tube?

L56 is the British aerospace specification for drawn, hydraulically tested tube in the 2 % magnesium alloy that corresponds to 5251. It is widely used for low- and medium-pressure fluid lines.

Can 5251 tube be used for hydraulic lines?

It suits low and moderate pressures. High-pressure hydraulic systems generally use 6061-T6, titanium or stainless tube, sized to the system pressure rating.

Which temper bends best?

O (annealed) tube gives the tightest bends and best flares. H22 or H32 is used where a stiffer line is wanted and the bends are gentle.

Is 5251 tube supplied in coils?

Small diameters in the O temper can be supplied in coils; larger and harder tubes are supplied in straight lengths.

What filler wire is used to weld 5251 tube?

ER5356 is the usual choice; ER5183 is an alternative. Avoid 4xxx silicon fillers on magnesium alloys.
