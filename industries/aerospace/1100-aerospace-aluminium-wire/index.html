1100 Aerospace Aluminium Wire

1100 wire is the commercially pure aluminium wire named in US aerospace documents: AMS 4180 for full-hard 1100-H18 wire, AMS-QQ-A-225/1 and ASTM B211 for general wire, and ASTM B316 for rivet and cold-heading stock. It is used for soft "A" rivets, lacing, safety and tie wire, mesh and formed clips. Metline supplies 1100 wire from O to H18, on spools, in coils or cut lengths, with mill certificates, worldwide.

Overview

Why aircraft use 1100 wire

1100 wire is chosen for ductility, not strength. It heads, bends and twists without cracking, it does not gall or dent the softer parts it touches, and it resists corrosion without any coating.

The most familiar aerospace product made from it is the "A" rivet. In the MS/NAS rivet code the letter A means 1100, and A rivets are driven in the as-supplied soft condition for non-structural joints in fairings, ducting, trim and other light parts. Rivet blanks are cold headed from 1100 wire supplied to ASTM B316 or an equivalent cold-heading specification.

Other uses include lacing and tie wire, mesh for screens and filters, formed clips and retainers, earthing and bonding pieces, and filler wire for welding 1100 and 3003 parts. Full-hard 1100-H18 wire to AMS 4180 is used where the wire must stay straight and stiff, for example in feeders and formed springs for very light duty.

Older data sheets sometimes list Cu as 0.05 % max and Si as 0.95 % max on its own; both are wrong for 1100. The correct values are Cu 0.05 – 0.20 % and Si + Fe 0.95 % combined.

Electrical conductivity of 1100 (about 59 % IACS annealed) is a little lower than 1350 electrical-conductor wire, which is guaranteed at 61 % IACS or more. For power conductors, use 1350; for formed hardware and rivets, use 1100.

Tempers

Choosing a temper

O

Fully annealed. Rivets, cold heading, lacing and tie wire, and anything twisted or bent tightly.

H12

Quarter hard. Formed clips and retainers that must keep their shape after light forming.

H14

Half hard. General-purpose wire with a balance of stiffness and ductility; mesh and formed parts.

H16

Three-quarter hard. Small headed pins and light fasteners where some extra strength helps.

H18

Full hard, to AMS 4180. Straight, stiff wire for feeders and very light springs; limited bendability.

Welding wire

1100 is also drawn as filler wire (ER1100) for welding 1100 and 3003; ask for welding-grade spools.

FAQ

1100 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) wire: FAQ

What are A rivets made of?

A rivets are made from 1100 aluminium wire. They are soft, driven as received, and used only for non-structural joints.

Which specification covers 1100-H18 wire?

SAE AMS 4180 covers 1100-H18 wire. General 1100 wire is also supplied to ASTM B211 and AMS-QQ-A-225/1, and rivet stock to ASTM B316.

Can 1100 wire be used as electrical conductor wire?

It conducts well (about 59 % IACS annealed), but conductor wire is normally 1350, which has a guaranteed conductivity minimum.

What diameters of 1100 wire can you supply?

Typically about 0.5 – 12 mm round, on spools, in coils or as cut lengths; square and flat wire to order.

Does 1100 wire need heat treatment after forming?

No. 1100 is non-heat-treatable. If forming has work-hardened it too much, it can be annealed at around 345 °C.

Need 1100 wire for rivets or formed parts?

Send the diameter, temper, put-up and specification. We will confirm availability, testing and delivery.
