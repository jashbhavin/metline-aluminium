5083 Aerospace Aluminium Tube & Pipe

5083 tube and pipe combine the highest strength of the non-heat-treatable aluminium alloys with excellent weldability, seawater resistance and toughness at cryogenic temperatures. In aerospace programmes it is used mainly for cryogenic propellant and liquid-gas lines, launch-site and test-stand piping, and welded ground-equipment frames. Metline supplies seamless 5083 tube and pipe in O, H111 and H112 to ASTM B241 and B221, worldwide.

Overview

Where 5083 tube is used in aerospace

5083 has about 4.4 % magnesium and 0.7 % manganese. It is not heat treatable, so welded joints do not lose the large share of strength that 6061-T6 loses, and its strength and toughness both increase as temperature drops toward −196 °C and below.

That combination makes 5083 a cryogenic and welded-structure alloy rather than an aircraft fluid-line alloy. On aircraft, fuel and oil lines use 5052-O or 6061-T6 and hydraulic pressure lines use 6061-T6, 2024-T3, titanium or steel. 5083 tube and pipe find their aerospace roles on the ground: transfer lines and vent stacks for liquid oxygen, liquid nitrogen and liquefied natural gas at launch sites and test stands, welded frames and handrails for ground support equipment, and piping in corrosive coastal installations.

The same properties make 5083 pipe a standard choice for LNG and industrial gas plants, shipbuilding and offshore platforms. Buyers in aerospace often share suppliers and specifications with those sectors, so ASTM B241 pipe schedules dominate orders.

Why specify 5083 tube

  • Strongest non-heat-treatable tube alloy, with welded strength close to the base metal
  • Excellent resistance to seawater, marine air and many industrial environments
  • No ductile-to-brittle transition: tough and stronger at cryogenic temperature
  • Available as seamless pipe in standard schedules and as extruded tube to drawing

Fabrication

Welding, bending and service limits

Welding

TIG and MIG with ER5183 filler to match strength. Clean oxide and hydrocarbons immediately before welding to avoid porosity, which matters for leak-tight cryogenic lines.

Bending

O and H111 bend well with mandrel support; allow larger radii than for 5052 because of higher strength and springback.

Temperature limit

Avoid sustained service above about 65 °C, where sensitisation can cause intergranular and stress corrosion. This rules 5083 out of hot-gas and engine-bay piping.

Cryogenic cleaning

Lines for oxygen service must be cleaned and certified to the oxygen-cleanliness standard of the system.

Testing

Hydrostatic or pneumatic pressure tests and NDT of welds per the piping code.

Corrosion

Excellent in marine air; isolate from copper and stainless parts in wet service.

Applications

Typical applications

  • Launch-site and test-stand piping for liquid oxygen, liquid nitrogen and LNG
  • Cryogenic storage transfer lines, vent stacks and vaporiser connections
  • Ground support equipment frames, handrails and platforms
  • Marine and offshore: seawater piping, railings and superstructure
  • Industrial gas and LNG plants built to ASME B31.3

A typical example is a liquid-nitrogen transfer line at a rocket test stand: Schedule 40 5083-O pipe, TIG welded with ER5183, cleaned and pressure tested, with stainless transition joints at the valve skids.

FAQ

5083 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) tube: FAQs

Is 5083 tube used for aircraft fuel or hydraulic lines?

Rarely. Aircraft lines use 5052-O, 6061-T6 or 2024-T3. 5083 tube is used mainly for cryogenic, launch-site and ground-equipment piping.

Why is 5083 pipe used for cryogenic service?

It becomes stronger and stays tough at very low temperature, welds without major strength loss and resists corrosion, so it is standard for LNG and industrial-gas lines.

Which filler metal is used to weld 5083 pipe?

ER5183 is the usual choice for full strength; ER5356 for lower-stress joints.

What pipe schedules are available in 5083?

Seamless 5083 pipe is commonly supplied in Schedule 10, 40, 80 and 160 to ASME B36.10 dimensions.

Can 5083 tube be used in hot environments?

Avoid sustained temperatures above about 65 °C; sensitisation can lead to intergranular and stress-corrosion cracking.

Do you ship 5083 pipe worldwide?

Yes. Metline supplies 5083 tube and pipe worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and export packing.
