2024 (2A12) Aircraft Aluminium Sheet & Plate

2024-T3 sheet is the classic fuselage-skin material: high strength, excellent fatigue performance and slow crack growth, usually supplied as Alclad for corrosion protection. 2024-T351 plate is machined into frames, spars and fittings. Metline supplies bare and Alclad 2024 sheet and plate, and the close equivalent 2A12, in O, T3, T4, T351, T81 and T851, certified to AMS, ASTM and EN standards, to customers worldwide.

Overview

Why 2024 sheet and plate is used on aircraft

Pressurised fuselages and lower wing skins are loaded in tension and cycled on every flight. The governing properties are fatigue strength, fracture toughness and resistance to fatigue crack growth, and 2024-T3 has been the reference material for those properties since the 1930s.

Sheet (up to about 6 mm) is normally supplied in T3: solution treated, cold worked by flattening and stretching, and naturally aged. Much of it is Alclad 2024, which carries a thin layer of high-purity 1230 aluminium on each face, typically about 5 % of thickness per side for sheet under 1.6 mm and 2.5 % per side for thicker material. The cladding corrodes sacrificially, so scratches and rivet holes do not expose the core to attack.

Plate (over about 6 mm) is supplied in T351, stretched to relieve quench stresses so it stays flat when machined into frames, spars, ribs and bulkheads. Where higher yield strength or better stress-corrosion resistance is needed, T851 plate (artificially aged) is used, and for the most demanding damage-tolerant applications the higher-purity derivatives 2124-T851 and 2524-T3 are specified.

Quick selection guide

  • Fuselage and wing skins: Alclad 2024-T3 sheet
  • Machined frames, spars and fittings: 2024-T351 plate
  • Parts needing higher yield or better SCC resistance: T81 sheet / T851 plate
  • Severe forming: O temper, then heat-treat to T42 or T62

Tempers

2024 sheet and plate tempers

T3

Solution treated, cold worked, naturally aged. Standard for sheet; best fatigue and crack-growth behaviour.

T351

Solution treated, stretched 1.5 – 3 %, naturally aged. Standard plate temper for machining.

T4 / T42

Solution treated and naturally aged without controlled cold work. T42 is the user-heat-treated equivalent, e.g. after forming from O.

T81 / T851

T3 sheet or T351 plate artificially aged. Higher yield strength, better stress-corrosion resistance and better elevated-temperature stability; lower toughness.

O

Annealed for stretch forming, drawing and tight bends before heat treatment.

T361 / T861

Higher cold-work variants offered for specific programmes; specify only when the drawing calls for them.

Tolerances & inspection

Tolerances, testing and documentation

Thickness, width, length and flatness tolerances follow ANSI H35.2 for ASTM and AMS orders and EN 485-3/-4 for EN orders. Aerospace plate is commonly ultrasonically inspected to AMS-STD-2154 or ASTM B594, with the class stated on the order.

  • Tensile tests per lot in the directions the specification requires
  • Electrical conductivity and hardness checks of heat treatment
  • Cladding thickness verification for Alclad material
  • Surface inspection for scratches, roll marks and corrosion
  • EN 10204 3.1 certificate with chemistry, properties and lot traceability
Working with 2024

Forming, machining, joining and protection

Forming

T3 sheet forms moderately; minimum bend radii are typically about 2.5 – 4 × thickness depending on gauge. Severe forming is done in O or freshly quenched (W) condition.

Machining

T351 plate machines well; balance material removal on both faces to keep parts flat.

Joining

Riveting and bolting are standard; adhesive bonding is used for panels. 2024 is not fusion-welded in structure.

Corrosion protection

Use Alclad sheet where possible; anodise or conversion-coat and prime bare parts; seal faying surfaces.

Heat exposure

Naturally aged tempers change if held above about 120 °C; T81/T851 are better for warm service.

Handling

Protect Alclad surfaces: scratches through the cladding reduce corrosion protection.

Applications

Applications

  • Fuselage skins and lower wing skins (Alclad 2024-T3)
  • Frames, bulkheads, ribs and spars machined from T351 plate
  • Stringers and stiffeners formed from sheet
  • Repair doublers and patches on 2024 airframes
  • Missile and space structures
  • Truck wheels, tooling and high-strength sporting parts

FAQ

2024 aircraft aluminium (aluminum) sheet: FAQ

What is Alclad 2024-T3?

2024-T3 sheet with a thin layer of 1230 high-purity aluminium bonded to each face. The cladding protects the core from corrosion and slightly lowers strength.

What is the difference between 2024-T3 and T351?

T3 applies to sheet that is cold worked by flattening; T351 applies to plate that is stretched to relieve stresses. Strength levels are similar.

Is 2A12 equivalent to 2024?

2A12 has essentially the same composition and is a close equivalent, but aerospace orders must state the designation and standard the drawing requires.

Why is 2024 used for fuselage skins instead of 7075?

2024-T3 resists fatigue crack growth and has higher toughness, which matters for pressurised skins loaded in tension. 7075 is stronger but less damage tolerant.

Can 2024 sheet be welded?

Not by conventional fusion welding for structure; riveting, bolting and bonding are used.

What sheet sizes are common?

1220 × 2440 mm, 1250 × 2500 mm, 1500 × 3000 mm and 48 × 144 in, with cut-to-size blanks on request.
