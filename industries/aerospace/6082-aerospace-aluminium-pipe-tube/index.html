6082 Aerospace Aluminium Tube & Pipe (T6)

6082-T6 tube is the standard European structural aluminium tube and, as BS L114 and EN 2390, an aerospace tube for frames, struts, push-pull rods, seat and cargo structures and ground equipment. It offers about 290 – 310 MPa minimum tensile strength, good weldability and good corrosion resistance. Metline supplies seamless drawn and extruded 6082 round, square and rectangular tube in O, T4 and T6, certified to aerospace and EN standards, worldwide.

Overview

Where 6082 tube is used in aerospace

Tube is the most efficient shape for members loaded in compression, torsion or bending in every direction. 6082-T6 combines good strength with weldability and corrosion resistance, which makes it a natural choice for tubular frames that must be welded, bent or mechanically joined.

In British and European aerospace practice the alloy is covered by BS L114 for drawn 6082-T6 tube and by EN 2390 for AL-P6082-T6 tubes for structures with walls from 0.6 to 12.5 mm. Typical uses are seat and cargo-system frames, interior monument structure, push-pull control rods for secondary systems, antenna masts, stretcher and equipment racks, UAV airframes and, above all, ground support equipment such as towbars, stairs and maintenance stands.

Tube for fuel, hydraulic and oxygen lines follows its own specifications: 6061-T6 (AMS 4083), 5052 or 3003 drawn tube for low-pressure lines and corrosion-resistant steel or titanium for high-pressure hydraulics. 6082 is not a standard hydraulic-line material, and it is not used for landing-gear members or engine exhaust parts, which need far stronger or hotter-running alloys.

Why engineers specify 6082 tube

  • Strong for a weldable 6xxx alloy: about 310 MPa minimum tensile in drawn T6
  • Low copper gives good corrosion resistance without cladding
  • Seamless drawn tube offers close tolerances and smooth bores for rods and struts
  • Matches 6082 bar, plate and extrusions used elsewhere in the assembly

Tube types

Seamless drawn, seamless extruded and porthole tube

Seamless drawn tube

Extruded from a pierced billet, then cold-drawn over a mandrel to size before heat treatment. Closest tolerances, best surface and the form used for BS L114 and most aerospace orders.

Seamless extruded tube

Extruded over a mandrel without drawing. Used for heavier walls and larger diameters; tolerances to EN 755-7 or ANSI H35.2.

Porthole (structural) tube

Extruded through a bridge die, which leaves longitudinal weld seams. Fine for many GSE and architectural uses but generally not accepted for pressure or aerospace structural tube unless the specification allows it.

Square and rectangular tube

Normally extruded; used for frames, rails and racks.

Tempers

T6 for structure, T4 for tube that will be bent or flared and then aged, O for severe forming.

Ask for seamless

If the drawing says "seamless", state it on the order; porthole tube is the cheaper default in many markets.

Tolerances & inspection

Tolerances and inspection for aerospace tube

  • Dimensions: OD, wall thickness, ovality and straightness to EN 754-7, EN 755-7 or the aerospace standard
  • Surface: inside and outside free of laps, die lines and corrosion; bores cleaned and ends capped
  • Mechanical tests: tensile tests per lot; flattening or expansion tests where specified
  • Non-destructive testing: eddy-current or ultrasonic inspection of seamless tube on request
  • Certification: EN 10204 3.1 with cast and lot traceability
Working with 6082 tube

Welding, bending and finishing 6082 tube

Welding

TIG and MIG with ER4043, ER5356 or ER5183. The heat-affected zone loses roughly 40 – 50 % of T6 strength; design welded frames to as-welded allowables or re-heat-treat.

Bending

Bend T4 or O tube with mandrel support, then age to T6. T6 tube bends only to large radii (typically at least 3 – 4 × OD).

End fittings

Swaged, riveted or bonded end fittings are common on push-pull rods; machined 6082 or 7075 inserts are used.

Finishing

Anodise or prime; boric-sulphuric or chromic anodising where aerospace process specifications require.

Corrosion

Good in atmospheric and marine service; seal tube ends and drain holes to avoid trapped water.

Machining

Good in T6; use sharp tools to avoid burrs on thin walls.

Applications

Typical aerospace uses of 6082 tube

  • Seat, cargo and interior frames where welded or riveted tube structures save weight
  • Push-pull rods and struts for secondary systems, doors and fairings
  • Antenna masts, sensor booms and equipment racks
  • UAV and light-aircraft airframes built from tubular frames
  • Ground support equipment: towbars, passenger stairs, maintenance stands, engine dollies and stretcher frames

A typical example is an aircraft towbar: the main beam is 6082-T6 round or rectangular tube, welded to machined 6082 end blocks and finished with anodising and paint for outdoor service.

FAQ

6082 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) tube: FAQs

Which aerospace standards cover 6082 tube?

BS L114 covers drawn 6082-T6 tube in the British aerospace series, and EN 2390 covers AL-P6082-T6 tubes for structures with walls from 0.6 to 12.5 mm. Commercial orders use EN 754-2 (drawn) or EN 755-2 (extruded).

Is 6082 tube suitable for hydraulic or fuel lines on aircraft?

Not normally. Aircraft fluid lines use alloys and specifications written for that purpose, such as 6061-T6 to AMS 4083 or 5052 and 3003 drawn tube for low pressure. 6082 tube is used for structure.

What is the strength of 6082-T6 tube?

Extruded tube to EN 755-2 has minimum tensile strength of 290 MPa for walls up to 5 mm and 310 MPa from 5 to 25 mm, with proof stress of 250 and 260 MPa respectively.

What is the difference between seamless and porthole tube?

Seamless tube is made over a mandrel and has no longitudinal welds; porthole tube is formed in a bridge die and contains seam welds. Aerospace and pressure applications normally require seamless tube.

Can 6082-T6 tube be bent?

Only to large radii in T6. For tight bends, use T4 or O tube with mandrel bending and age it to T6 afterwards.

Do you cut 6082 tube to length?

Yes. Metline supplies full lengths or cut pieces, deburred and capped, with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate, shipped worldwide.
