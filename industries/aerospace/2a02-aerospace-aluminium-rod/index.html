Overview

What 2A02 aluminium is

2A02, known before the 1990s designation reform as LY2, belongs to the Chinese duralumin family but with an unusual balance: relatively low copper (2.6 – 3.2 %) and high magnesium (2.0 – 2.4 %). This chemistry produces S-phase (Al2CuMg) precipitates that resist coarsening, giving better strength retention at temperature than the standard 2A12 (2024-type) alloy.

The alloy was developed for parts of jet engines that are too warm for ordinary aluminium but do not justify titanium: low-pressure axial compressor blades, impellers and vanes, plus brackets and fittings in warm zones. It is mainly used in the artificially aged T6 condition, sometimes in T4 for parts that will be formed or forged before final ageing.

2A02 is a national Chinese grade and has no exact Aluminum Association equivalent. Buyers outside China usually encounter it on drawings for equipment or engines designed to GB or HB standards. When a Western alternative is needed, 2618A or 2219 are the usual candidates, subject to engineering approval.

Key points for buyers

  • GB grade with no exact ASTM or EN equivalent; certify to GB/T 3190 chemistry
  • Mainly supplied as extruded bar in T6, also T4
  • Chosen for heat resistance rather than maximum room-temperature strength
  • Not covered by ASTM B221 or ISO 209, despite appearing with them on some listings

Properties

Properties of 2A02 bar

Mechanical property minimums for 2A02 extruded bar are set by GB/T 3191 according to temper and diameter, and are reported on the mill certificate for each lot. We do not publish a single figure here because published values vary with bar size and test direction, and older data sheets mix typical and minimum values. As a guide, 2A02-T6 sits in the same strength class as 2A12-T4 at room temperature but keeps its properties better after long exposure in the 150 – 250 °C range.

Tempers

2A02 tempers

T6

Solution heat-treated and artificially aged. The normal service condition for compressor blades and hot parts, giving the best heat resistance.

T4

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged. More ductile; used for parts formed or upset before final ageing.

O / F

Annealed or as-fabricated stock for forging and forming, heat treated by the user afterwards.

Some older listings show T651, T3 and T8 for 2A02 rod. Those are not normal supply conditions for this alloy; if a drawing calls for them, we clarify the intended condition with the designer before quoting.

Applications

Applications of 2A02

  • Axial compressor blades and vanes in the cooler stages of turbojet and turbofan engines
  • Impellers and rotating parts that run warm
  • Engine-area fittings and brackets that see sustained heat
  • Precision turned parts that must hold strength at operating temperature

FAQ

2A02 aluminium (aluminum) rod: FAQ

What is the equivalent of 2A02 aluminium?

There is no exact ASTM or EN equivalent. 2A02 (formerly LY2) is a Chinese heat-resistant Al-Cu-Mg alloy; 2618A and 2219 are the nearest Western options for similar duties, but only with design approval.

What is 2A02 used for?

Mainly axial compressor blades, impellers and other engine parts that run at raised temperatures, plus fittings in warm zones.

Which temper is 2A02 supplied in?

Normally T6 (solution treated and artificially aged) for service parts, and T4 for parts that will be formed before final ageing.

Is 2A02 covered by ASTM B221?

No. 2A02 is a GB grade; extruded bar is supplied to GB/T 3191 with chemistry to GB/T 3190.

Can 2A02 be welded?

It is not intended for fusion welding. Parts are machined from bar or forged and joined mechanically.
