7014 Aerospace Aluminium Bar & Rod

7014 is a British high-strength Al-Zn-Mg-Mn-Cu aerospace alloy, used as extruded or rolled bar, forging stock and machined parts in the solution-treated and overaged condition. It appears mainly on UK and legacy airframe and engine drawings. Overview

What is 7014 aluminium bar?

7014 belongs to the 7xxx family, strengthened by zinc and magnesium precipitates. Compared with 7075 it uses more magnesium and less zinc, and replaces chromium with about 0.5 % manganese for grain control, while a modest copper addition supports stress-corrosion resistance.

The alloy was developed for British aerospace use and is specified through BS aerospace (L-series) and Ministry of Defence specifications. Bar is supplied as extruded or rolled product, either for machining or as forging stock under BS L 172. Finished parts are used in the solution-treated and overaged condition, which gives high strength together with much better resistance to stress-corrosion cracking than the peak-aged T6 condition.

Today 7014 is a specialised, low-volume material. Most demand comes from maintenance, spares and re-manufacture of existing aircraft and engines whose drawings call for it. For new designs, engineers generally choose 7075-T73, 7050 or 7175, which are covered by SAE AMS specifications and are widely available.

Chemical composition

7014 chemical composition (wt %)

7014 is defined by British aerospace specifications rather than by a widely published international data sheet. Its nominal composition, given in the titles of BS L 171 and BS L 172, is Zn 5.7 – Mg 2.7 – Mn 0.5 – Cu 0.5. The ranges below are the indicative limits most often quoted for the alloy; the governing values are those of the specification and revision on your order, and every lot is certified against it.

Properties

Properties and heat treatment

Mechanical properties of 7014 are set by the governing specification for each product form and section size, and are reported on the certificate for the lot. We do not publish single "typical" figures because the published data for this alloy are sparse and older sources disagree; quoting them would be misleading.

In practice, 7014 bar in the solution-treated and overaged condition is used where high strength is needed together with good resistance to stress-corrosion cracking, similar in purpose to 7075-T73. Physical properties are those of typical 7xxx alloys: density about 2.78–2.80 g/cm³, modulus of elasticity about 70–72 GPa and a thermal expansion of about 23 µm/m·°C.

FAQ

7014 aerospace bar: frequently asked questions

What is 7014 aluminium?

A British high-strength 7xxx aerospace alloy with nominal composition Zn 5.7, Mg 2.7, Mn 0.5 and Cu 0.5, used in the solution-treated and overaged condition.

Which standard covers 7014 bar?

BS L 172 covers 7014 forging stock, including extruded and rolled bar, and BS L 171 covers the forgings made from it. Legacy UK MoD specifications and purchaser specifications are also used.

Is 7014 the same as 7075?

No. 7014 has more magnesium, less zinc and copper, and uses manganese instead of chromium. 7075-T73 is a common approved alternative, but only with design authority approval.

Is 7014 bar readily available?

It is a low-volume material made to order. Send the specification and size, and we will confirm the sourcing route or suggest an AMS-specified alternative.

Need 7014 bar for a legacy drawing?

Send the drawing, specification and quantity. We will confirm sourcing, certification and a delivered price, or propose an alternative.
