Aerospace tube and pipe

2219 Aerospace Aluminium Tube and Pipe

2219 tube is chosen for aerospace fluid lines, struts and welded tubular structures that must work at cryogenic or raised temperatures, or be welded to 2219 tanks and fittings. It is supplied as drawn seamless tube, for example 2219-T8511 to AMS 4066, and as extruded seamless tube and pipe to ASTM B241. Metline supplies 2219 tube cut to length and fully certified to customers worldwide.

Overview

Where 2219 tube fits

Most aircraft hydraulic and fuel tube is 6061-T6, 2024-T3 or, for high pressure, titanium or stainless steel. 2219 tube fills a narrower but important niche: systems that are welded, that run cold enough to embrittle other materials, or that must keep strength at temperatures where 6061 and 2024 soften.

Typical cases are propellant feed and vent lines on launch vehicles, which are welded to 2219 tanks and carry liquid oxygen or hydrogen; ducting near engines; and tubular struts in spacecraft structures. Because the tube and the tank are the same alloy, welds join like to like with 2319 filler and the system has uniform thermal expansion.

Seamless tube is standard for pressure service. Drawn seamless tube gives thinner walls, tighter tolerances and a better surface; extruded seamless tube and pipe cover larger diameters and heavier walls.

Benefits of 2219 tube

  • Weldable to 2219 tanks, fittings and forgings
  • Strength and toughness retained at cryogenic temperatures
  • Better strength retention than 6061 or 2024 at 150 – 250 °C
  • Good stress-corrosion resistance in T8 tempers

Fabrication

Bending, welding and finishing

Bending

Bend in O or T3-type tempers where possible, then age. T8 tubes need larger bend radii and mandrel support.

Welding

Orbital GTAW with 2319 filler is common for lines. Clean joint ends thoroughly to avoid porosity.

Fittings

Flared and swaged fittings work with suitable tempers; check flaring test requirements in the specification.

Protection

Anodise or conversion coat the outside; internal surfaces are cleaned to the service requirement.

Applications

  • Welded tubular struts and frames in spacecraft and launch vehicles

FAQ

2219 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) tube: FAQ

Which specification covers 2219 drawn seamless tube?

SAE AMS 4066 covers drawn round seamless 2219 tube in T8511 (early revisions called it T851). ASTM B210 is the general drawn-tube standard.

Is 2219 tube suitable for liquid hydrogen?

Yes. 2219 keeps its strength and toughness at liquid-hydrogen temperature and is widely used for cryogenic propellant lines.

Can 2219 tube be welded to 2219 tanks?

Yes, with 2319 filler. Matching alloys give consistent weld behaviour and thermal expansion.

What is the difference between drawn and extruded seamless tube?

Drawn tube is cold drawn after extrusion, giving thinner walls, tighter tolerances and better surface. Extruded seamless tube covers larger sizes and heavier walls.

Can you clean tube for oxygen service?

Cleaning and capping can be arranged to the standard named on your order.
