5083 Aerospace Aluminium Sheet & Plate

5083 plate is the strongest non-heat-treatable aluminium plate in general supply, with excellent weldability, seawater resistance and toughness at cryogenic temperatures. In aerospace it is used for cryogenic tanks, launch and test-site structures, ground support equipment, shelters and tooling plate. Metline supplies 5083 sheet and plate in O, H111, H116, H321 and H32 to AMS 4056, ASTM B209 and B928, worldwide.

Overview

What 5083 plate does in aerospace

5083 is an aluminium-magnesium-manganese alloy with about 4.4 % magnesium. It reaches the highest strength of the non-heat-treatable alloys, keeps that strength well after welding and is one of the few aluminium alloys qualified for service down to liquid-gas temperatures.

Aircraft primary structure uses 2xxx and 7xxx plate; statements that 5083 forms fuselages, wings or tail surfaces are not borne out by airframe practice. 5083 plate is used where welded, corrosion-resistant plate is needed: cryogenic propellant and storage tanks, launch pads and test stands, ground support equipment, air-transportable shelters and containers, ramp and deck structures on ships that operate aircraft, and cast or rolled tooling plate for jigs and fixtures.

Outside aerospace, the same plate is the default for ship hulls, LNG tanks, road tankers and armour, which keeps it widely available in a broad range of sizes and tempers.

Tempers

Choosing a 5083 temper

O / H111

Annealed; most formable and toughest. Standard for cryogenic tanks and AMS 4056 orders.

H116 / H321

Strain-hardened with controlled processing and tested for exfoliation and intergranular corrosion (ASTM B928). The marine and exposed-structure tempers.

H32

Strain-hardened and stabilised; similar strength to H321 without the corrosion testing.

H112

As rolled for thick plate; properties guaranteed at a lower level than H116.

Cast tooling plate

Cast and precision-milled 5083 plate for flat, stress-free jigs and fixtures.

Armour

H131 and related tempers to MIL-DTL-46027 for armoured structures.

Fabrication

Welding, forming and service limits

Welding

MIG and TIG with ER5183; as-welded joints reach about the O-temper minimum strength.

Forming

O and H111 form well; H321 and H116 need larger bend radii.

Machining

Gummy; use sharp, polished tools and coolant. Cast tooling plate machines cleanly and stays flat.

Temperature

Avoid sustained service above about 65 °C, where sensitisation can cause intergranular and stress corrosion.

Cryogenic

Strength rises and toughness is retained at −196 °C; 5083-O is a standard LNG and liquid-oxygen tank alloy.

Corrosion testing

For exposed structure, order H116 or H321 to ASTM B928, which requires G67 mass-loss and G66 exfoliation tests.

Applications

Typical applications

  • Cryogenic tanks for liquid oxygen, nitrogen and LNG at launch and test sites
  • Ground support equipment platforms, ramps and welded frames
  • Air-transportable shelters, containers and transit cases
  • Tooling plate for jigs, fixtures and assembly tools
  • Naval aviation: deck structures and equipment on ships that operate aircraft
  • Other sectors: marine hulls, tankers, armour and pressure vessels

FAQ

5083 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) plate: FAQs

What does AMS 4056 cover?

AMS 4056 covers 5083 sheet and plate in the annealed O temper, about 1.3 to 203 mm thick. Other tempers are ordered to ASTM B209, B928 or EN 485.

Is 5083 plate used for aircraft wings or fuselages?

No. Airframe structure uses heat-treatable 2xxx and 7xxx alloys. 5083 is used for cryogenic tanks, ground equipment, shelters and tooling.

What is the difference between H116 and H321?

Both are strain-hardened tempers with the same minimum strength and are tested for exfoliation and intergranular corrosion under ASTM B928; they differ in processing route.

Why is 5083 used for cryogenic tanks?

It becomes stronger and stays tough at very low temperature, and it welds well, so large tanks can be fabricated and used safely.

Can 5083 plate be used above 65 °C?

Sustained service above about 65 °C is not recommended because of sensitisation; 5454 is the usual alternative for warm service.

Do you supply cut-to-size 5083 plate worldwide?

Yes. Metline ships 5083 plate cut to size worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and export packing.
