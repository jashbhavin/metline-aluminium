5B05 Aerospace Aluminium Rivet Wire

5B05, formerly LF10, is a Chinese aluminium-magnesium alloy made specifically for rivet wire. With about 5 % magnesium it gives rivets that resist corrosion, need no heat treatment before driving and are suited to joining magnesium alloy and Al-Mg structures. Metline supplies 5B05 round and flat wire in O and hard tempers to GB/T 3196, in coils or cut lengths, to buyers worldwide.

Overview

Why a dedicated rivet alloy

Solid rivets must be soft enough to head without cracking, strong enough in shear once driven, and compatible with the parts they join. Heat-treatable rivet alloys such as 2117 and 2017 need ageing or ice-box storage, and their copper content can cause galvanic problems in magnesium and Al-Mg structures.

5B05 solves this with about 5 % magnesium and no copper of significance. The rivets are driven in the as-supplied condition, gain strength from the cold work of heading, and behave well against magnesium alloy castings and 5xxx sheet. In aircraft and helicopters, 5B05 rivets are used for joints in magnesium gearbox housings and castings, in Al-Mg tanks and panels, and in areas exposed to moisture where copper-bearing rivets could corrode.

The alloy is comparable in purpose to 5056 rivet wire in western practice. Both are Al-5Mg alloys used where corrosion compatibility matters more than the highest shear strength.

Properties

Rivet properties and physical data

Rivet wire is judged mainly by shear strength, measured on the wire or on driven test rivets. 5B05 gives shear strength in the same general class as 5056 rivets; for reference, US 5056 solid rivets are rated at 24 ksi (about 165 MPa) minimum single-shear strength. Exact acceptance values for 5B05 are set by GB/T 3196 by temper and diameter and are reported on the certificate.

FAQ

5B05 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) rivet wire: FAQs

What is 5B05 used for?

Mainly for solid rivets that join magnesium alloy parts and Al-Mg structures, where copper-bearing rivets could cause galvanic corrosion.

Is 5B05 the same as LF10?

Yes. LF10 is the former Chinese designation for the alloy now called 5B05.

Do 5B05 rivets need heat treatment?

No. They are driven in the as-supplied condition, unlike 2017 or 2024 rivets that need solution treatment or cold storage.

What is the closest western equivalent?

5056 rivet wire is the nearest in purpose and composition, though limits differ. Substitution requires approval from the design authority.

Which temper should I order for heading?

Annealed (O) wire heads without cracking. Hard (HX8) wire is used for straight lengths or special parts.
