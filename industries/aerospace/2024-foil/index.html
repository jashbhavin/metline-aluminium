2024 Aerospace Aluminium Foil and Thin Sheet

2024 aerospace foil is thin-gauge 2024 aluminium, typically 0.1 to 0.5 mm, rolled from the classic high-strength Al-Cu-Mg airframe alloy and usually supplied in the T3 or O temper. It is used where a very thin layer must still carry real load: fibre-metal laminates, shims, doublers, bonded skins and fatigue test specimens. Overview

What is 2024 aerospace foil?

In aerospace purchasing, "2024 foil" rarely means kitchen-style foil below 0.2 mm. It usually refers to very thin 2024 sheet and strip, from about 0.1 mm up to roughly 0.5 mm, rolled to the same chemistry and heat-treatment rules as heavier 2024 aircraft sheet.

2024 contains around 4.4 % copper and 1.5 % magnesium. After solution heat treatment and natural ageing it reaches a tensile strength close to 480 MPa, which is roughly three times that of the 1xxx and 8xxx alloys used for packaging foil. That strength, combined with excellent fatigue crack growth resistance, is why 2024-T3 remains the standard fatigue-critical skin alloy decades after it was introduced.

At foil gauges, the alloy behaves differently from plate. Thin material cools almost instantly when quenched, so properties are uniform through the thickness, but it is also more sensitive to surface damage, handling kinks and corrosion, because a small pit consumes a larger share of the section. Most buyers therefore choose Alclad 2024 or specify careful surface protection and packing.

Typical reasons to specify 2024 foil

  • Fibre-metal laminates such as GLARE, which bond thin 2024-T3 sheets with glass-fibre prepreg layers
  • Precision shims and spacers that must not creep or crush under bolt loads
  • Bonded doublers, repair patches and fatigue-test coupons that must match parent 2024 skin
  • Thin structural laminations in missiles, drones and instrument housings

Working with 2024 foil

Forming, bonding and protection

Forming

T3 foil bends readily around moderate radii. For tight shapes, form in the O or freshly quenched (W) condition and then age; parts left too long in W harden naturally and lose formability within hours.

Adhesive bonding

Laminates and bonded doublers depend on surface preparation. Phosphoric-acid or chromic-acid anodising, or approved sol-gel treatments, are normally applied before priming and bonding.

Welding

2024 is not considered fusion-weldable for structure: it is prone to hot cracking and loses strength. Join foil by bonding, riveting or, in some cases, friction stir or resistance methods qualified by the user.

Corrosion protection

Bare 2024 must be anodised, conversion coated or primed. Alclad material carries its own sacrificial layer, which is why it dominates for external skins.

Handling

Thin gauges kink and scratch easily. Specify interleaving or film, avoid sliding sheets across each other, and store indoors away from condensation.

Cutting

Shear, slit or laser cut; avoid excessive heat input, which locally over-ages the alloy at the cut edge.

Applications

Where 2024 aerospace foil is used

  • Fibre-metal laminates: thin 2024-T3 sheets alternated with glass-fibre layers give skins with very slow fatigue crack growth and good impact and fire resistance
  • Shims and spacers: peel shims and solid shims in assemblies where the shim must match the strength of surrounding 2024 parts
  • Repairs: bonded or riveted doublers and patches for 2024 skins
  • Test programmes: fatigue and crack-growth coupons, adhesive lap-shear specimens
  • Missiles and UAVs: thin load-carrying skins and internal webs
  • Instruments: diaphragms and thin structural parts that need high strength and a stable modulus

For honeycomb cores, buyers should note that aerospace honeycomb is normally made from 5052, 5056 or 3003 foil rather than 2024; see our aircraft honeycomb guide.

FAQ

2024 aerospace aluminium foil: frequently asked questions

Is 2024 available as true foil below 0.2 mm?

Very thin heat-treated 2024 is a specialist product made in limited mill runs. Gauges around 0.2 – 0.5 mm are more commonly available. Tell us your gauge and tolerance early so we can confirm feasibility.

Should I order bare or Alclad 2024 foil?

Alclad is the usual choice for parts exposed to the atmosphere, because the pure aluminium cladding protects the copper-bearing core. Bare material is chosen when the surface will be anodised and bonded, or when the slight strength loss from cladding is not acceptable.

Can 2024 aluminum foil be welded?

Not for structural joints. 2024 cracks during fusion welding and loses strength in the heat-affected zone. Use adhesive bonding, riveting or a joining method qualified for your part.

Why does my drawing call for T351 on thin material?

T351 is a stretched plate temper. Thin sheet and foil are normally supplied as T3 or T42. We will confirm the equivalent temper with you before ordering.

Do you supply mill certificates for aerospace foil?

Yes. Every lot carries an EN 10204 3.1 certificate with chemical analysis and mechanical results, traceable to the coil or sheet.

Need 2024 foil or thin sheet?

Send gauge, width, temper, cladding, quantity and specification. We will confirm what can be supplied and how it will be certified.
