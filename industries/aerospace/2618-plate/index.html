2618 / 2618A Aerospace Aluminium Sheet & Plate

2618A sheet and plate is the heat-resistant aluminium chosen for skins, panels and machined parts that run at 120 – 250 °C, from supersonic airframes to engine nacelles and pistons cut from plate. Overview

Where 2618A plate fits in aerospace

2618A sheet was developed in the UK as RR58 and became famous as the skin and structural material of Concorde, whose surface reached roughly 100 – 125 °C during Mach 2 cruise. No ordinary airframe aluminium could hold its properties for tens of thousands of hours at that temperature without losing strength or creeping.

The secret is in the chemistry: about 1 % iron and 1 % nickel form heat-stable intermetallic particles, while copper and magnesium provide age-hardening. The result is a plate that keeps more of its strength after long exposure at temperature than 2024, 7075 or even 2014, and resists creep under sustained load.

Today 2618A plate is used less for skins and more for machined parts: engine-mount brackets, nacelle fittings, compressor housings, heat-shield supports, and blanks for pistons and impellers where forging is not justified. Stretched T851 or T651 plate stays flat when heavily machined, which matters for large parts with deep pockets.

Choose 2618A plate when

  • Machined parts operate continuously above about 120 °C
  • Creep or long-term strength retention governs the design
  • Low distortion during machining is needed (stretched T851 / T651)
  • The programme already specifies DTD 5070, AIR 9048 or EN aerospace 2618A plate

Tempers

2618A plate tempers and when to use them

T851

Solution treated, stretched 1.5 – 3 % and artificially aged. The standard plate temper for machined aerospace parts: full strength with low residual stress.

T651

Equivalent stretched-and-aged condition under different specification naming; machines with minimal distortion.

T6

Solution treated and aged without stretching. Normal for sheet and thin plate where residual stress is low.

T351

Stretched and naturally aged. Sometimes supplied for parts that the user will age after forming or machining.

T62

Heat treated by the user from O or F material, for parts formed in the soft condition first.

O

Annealed for forming. Strength is developed by full heat treatment after forming.

Working with 2618A

Machining, forming and joining

  • Machining: very good in T851; use sharp carbide tools and flood coolant. Stretched plate resists distortion during deep pocketing.
  • Forming: form sheet in O or freshly quenched condition, then age. Fully aged plate has limited bend capability.
  • Welding: not recommended for structural joints; the alloy is crack-sensitive. Use mechanical fastening.
  • Surface protection: anodise or conversion coat and prime; bare plate has moderate corrosion resistance.
  • Creep design: use long-time elevated-temperature data for the expected service hours, not short-time tensile values.
Applications

Applications of 2618A sheet and plate

  • Supersonic and high-speed airframe skins and structure exposed to aerodynamic heating
  • Engine mounts, nacelle fittings and brackets near hot zones
  • Compressor and gearbox housings machined from plate
  • Piston and impeller blanks cut from thick plate for prototypes and short runs
  • Heat-shield supports and ducting brackets
  • Motorsport components such as brake and suspension parts near heat sources

FAQ

2618A aluminium (aluminum) plate: FAQ

What is 2618A plate used for?

Machined parts and panels that operate at elevated temperatures, such as engine mounts, nacelle fittings, housings and supersonic airframe structure. It was the main structural alloy of Concorde.

Are 2618 and 2618A the same alloy?

They are close variants. 2618 is the US registration and 2618A the European one; 2618A allows slightly wider iron, nickel and titanium ranges. Mechanical properties are similar, and many programmes accept either, but the specification on your drawing decides which is certified.

Which temper should I order for machined parts?

T851 (or T651), which is stretched to relieve quench stresses and artificially aged. It gives full strength and keeps the plate stable during machining.

Is 2618A plate weldable?

Not for structural joints. Like most high-strength 2xxx alloys it is prone to hot cracking; parts are bolted or riveted.

How does 2618A compare with 2024 plate?

2024 is slightly stronger at room temperature and easier to find, but 2618A keeps more strength and resists creep above about 120 °C, so it is chosen for hot service.
