DTD 5004 Aerospace Aluminium Forgings

"5004" in aerospace forging usually means DTD 5004, a British specification for forging stock and forgings in an aluminium-copper-manganese alloy (nominally 6 % Cu, 0.3 % Mn), solution treated and precipitation treated for service at elevated temperatures. It is not an Al-Mg 5xxx alloy. Metline supplies forgings to DTD 5004A or approved alternatives, worldwide.

Overview

What DTD 5004 forgings are

DTD specifications were issued by the British Directorate of Technical Development for aircraft materials. Many were later replaced by BS L-series and BS 2L-series documents, but DTD numbers remain on drawings for aircraft, engines and equipment still in production or service.

DTD 5004A covers forging stock and forgings of an aluminium-copper-manganese alloy with nominal 6.0 % copper and 0.3 % manganese, solution treated and precipitation treated, and intended for use at elevated temperatures. That composition places it in the same family as the American alloy 2219 (5.8 – 6.8 % Cu, 0.20 – 0.40 % Mn), which is known for keeping strength from cryogenic temperatures up to roughly 250 – 300 °C and for good weldability.

The similarity is useful for understanding the material, but it does not make the two interchangeable. DTD 5004A has its own composition limits, mechanical requirements and test rules. Any substitution, for example by 2219 forgings to AMS 4143 or ASTM B247, needs written approval from the design authority.

Correcting older information on this product

  • DTD 5004 is an Al-Cu-Mn heat-treatable alloy, not a 5xxx Al-Mg alloy
  • Composition tables showing 0.5 – 1.2 % Mg and 0.2 – 0.6 % Si describe a different (6xxx-type) alloy
  • H24 properties do not apply to a solution-treated and precipitation-treated forging
  • Generic forging size tables are not a substitute for confirming feasibility against your drawing

Properties

Mechanical behaviour

Mechanical requirements for DTD 5004A forgings are set by the specification and depend on section size and test direction. To give a sense of the alloy family, typical values for 2219-T6 forgings are about 400 – 415 MPa tensile strength, about 260 – 290 MPa yield strength and 8 – 10 % elongation. Use the DTD figures, not these reference values, for design and acceptance.

Elevated temperature

High-copper Al-Cu-Mn alloys keep strength and creep resistance better than 6xxx and 7xxx alloys at around 150 – 250 °C, which is why they were chosen for engine and hot-zone parts.

Cryogenic temperature

The 2219 family gains strength and keeps toughness at very low temperature, a reason the US alloy is used for propellant tanks.

Weldability

Unusually weldable for a 2xxx alloy; 2219 is welded with 2319 filler. Check whether welding is permitted on DTD 5004 parts.

Corrosion

Like other copper-rich alloys, corrosion resistance is moderate; forgings are normally anodised, chromate- or chrome-free conversion coated and painted.

Applications

Where DTD 5004 forgings are used

  • Engine parts: compressor casings, impellers and housings running warm
  • Hot-zone fittings: brackets and fittings near engines and exhausts
  • Legacy airframes and equipment: spares and repeat production for UK-designed aircraft and systems
  • Defence equipment: forgings for missile and vehicle components specified to DTD documents

FAQ

DTD 5004 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) forgings: questions

Is 5004 an aluminium-magnesium (5xxx) alloy?

Not in this context. DTD 5004 is a British aerospace specification for an Al-Cu-Mn heat-treatable forging alloy with nominal 6 % copper and 0.3 % manganese.

Is DTD 5004 the same as 2219?

The nominal compositions are close, but the documents differ in limits and test requirements. 2219 forgings can replace DTD 5004 parts only with design-authority approval.

What condition are DTD 5004 forgings supplied in?

Normally solution treated and precipitation treated (the T6-type condition). Forging stock and solution-treated intermediate conditions are also available.

Why is this alloy used at elevated temperature?

High-copper Al-Cu-Mn alloys retain strength and resist creep better than 6xxx and 7xxx alloys at around 150 – 250 °C.

Do you supply DTD 5004 forgings internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies forgings worldwide from Mumbai, certified to the specification on your order, with export packing.
