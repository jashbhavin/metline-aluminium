2A70 Aerospace Aluminium Forgings

2A70 is the heat-resistant forging alloy of the Chinese GB system and the close counterpart of 2618: iron and nickel additions let forgings keep their strength at 150 – 250 °C. It is used for compressor impellers, engine parts and hot-running airframe fittings. Metline supplies 2A70-T6 die and free forgings to HB 5204 and GJB 2351, worldwide.

Overview

What 2A70 is

2A70, formerly LD7, combines copper and magnesium for age-hardening with about 1.2 % each of iron and nickel. The iron and nickel form Al9FeNi intermetallic particles that remain stable at temperature, so 2A70 forgings resist softening and creep far better than 2A50 or 2A14 forgings.

Its chemistry closely matches 2618, and the two are generally treated as counterparts. Typical parts are compressor impellers, fans and rotors in aero engines and auxiliary power units, pistons, and airframe fittings exposed to engine heat or aerodynamic heating.

A well-known example of the alloy's capability is the closed-die forged impeller or rotor with many thin, twisted radial blades: such parts demand an alloy that fills fine die detail during forging and then holds strength and fatigue resistance in hot, high-speed rotation.

Why engineers choose 2A70

  • Strength retention and creep resistance at 150 – 250 °C
  • Good forgeability for complex rotating parts
  • Close match to 2618, easing comparison with Western data
  • Specified on GB/HB-standard engine and airframe drawings

Processing

Forging, heat treatment and machining

  • Forging: billets are heated to the upper part of the forging range; the alloy's good thermal conductivity helps uniform heating.
  • Heat treatment: solution treatment around 525 – 535 °C, quench, and artificial ageing around 180 – 200 °C to T6.
  • Machining: good, with slightly higher tool wear from Fe-Ni particles; carbide tools recommended.
  • Joining: not intended for fusion welding; use mechanical joints.
  • Surface protection: anodising or conversion coating and paint.
  • Inspection: ultrasonic testing, macro-etch grain-flow checks and conductivity/hardness checks are common.
Applications

Applications of 2A70 forgings

  • Aero engines: compressor impellers, fans, rotors and casings that run warm
  • Missile and APU engines: closed-die forged rotors with thin twisted blades
  • Pistons: high-load pistons where 2618-type alloys are specified
  • Airframe: fittings, brackets and frames near engines or in heated zones
  • Flight-control hardware: levers and fittings in warm areas

FAQ

2A70 aluminium (aluminum) forgings: FAQ

What is the equivalent of 2A70?

2618 / 2618A is the closest Western counterpart, with very similar Cu-Mg-Fe-Ni chemistry. The Russian AK4-1 alloy is also comparable.

Why is 2A70 used for impellers?

It keeps strength and fatigue resistance at the temperatures reached in compressors, and it forges well into thin-bladed shapes.

What is the strength of 2A70-T6 forgings?

Commonly quoted minimums are about 375 – 380 MPa tensile strength in all directions; typical values are around 440 MPa.

What temperature can 2A70 withstand?

It is generally used for long-term service up to about 250 °C, with strength decreasing progressively above 200 °C.

Can 2A70 be welded?

It is not intended for fusion welding; forgings are machined and joined mechanically.
