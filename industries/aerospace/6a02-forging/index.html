6A02-T6 Aerospace Aluminium Forgings

6A02 (formerly LD2) is a heat-treatable aluminium-magnesium-silicon-copper forging alloy with excellent hot plasticity, good corrosion resistance and medium strength in T6. It is specified on drawings made to GB, HB and GJB standards, and is close to 6151 and 6061 in other systems. Metline supplies 6A02 die forgings, free forgings, rings and forged bar to HB 5204 and GJB 2351, certified and shipped worldwide.

Overview

What is 6A02 aluminium?

6A02 belongs to the 6xxx family. Its magnesium and silicon form Mg2Si, while 0.2–0.6 % copper adds strength beyond 6061-level and either manganese or chromium controls grain growth. The alloy is valued above all for its plasticity when hot, which lets it be forged into complex shapes at modest pressure.

In T6 temper 6A02 offers medium strength, good fatigue behaviour and better corrosion resistance than the 2xxx forging alloys. It also stays ductile at low temperatures. These qualities made it a standard choice for moderately loaded aircraft forgings, engine accessory housings and helicopter parts, especially where complex shapes with thin webs are required.

For buyers working in other standards, 6A02 sits between 6061 and 6151: it has a little more copper than 6061 and less silicon and chromium than 6151. It is not formally identical to any AA alloy, so substitution must be approved by the design authority.

Strengths of 6A02 forgings

  • Excellent hot plasticity for complex closed-die shapes
  • Good general corrosion resistance and low stress-corrosion sensitivity
  • Medium strength in T6 with good low-temperature toughness
  • Machines well and accepts anodic and chemical conversion coatings

Heat treatment

Tempers and heat treatment

T6

Solution heat-treated at about 515–530 °C, water quenched and artificially aged. The standard delivery temper for aerospace forgings.

T4

Solution treated and naturally aged; more ductile. Used where parts are formed or straightened before final ageing.

O / as-forged

Supplied for customers who carry out their own heat treatment after rough machining.

Quench delay and water temperature have a strong effect on thick forgings. For sections over about 75 mm, ask for properties to be tested at mid-thickness, not only near the surface.

Applications

Where 6A02 forgings are used

  • Airframe: moderately loaded fittings, hinge brackets and frame joints
  • Engine accessories: gearbox and pump housings, mounting brackets
  • Helicopters: complex forgings with thin ribs and webs
  • Instruments and avionics: structural housings and chassis
  • Industrial: pneumatic and hydraulic bodies, machine parts that need good corrosion resistance

6A02 is not used for turbine blades or hot-section engine parts: like all aluminium alloys, it loses strength rapidly above about 150 °C.

FAQ

6A02 aerospace forgings: frequently asked questions

What is the international equivalent of 6A02?

There is no exact match. The Russian grade AV is very close, and among AA alloys 6151 and 6061 are the nearest. Any substitution needs approval from the design authority.

What is the old name for 6A02?

LD2. Older drawings and specifications often still use it.

What strength does 6A02-T6 reach?

Die forgings to HB 5204 are commonly quoted at a minimum of 295 MPa tensile and 215 MPa yield in the longitudinal direction, with typical values somewhat higher.

Is 6A02 weldable?

It can be welded by TIG or MIG with 4043-type filler, but the heat-affected zone loses T6 strength. Forgings are usually joined mechanically.

Can you supply 6A02 forgings to a European or US specification?

Where the design authority allows it, we can offer 6061 or 6151 forgings to ASTM B247, AMS or EN 586 as an alternative.
