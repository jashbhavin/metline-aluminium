6066 Aerospace Aluminium Bar & Rod

6066 is a high-copper, high-silicon Al-Mg-Si alloy and, in T6, the strongest of the commonly extruded 6xxx grades, with a minimum tensile strength of 345 MPa. It is used for machined and forged aircraft fittings, ground equipment and defence hardware that need more strength than 6061 without moving to 2xxx or 7xxx alloys. Metline supplies 6066 round, square, hex and flat bar in O, T4, T6 and stress-relieved tempers to ASTM B221, worldwide.

Overview

What makes 6066 different from 6061

6066 carries roughly double the silicon, three times the copper and a deliberate manganese addition compared with 6061. After solution treatment and ageing this gives a T6 bar with a typical tensile strength near 395 MPa and yield near 360 MPa, about a quarter stronger than 6061-T6.

The extra alloy content has costs. Copper lowers corrosion resistance, so 6066 parts are normally anodised, conversion-coated or primed. The alloy is crack-sensitive in fusion welding and is generally treated as non-weldable for structural joints. And it is harder to extrude than 6061, so it is produced mainly as solid bar, rod and simple shapes rather than intricate hollows.

Those trade-offs define where 6066 bar is used in aerospace: machined fittings, brackets, bellcranks, hinge blocks and housings that are bolted rather than welded, forged components made from extruded stock, and high-load ground-support and defence hardware. It appeared widely in US designs through the federal extrusion standard QQ-A-200/10, now replaced by ASTM B221.

Why engineers specify 6066 bar

  • Highest standard strength of the common 6xxx extrusion alloys (T6 minimum 345 MPa tensile, 310 MPa yield)
  • Machines well in T6, producing short chips and good finish
  • Good forging stock for medium-strength fittings
  • Lower cost and easier processing than 2xxx or 7xxx bar

Tempers

Choosing a 6066 bar temper

6066-O

Annealed. Used as forging or heavy-forming stock that will be heat-treated afterwards.

6066-T4 / T4510 / T4511

Solution treated and naturally aged; T4510 and T4511 are also stretched for stress relief. Formable stock for parts aged after shaping.

6066-T6

Solution treated and artificially aged for full strength. The standard condition for machined fittings.

6066-T6510 / T6511

T6 bar stretched 1 – 3 % to remove quench stress; T6511 allows minor straightening. Preferred where parts are machined deeply and must stay true.

6066-T42 / T62

Heat-treated by the user after receipt, or by the producer to demonstrate response.

About "T651"

T651 is a plate designation. For bar, ask for T6510 or T6511, which give the same stress relief.

Working with 6066

Machining, forging, joining and finishing

Machining

Very good in T6: short chips, low built-up edge and good surface finish with carbide tooling. O and T4 are gummier.

Forging

Forges well from extruded or cast stock at about 430 – 480 °C, then is solution treated and aged to T6.

Welding

Prone to hot cracking in fusion welding and heavily weakened in the heat-affected zone. Design for bolted or riveted joints; weld only non-structural attachments after qualification.

Corrosion

Fair because of the copper content. Anodise, apply chemical conversion coating or prime, especially for exposed fittings.

Heat treatment

Solution treat at about 515 – 545 °C, quench, and age at about 175 °C for several hours to reach T6 (follow AMS 2772 or the governing specification).

Anodising

Accepts sulphuric and hard anodising; the film is slightly grey because of silicon and copper.

Applications

Typical aerospace uses of 6066 bar

  • Machined fittings: brackets, hinge blocks, bellcranks and mounting lugs bolted into secondary structure
  • Forged parts: medium-strength fittings and housings forged from 6066 bar stock
  • Ground support equipment: towbar heads, jacking pads, lifting fittings and engine-stand parts
  • Defence: mounts, housings and launcher hardware where strength and machinability matter
  • Hydraulic and pneumatic hardware: manifolds and valve bodies that are not welded

A typical example is a GSE towbar head: it is machined from 6066-T6511 round bar, which gives higher strength than 6061 for the same section, while the welded towbar tube itself is 6061-T6 because 6066 should not be welded.

FAQ

6066 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) bar: FAQs

How strong is 6066-T6 bar?

ASTM B221 requires at least 345 MPa (50 ksi) tensile and 310 MPa (45 ksi) yield with 8 % elongation. Typical values are about 395 MPa tensile and 360 MPa yield.

Can 6066 be welded?

It is not recommended for structural welds. The high copper and silicon make it crack-sensitive, and the heat-affected zone loses much of its strength. Use bolted or riveted joints.

What is the European equivalent of 6066?

There is no direct EN AW equivalent. 6082-T6 is the closest widely available 6xxx alloy but has lower strength, so substitution needs engineering approval.

What replaced QQ-A-200/10?

QQ-A-200/10 for 6066 extrusions is cancelled. ASTM B221 is the usual replacement for new orders.

Is 6066 bar available in T651?

For bar the stress-relieved tempers are T6510 and T6511. T651 is the equivalent designation for plate.

Do you ship 6066 bar internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies 6066 bar cut to length or as full bars, with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate, to customers worldwide.
