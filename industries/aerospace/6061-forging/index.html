6061 Aerospace Aluminium Forgings

6061 aerospace forgings are die forgings, open-die (hand) forgings and rolled rings in 6061, heat-treated to T6 or T652 and certified to specifications such as AMS 4127, AMS 4248 and ASTM B247. Forging aligns the grain with the part shape and closes internal porosity, giving better toughness and ductility in all directions than a part machined from plate or bar. Overview

Why use 6061 forgings in aerospace?

A forging is shaped by pressing or hammering a hot billet, so the metal's grain flows along the contour of the part. For a fitting, hub or ring that carries load in several directions, this gives more consistent ductility and fatigue behaviour than the same shape cut from plate, where properties in the thickness direction are weakest.

6061 is one of the easiest aluminium alloys to forge. It flows well at forging temperature, takes intricate die shapes and, after heat treatment to T6, gives a strong, weldable and corrosion-resistant part. It is chosen for moderately loaded fittings, wheels and hubs, rings, housings and valve bodies where 2014, 7050 or 7075 forgings would be stronger than needed and harder to protect from corrosion.

Product types

6061 forging types and tempers

Closed-die forgings

Near-net-shape parts pressed in matched dies: fittings, brackets, hubs, wheels and housings. Least machining and best grain flow.

Open-die (hand) forgings

Blocks, discs, shafts and bars shaped between flat or simple tools. Used for prototypes, low quantities and large parts machined to shape.

Rolled rings

Seamless rings rolled from a pierced blank, for flanges, bearing housings, adaptor rings and launch-vehicle joints.

T6

Solution treated, quenched and artificially aged. The standard temper for 6061 forgings.

T652

T6 with 1 – 5 % cold compression after quenching to relieve stress. Chosen for large hand forgings and rings that will be heavily machined.

F / O / T4

As-forged, annealed or naturally aged conditions, for parts that will be further worked or heat-treated by the user.

Applications

Where 6061 forgings are used

  • Airframe fittings and brackets in secondary structure and systems installations
  • Wheels and hubs for light aircraft, UAVs and ground-support equipment
  • Hydraulic and fuel-system housings, valve bodies and manifolds
  • Rings and flanges for launch-vehicle tank joints, adaptors and satellite interfaces
  • Rotor, gimbal and antenna mounts where weldability and machinability help assembly

For highly loaded primary structure such as wing-to-fuselage fittings or landing-gear parts, 7050-T7452 or 7075-T73 forgings are normally specified. 6061 suits parts where the design stress is moderate and corrosion resistance or weldability is important.

FAQ

6061 aluminium (aluminum) forgings: frequently asked questions

Which AMS specification covers 6061-T6 forgings?

AMS 4127 covers 6061-T6 die forgings, hand forgings and forged or rolled rings. AMS 4248 covers T652 hand forgings and rolled rings that are stress-relieved by compression.

Are 6061 forgings stronger than 6061 plate?

Typical strength is similar, but forgings give more uniform properties in all directions and better fatigue behaviour at features such as lugs and bosses, because the grain follows the part shape.

What is the T652 temper?

T6 with a controlled cold compression of about 1 – 5 % after quenching, which reduces residual stress so large forgings and rings distort less when machined.

Can 6061 forgings be welded?

Yes. 6061 forgings weld with 4043 or 5356 filler, which is one reason they are chosen over 2xxx and 7xxx forgings. The heat-affected zone loses some strength unless re-heat-treated.

Should I choose open-die or closed-die forging?

Closed-die forging suits larger quantities and gives near-net shape with the best grain flow. Open-die forging suits prototypes, small quantities and large parts that will be machined.

Do you supply 6061 forgings with ultrasonic test reports?

Yes. Forgings can be supplied with ultrasonic inspection to AMS-STD-2154 or ASTM B594 and an EN 10204 3.1 certificate, shipped worldwide.
