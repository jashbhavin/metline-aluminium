6262 Aerospace Aluminium Wire (AMS-QQ-A-225/10)

6262 wire is cold-drawn, heat-treated free-machining aluminium wire, made to AMS-QQ-A-225/10 and ASTM B211. Its lead and bismuth additions break chips on automatic lathes, so small fittings, pins and connector bodies can be turned quickly from coil. Metline supplies 6262 wire and small-diameter rod in T6, T8 and T9, in coils or straight lengths, with full certification, worldwide.

Overview

What is 6262 aluminium wire?

In the ASTM and AMS system, "wire" is drawn product under about 10 mm (0.375 in) in diameter, and "rod" is the same product above that size. 6262 wire is therefore the small-diameter end of the 6262 range: drawn through dies to close tolerances, heat-treated, and often cold-drawn again after ageing.

The point of 6262 wire is speed on coil-fed and bar-fed automatics. Lead and bismuth particles (0.4–0.7 % each) act as internal chip breakers, so parts as small as electrical contacts and instrument pins can be turned without tangled swarf. The base chemistry is that of 6061, so finished parts resist corrosion and anodise well.

AMS-QQ-A-225/10 is the aerospace specification for 6262 bar, rod and wire produced by rolling, drawing or cold finishing. It is the document most aircraft drawings cite for 6262 wire, while ASTM B211 is used for commercial and general engineering orders.

What 6262 wire is not

  • It is not an electrical conductor wire: conductivity is roughly 44 % IACS, well below 1350 or 6101 conductor grades
  • It is not weldable: lead and bismuth cause hot cracking
  • It is not a rivet wire: for driven rivets, use 2117, 2017, 5056 or 7050 rivet wire

Tempers

Which 6262 wire temper to order

6262-T9

The standard screw-machine temper: aged, then cold drawn for maximum strength and the cleanest chip break.

6262-T8

Cold worked between solution treatment and ageing. Used where a specific strength-ductility balance is called for.

6262-T6

Solution treated and aged without final cold work. Slightly lower strength, slightly higher ductility.

6262-O

Annealed wire for forming, or for customers who heat-treat finished parts themselves.

Some offers list 6262 wire in T4 or T6511. T6511 is an extruded-product temper for larger rod, and T4 wire is rarely stocked because the alloy is almost always used in aged condition. We quote to the temper your drawing requires.

Working with 6262 wire

Machining and finishing tips

  • Speeds: run high surface speeds with sharp, polished positive-rake tools; built-up edge is rare
  • Coolant: soluble oil or neat cutting oil gives the best finish on threads and small diameters
  • Straightness: order straightened cut lengths for bar feeders to reduce whip at high spindle speeds
  • Anodising: clear and hard anodising both work; keep thick hard coats on critical features under review
  • Joining: use threads, press fits or crimping; do not weld or braze
Applications

Typical uses of 6262 wire

  • Aircraft hydraulic and pneumatic systems: small fittings, sleeves and adaptor nuts
  • Connectors: shells, pins and inserts for circular and avionics connectors
  • Instruments: pivots, spindles and small housings
  • Fasteners: screws and threaded inserts turned from coil
  • General engineering: optics hardware, small valve parts and pneumatic components

FAQ

6262 aerospace wire: frequently asked questions

What does QQ-A-225/10 cover?

It is the specification for 6262 aluminium alloy bar, rod and wire produced by rolling, drawing or cold finishing. The original federal document has been taken over by SAE as AMS-QQ-A-225/10.

Can 6262 wire be used as electrical wire?

No. Its conductivity is about 44 % IACS and it is designed for machining. For conductors, use 1350 or 6101 conductor wire.

Why does 6262 contain lead and bismuth?

Both form low-melting particles that weaken the chip at the cutting edge, so chips break short. This allows fast, unattended turning on automatic lathes.

What is the strongest temper for 6262 wire?

T9, which is aged and then cold drawn. Minimum tensile strength is about 360 MPa in smaller diameters, with typical values near 400 MPa.

Do you supply 6262 wire in straight lengths?

Yes. Wire and small rod can be straightened and cut to bar-feeder lengths, or supplied in coils.
