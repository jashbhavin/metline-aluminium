2031 Aerospace Aluminium Wire and Rod

2031 is a British-origin heat-treatable aluminium alloy containing copper, nickel, magnesium, iron and silicon, designed to keep its strength at moderately raised temperatures. It is mainly produced as forging stock, extruded rod and tube, and can be drawn into wire and small rod for fasteners and machined parts. Metline supplies 2031 rod and wire in the solution-treated (T4/TB) and fully aged (T6/TF) conditions, certified to the standard on your order.

Overview

What makes 2031 different

Most 2xxx alloys lose strength quickly above about 150 °C because their copper-magnesium precipitates coarsen. 2031 belongs to the small group of nickel-bearing aluminium alloys, alongside 2618 and the older "RR" and Hiduminium alloys, that were developed to resist this softening.

Nickel and iron combine with aluminium to form hard, thermally stable intermetallic particles. These particles do not dissolve during heat treatment and do not coarsen at service temperatures, so they continue to pin the structure when the normal age-hardening precipitates start to lose their effect. The result is an alloy with moderate room-temperature strength and better retention of strength and creep resistance near engines and other warm areas.

In British aerospace practice, 2031 is known through the BS H12 specification and trade names such as Hiduminium 56. Its main forms are forging stock and tube. Wire and small-diameter rod are produced by drawing extruded rod and are generally made to order.

Choose 2031 when

  • A part runs warm, for example near engines, exhausts or hot air ducts
  • Moderate strength and good dimensional stability matter more than peak strength
  • The drawing calls for BS H12 or an equivalent legacy British specification

Mechanical properties

Mechanical properties

British specifications use TB for solution treated and naturally aged (equivalent to T4) and TF for solution treated and artificially aged (equivalent to T6). The minimums below are those published for forged and extruded 2031 up to about 150 – 200 mm; drawn wire is normally tested to the same temper requirements or to agreed values.

Processing

Forming, heat treatment and protection

Cold heading

Head fasteners in the annealed or freshly quenched condition, then age to T6. Heading after full ageing risks cracking.

Heat treatment

Solution treat, quench rapidly and age. Users should follow the time and temperature in the governing specification and control quench delay.

Machining

The intermetallic particles give short chips and good surface finish in T6.

Corrosion

Copper and nickel reduce corrosion resistance. Anodise, conversion coat or seal fastener holes in assemblies.

Ordering

How to order 2031 wire and rod

  1. SpecifyDiameter and tolerance, temper (O, T4/TB or T6/TF), coil weight or cut length, quantity and governing specification.
  2. Confirm the specificationWe check the order against the governing AMS, ASTM or EN document, confirm tempers, sizes, tolerances and any customer flow-down clauses, and quote on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
  3. ProcessWire is drawn, heat treated as ordered, tested and wound on spools or bundled in lengths.
  4. DocumentEach lot ships with a mill test certificate to EN 10204 3.1 showing heat or lot number, chemical analysis and mechanical test results, plus any ultrasonic or other inspection reports called for on the order.
  5. Pack and shipMaterial is protected against moisture and handling damage, packed in seaworthy cases or on pallets, marked for traceability and shipped by sea or air.

FAQ

2031 aerospace aluminium (aluminum) wire: FAQ

What is 2031 aluminium alloy?

A heat-treatable Al-Cu-Ni-Mg-Fe-Si alloy of British origin, known through BS H12 and the Hiduminium family, designed to retain strength at moderately raised temperatures.

What do TB and TF mean?

They are British temper codes. TB is solution treated and naturally aged (like T4); TF is solution treated and artificially aged (like T6).

How strong is 2031-T6 wire?

Published minimums for the TF condition are about 385 MPa tensile and 285 – 300 MPa proof stress with 6 % elongation. Actual values are reported on the certificate.

Is 2031 a good rivet alloy?

It can be headed in the soft condition, but general aircraft rivets use 2117, 2017, 2024 or 7050. 2031 is chosen when heat resistance or a legacy specification requires it.

Can you supply small quantities?

Wire is made to order, so quantity, diameter and test plan are agreed before production. Send your requirement and we will confirm the options.

Need 2031 wire or rod?

Send diameter, temper, quantity and specification, and we will confirm feasibility and certification.
