Aluminium Casement Windows for Projects and OEM Buyers

Buying aluminium casement windows for a project or a distribution programme is mostly about matching one opening type to many different requirements: the local market's opening direction, its test standards, the energy target, the hardware groove your installers know, and how units are labelled and packed. This page sets out those choices for contractors, developers, importers and window fabricators. Metline supplies casement profiles, cut-to-size sections and accessories in 6063 and 6060 aluminium to window fabricators worldwide.

Overview

Why projects specify casement windows

When a casement is closed, its locking points draw the sash tight onto continuous gaskets. Nothing has to slide past a seal, so air, rain and sound have no easy path through. That single feature explains why casements consistently test better than sliding windows for air permeability, watertightness and acoustic reduction, and why energy and acoustic specifications so often name them.

Casements are also flexible. The same profile family can produce outward and inward sashes, tilt-turns, fixed lights and combinations, so one system can cover most of a building's openings with matching sightlines and colour. For a distributor or window company, that means fewer profile codes in stock and simpler fabrication.

The trade-off is that an open casement projects from the wall. Outward sashes need clearance from walkways and balconies and restrictors at height; inward sashes need clear space for curtains and furniture. Where neither is possible, a sliding window remains the practical choice.

Configuration

Hardware, glass, gasket and finish options

Hardware groove

Most export systems use the Euro groove (often called C-groove) so standard European espagnolettes, hinges and tilt-turn gear fit. Some markets use their own groove geometry; match it to the hardware your installers already stock.

Hinges and stays

Butt hinges, friction stays or concealed hinges. Confirm the rated sash weight: a 1.0 × 1.6 m sash with 6-16-6 glass already weighs around 55 – 60 kg.

Locking

Multi-point espagnolettes for sashes over about 1 m high or in exposed sites; keyed handles and secured-by-design hardware where security rules apply.

Restrictors

Opening limiters and child-safety catches for upper floors, schools, care homes and hotels.

Glass

Double or triple IGUs, Low-E and solar-control coatings, laminated and acoustic laminated glass, toughened safety glass at low level.

Gaskets

EPDM single, double or triple sealing; moulded corners reduce leakage risk; brush seals only at screens.

Insect screens

Fixed, hinged or retractable screens, mounted inside for outward sashes and outside for inward ones.

Finish

Polyester or super-durable powder, PVDF, anodising, wood-grain transfer and dual colour on thermally broken profiles.

Brand matching

Project hardware brands or equivalents can be matched to a specification on request, subject to the system test.

FAQ

Aluminium (aluminum) casement windows for projects: frequently asked questions

Should a project choose casement or sliding windows?

Choose casements where air tightness, watertightness, acoustics or a low U-value are specified, because the sash closes onto compressed gaskets. Choose sliders for very wide openings, where a projecting sash would obstruct a balcony or walkway, or where budget outweighs performance.

What sets the final U-value of an aluminium casement?

Mainly the glass unit, then the thermal break width and shape, the frame face width and the spacer bar. Compare whole-window values calculated at the same reference size to EN ISO 10077 or NFRC 100.

Are aluminium casements suitable for coastal, humid or high-wind sites?

Yes, with a system tested to the required wind and water class, adequate mullion sections, a marine-grade finish such as super-durable powder, PVDF or thick anodising, and stainless or coated hardware.

Which projects suit tilt-turn casements?

Apartments, schools, hotels and high-rise housing, where secure tilt ventilation and cleaning from inside are valuable, and in markets such as continental Europe where inward opening is standard.

Do you supply finished casement windows?

No. We supply cut-and-machined profiles with gaskets, cleats and hardware for fabricators who assemble and glaze locally, as well as stock profile lengths for window companies.

Do you support OEM labelling and packing?

Yes. Profile bundles and accessory cartons can carry the buyer's labels, barcodes, schedule references and part numbers, packed in crates or cartons to the agreed format.

What do you need for a quotation?

Project location or target market, window schedule or drawings, opening types, performance targets, glass, hardware, finish, supply model (profile lengths or cut-and-machined profiles), quantity and delivery destination.

Sourcing casement window profiles for a programme?

Send the schedule, target market, glass, hardware and finish. Our team will propose a profile series and quote profiles and accessories.
