Aluminium Window Screens, Screen Frames & Mesh

Aluminium window screens are framed panels of woven aluminium mesh that let air and light through a window while keeping out insects, leaves and debris. They are light, do not rust and hold their tension for years. Metline supplies extruded and roll-formed screen frame profiles, corner keys and spline, aluminium mesh rolls and aluminium-magnesium screen wire to window makers, distributors, fabricators and projects worldwide.

Overview

What is an aluminium window screen?

A window screen has two parts: a rigid frame that fits the window opening and a mesh stretched across it and locked into a groove with a flexible spline. Both parts can be made from aluminium, and that combination is the long-standing standard in North America and much of the rest of the world because it is light, strong, stable in sunlight and resistant to corrosion.

Aluminium mesh has three advantages over the main alternative, vinyl-coated fibreglass. It is stiffer, so it stays flat and taut across large openings, although a hard knock can crease it where fibreglass would spring back. It does not soften or sag in heat. And it is more resistant to tearing by cats, birds and wind-blown debris. Fibreglass is cheaper, softer and easier to install, which is why both are widely used; the choice depends on the application.

The frame is just as important. A frame that twists or bows lets insects in around the edges, so screen frames are made from extruded 6063 aluminium or roll-formed coated aluminium strip, both of which are rigid, light and finished to match the window.

Why aluminium screens

  • Al-Mg mesh wire does not rust and resists corrosion in humid and coastal air
  • Holds tension and stays flat; does not sag in heat like plastic meshes
  • More tear-resistant than fibreglass, with good visibility and airflow
  • Frames are light, rigid and colour-matched to the window with powder coat or anodising
  • Mesh, frames and wire are all fully recyclable
Product forms

Frame profile, components, mesh roll or wire?

Buyers of window screen materials fall into a few groups, and each needs a different product. Deciding which one you need is the first step in an enquiry.

Screen components

Corner keys, spline, pull tabs, springs, clips and rollers supplied with frame profiles, so window makers and screen shops can assemble screens to their own window sizes.

Screen frame profiles

Extruded or roll-formed frame sections in lengths, with corner keys and spline, for window manufacturers and local fabricators who assemble screens themselves.

Mesh rolls

Woven aluminium insect mesh in standard widths and roll lengths, for screen shops, window makers and retail distribution.

Screen wire

Drawn aluminium-magnesium wire in coils for mesh weavers and wire-cloth producers.

Integrated window and screen

Windows designed with a screen track or sash built into the frame, so the screen is aligned and sealed by design. Common in sliding and casement systems.

Spare parts

Corner keys, spline, pull tabs, leaf springs, plunger latches and turn buttons for existing screens.

The key compatibility question is the direction the window opens. A casement window that opens outwards needs a screen on the inside, while a sliding or hung window can take a screen on the outside. Integrated systems, where the screen track is part of the window frame, solve this at the design stage and are the cleanest option for new construction.

Mesh

Aluminium screen mesh sizes and specifications

Frames

Screen frame profiles: extruded vs roll-formed

Screen frames come in two constructions. Both are aluminium; they differ in how the section is made, which affects stiffness, appearance and cost.

FeatureExtruded frameRoll-formed frame
ProcessHot aluminium pushed through a die into a hollow or open sectionPre-painted coil bent through forming rolls into a tube
Material6063-T5 or 6060-T5 extrusionCoated 3105, 3003 or 5052 strip
Typical wallAbout 0.8 – 1.5 mmAbout 0.4 – 0.6 mm
StiffnessHigh; resists bowing on large screensAdequate for typical window sizes
FeaturesIntegrated spline groove, hardware channels, Euro-groove, brush-seal groovesSimple spline groove; fewer features
FinishPowder coat or anodising after extrusionCoil-coated before forming
Best forSliding screens, large and hinged screens, premium windowsHigh-volume replacement screens for hung and slider windows

Corners are joined with internal corner keys, usually die-cast zinc, aluminium or nylon, pressed or crimped into the frame ends. Extruded frames can also be mitred and crimped or screwed through the corner. A brush or pile seal along the edges closes the gap between the screen and the window frame, which is where most insects actually get in.

For window manufacturers, the frame section should be designed around the window: groove positions, the visible face width, hardware fit and the colour all need to match. Custom dies make this possible on larger programmes. For other frame profiles see 6063 aluminium extrusions and aluminium window profiles.

Aluminium is the balanced choice for most windows. Choose stainless steel when security, bushfire (ember) protection or a very long life in coastal air is the priority, and pet-resistant polyester screening on low doors that animals push against. In salt-spray zones, charcoal or black coated aluminium mesh lasts noticeably longer than bright mesh.

Quality

Standards, testing and quality checks

Screens are simple products, but a few specifications separate a durable screen from one that sags, rattles or lets insects in around the edge.

  • Mesh: mesh count, wire diameter and weave consistency checked against the order, with woven wire cloth specified to ASTM E2016
  • Frame material: extruded frames to ASTM B221 or EN 755, roll-formed strip to ASTM B209 or EN 485, with mill test certificates
  • Frame performance: North American tubular-frame window screens are commonly specified to ANSI/SMA 1004 from the Screen Manufacturers Association
  • Fit: overall size tolerances of about ±1.5 mm (1/16 in) so screens sit snugly without binding
  • Tension: mesh stretched evenly with no ripples, bulges or loose areas at the spline
  • Corners: square, tight and free of gaps; the frame should not twist when lifted by one corner
  • Coating: film thickness, adhesion and colour checked against the specified standard
Applications

Who uses aluminium window screens

Window and door manufacturers

Frame profiles, mesh and hardware matched to their window systems, so they can ship a screen with each window.

Distributors and importers

Mesh rolls in retail widths and lengths, and frame profiles with corner keys in standard lengths for resale.

Residential projects

Frame profiles and mesh for every window in an apartment block or housing estate, cut, labelled and packed by building and floor for the screen fabricator.

Hotels, schools and hospitals

Durable, easy-to-clean screens that allow natural ventilation without insects, often with security mesh at ground level.

Replacement programmes

Measured replacement screens for older windows, often as outward-mounted retrofit frames.

Agricultural and industrial buildings

Large insect screens for ventilation openings in food processing, warehouses and livestock buildings.

A typical apartment project might use 18 × 16 charcoal mesh in white extruded frames on every sliding window, with brush seals on the meeting edges, and 14 × 14 heavy-duty mesh on balcony screen doors where traffic is higher.

FAQ

Aluminium (aluminum) window screens: frequently asked questions

What is the standard mesh size for window screens?

18 × 16 mesh with 0.011 in (0.28 mm) wire is the most common insect screen. It keeps out flies and mosquitoes while giving roughly two-thirds open area for airflow. Use 20 × 20 where midges and sand flies are a problem, and 14 × 14 heavy-duty mesh for high-traffic doors.

Is aluminium or fibreglass screen better?

Aluminium is stiffer, more tear-resistant and stays taut in heat, and it gives very good visibility. Fibreglass is cheaper, easier to install and does not crease. Aluminium suits long-life windows and doors; fibreglass suits budget and DIY replacement.

Does aluminium window screen rust?

No. Aluminium does not rust. Screen wire is an aluminium-magnesium alloy, commonly 5154, which resists corrosion well. Near the sea, bright mesh can slowly oxidise and dull, so charcoal or black coated mesh is recommended for coastal sites.

Why choose charcoal or black mesh?

Dark mesh reflects less light, so it is less visible from outside and gives a clearer, less hazy view from inside. Its coating also adds some protection against corrosion.

Can you supply screen frame profiles to match our windows?

Yes. Metline supplies extruded screen frame profiles with the groove positions, face width and colour to suit your window system, along with corner keys, spline and mesh, so you can assemble screens locally.

Do you supply finished, assembled screens?

No. We supply frame profiles cut to your screen sizes, with mesh, spline, corner keys and fixing hardware, for your fabricator to assemble. To quote, send screen sizes and quantities by type, frame colour, mesh type and colour, the fixing method and the destination.
