Coordinate appearance and fabrication acceptance

Anodized colour and uniformity depend on substrate and preparation. Organic coating acceptance also involves pretreatment, application and curing, with QUALICOAT distinguishing product approvals from production-site licences. Specify exposed faces, viewing conditions, panel orientation and the cutting or forming sequence. A finish name or powder brand does not establish acceptance for a completed façade component.

Overview

Why architects specify aluminium

Aluminium does not rust, needs little maintenance and can be anodised or coated in almost any colour. Its light weight reduces loads on the structure, and extrusion allows complex window, door and curtain wall sections with integrated drainage and thermal breaks.

At the end of a building's life, aluminium can be recovered and recycled without loss of quality, which supports green building certification.

Roofing and cladding

Composite panels

3003 and 5005 skins for aluminium composite panels.

Structural elements

6061 and 6082 sections for canopies, walkways and roof structures.

Louvres and sun shading

Extruded blades and frames for solar control.

FAQ

Building & Construction questions

Which aluminium alloy is used for windows?

6063 in T5 or T6 temper is the standard window and door alloy, thanks to its excellent extrudability and finish.

Is aluminium cladding durable?

Yes. With anodising or a quality coating such as PVDF, aluminium cladding can last for decades with minimal maintenance.
