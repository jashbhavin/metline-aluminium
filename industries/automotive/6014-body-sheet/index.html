6014 Aluminium Auto Body Sheet

6014 is a lean, low-copper 6xxx body sheet alloy with excellent formability and hemming, used for bonnets, doors, tailgates and inner panels. Supplied in T4 or pre-aged T4P, it stamps easily and then gains strength in the paint oven. Metline supplies 6014 sheet, blanks and coil with MF or EDT surfaces, certified to EN 485 or the OEM specification, worldwide.

Overview

What makes 6014 a closure-panel alloy

Closure panels (bonnet, doors, tailgate, boot lid) are formed and then joined by hemming: the outer skin is folded 180 degrees around the inner panel edge. That flat hem is one of the most demanding forming operations on a car, and it is where lean 6xxx alloys such as 6014 shine. With low silicon and magnesium and only a trace of copper, 6014 hems without cracking even after the strain of a deep stamping.

6014 adds 0.05 – 0.20 % vanadium, which helps control grain size and keeps the alloy tough. Compared with 6016, it gives a little less strength after paint baking but better formability and hemming, which is why it is often chosen for complex inner panels and for outer panels with tight feature lines. Because it is lean and copper-free in practice, it also has very good filiform and intergranular corrosion resistance.

Many vehicle makers use one 6xxx alloy for both inner and outer closure panels. 6014 suits that approach well, simplifying press-shop scrap handling and allowing closed-loop recycling of offal back into new body sheet.

Why body engineers choose 6014

  • Excellent flat-hem performance for outer panels
  • Very good deep drawability for complex inners
  • Paint-bake hardening for dent resistance in service
  • Low copper for strong corrosion resistance under paint

How it works

How 6014 gains strength in the paint shop

1. Deliver in T4 or T4P

Sheet arrives solution treated and quenched. T4P (pre-aged) sheet has had a short warm treatment at the mill that stabilises it and speeds the later bake response.

2. Press and hem

Soft T4 material is stamped and hemmed. Forming adds a few per cent of strain, which also helps the later hardening.

3. Paint bake

The e-coat and paint ovens hold the body at roughly 170 – 185 °C for about 20 minutes. Fine Mg-Si precipitates form and the yield strength rises.

4. In service

The finished panel has much higher dent resistance than the sheet had in the press, without a separate heat-treatment step.

The bake response of 6014 is slightly lower than that of 6016 because it contains less silicon and magnesium. Designers compensate either with a slightly thicker outer skin or by using 6014 for inners and 6016 for outers.

Fabrication

Pressing, hemming and joining

Stamping

Larger die radii and controlled blank-holder force than for steel; aluminium springs back more because of its lower modulus.

Flat hemming

6014 performs very well in flat and roller hemming; keep the iron content low and avoid excessive natural ageing before hemming.

Joining

Adhesive bonding, self-piercing riveting, clinching and resistance spot welding are all used in closure assembly.

Pre-treatment

A conversion pre-treatment and dry-film lubricant improve bond durability and press consistency.

Painting

Accepts standard phosphate or zirconium pre-treatments and e-coat used on mixed-material bodies.

Corrosion

Very good resistance to filiform corrosion at cut edges and hems compared with copper-bearing body alloys.

FAQ

6014 auto body sheet: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6014 and 6016?

6014 has less silicon and magnesium and adds vanadium, giving better formability and hemming but slightly lower strength after paint baking. 6016 is preferred for dent-resistant outer skins; 6014 for complex panels and inners.

What does T4P mean?

T4 with a pre-ageing treatment at the mill. It stabilises the sheet against natural ageing and gives a faster, larger strength gain in the paint oven.

How much does 6014 strengthen in the paint bake?

Typically yield rises from about 90 – 120 MPa to roughly 170 – 210 MPa after a standard 2 % pre-strain and bake test, depending on processing.

How long can 6014 aluminum sheet be stored before pressing?

Natural ageing slowly raises yield and reduces formability, so OEM specifications set a maximum storage period. Pre-aged T4P material is more stable.

Can 6014 be used for outer panels?

Yes. Its excellent hemming makes it suitable for outer panels, though some makers prefer 6016 for higher dent resistance.
