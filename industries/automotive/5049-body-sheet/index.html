Automotive aluminium body sheet

5049 O / H111 Aluminium Automotive Body Sheet

5049 (EN AW-5049, AlMg2Mn0.8) is a medium-strength aluminium-magnesium-manganese alloy that forms well in the annealed O and H111 tempers and does not become sensitive to intergranular corrosion in warm service. It is used for inner panels, reinforcements, floor and cab panels, heat shields and tanks. Metline supplies 5049 automotive sheet and coil to EN 485, GB/T 33227 and customer specifications worldwide.

Overview

What is 5049 automotive sheet?

5049 combines 1.6 – 2.5 % magnesium with 0.5 – 1.1 % manganese. The magnesium provides solid-solution strength, and the manganese adds further strength and a fine, stable grain structure. The result is an annealed sheet that is noticeably stronger than 5052-O while keeping excellent drawability.

For body engineers, 5049 sits between the formability-led alloys (3xxx, 5005) and the high-magnesium inner-panel alloys (5182, 5754). Its main advantage over 5182 is that, with magnesium below about 3 %, it does not form continuous grain-boundary β-phase during long service above roughly 65 °C, so it remains resistant to intergranular and stress corrosion near engines, exhausts and battery heat sources.

The O temper is fully recrystallised and gives the best drawing and stretch-forming performance. H111 is annealed sheet that has received a slight amount of strain from levelling or stretching, which improves flatness for blanking and laser cutting with practically no change in properties.

Why buyers choose 5049 body sheet

  • Annealed tensile strength of 190 – 240 MPa, above 5052-O
  • Good deep-drawing and stretch-forming behaviour for inner parts
  • Suitable for warm locations where higher-magnesium alloys risk sensitisation
  • Welds by MIG, TIG, laser and resistance spot welding

Working with 5049

Forming, joining and finishing

Drawing and stretching

O temper draws deeply; Lüders (stretcher-strain) bands are less pronounced than in 5182, but exposed surfaces should still be checked. Use lubricated or EDT-textured surfaces for deep draws.

Spot and laser welding

Resistance spot welding needs controlled surface oxide; a conversion-coated or pickled surface gives more consistent electrode life. Laser and MIG welding with 5xxx fillers work well.

Adhesive bonding

Titanium/zirconium pre-treatment improves bond durability for bonded and riveted inner structures.

Corrosion

Very good resistance to road salt and humidity; stable in warm service where 5182 and 5083 would need caution.

Painting

Compatible with phosphate or zirconium pre-treatment and e-coat lines used for aluminium bodies.

Recycling

Body-shop offcuts of 5049 can be returned as clean, segregated 5xxx scrap.

Applications

Where 5049 sheet is used in vehicles

  • Inner panels and reinforcements: door, bonnet and tailgate inners and local reinforcements where an annealed 5xxx sheet is specified
  • Floor and cab panels: commercial-vehicle cabs, floors and load-area panels
  • Heat shields and tunnel covers: parts in warm zones where sensitisation must be avoided
  • Tanks and housings: fluid tanks, battery-pack covers and housings
  • Seat and interior structures: pressed brackets and pans

Older descriptions list 5049 sheet for brake discs, cylinder blocks and suspension arms. Those parts are cast, forged or made of iron; sheet is not used for them.

FAQ

5049 automotive aluminium (aluminum) sheet: frequently asked questions

What is 5049 aluminium used for in cars?

Inner panels, reinforcements, floor and cab panels, heat shields, tanks and battery-pack covers: formed parts that need more strength than 3xxx alloys and must resist corrosion.

What is the difference between 5049-O and 5049-H111?

Both are annealed with the same property range. H111 has a little strain from levelling or stretching, which improves flatness for blanking.

Is 5049 the same as 5754?

No, but they are close. 5754 has more magnesium (2.6 – 3.6 %), while both carry the same O-temper property range of 190 – 240 MPa tensile.

Can 5049 be used near hot components?

Yes. With magnesium below about 3 %, 5049 is not prone to sensitisation in warm service, unlike 5182 or 5083.

Can 5049 be spot welded?

Yes. Consistent surface condition matters; conversion-coated or cleaned surfaces give more stable spot-weld quality.

Do you supply 5049 sheet internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 5049 sheet and coil worldwide from Mumbai with EN 10204 3.1 certificates, on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
