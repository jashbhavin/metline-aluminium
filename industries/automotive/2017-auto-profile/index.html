2017 T4 Aluminium Automotive Profiles

2017 is the original duralumin: an aluminium-copper-magnesium-manganese alloy that reaches high strength by natural ageing in the T4 temper. As extruded bar, tube and profiles it gives automotive engineers a strong, very machinable material for fittings, hydraulic and pneumatic parts and precision brackets. Overview

What is 2017 aluminium?

2017 descends directly from the first age-hardening aluminium alloy, discovered in the early twentieth century and sold as duralumin. With about 4 % copper, 0.6 % magnesium and 0.7 % manganese, it is solution heat-treated, quenched and left to age at room temperature to the T4 temper, where it reaches roughly the strength of mild steel at one-third of the weight.

For aircraft, 2017 has largely been replaced by 2024, which is stronger. In general engineering and automotive supply, however, 2017 and its European sister 2017A remain popular because they machine beautifully, keep good toughness in T4 and are widely available as extruded and drawn bar, tube and simple profiles.

In vehicles, 2017 is a material for machined and fastened parts, not for welded structures or body panels. It is chosen for fittings, connectors, valve and pump bodies, gear-shift and pedal components, screw-machine parts and rivets, where high strength in a compact part matters more than weldability or atmospheric corrosion resistance.

Why engineers specify 2017 T4

  • High strength without artificial ageing: about 425 MPa typical tensile strength
  • Very good machinability with short chips and a fine finish
  • Good fatigue resistance for cyclically loaded fittings
  • Widely standardised: ASTM, EN, ISO, JIS and GB designations

Tempers

T4, T451 and T6: what to order

T4

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged. The standard temper for 2017 extrusions and bar, giving the property set above.

T451 / T4511

T4 plus controlled stretching to relieve quench stresses. Preferred for parts with heavy machining, because they move less.

T6

Artificial ageing is possible but not the normal practice for 2017, and properties are not tabulated in the common standards. If you need higher strength than 2017-T4, 2024-T3 or 2014-T6 is usually the better choice.

O

Annealed for forming or cold heading, followed by solution treatment and ageing.

Working with 2017

Machining, joining and protection

Machining

Excellent in T4: short broken chips, good finish and accurate threads. Use sharp carbide or HSS with positive rake and coolant.

Welding

Not recommended for fusion welding: the alloy is prone to hot cracking and loses strength in the heat-affected zone. Use mechanical fastening or adhesive bonding.

Corrosion

Copper reduces atmospheric corrosion resistance. Anodise, apply conversion coating and paint, or keep the part in a protected environment such as inside a housing filled with oil.

Heat

Service above about 120 – 150 °C over long periods over-ages the alloy and lowers strength.

Cold heading

Rivets and cold-headed parts are formed freshly quenched or in O temper, then naturally aged to T4.

Anodising

Takes clear and hard anodic coatings, with a slightly less bright appearance than 6xxx alloys.

Applications

Automotive applications of 2017 extrusions

  • Hydraulic and pneumatic components: valve bodies, manifolds, cylinder end caps and couplings machined from bar or profile
  • Fasteners and rivets: rivets, studs and special screws for assemblies where steel weight is not wanted
  • Power-train fittings: machined brackets, spacers and sensor housings inside protected areas
  • Controls: gear-shift, pedal and steering-column components machined from extruded bar
  • Motorsport parts: lightweight machined fittings and suspension hardware
  • Tooling and fixtures: jigs, gauges and assembly-line fixtures in vehicle plants

Engine blocks, oil pans, wheels and brake discs are not made from 2017. Engine blocks and oil pans are cast (A319, A356 or similar), wheels are cast A356 or forged 6061 / 6082, and brake discs are cast iron or ceramic composites.

FAQ

2017 aluminium (aluminum) automotive profiles: frequently asked questions

Is 2017 the same as duralumin?

Yes, in practice. 2017 is the standardised form of the original duralumin alloy: aluminium with about 4 % copper plus magnesium and manganese, strengthened by natural ageing.

What is the difference between 2017 and 2017A?

2017A is the European registration with slightly different limits for silicon, magnesium and minor elements. Properties are very close, and most machined parts can use either.

Can 2017 extrusions be welded?

Fusion welding is not recommended because of hot cracking and strength loss. Join 2017 parts mechanically or by adhesive bonding.

Why is 2017 normally supplied in T4 rather than T6?

2017 is designed to reach its useful strength by natural ageing. Artificial ageing adds little practical benefit and is not covered by the common standards; 2014-T6 or 2024 is chosen when higher strength is needed.

Is 2017 corrosion resistant?

Moderately. Its copper content makes it less resistant than 5xxx and 6xxx alloys, so exposed parts should be anodised or painted.

Do you supply 2017 worldwide?

Yes. Metline supplies 2017 and 2017A extrusions, bar and tube worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.
