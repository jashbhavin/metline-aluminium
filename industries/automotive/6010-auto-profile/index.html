6010-T4 Aluminium for Automotive Profiles

6010 is the higher-strength member of the 6009/6010 automotive alloy pair. Supplied in T4, it can be bent and formed, then gains strength in the paint oven or a full ageing cycle. Overview

What 6010 aluminium offers

6010 was introduced together with 6009 as a body-sheet alloy that could be formed soft and hardened by paint baking. Compared with 6009 it carries more silicon and magnesium, so it is stronger in every temper. Its T4 formability was reported as similar to the copper-rich 2036-T4 body alloy, while delivering more strength after ageing.

The extra strength made 6010 attractive for outer panels that had to resist denting with thinner gauges, and for structural inner parts. In extruded form, 6010-T4 sections are bent, flared or hydroformed and then aged, giving a part that is stronger than the equivalent 6063 profile but easier to form than 6061-T6.

Like 6009, 6010 is now mostly found on established vehicle programs and in service parts. New designs more often use 6016, 6014, 6022 or 6111 sheet and 6005A, 6008 or 6082 extrusions. When a drawing calls for 6010, matching it exactly keeps validated properties, weld behaviour and corrosion performance unchanged.

6010 in brief

  • Higher T4 and aged strength than 6009
  • Formable in T4, strengthened by paint bake or T6 ageing
  • Good spot weldability and no stretcher-strain marks
  • Compatible in scrap with 6009, simplifying recycling

Fabrication

Working with 6010

Bending and hydroforming

Form in T4 within the agreed shelf life; natural ageing slowly raises strength and reduces formability over months.

Ageing

Paint-bake cycles give part of the available strength. A full artificial ageing cycle gives T6 strength for load-bearing parts.

Welding

MIG, TIG, laser and spot welding all work. Use 4xxx filler where hot cracking is a concern.

Finishing

Copper content makes painting or e-coating the normal finish; anodised appearance is less uniform than 6063.

Machining

Better machinability in T6 than T4; use sharp positive-rake tools and coolant.

Corrosion

Good under paint. For bare exposure in salt environments, copper-free alloys perform better.

Applications

Where 6010 is used

  • Outer and inner panels on programs validated in 6010
  • Bent and hydroformed tubes for frames and seat structures
  • Brackets, reinforcements and cross members formed in T4
  • Bumper reinforcements on established designs
  • Truck and trailer components needing formable, ageable sections
  • Service parts that must match original material

FAQ

6010 automotive aluminium: frequently asked questions

What does T4 mean for 6010?

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged. The alloy is relatively soft and formable, and it can be strengthened later by paint baking or artificial ageing to T6.

How strong is 6010-T4?

Typical values are about 290 MPa tensile strength, 170 MPa yield and 24 % elongation, noticeably stronger than 6009-T4.

Is 6010 still used in new cars?

Less often. Copper-free 6016 and 6014 and North American 6022 and 6111 have largely replaced it for new panels, but 6010 remains on existing programs and service parts.

Can 6010 aluminum be extruded?

Yes. It is best known as sheet, but it extrudes into tubes, angles, channels and custom sections that are formed in T4 and aged afterwards.

Does 6010 need painting?

Painting or e-coating is normal because its copper content lowers bare corrosion resistance compared with copper-free 6xxx alloys.
