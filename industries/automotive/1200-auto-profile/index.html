1200 and 1100 H112 Automotive Aluminium Tube Coil

1200 and 1100 tube coil is small-diameter, seamless round tube in commercially pure aluminium, wound in long continuous lengths. It bends easily, conducts heat well and brazes cleanly, so vehicle and heat-exchanger makers use it for connecting lines, cooler circuits and refrigerant pipework. Overview

What is aluminium tube coil?

Tube coil is round tube supplied wound on itself rather than in straight lengths. Long coils let a manufacturer straighten, cut and bend tube automatically, with far less scrap and fewer joints than straight 6 m lengths. Fewer joints mean fewer potential leak paths, which matters in any sealed fluid circuit.

Small-diameter coils in 1100 and 1200 are usually produced by continuous extrusion, where aluminium rod is fed into a rotating grooved wheel and forced through a die without the stop-start of a billet press. The process gives seamless tube with a uniform structure in very long lengths. Larger or tighter-tolerance tube may be extruded on a press and then drawn.

The as-extruded H112 temper is soft and ready for bending. Where tube will be bent very tightly, flared or expanded, it is supplied annealed (O temper). Neither alloy can be heat-treated for strength, so post-braze properties equal those of O temper whatever the starting condition.

Why choose 1100 / 1200 tube coil

  • Very good bendability for complex routing in engine bays and HVAC modules
  • Thermal conductivity above 210 W/m·K for heat-transfer circuits
  • Brazes and welds readily, including flame and induction brazing of end fittings
  • Lower weight and cost than copper tube of the same size

Tolerances & testing

Tolerances, cleanliness and leak testing

Dimensions

Outside diameter and wall to ASTM B491 / B210 or EN 754-7 tables, or tighter on drawing. Ovality is controlled so that end fittings and O-rings seal.

Inside cleanliness

Refrigerant and oil circuits need low residual oil and particles. Ask for internal cleanliness limits and dry, capped ends.

Leak and eddy-current testing

In-line eddy-current testing finds wall defects along the full coil length; pressure or helium leak testing is done on the finished assembly.

Bend testing

Flattening, flaring and expansion tests confirm that the tube will form without cracking.

Certification

EN 10204 3.1 certificate with chemistry and mechanical results for each lot.

Packing

Coils wrapped in film with desiccant, palletised or cased for export.

Applications

Automotive uses of 1100 / 1200 tube coil

  • Heat-exchanger connecting tube: inlet and outlet tubes and jumpers on condensers, evaporators, heater cores and oil coolers
  • Air-conditioning circuits: low-pressure and small-diameter refrigerant lines and capillary-style tubes
  • Oil and transmission cooler lines: bent tube connecting radiator-mounted coolers to the gearbox or engine
  • Coolant and auxiliary circuits: battery-cooling distribution lines and small coolant tubes in electric vehicles
  • Instrument and vent lines: low-pressure tubing where corrosion resistance and easy routing matter

Brake lines are a common error in older descriptions of aluminium tube coil. Hydraulic brake pipes are almost always steel or copper-nickel because of their very high pressures and safety rules; 1xxx aluminium is not used for them. For higher-pressure refrigerant lines, 3003 or 6063 tube is normally specified.

FAQ

Aluminium (aluminum) tube coil: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between H112 and O temper tube coil?

H112 is the as-extruded condition, soft enough for normal bending. O is fully annealed, with the highest elongation, and is chosen for tight bends, flaring and expansion into fins or fittings.

Is 1200 tube the same as 1100 tube?

Both are 99.0 % aluminium. 1100 has a small copper addition that makes it marginally stronger; for most tube uses the two are interchangeable.

What sizes of tube coil are available?

Typically 4 – 19 mm outside diameter with 0.4 – 3 mm walls, in metric or inch sizes, as pancake or level-wound coils.

Can aluminium tube coil be used for brake lines?

No. Brake pipes run at very high pressures and are made from steel or copper-nickel. 1100 / 1200 tube suits low- and moderate-pressure fluid and heat-exchanger lines.

How is tube coil cleaned and packed?

Tube is cleaned internally, dried, capped at both ends and wrapped with desiccant, then palletised or cased for export.

Do you supply tube coil outside India?

Yes. Metline ships tube coil worldwide with mill certificates and EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
