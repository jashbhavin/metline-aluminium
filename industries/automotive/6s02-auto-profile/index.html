6S02 T6 Automotive Aluminium Extrusions

6S02 is a GB/T 3190 designation for a high-strength, silicon-rich Al-Mg-Si-Cu extrusion alloy aimed at automotive structures. With a guaranteed T6 tensile strength of about 315 MPa and proof strength of 285 MPa, it sits above 6061 and 6082 in strength while keeping good elongation. Metline supplies 6S02-T6 profiles to drawing for vehicle makers and tier suppliers worldwide.

Overview

What is 6S02 aluminium?

6S02 uses an excess-silicon chemistry: silicon of about 0.9 – 1.15 % against magnesium of 0.5 – 0.7 %, so there is more silicon than needed to form Mg2Si. The surplus silicon and a 0.32 – 0.50 % copper addition raise the ageing response, while manganese and chromium form dispersoids that keep a fine, partly unrecrystallised grain structure. That structure is what gives high strength together with good bending and energy absorption.

Alloys of this type are used for structural profiles in body-in-white, sub-frames, battery enclosures and crash-relevant members, where engineers want T6 proof strength near 300 MPa without the weldability and stress-corrosion concerns of 7xxx alloys.

The designation is specific to the GB system. For global programmes, the closest international comparisons are high-strength crash and structural 6xxx alloys such as 6082 and copper-bearing variants; any substitution must be validated by the part owner, as crash behaviour depends on processing as much as chemistry.

Why 6S02 is specified

  • T6 strength above 6061 and 6082 in thin-walled sections
  • Fine-grained structure for good bendability and crash energy absorption
  • No 7xxx stress-corrosion risk; welds and joins like other 6xxx alloys
  • Listed for automotive profiles under GB/T 33910

Tempers & processing

Tempers and heat treatment

T6

Solution heat-treated by intensive press quench (usually water) and artificially aged. Standard delivery condition.

Quench sensitivity

Excess-silicon, copper-bearing alloys need fast quenching; section design with even wall thickness helps reach T6 minimums.

Under-aged options

Slightly under-aged tempers can be agreed to improve crush folding at a small cost in strength.

Bend and crush testing

Three-point bend (for example per VDA 238-100) or axial crush tests can be added to the inspection plan.

Tolerances & inspection

Tolerances and inspection

Automotive profiles are almost always made to a part drawing, and the drawing tolerances override the general tables. Where the drawing is silent, the general tolerance standard of the order applies. Critical dimensions, such as mating faces, screw bosses and crash-relevant wall thicknesses, should be marked so they can be measured on every lot.

Tolerance and inspection framework

General dimensional tolerances
EN 755-9 for EN orders; ASTM B221 with ANSI H35.2 tables for ASTM orders; GB/T 14846 where a GB drawing applies
Straightness and twist
Per the same standards, or tighter values agreed for long members and parts that go straight into robotic welding
Wall thickness
Measured at drawing-defined points; closed hollow sections are checked for eccentricity and die-line quality
Mechanical testing
Tensile test per lot to EN ISO 6892-1 or ASTM B557, with hardness (Webster or Brinell) for in-process checks
Microstructure
Grain size and recrystallised surface layer checked on request, which matters for crash and bending performance
Documentation
EN 10204 3.1 certificate; PPAP-style first-article dimensional report on request for series parts
Fabrication

Joining, machining and finishing

Like other copper-bearing 6xxx alloys, 6S02 machines cleanly in T6 and can be MIG, TIG, laser or friction-stir welded, with ER4043 or ER5356 filler for arc processes. The heat-affected zone loses a large share of its T6 strength, so crash and load paths are usually designed around mechanical joints, adhesive bonding or welds in low-stress areas. The copper content means painted or anodised finishes are recommended where parts see road salt.

Mill finish

Supplied as extruded and aged, for parts that are welded, bonded or painted later in the body shop or e-coat line.

Anodising

Clear or coloured anodic films for trim, roof rails and visible parts. Film class is specified in micrometres, for example AA15 or AA20 for exterior exposure.

E-coat (electrophoretic paint)

Thin, uniform paint that penetrates hollow sections; often used after anodising for a smooth, durable finish on trim.

Powder coating

Thicker polyester or epoxy layers for chassis brackets, racks and accessories that see stone chipping.

Conversion coating

Chromium-free titanium or zirconium pre-treatment before adhesive bonding or painting, improving bond durability.

Machining and fabrication

Cut-to-length, CNC machining, punching, bending and drilling on request, so profiles arrive ready for assembly.

Applications

Where 6S02 profiles are used

  • Body-in-white: sills, A/B-pillar reinforcements, roof and floor cross-members
  • Crash management: bumper beams and crash boxes where the design calls for a high-strength 6xxx alloy
  • EV battery enclosures: side-impact rails and cross-beams
  • Sub-frames and chassis: extruded arms and cradle members
  • Two-wheelers and light vehicles: frame tubes and structural sections

For decorative trim, door frames or complex thin housings, lower-strength alloys such as 6063, 6463 or 6106 are easier to extrude and finish.

FAQ

6S02 automotive aluminium (aluminum) extrusions: frequently asked questions

What kind of alloy is 6S02?

A GB-registered, silicon-rich Al-Mg-Si alloy with copper, manganese and chromium additions, designed for high-strength automotive extrusions.

How strong is 6S02-T6?

Minimum tensile strength is about 315 MPa and minimum proof strength about 285 MPa, with about 15 % elongation.

Is there an international equivalent?

There is no exact AA or EN equivalent. 6082-T6 is the nearest common alternative, though it is somewhat weaker; substitutions must be approved by the part owner.

Can 6S02 be welded?

Yes, by MIG, TIG, laser or friction stir welding, with strength loss in the heat-affected zone as for other 6xxx alloys.

Do you supply 6S02 outside China?

Yes. Metline supplies 6S02 profiles worldwide with certificates and export packing.
