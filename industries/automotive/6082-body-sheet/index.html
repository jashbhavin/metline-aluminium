6082 Aluminium Sheet and Plate for Automotive Structures

6082 sheet and plate is the European workhorse for structural aluminium parts that are cut, machined, welded or bent rather than deep drawn. In vehicles it is used for battery-enclosure plates and lids, brackets and reinforcements, motor and inverter mounting plates, and the floors, side walls and chassis parts of trucks, vans and trailers. Metline supplies 6082 in O, T4, T6 and T651 to EN 485 and ASTM B209, cut to size and certified, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What role does 6082 sheet play in vehicles?

6082 is a heat-treatable Al-Si-Mg-Mn alloy that reaches the highest strength of the common 6xxx sheet and plate grades. In many applications it has replaced 6061, especially in Europe, because it offers equal or slightly higher strength in thick plate, good weldability and corrosion resistance, and wide availability.

On vehicles, 6082 sheet and plate is a structural and engineering material, not an outer-panel alloy. Body skins use bake-hardening grades such as 6016 and 6022, and inner panels use 5182 or 5754. 6082 is chosen when a part needs strength and stiffness in a flat or simply formed shape: battery-enclosure lids and base plates, crash-relevant reinforcements, mounting plates for electric drive units, tipper and truck bodies, trailer chassis parts and loading ramps.

Thicker 6082-T651 plate is machined into fixtures and jigs used on body-in-white lines, and into cooling plates and structural nodes in EV battery systems.

Why 6082 sheet and plate is specified

  • Minimum T6 yield of 260 MPa in sheet, the highest of the common 6xxx sheet grades
  • Good weldability and corrosion resistance for exposed chassis and body parts
  • T651 plate machines flat for fixtures and mounting plates
  • Standard grade across European specifications and supply chains

Stretched plate for machining. Stays flat as material is removed, so it is the normal choice above 6 mm.

Working with 6082

Welding, forming and machining 6082

Welding

MIG and TIG weld readily. ER5356 or ER5183 filler gives higher joint strength; ER4043 reduces hot-cracking risk. The heat-affected zone drops to roughly half of T6 strength, which design codes such as Eurocode 9 take into account.

Forming

Bend T6 sheet across the rolling direction with inside radii of about 2 – 3 × thickness. For tighter bends, form in O or T4 and age afterwards.

Machining

T6 and T651 machine well with short chips; use sharp carbide tools and flood coolant. Higher silicon makes it slightly more abrasive than 6061.

Corrosion

Good resistance in road-salt and humid environments. Anodise or paint for appearance; isolate from steel fasteners.

FAQ

6082 aluminium (aluminum) sheet and plate: frequently asked questions

What are the minimum properties of 6082-T6 sheet?

EN 485-2 requires 310 MPa tensile and 260 MPa yield for 6082-T6 sheet up to 6 mm, with minimum elongation from 6 to 10 % depending on thickness.

Is 6082 the same as 6061?

No, but they are close. 6082 has more silicon and manganese and less copper; it is slightly stronger in thick plate and common in Europe. 6061 is the standard in the Americas. Do not substitute one for the other without approval.

Is 6082 used for car body panels?

Not for outer skins. Those use 6016, 6014 or 6022. 6082 sheet and plate is used for structural parts, battery enclosures, mounting plates and commercial-vehicle bodies.

Can 6082-T6 sheet be bent?

Yes, with inside radii of roughly 2 – 3 × thickness, bending across the rolling direction. For tight bends, form in O or T4 and age afterwards.

What is the old British name for 6082?

HE30 for extrusions, with H30 and HP30 used for other forms. The current European designation is EN AW-6082 (AlSi1MgMn), Werkstoff 3.2315.

Do you ship 6082 sheet and plate worldwide?

Yes. Metline supplies 6082 worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and export packing on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
