6005 and 6005A Aluminium Profiles for Automotive

6005 and 6005A are medium-strength structural extrusion alloys that sit between 6063 and 6082: stronger than 6063, far easier to extrude into thin-walled hollow sections than 6082. That makes them a natural choice for vehicle frames, roof rails, battery-tray members, bus and trailer structures. Metline supplies 6005 and 6005A profiles to drawing in T4, T5 and T6, certified to EN 755 and ASTM B221, worldwide.

Overview

Where 6005 and 6005A fit in vehicle design

Automotive extrusions fall into three broad strength classes. 6060 and 6063 are easy to extrude and finish but modest in strength. 6082 and 6061 are strong but extrude slowly and struggle with thin walls and complex hollows. 6005 and 6005A fill the gap: they reach a T6 proof stress of more than 200 MPa while still running through multi-cavity hollow dies at reasonable speeds.

6005 is the original Aluminum Association alloy with tight limits on copper, manganese and chromium. 6005A, the European variant, allows more manganese, chromium and copper. Those additions control grain structure and raise toughness, which helps crash and fatigue performance, and they make 6005A slightly less sensitive to quench rate in thicker walls. In practice 6005A is the more common choice in Europe and in automotive platforms, while 6005 appears more often in North American specifications.

Both alloys can be press-quenched with air or water mist at the extrusion press exit and then artificially aged (T5), avoiding a separate solution heat-treatment step for thin and medium walls. Thicker or heavily loaded parts are solution-treated and aged to full T6.

Why engineers choose 6005A for vehicle structures

  • Higher strength than 6063 with much better extrudability than 6082
  • Suits thin-walled, multi-cavity hollow profiles for frames and battery trays
  • Good weldability by MIG, TIG and friction-stir welding
  • Good corrosion resistance and a clean anodised or painted finish

Tempers

T1, T4, T5 or T6: choosing the temper

T1

Cooled from the press and naturally aged. Low strength; used for parts that will be formed and aged later.

T4

Solution treated and naturally aged. Softer and more ductile for bending and hydroforming, then aged to T6.

T5

Press-quenched and artificially aged. Saves a furnace step; suitable for thin and medium walls where the press quench is fast enough.

T6

Separately solution treated, quenched and aged. Highest guaranteed strength, used for thicker walls and critical structural parts.

T64 / T66 variants

Some vehicle makers specify under- or controlled-aged conditions to balance strength and crush behaviour. These are agreed on the drawing.

Post-forming age

Parts bent in T4 can be aged after forming, often combined with the paint-bake cycle for part of the strength gain.

Fabrication

Welding, bending and finishing

Welding

MIG and TIG with ER4043 or ER5356 filler; friction-stir welding is widely used for battery-tray floors. Expect the heat-affected zone to drop to roughly T4-level strength in the as-welded state.

Bending

Bend in T4 for tight radii and age afterwards, or use stretch-bending for large-radius roof rails. Hollow sections need mandrels or filler to prevent wall collapse.

Machining

Machines cleanly in T5/T6 with sharp carbide tools. CNC machining of mounting faces and holes is routine.

Joining to steel

Isolate from steel with coatings, sealants or insulating washers; use self-piercing rivets or flow-drill screws for mixed joints.

Finishing

Accepts anodising, e-coating and powder coating. Mill-finish parts inside a painted body are e-coated with the body.

Corrosion

Good general corrosion resistance. Copper is low enough in 6005 and 6005A that filiform and intergranular attack are rarely a problem under paint.

FAQ

6005 and 6005A automotive profiles: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6005 and 6005A?

6005A allows more manganese, chromium and copper (Mn + Cr 0.12 – 0.50 %). That refines grain structure and improves toughness and quench tolerance. Strength is similar; 6005A is more common in Europe and in vehicle platforms.

Is 6005A stronger than 6063?

Yes. In T6, 6005A open profiles up to 5 mm wall have a minimum proof stress of 225 MPa under EN 755-2, against about 170 MPa for 6063-T6, while still extruding thin-walled hollows well.

Can 6005A replace 6082 in automotive frames?

Often, yes, for thin-walled hollows where 6082 is hard to extrude. 6082 remains stronger in thick sections, so check the design loads before substituting.

What does T5 mean for 6005 aluminum extrusions?

T5 means the profile is quenched at the press exit and then artificially aged, without a separate solution heat treatment. It suits thin and medium walls and reduces cost.

Is 6005A suitable for EV battery trays?

Yes. It is widely used for battery-tray side members and floor profiles because it extrudes complex multi-cavity sections and welds well, including by friction-stir welding.

What documentation do you provide?

An EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate with chemistry and tensile results for each lot, plus dimensional reports and PPAP-style documents on request.
