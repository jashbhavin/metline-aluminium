2024 and 2024A T3 Aluminium Extrusions for Automotive

2024 is a high-strength aluminium-copper-magnesium alloy, best known from aircraft structures, that is also extruded as bar, tube and profiles for highly loaded automotive and motorsport parts. In the stretched T3511 temper it combines high strength with excellent fatigue resistance. Metline supplies 2024 and 2024A extrusions to ASTM B221, EN 755 and AMS specifications worldwide.

Overview

What are 2024 extrusions used for in vehicles?

2024 contains about 4.4 % copper and 1.5 % magnesium. After solution treatment, quenching, stretching and natural ageing (T3511), extrusions reach tensile strengths of roughly 400 – 500 MPa depending on section thickness, with fatigue performance that has kept the alloy in aircraft wings and fuselages for decades.

In automotive work 2024 is a specialist material. It appears where a machined or extruded part is highly loaded and weight is critical: racing suspension links and uprights machined from bar, high-performance driveline and steering components, fasteners, and precision fittings in hydraulic systems. Mass-produced body and chassis structures use 6xxx extrusions instead, because 2024 cannot be fusion welded and needs corrosion protection.

2024A is a registered variant of 2024 with tighter limits on iron, silicon and some alloying elements, developed to improve toughness and reduce residual stress. Unless your drawing specifies 2024A, standard 2024 is the more widely available choice.

When 2024 is the right choice

  • High static strength and fatigue resistance in a machined part
  • Mechanical fastening or bonding rather than welding
  • Parts that can be anodised, painted or kept in a protected environment
  • Motorsport and low-volume performance vehicles where cost is secondary to weight

Tempers

2024 extrusion tempers

T3 / T3510 / T3511

Solution heat-treated, stretched and naturally aged. T3510 is stretched with no further straightening; T3511 allows minor straightening. The standard choice for most parts.

T4

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged without controlled stretching; used for some bar and rivet stock.

T8510 / T8511

Stretched then artificially aged. Higher yield strength and much better resistance to stress-corrosion cracking, with slightly lower elongation; preferred for parts exposed to sustained stress or moderate heat.

O

Annealed for forming, followed by heat treatment by the user.

Working with 2024

Machining, joining and corrosion protection

Machining

Machines well in T3 / T3511 with good chip control; T8511 machines even more cleanly. Stretched tempers keep distortion low.

Welding

Not suitable for arc welding: hot cracking and large strength loss. Friction-stir welding is possible in specialised applications. Fasten mechanically or bond.

Corrosion

Lower resistance than 6xxx alloys, with susceptibility to intergranular corrosion and stress-corrosion cracking in T3. Anodise (sulphuric or chromic-free processes), prime and paint exposed parts.

Temperature

T3 strength declines with long exposure above about 120 °C; T8 tempers are more stable.

Galvanic contact

Isolate from steel and carbon fibre with coatings, sealants and washers.

Surface finish

Hard anodising gives a wear-resistant surface for sliding parts.

Applications

Automotive and motorsport applications

  • Suspension components: machined uprights, rocker arms, links and brackets in racing and performance cars
  • Driveline and steering: splined couplings, spacers and steering-column parts machined from bar
  • Fasteners: high-strength aluminium bolts and rivets
  • Hydraulic fittings: couplings and housings for brake-system and clutch hydraulics, away from road spray
  • Motorsport structures: roll-hoop fittings, wing mounts and pick-up points

Pistons, connecting rods and engine blocks are usually cast or forged from other alloys (such as 4032, 2618 and cast Al-Si), and wheels and brake discs are not made from 2024. These claims in some product literature are not correct.

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) extrusions for automotive: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 2024 and 2024A?

2024A is a registered variant with narrower composition ranges and lower impurities, aimed at better toughness and lower residual stress. Standard 2024 is more widely stocked; order 2024A when the drawing requires it.

What does T3511 mean?

Solution heat-treated, stress-relieved by stretching, with minor straightening allowed, then naturally aged. It is the most common temper for 2024 extrusions.

Can 2024 extrusions be welded?

Not by conventional arc welding. The alloy cracks and loses much of its strength. Use bolts, rivets or adhesive bonding.

Is 2024 used for car wheels or brake discs?

No. Wheels are cast or forged in alloys such as A356, 6061 and 6082, and brake discs are cast iron or ceramic composites. 2024 is used for machined, highly loaded fittings and fasteners.

How strong is 2024-T3511?

ASTM B221 minimum tensile strength ranges from about 395 MPa for thin sections to 485 MPa for sections over 38 mm thick.

Do you supply 2024 extrusions internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 2024 bar, tube and profiles worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and export packing.
