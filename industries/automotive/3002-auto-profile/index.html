3002 H112 Aluminium Extrusions for Automotive Trim

3002 is a high-purity aluminium alloy with tiny, controlled additions of manganese and magnesium, designed to give a brilliant, uniform finish after chemical or electrolytic brightening and anodising. It is used for decorative automotive trim rather than structural parts. Metline supplies 3002 extrusions and sheet in the H112 and other tempers to trim makers worldwide.

Overview

What is 3002 aluminium?

3002 is unusual in the 3xxx series. Most 3xxx alloys, such as 3003, contain about 1 – 1.5 % manganese for strength. 3002 instead starts from very pure aluminium, with iron and silicon each limited to around 0.1 % or less, and adds only small amounts of manganese and magnesium. The result is an alloy whose surface brightens to a mirror-like, colourless finish.

The reason is metallurgical. Iron- and silicon-rich particles in ordinary aluminium scatter light and make an anodised film cloudy or grey. By keeping those impurities very low, 3002 produces a clear anodic film over a highly reflective metal surface. The small manganese and magnesium additions give a little more strength and grain control than pure 1xxx metal while preserving that brightness.

In automotive use, 3002 belongs with the bright-trim family alongside 5252, 5457, 5657 and 6463. It suits window surrounds, roof-rail caps, grille surrounds, badges, bright mouldings and interior trim strips, where appearance matters far more than structural strength.

Choose 3002 when you need

  • A bright, clear anodised or electropolished finish with high specular reflectance
  • Uniform colour from part to part and batch to batch
  • Good formability for bending and roll-forming trim
  • A softer, lower-strength alternative to 6463 or 5657 for light trim

Properties

Mechanical and physical properties

With less than 0.5 % total alloying content, 3002 behaves mechanically much like commercially pure aluminium: soft, ductile and easily formed, with strength in the same low range as 1100 and below that of 3003. It cannot be heat-treated; strength comes only from cold work.

That is not credible for an alloy this dilute in the as-extruded condition; design with values comparable to 1xxx alloys and confirm by test if strength matters. Choose 5657, 5457 or 6463 when more strength is required with a bright finish.

Surface finishing

How 3002 trim is brightened and anodised

  1. Mechanical polish or buffExtrusions or formed parts are polished to remove die lines and handling marks.
  2. Chemical or electrolytic brighteningPhosphoric-nitric bright dipping or electropolishing levels the surface on a microscopic scale and raises specular reflectance.
  3. AnodiseA thin, clear sulphuric-acid anodic film, typically about 3 – 10 µm for bright trim, protects the bright surface without clouding it.
  4. SealHot-water or other sealing closes the pores and improves corrosion and stain resistance.
  5. Optional colourDyeing or electrolytic colouring before sealing gives bronze, black or other tones.

Thicker anodic films improve durability against road salt and car-wash chemicals but slightly reduce brightness. Agree film thickness and test requirements, for example CASS or neutral salt spray and alkaline resistance, with your vehicle maker.

Applications

Automotive uses of 3002

  • Exterior bright trim: window and door belt mouldings, roof-rail caps, grille surrounds
  • Badges and emblems: stamped and brightened sheet parts
  • Interior trim: dashboard and door inserts, speaker grille frames, sill plates
  • Lighting: reflector components and lamp bezels
  • Commercial vehicles: decorative strips on buses and coaches

3002 is not used for body structure, engine or chassis parts. Load-bearing extrusions use 6xxx alloys such as 6063, 6061 and 6082.

FAQ

3002 aluminium (aluminum) for automotive trim: frequently asked questions

What is 3002 aluminium used for?

Decorative parts that are brightened and anodised, such as automotive trim, mouldings, badges and reflectors. It is chosen for appearance, not strength.

Why does 3002 anodise brighter than 3003?

3003 contains about 1.2 % manganese and more iron, which form particles that make the anodic film grey. 3002 has very low iron and silicon and only traces of manganese, so the film stays clear.

Is 3002 strong enough for structural parts?

No. Its strength is similar to commercially pure aluminium. Use 6063, 6061 or 6463 where the trim must carry load.

What does H112 mean for 3002 extrusions?

As extruded, with only incidental strain hardening and no further thermal treatment. It is the normal temper for 3002 profiles.

Can 3002 be coloured?

Yes. After brightening and anodising, the film can be dyed or electrolytically coloured before sealing.

Does Metline supply 3002 internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies 3002 extrusions and sheet to order worldwide with certificates and protective export packing.
