5457 H112 Automotive Aluminium Extrusion Profile

5457 is a high-purity aluminium alloy with about 1 % magnesium and a small manganese addition, designed to be chemically brightened and clear-anodised to a bright, durable finish. As H112 extrusions it is used for vehicle mouldings, window and door frames, bezels and interior accents where appearance comes first and loads are light. Overview

What is 5457 aluminium?

5457 is one of the classic automotive bright-trim alloys. It combines 0.8 – 1.2 % magnesium and 0.15 – 0.45 % manganese with a very pure aluminium base: iron no more than 0.10 % and silicon no more than 0.08 %. The low impurity level keeps the surface free of light-scattering particles, so the metal brightens to a high reflectivity and holds it under a thin clear anodic film.

Compared with the leaner 5657, the manganese and slightly higher magnesium of 5457 add a little strength and help control grain size, which reduces orange-peel and streaking on formed trim. Compared with 5252, it is softer but brightens to a higher brilliance. It is non-heat-treatable; strength comes from the alloy content and, in rolled tempers, from cold work.

Historically 5457 was used for bright bumper face bars and large trim pieces; today it is found mostly in mouldings, frames and bezels. Extrusions in H112 are used for trim with a profiled cross-section, then cut, bent and finished.

Why designers choose 5457

  • Bright, uniform finish after chemical brightening and clear anodising
  • Slightly stronger and more grain-stable than 5657
  • Good formability for bending trim to body contours
  • Good corrosion resistance once anodised and sealed

Finishing

Brightening and anodising 5457

Brightening

Chemical brightening (phosphoric-nitric acid baths) or electrobrightening dissolves surface peaks to produce a mirror-like finish. Low iron and silicon keep the surface free of intermetallic pits.

Clear anodising

A thin, clear anodic film, typically about 3 – 10 µm for bright trim, protects the brightened surface while preserving reflectivity. Thicker films improve durability but reduce brilliance.

Sealing

Hot-water or nickel-acetate sealing closes the pores of the film for weather and car-wash resistance.

Handling

Bright trim stock is packed with interleaving and protected from scratches, fingerprints and moisture; one handling mark can scrap a visible part.

Tolerances & quality

Tolerances and inspection

Unless the drawing states tighter values, profile dimensions follow the general tolerance tables of the order standard. Automotive programmes often add their own limits on critical features such as mounting faces, wall thickness at crush zones and twist over the part length. For bright trim, agree a limit sample and inspection lighting for surface defects before series supply.

Applications

Where 5457 profiles are used

  • Exterior mouldings: belt-line, window and side mouldings
  • Door and window frames: bright surrounds and channel covers
  • Roof racks and rails: bright covers and end caps on light-duty rails
  • Interior accents: door-handle surrounds, gear-lever and dashboard trim
  • Lamp bezels and reflector frames

5457 is not a structural alloy. Bumper beams, body structure and engine parts use 6xxx, 7xxx or cast alloys; listings that put 5457 in those roles describe historic bright bumper covers at most.

FAQ

5457 automotive aluminium (aluminum) extrusions: frequently asked questions

What is 5457 aluminium used for?

Bright-anodised vehicle trim: mouldings, frames, bezels and interior accents.

How does 5457 differ from 5657?

5457 adds 0.15 – 0.45 % manganese and slightly more magnesium, giving a little more strength and better grain control; 5657 brightens to a marginally higher brilliance.

Is 5457 strong enough for structural parts?

No. With about 130 MPa typical tensile strength as extruded, it is a trim alloy. Use 6xxx alloys for structural profiles.

Can 5457 be heat treated?

No. It is non-heat-treatable; strength comes from magnesium, manganese and cold work.

What finish is applied to 5457 trim?

Chemical or electrolytic brightening, followed by a thin clear anodic film and sealing.

Do you supply 5457 internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 5457 profiles worldwide from Mumbai with 3.1 certificates and protective export packing.
