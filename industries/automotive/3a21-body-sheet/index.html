Automotive aluminium sheet

3A21 H14 / H24 Aluminium Sheet for Automotive Parts

3A21 is the Chinese GB designation for an aluminium-manganese alloy that is close to 3003, with slightly wider manganese limits and lower magnesium and zinc. In H14 and H24 tempers it gives a moderately strong, very formable and corrosion-resistant sheet for heat shields, tanks, air ducts, trim and other non-structural vehicle parts. Overview

What is 3A21 aluminium sheet?

3A21 (formerly LF21 in China, and the counterpart of the Russian AMts) is a non-heat-treatable aluminium-manganese alloy. Manganese in the range 1.0 – 1.6 % gives it about 20 % more strength than commercially pure aluminium while keeping excellent formability, weldability and corrosion resistance.

In vehicles, 3A21 is a workhorse for parts that need to be formed, joined and left in service for years without corrosion, but that do not carry crash or structural loads. Typical examples are heat shields over exhausts and turbochargers, fuel and fluid tanks for commercial vehicles, air and HVAC ducts, battery-pack covers and liners, wheel-arch and underbody shields, and interior trim backings.

It is worth being clear about what 3A21 is not used for. Modern aluminium outer panels (bonnets, doors, roofs) are made from bake-hardenable 6xxx alloys such as 6016 or 6014, and inner panels from high-magnesium 5xxx alloys such as 5182 or 5754, because those grades reach much higher strength and dent resistance. 3A21 cannot be hardened by heat treatment, so where older literature suggests it for bonnets or doors, a 6xxx or 5xxx body sheet is the right choice.

Why buyers specify 3A21 sheet

  • Very good deep-drawing and bending behaviour in O and H24 tempers
  • Excellent resistance to atmospheric corrosion, road salt splash and many fluids
  • Welds and brazes easily; widely used for tanks and ducting
  • Specified directly on GB drawings and close enough to 3003 for most Western substitutions

Working with 3A21

Forming, joining and where 3A21 is used

Pressing and drawing

O and H24 sheet draws well; H14 is better for flat panels and shallow forms. Embossing for heat shields is routine and adds stiffness.

Welding and brazing

TIG, MIG and resistance welding all work, with ER4043 or ER1100 filler. 3A21 also brazes easily, which suits fluid tanks and duct assemblies.

Corrosion

Very good resistance to road spray and atmosphere. Avoid direct contact with steel or copper without isolation to prevent galvanic attack.

  • Heat shields: flat or embossed shields for exhausts, turbochargers and fuel lines
  • Tanks: fuel, hydraulic oil, washer and air tanks, mainly on commercial vehicles
  • Ducts and HVAC housings: air ducts, blower and heater boxes
  • Battery packs: covers, liners and spacers where formability counts more than strength
  • Underbody and wheel-arch shields: light protective covers
  • Interior parts: trim backings, panel inserts and decorative surfaces

FAQ

3A21 aluminium (aluminum) sheet: frequently asked questions

Is 3A21 the same as 3003?

Very close but not identical. 3A21 allows 1.0 – 1.6 % manganese and no minimum copper; 3003 requires 0.05 – 0.20 % copper and 1.0 – 1.5 % manganese. Mechanical properties in the same temper are practically the same.

What is the difference between 3A21 H14 and H24?

Both are half-hard. H14 gets its strength from rolling alone; H24 is rolled harder and then partially annealed back to the same strength level, which leaves more elongation and better formability.

Can 3A21 be heat treated for higher strength?

No. It is non-heat-treatable. Strength comes only from manganese and cold work. If a part needs bake-hardening or high strength, use a 6xxx or 5xxx automotive sheet.

Is 3A21 suitable for car bonnets or doors?

Not for modern panels. Outer panels use bake-hardenable 6xxx sheet for dent resistance, and inner panels use 5182 or 5754. 3A21 suits heat shields, tanks, ducts and trim.

Does 3A21 weld well?

Yes. It welds by TIG, MIG and resistance methods and brazes easily, which is why it is common for tanks and ducting.

Do you export 3A21 sheet?

Yes. Metline ships 3A21 sheet and coil worldwide from Mumbai with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and seaworthy packing, on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
