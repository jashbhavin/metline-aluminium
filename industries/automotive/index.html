Overview

Lightweighting drives the shift to aluminium

Replacing steel with aluminium can cut the weight of a body or chassis part significantly. For combustion vehicles that means lower fuel use and emissions. For electric vehicles it offsets the heavy battery pack and adds range.

Aluminium also absorbs crash energy well when designed as extruded crush rails, conducts heat away from batteries and power electronics, and is easy to recycle at the end of the vehicle's life.

FAQ

Automotive & EV questions

Why are EV battery trays made of aluminium?

Aluminium is light, stiff when extruded into hollow sections, conducts heat away from the cells and resists corrosion, which makes it ideal for battery enclosures.

Which aluminium is used for car body panels?

6xxx alloys such as 6016 for outer panels, because they harden during paint baking, and 5xxx alloys such as 5182 and 5754 for inner panels.
