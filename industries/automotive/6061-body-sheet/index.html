6061 O, T4 and T6 Aluminium Sheet and Plate for Automotive Use

6061 sheet and plate is the go-to heat-treatable material for vehicle parts that need more strength than 5xxx sheet: brackets, reinforcements, battery-enclosure plates, cross-members, truck and trailer floors and machined mounting plates. It is formed in the soft O or T4 temper and aged to T6, or used directly as T6 and T651 plate. Metline supplies 6061 sheet, coil, tread plate and plate to ASTM B209 and EN 485, cut to size and certified, to automotive manufacturers worldwide.

Overview

Where 6061 sheet fits in a vehicle

Aluminium body sheet falls into two families. Visible closure panels use bake-hardening alloys such as 6016, 6014 and 6022, and deep-drawn inner panels use 5182 or 5754. 6061 sits alongside them as a structural and engineering sheet: stronger than the panel alloys once fully aged, readily machined and welded, but not designed for A-class painted surfaces or the paint-bake hardening cycle.

That makes 6061 the logical choice for parts that are formed once and then need high strength: brackets, gussets, seat and bumper reinforcements, battery-module end plates and enclosure lids, inverter and motor mounting plates, and floors and side walls of trucks, vans and trailers. Thick 6061-T651 plate is machined into fixtures, jigs and battery cooling plates.

The usual production route is to blank and form the part in the soft O or freshly quenched T4 temper, then solution treat if needed and artificially age to T6. Flat parts can be laser-cut or machined directly from T6 or T651 stock.

Why engineers specify 6061 sheet and plate

  • T6 yield strength of 240 MPa minimum, about twice that of 5754-O
  • Formable in O or T4, then heat-treatable to full strength
  • Weldable and easy to machine for brackets and mounting plates
  • Widely available in standard and wide sizes, including tread plate

Tempers

O, T4, T6 and T651 for automotive parts

6061-O

Fully annealed. Best for deep drawing and tight bends; parts are solution treated, quenched and aged after forming.

6061-T4 / T451

Solution treated and naturally aged; T451 is also stretched. Good formability with moderate strength; can be aged to T6 after forming.

6061-T6

Fully aged sheet. Used flat or with generous bends for brackets, floors and cover plates.

6061-T651

Stretched plate that stays flat during machining. Standard for mounting plates, fixtures and cooling plates.

Working with 6061

Forming, heat treatment, joining and machining

  • Forming: form in O or T4; T6 needs inside bend radii of roughly 1.5 – 3 × thickness and bends across the rolling direction
  • Heat treatment after forming: solution treat at about 530 °C, quench quickly, then age at about 160 – 175 °C; fixture parts to control distortion
  • Welding: MIG or TIG with ER4043 or ER5356; expect strength loss in the heat-affected zone unless re-aged
  • Mechanical joining: self-pierce riveting and clinching work best in T4; T6 may crack at the die side
  • Machining: T6 and T651 plate machine cleanly at high speed
  • Finishing: anodises well; for painted parts use a conversion coating before primer

FAQ

6061 aluminium (aluminum) automotive sheet: frequently asked questions

Is 6061 used for car body panels?

Not for visible outer panels. Those use bake-hardening alloys such as 6016, 6014 and 6022. 6061 sheet is used for structural parts such as brackets, reinforcements, battery-enclosure plates and commercial-vehicle floors.

Which 6061 temper is best for forming?

O temper gives maximum formability; T4 is also formable and needs only artificial ageing afterwards to reach T6. Forming in T6 is limited to generous bend radii.

What are the minimum properties of 6061-T6 sheet?

ASTM B209 and EN 485-2 require at least 290 MPa tensile and 240 MPa yield strength, with minimum elongation between 6 and 10 % depending on thickness.

What is 6061-T451?

T451 is solution treated, stretched to relieve stress and naturally aged. It is flatter and more stable than T4 and is often aged to T651 after machining or forming.

Can 6061 sheet be self-pierce riveted?

Yes, preferably in T4. In T6 the lower ductility can cause cracks on the die side, so joint trials are recommended.

Do you ship 6061 automotive sheet internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies 6061 sheet and plate worldwide with mill certificates and export packing on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
