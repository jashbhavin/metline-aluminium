Automotive body sheet

6181 T4P Automotive Body Sheet

6181 (EN AW-6181, AlSi1Mg0.8) is a copper-lean 6xxx body sheet alloy with more silicon and magnesium than 6016, giving higher strength after paint baking while keeping good hemming and corrosion behaviour. It is supplied in pre-aged T4P for outer and inner panels and in high-strength grades for structural parts. Metline supplies 6181 sheet and coil worldwide with mill, EDT or Ti/Zr pre-treated surfaces.

Overview

What is 6181 body sheet?

6181 is a European-registered Al-Mg-Si alloy with silicon of 0.8 – 1.2 % and magnesium of 0.6 – 1.0 %. That is a richer solute content than 6016, so more strengthening Mg-Si precipitates form in the paint bake. Copper is held to 0.10 % or less, which keeps the alloy's resistance to filiform corrosion close to that of 6016.

The result is a sheet that slots between 6016 and copper-bearing 6111: higher bake-hardened strength than 6016 for better dent resistance or down-gauging, with better hemming and corrosion margins than 6111. Many body sheet specifications list 6181 as a high-strength (HS) option for outer panels, roofs and structural inners.

A related alloy, 6181A, has slightly different silicon and iron windows and is used for extrusions and sheet in some programmes; the two are not automatically interchangeable, so the drawing should name one.

Why 6181 is specified

  • Higher post-bake strength than 6016 at the same gauge
  • Low copper for good filiform corrosion resistance
  • Good flat-hem performance for outer closures
  • Suitable for both visible panels and structural inners

Tempers & paint bake

T4, T4P and the paint-bake response

6xxx body sheet is delivered soft enough to stamp and gains most of its in-service strength in the paint shop. After the body is e-coated, the bake oven (typically 170 – 190 °C for 20 – 30 minutes) acts as an artificial ageing treatment: fine Mg-Si precipitates form and the yield strength of the panel rises. Strain from stamping adds a further work-hardening increment. This is the bake-hardening or paint-bake response, and it is why a door skin can be pressed easily yet resist dents on the road.

ConditionWhat it meansWhy it matters for 6181
T4Solution heat-treated and naturally aged at room temperatureNatural ageing continues for weeks; formability slowly falls and bake response weakens

Fabrication

Stamping, hemming and joining 6181

Stamping

Aluminium has lower formability limits and higher springback than mild steel. Generous radii, optimised draw beads and simulation with the actual 6181 forming-limit curve keep splits and wrinkles under control.

Hemming

Outer panels are hemmed around inner panels at doors, bonnets and tailgates. Good flat-hem performance depends on low iron-particle content and a fine grain; roller hemming is common for tight radii.

Joining

Bodies are joined by self-piercing rivets, clinching, adhesive bonding, resistance spot welding with special electrodes, laser and friction stir welding. Ti/Zr pre-treated surfaces give the most durable adhesive joints.

Shelf life

T4-type sheet ages at room temperature. Use coils within the shelf life agreed with the supplier, and store them cool and dry.

Corrosion

With phosphate or zirconium pre-treatment and e-coat, 6xxx panels perform well. Copper-bearing alloys need good coating integrity to avoid filiform corrosion at edges.

Recycling

Press-shop offcuts are segregated by alloy and returned for closed-loop recycling into new body sheet.

Because 6181 has a high solute content, it is somewhat more sensitive to natural ageing than 6016; use T4P coils within the agreed shelf life to keep forming and bake response consistent.

Applications

Where 6181 sheet is used

  • Outer closures: bonnets, doors, boot lids and tailgates where better dent resistance than 6016 is needed
  • Roof panels: large, flat panels where stiffness and dent resistance matter
  • Inner panels and reinforcements for doors and bonnets
  • Structural parts: pillar and sill reinforcements, cross-members in mixed-material bodies
  • Commercial vehicles: cab panels and load-compartment structures

FAQ

6181 automotive body sheet (aluminum): frequently asked questions

What does T4P mean for 6181?

Solution heat-treated, quenched and immediately pre-aged at a low temperature. Pre-ageing stabilises the sheet during storage and gives a faster, stronger paint-bake response than plain T4.

How is 6181 different from 6016?

6181 has less silicon but much more magnesium (0.6 – 1.0 % against 0.25 – 0.6 %), so it reaches higher strength after baking. Both are copper-lean and resist filiform corrosion well.

Is 6181 the same as 6181A?

No. They are related, but their silicon and iron limits differ. Order the alloy named on the drawing.

What tests are reported for body sheet?

Tensile properties, n and r values, post-bake proof strength, and on request hemming, roping (paint-brush lines), forming-limit curve and high-strain-rate data.

What surfaces are available?

Mill finish or EDT texture, with optional Ti/Zr pre-treatment and dry lubricant or electrostatic oil.

Do you supply 6181 worldwide?

Yes. Metline supplies 6181 sheet and coil worldwide with mill certificates and sealed export packing.
