6061 Aluminium Rectangular Tube

6061 aluminium rectangular tube is an extruded, heat-treated hollow section that gives high bending stiffness about its deep axis at about a third of the weight of steel. In T6 it combines good strength, weldability, machinability and corrosion resistance, which makes it the standard rectangular tube for trailer and truck frames, machine bases, platforms, marine structures and solar mounts. Overview

Why use 6061 rectangular tube?

A rectangular tube places most of its material in the two wide flanges, far from the bending axis, so it resists bending much better about its depth than a square tube of the same weight. Oriented correctly, it is one of the most efficient beam shapes available in aluminium.

6061 is the most widely used heat-treatable structural aluminium alloy. Extruded rectangular tube is solution heat-treated, quenched and artificially aged to T6, or stretched before ageing to T6511 so it stays straight when machined. Compared with 6063 rectangular tube, which is typically used for architectural framing, 6061 offers roughly 25 – 40 % higher strength and better machinability, so it is chosen when the section carries real structural load.

Because the shape is closed, rectangular tube also performs well in torsion, resists local buckling better than open channels of the same depth, and has clean flat faces for bolting, welding brackets or mounting equipment. Common uses range from trailer chassis rails and cross-members to conveyor frames, gantries, boat T-tops and photovoltaic mounting rails.

Advantages of 6061 rectangular tube

  • High bending stiffness about the deep axis for its weight
  • T6 strength with good weldability and corrosion resistance
  • Flat faces for brackets, bolting and machining
  • Widely available in both metric and imperial sizes

Design

Design and fabrication tips

Orientation

Put the long side in the plane of bending. A 100 × 50 × 3 mm tube is about three times stiffer on edge than flat.

Deflection

With a modulus about one-third of steel, aluminium sections must be deeper or thicker for the same stiffness; check deflection, not just strength.

Welding

MIG or TIG with ER4043 or ER5356. Weld zones fall to about 165 MPa tensile; place welds in low-stress areas or design to welded allowables.

Machining

T6511 stays straight when slotted or pocketed. Carbide tools, high speed, flood coolant.

Bending

T6 rectangular tube bends to large radii only; for tight bends, form in O or T4 and re-age.

Corrosion

Good atmospheric resistance; anodise or paint for marine and coastal exposure, and isolate from steel fasteners.

Applications

Where 6061 rectangular tube is used

  • Transport: trailer chassis rails and cross-members, truck bodies, ramps and bus frames
  • Machinery: conveyor and machine frames, gantries, automation bases and guards
  • Marine: T-tops, towers, gangways and dock structures (anodised or painted)
  • Construction and access: platforms, stair stringers, scaffolding and ladders
  • Energy: solar mounting rails and frames
  • Aerospace ground support and defence: equipment frames and shelters

FAQ

6061 aluminium (aluminum) rectangular tube: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6061-T6 and T6511 rectangular tube?

Both are solution treated and artificially aged to the same strength. T6511 is also stretched to relieve quench stresses, with minor straightening allowed, so it stays straight when machined.

How strong is 6061-T6 rectangular tube?

Specified minimums for extruded tube under ASTM B221 are 260 MPa (38 ksi) tensile and 240 MPa (35 ksi) yield. Typical values are about 310 MPa tensile and 276 MPa yield.

Can 6061 rectangular tube be welded?

Yes, by MIG or TIG with ER4043 or ER5356. The heat-affected zone falls to about 165 MPa tensile, so welded frames are designed to reduced allowables or re-heat-treated.

How much does 6061 rectangular tube weigh?

Mass per metre is (2 × (depth + width) − 4 × wall) × wall × 2.70 g/cm³. A 100 × 50 × 3 mm tube weighs about 2.33 kg/m; a 4 × 2 × 1/4 in tube about 4.79 kg/m (3.22 lb/ft).

Is 6061 or 6063 better for rectangular tube?

6061 for structural and load-bearing uses; 6063 for architectural framing where finish matters more than strength.

Do you supply 6061 rectangular tube internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 6061 tube worldwide from Mumbai with mill certificates and export packing.
