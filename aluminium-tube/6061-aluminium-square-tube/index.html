Overview

What is 6061 square tube?

6061 square tube is an extruded hollow section with four equal sides, made from the heat-treatable 6061 alloy and normally supplied in the T6 temper. It carries roughly 50 % more load than the same section in 6063-T6, which is why it is used for structures rather than trim.

Square tube is extruded either over a mandrel (seamless) or through a porthole die, where the metal splits around a bridge and re-joins under pressure inside the die. Porthole tube has longitudinal seam welds that are metallurgically sound for structural work and is how most square sections are made. Seamless square tube is specified when the part must hold internal pressure or pass a flattening or expansion test.

After extrusion the tube is solution heat-treated (usually on the press with a water or air quench), stretched to straighten it and relieve stresses, and artificially aged to T6. The result is a section with a flat-to-flat torsional stiffness close to a round tube of the same weight, but with flat faces that make bolting, welding gussets, mounting panels and clamping in a vice simple.

Why engineers choose 6061 square tube

  • Higher strength than 6063 and 6060 square tube at a similar price per kilogram
  • Flat faces are easy to drill, tap, bolt and weld, so frames go together quickly
  • Good corrosion resistance and an excellent base for anodising or powder coating
  • Specified under ASTM, EN, JIS and GB standards, so one grade suits projects in any region

Tolerances & inspection

Tolerances, straightness and inspection

Square tube is checked for outside size, wall thickness, corner radius, squareness of the corners, twist and straightness. For ASTM orders the tolerance tables of ANSI H35.2 apply; for EN orders, EN 755-8 (porthole tube) or EN 755-7 (seamless). Twist is the parameter most often overlooked on long frame members: if your assembly is jig-welded, state a twist limit per metre on the drawing.

Working with 6061

Cutting, welding, bending and finishing square tube

Cutting and drilling

Saw with a fine-pitch carbide blade and lubricant. T6 drills and taps cleanly; for thin walls use rivet nuts or threaded inserts instead of tapping directly into the wall.

Welding

TIG and MIG weld well with ER4043 or ER5356 filler. Strength near the weld drops to about T4 level, so place welds away from peak bending moment or design to welded allowables.

Bending

Square tube tends to buckle on the inside face and bow on the outside. Use a mandrel or filler, generous radii (at least 3 – 4 × the outside size in T6), or bend in T4 and age afterwards.

Machining

Machines with short chips and a good finish. Clamp thin walls with soft jaws or internal plugs to avoid crushing.

Anodising

Takes clear and coloured anodising; the finish is slightly less uniform than 6063, so choose 6063 if appearance is the priority.

Corrosion

Good in atmosphere and fresh water. In marine splash zones anodise or paint, and isolate steel fasteners to prevent galvanic corrosion.

Applications

Where 6061 square tube is used

  • Machine frames and guarding: welded or bolted bases, enclosures and conveyor stands
  • Transport: trailer frames, truck bodies, ramps, roof racks and EV battery-pack frames
  • Marine: boat T-tops, rails, ladders and deck furniture, usually anodised
  • Construction: handrails, balustrade posts, platforms, access structures and curtain-wall sub-frames
  • Solar and outdoor: PV mounting frames and signage structures
  • Robotics and automation: gantries, end-of-arm tooling and test rigs
  • Sport and leisure: fitness equipment, go-kart chassis and stage trusses

FAQ

6061 square tube: frequently asked questions

How much does 6061 square tube weigh?

For sharp corners, weight in kg/m = 2 × (2 × side − 2 × wall) × wall × 2.70 / 1000. A 50 × 50 × 3 mm tube weighs about 1.52 kg/m and a 100 × 100 × 6 mm tube about 6.09 kg/m. Radiused corners make it slightly lighter.

Is 6061 square tube stronger than 6063?

Yes. Typical yield strength in T6 is about 276 MPa for 6061 against 214 MPa for 6063, so 6061 carries roughly 30 % more load in the same section. 6063 has the better surface finish.

Is 6061 aluminum square tubing seamless?

Most square tube is porthole-extruded and contains seam welds formed inside the die, which are sound for structural use. Seamless square tube can be supplied when pressure duty or a specification requires it; state this on the order.

Can 6061-T6 square tube be welded without losing strength?

Welding reduces strength near the joint to about 165 MPa tensile. Most welded frames are designed to this reduced value; full T6 strength returns only if the assembly is solution treated and aged again.

What is the difference between T6 and T6511 square tube?

Both have the same strength. T6511 has been stretched to relieve stresses and may receive minor straightening, which keeps machined parts more stable. For most tube orders T6 is specified.

Do you supply 6061 square tube internationally?

Yes. Metline ships from Mumbai to customers worldwide with mill certificates, export packing and EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
