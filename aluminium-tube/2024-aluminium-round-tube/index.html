2024 Aluminium Round Tube

2024 aluminium round tube is a copper-alloyed, heat-treatable tube with roughly 50 % more strength than 6061-T6 and excellent fatigue resistance, which is why it is used for aircraft struts, control rods and other highly stressed tubular parts. It is supplied mainly as drawn seamless tube in T3 to AMS 4088 and ASTM B210, and as extruded tube in T3511. Metline supplies 2024 round tube with full certification, worldwide.

Overview

What is 2024 aluminium round tube?

2024 is the classic aircraft alloy of the 2xxx series, strengthened by about 4.4 % copper and 1.5 % magnesium. In tube form it combines high tensile strength, a stiff section and very good resistance to fatigue crack growth, so it is chosen for tubes that are loaded repeatedly.

The common condition for drawn tube is T3: solution heat-treated, cold worked by the drawing process, then naturally aged at room temperature. Drawing after solution treatment adds strength and gives tight dimensional control and a smooth surface, which matters for push-pull rods, hydraulic actuator parts and struts that slide in bushes. Extruded 2024 tube is supplied in T3511 (stress-relieved by stretching, with minor straightening allowed) for larger diameters and thicker walls.

The trade-off for that strength is corrosion behaviour. Copper-rich 2024 has the lowest general corrosion resistance of the common structural alloys and should be protected by anodising (often chromic or sulphuric acid), conversion coating and primer in service. It is also not suitable for fusion welding. Where welding or marine exposure matters more than fatigue strength, 6061 or 5xxx tube is the better choice.

Why engineers specify 2024 tube

  • High strength: T3 drawn tube has specified minimums of about 441 MPa tensile and 290 MPa yield
  • Excellent fatigue performance for cyclic loads
  • Good machinability in T3 and T351 conditions, with clean chips
  • Long-established aerospace specifications (AMS 4088, AMS-WW-T-700/3) and wide data in design handbooks

Tempers

2024 tube tempers explained

T3

Solution treated, cold drawn, naturally aged. The standard drawn seamless tube temper for aircraft and high-stress parts.

T3511

Extruded tube, solution treated, stretched for stress relief with minor straightening, naturally aged. For larger and thicker tube that will be machined.

T4 / T42

Solution treated and naturally aged without controlled cold work. T42 is the condition achieved when the user heat-treats from O.

O

Annealed for bending, flaring and forming; parts are then heat-treated to T42 by the user.

T8 tempers

Artificially aged after cold work for higher yield and better stress-corrosion resistance, available on request for specific programmes.

Working with 2024

Machining, joining and protecting 2024 tube

Machining

Machines well in T3 and T3511 with carbide tools, producing short chips and good finishes. Annealed material is gummy; machine in a heat-treated temper.

Welding

Not recommended for arc welding: hot cracking and a large loss of strength in the heat-affected zone. Use mechanical joints, adhesive bonding or friction processes.

Bending

T3 tube accepts moderate bends with mandrel support. For tight bends, form in O or freshly solution-treated (W) condition, then age.

Corrosion protection

Anodise (chromic, sulphuric or boric-sulphuric), apply conversion coating and primer, and seal tube ends. Avoid direct contact with carbon fibre or stainless steel without isolation.

Temperature

Retains strength better than 6061 up to about 120 – 150 °C; long exposure above that alters the naturally aged structure.

Inspection

Aerospace orders usually require traceability to heat lot, tensile results and, where specified, non-destructive inspection.

Applications

Where 2024 round tube is used

  • Aircraft structure: wing and landing-gear struts on light aircraft, push-pull control rods, torque tubes, seat and cargo-system tubes
  • Hydraulics and actuation: actuator housings, pistons and bushings machined from heavy-wall tube
  • Motorsport: suspension links, roll and steering components where weight and fatigue strength matter
  • Defence: launcher tubes, mast sections and equipment frames
  • Precision engineering: spacers, sleeves and rollers machined from tube to save material

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) round tube: frequently asked questions

What does T3 mean on 2024 tube?

Solution heat-treated, cold worked and naturally aged. For drawn tube the cold work comes from the final drawing pass, which adds strength and gives accurate dimensions.

How strong is 2024-T3 drawn tube?

Specified minimums under ASTM B210 and AMS 4088 are about 441 MPa (64 ksi) tensile and 290 MPa (42 ksi) yield, with elongation of 10 – 16 % depending on wall. Typical values are higher, around 470 – 480 MPa tensile.

Can 2024 aluminium tube be welded?

Not by normal arc welding: it is prone to hot cracking and loses much of its strength near the weld. Use mechanical fastening, bonding or friction welding instead.

Is 2024 tube corrosion resistant?

Less so than 5xxx or 6xxx alloys because of its copper content. In service it is normally anodised or conversion coated and painted, and ends are sealed.

What is the difference between AMS 4088 and ASTM B210?

AMS 4088 is an aerospace specification specific to 2024-T3 drawn seamless tubing, with aerospace testing and traceability requirements. ASTM B210 is a general specification for drawn seamless tube covering many alloys and tempers.

Do you ship 2024 tube internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies 2024 tube worldwide from Mumbai, with mill certificates, capped ends and export crating.
