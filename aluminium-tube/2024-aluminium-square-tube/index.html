2024 Aluminium Square Tube

2024 aluminium square tube is a high-strength, heat-treatable hollow section used where a frame member must resist bending, torsion and repeated loading at low weight: aircraft and defence structures, motorsport frames and precision equipment. It is supplied mainly as extruded tube in T3511 to AMS-QQ-A-200/3 and ASTM B221, and as drawn tube in T3. Metline supplies 2024 square tube cut to length and certified, worldwide.

Overview

What is 2024 aluminium square tube?

A square hollow section puts material far from the neutral axis on all four sides, so it is stiff in bending about both axes and strong in torsion. Made in 2024, it adds high strength and fatigue resistance to that geometry.

2024 is a copper-magnesium alloy that is solution heat-treated and naturally aged. Extruded square tube is normally supplied in T3511: after quenching it is stretched by a controlled amount to remove residual stress, with minor straightening allowed, and then aged at room temperature. The stress relief matters because square tube is often slotted, drilled or machined on one face, and unrelieved tube would bow as material is removed.

Square 2024 tube is less common than 6061 or 6063 square tube and is usually specified for a reason: a strut, longeron, seat track, launcher frame or race-car member that sees cyclic loads where 6061-T6 would be too weak or would crack in fatigue. Because 2024 cannot be arc welded reliably, square tube frames in this alloy are assembled with fasteners, rivets, bonded joints or machined end fittings.

Choose 2024 square tube when

  • The member carries high or cyclic loads and must stay light
  • Joints will be bolted, riveted or bonded rather than welded
  • The part will be machined and must stay straight (T3511)
  • An aerospace or defence specification calls for 2024 extrusions

Design

Designing with 2024 square tube

Joints

Use bolted or riveted gussets, machined end fittings or structural adhesive. Avoid fusion welds, which crack and lose strength.

Corrosion

Anodise or conversion coat and prime. Seal the tube interior or provide drainage where moisture can collect.

Galvanic contact

Isolate from carbon fibre and stainless steel with sealant, washers or primer.

Machining

T3511 keeps tube straight during slotting and pocketing. Use sharp carbide tools; chips break well.

Local buckling

Thin walls on wide faces can buckle before the material yields. Check width-to-thickness ratio for compression members.

Temperature

Stable to about 120 – 150 °C. For hotter service consider 2219 or 2618.

Applications

Where 2024 square tube is used

  • Aircraft: seat structures, cargo-system rails, floor support members and equipment racks
  • Defence: launcher frames, shelters, vehicle equipment mounts and portable structures
  • Motorsport and drones: chassis members, wing supports and airframe booms
  • Industrial equipment: high-load machine frames and test rigs where members are bolted
  • Precision tooling: fixtures and gantries machined from square section

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) square tube: frequently asked questions

What temper is 2024 square tube supplied in?

Usually T3511 for extruded tube: solution heat-treated, stretched for stress relief, lightly straightened and naturally aged. Drawn square tube is supplied in T3.

How strong is 2024-T3511 square tube?

For walls up to 6.3 mm (0.249 in), specified minimums are about 393 MPa (57 ksi) tensile and 290 MPa (42 ksi) yield with 12 % elongation. Typical values are higher, and thicker sections have higher minimums.

Can 2024 square tube be welded into a frame?

Arc welding is not recommended because 2024 is prone to hot cracking and loses strength near welds. Frames are bolted, riveted or bonded; for welded frames use 6061 or 7005.

How do I protect 2024 square tube from corrosion?

Anodise or apply a conversion coating and primer, seal or drain the inside of the tube, and isolate it from dissimilar metals and carbon fibre.

Is 2024 stronger than 6061 square tube?

Yes. Typical tensile strength is about 470 – 480 MPa against 310 MPa for 6061-T6, and fatigue strength is around 40 % higher.
