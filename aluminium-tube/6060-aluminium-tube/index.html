6060 Aluminium Tube

6060 aluminium tube is extruded from the leanest and most easily extruded of the common 6xxx alloys, which gives smooth, precise tube with an excellent decorative anodised finish. It is the standard European alloy for architectural, furniture, lighting and interior tube where appearance matters more than high strength. Metline supplies 6060 round, square and rectangular tube in T4, T5, T6, T64 and T66 to EN 755, worldwide.

Overview

What is 6060 aluminium tube?

6060 is a heat-treatable aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloy with less magnesium and silicon than 6063 and much less than 6061 or 6082. The low alloy content lets it extrude at high speed through complex dies and quench effectively straight off the press, so tube can be aged to T5 or T6 without a separate solution heat treatment.

The result is tube with very good surface quality and tight dimensions, which anodises to a bright, even finish and takes powder coating well. Those qualities make 6060 the default tube alloy in much of Europe for window and door systems, curtain-wall elements, handrails, shopfitting, furniture, shower and bathroom fittings, lighting and exhibition systems.

The price of that easy extrusion is moderate strength. 6060-T6 tube has minimum tensile strength in the range of about 170 – 190 MPa depending on wall, below 6063 and well below 6061 or 6082. For load-bearing frames, use 6082 or 6061; for most visible and lightly loaded tube, 6060 is the economical, good-looking choice.

Why buyers choose 6060 tube

  • Excellent surface for decorative anodising and powder coating
  • High extrusion speed keeps cost down and allows thin, precise walls
  • Press-quenchable, so T5 and T6 are achieved economically
  • Good corrosion resistance and easy to bend in T4 or T64

Tempers

6060 tube tempers explained

T4

Press-quenched and naturally aged. Soft and ductile for bending and forming; can be aged to T6 afterwards.

T5

Press-quenched and artificially aged. Economical temper for architectural tube that is not highly loaded.

T6

Fully aged after an effective quench. The usual temper for general and architectural tube.

T64

Under-aged for bending quality: higher strength than T4 with good formability, used for bent furniture and handrails.

T66

T6 with tighter process control giving higher minimum strength; used where 6060 must carry more load.

Working with 6060

Bending, finishing and joining 6060 tube

Bending

Bend in T4 or T64 for tight radii; T6 accepts moderate bends with mandrel support.

Anodising

Gives a bright, uniform finish in clear and colour anodising; specify decorative quality and visible faces.

Powder coating

Excellent adhesion after pretreatment; standard for window and door systems.

Welding

TIG and MIG with ER4043 or ER5356; heat-affected zone strength drops towards T4 level.

Machining

Fair in T6; softer than 6061, so use sharp tools and good chip clearance.

Mixed walls

Specify wall and temper together: on profiles with several wall thicknesses the lowest requirement applies.

Applications

Where 6060 tube is used

  • Architecture: window and door frames, curtain-wall mullions, louvres and handrails
  • Furniture and interiors: chair and table frames, shopfitting, display and exhibition systems
  • Lighting and electrical: luminaire bodies, LED housings, heat-sink tube
  • Bathroom and kitchen: shower rails, towel rails, appliance trims
  • Leisure: ladders, tent poles and garden structures

FAQ

6060 aluminium (aluminum) tube: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6060 and 6063 tube?

6060 has slightly less magnesium and silicon, so it extrudes faster and gives a very good surface, but has lower strength: about 190 MPa minimum tensile in thin-wall T6 against 215 MPa for 6063. They are often interchangeable for architectural tube.

Is 6060 tube strong enough for structures?

For lightly loaded frames, handrails and furniture, yes. For primary structures, use 6082 or 6061, which are roughly 40 – 60 % stronger.

Which 6060 temper is best for bending?

T4 for tight bends, followed by ageing if needed, or T64, which is designed as a bending temper with better strength than T4.

Does 6060 anodise well?

Yes. Its low alloy content and controlled iron give a bright, uniform anodised finish, which is why it is popular for visible architectural and interior tube.

Is 6060 available in the US as AA 6060?

6060 is a registered alloy but is mainly produced in Europe. In the US, 6063 is the usual equivalent for the same uses.
