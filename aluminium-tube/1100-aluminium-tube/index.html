1100 Aluminium Tube

1100 aluminium tube is commercially pure (99.0 % minimum) aluminium, drawn or extruded into round, square and coiled tube. It is the most formable and one of the most corrosion-resistant and conductive tube grades, used for heat-exchange lines, chemical and food-handling tubing, instrument lines and decorative work. Metline supplies 1100 tube in O, H14, H18 and H112 to ASTM and EN standards, worldwide.

Overview

What is 1100 aluminium tube?

1100 is the commercially pure grade of the 1xxx series: at least 99.0 % aluminium with a small, deliberate copper content of 0.05 – 0.20 %. It cannot be heat-treated; any extra strength comes from cold work, which is why its tempers are designated O and H.

As tube, 1100 is chosen for what it does well rather than for strength. It bends, flares, expands and swages without cracking, even in tight radii; it resists corrosion in fresh water, many chemicals and food products; and it conducts heat and electricity far better than alloyed grades. Annealed 1100 tube can be hand-bent into coils and fitted with flare or compression fittings.

Its weakness is low strength: about 90 MPa tensile in the O temper, roughly a third of 6061-T6. If the line carries significant pressure, vibration or structural load, 3003 (about 20 % stronger) or 6061 is usually specified instead. For many heat-transfer, instrumentation and fluid-handling jobs, though, 1100 is the simplest and most forgiving choice.

Main advantages of 1100 tube

  • Excellent formability for bending, flaring, beading and expanding
  • High thermal conductivity (about 222 W/m·K annealed) for heat exchange
  • Very good corrosion resistance and a clean, non-toxic surface for food and chemical use
  • Easy to weld and braze; anodises to a bright, clear finish

Tempers

Choosing a temper for 1100 tube

O (annealed)

For bending, coiling, flaring and expanding into fins. Often supplied in coils for refrigeration and instrument lines.

H14

The standard straight-length drawn temper: stiffer, holds its shape, still bends to moderate radii.

H18

Full hard for maximum strength and stiffness where the tube is used straight, such as antenna or decorative tubing.

H112

Extruded tube supplied as pressed, for larger diameters and thicker walls where cold drawing is not needed.

Working with 1100

Bending, joining and finishing 1100 tube

Bending

O-temper tube bends tightly with simple tooling; use a mandrel or spring for thin walls to prevent kinking. H14 needs larger radii.

Flaring and expanding

Very ductile, so it accepts flare fittings and mechanical expansion into fin packs without splitting.

Welding

TIG and MIG weld easily with ER1100 or ER4043 filler. Strength in the weld zone falls to the O level.

Brazing

Brazes readily with Al-Si filler (4047 or 4343-clad parts) because of its high melting point.

Machining

Soft and gummy; use sharp, polished tools with large rake angles and plenty of lubricant.

Finishing

Anodises to a clear, bright finish and polishes well for decorative tube.

Applications

Where 1100 tube is used

  • Heat transfer: heat-exchanger and evaporator tube, refrigeration lines, solar-thermal collectors
  • Chemical and food processing: product lines, cooling coils and equipment where cleanliness and corrosion resistance matter
  • Instrumentation: gauge, sampling and pneumatic control lines
  • Electrical: conductors, shielding and antenna elements
  • Decorative and furniture: trim, frames and bright-anodised tube
  • Cryogenic and laboratory equipment: transfer lines where ductility at low temperature matters

FAQ

1100 aluminium (aluminum) tube: frequently asked questions

What is 1100 aluminium tube used for?

Heat-exchanger and refrigeration lines, chemical and food-processing tubing, instrument and pneumatic lines, electrical conductors and decorative tube: jobs that need formability, conductivity and corrosion resistance more than strength.

Can 1100 tube be heat-treated for strength?

No. 1100 is non-heat-treatable; strength comes only from cold work (H tempers). Annealing returns it to the soft O temper.

What is the difference between 1100-O and 1100-H14 tube?

O is fully annealed, about 90 MPa tensile, and bends and flares easily. H14 is half hard, about 124 MPa tensile, stiffer and better for straight lengths, but needs larger bend radii.

Is 1100 or 3003 better for tubing?

3003 is about 20 % stronger with similar formability and corrosion resistance, so it is preferred for pressure lines and HVAC. 1100 has higher conductivity and slightly better ductility.

Can 1100 tube be welded?

Yes, very easily by TIG or MIG using ER1100 or ER4043 filler. It is also readily brazed.

Do you supply 1100 tube in coils?

Yes, annealed 1100 tube in small diameters and thin walls can be supplied in coils, as well as straight lengths cut to size.
