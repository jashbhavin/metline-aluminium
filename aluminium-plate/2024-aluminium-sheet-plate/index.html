Overview

What is 2024 aluminium?

2024 contains about 4.4 % copper, 1.5 % magnesium and 0.6 % manganese. It is naturally aged to the T3 (sheet) and T351 (plate) tempers, which give an exceptional combination of tensile strength, fatigue resistance and fracture toughness. That balance, not peak strength, is why 2024-T3 has been the standard fuselage skin alloy for decades.

Compared with 7075-T6, 2024-T3 is weaker in static strength but grows fatigue cracks more slowly and tolerates damage better, so it is used for tension-dominated structure such as lower wing skins and pressurised fuselage panels. Compared with 6061, it is roughly 50 % stronger but much less resistant to corrosion and essentially unweldable by fusion methods.

Outside aerospace, 2024 plate is machined into high-strength fittings, gears, hydraulic parts, truck wheels and defence components, and thin 2024 sheet is used for rivets, scientific instruments and high-performance sporting goods.

Key strengths of 2024

  • High tensile strength with excellent fatigue performance
  • Good fracture toughness and slow fatigue-crack growth in T3/T351
  • Machines well in T351 and T851, with low distortion in stretched plate
  • Available as Alclad sheet with a pure-aluminium protective layer

Tempers

2024 tempers: T3, T4, T351, T851 and O

T3

Solution heat-treated, cold-worked (by levelling or stretching) and naturally aged. The standard sheet temper for aircraft skins.

T351

Solution heat-treated, stretched 1.5 – 3 % and naturally aged. The standard plate temper for machined parts, with low residual stress.

T4 / T42

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged without the cold work; T42 indicates heat treatment by the user from O or F material.

T851

T351 plate artificially aged. Higher yield strength, better resistance to stress corrosion and better stability at moderately elevated temperatures, but lower elongation and toughness.

O

Annealed for forming. Parts are formed and then heat-treated to T42 or T62.

Alclad

Any sheet temper with protective cladding. Choose it for exposed skins; use bare sheet where the surface will be machined or chemically milled.

Fabrication

Machining, forming, joining and protection

Machining

Very good in T351 and T851 with carbide tools; chips break well. T851 machines slightly better and holds tolerance with less distortion.

Forming

Form in O or freshly quenched W temper; T3 accepts only moderate bends (about 2.5 – 4 × t radius depending on thickness).

Joining

Riveting and bolting are standard. Fusion welding causes hot cracking and severe strength loss; friction-stir welding is used in some aerospace applications.

Corrosion

Bare 2024 needs protection: Alclad sheet, anodising, conversion coating and primer. T3/T351 can be sensitive to stress corrosion in the short-transverse direction of thick plate; T851 improves this.

Applications

Applications of 2024 sheet and plate

  • Aircraft structure: fuselage skins, lower wing skins, stringers, bulkheads and fittings
  • Machined parts: high-strength fittings, gears, shafts, hydraulic valve bodies
  • Transport: truck wheels, structural parts under cyclic load
  • Defence and space: airframe and launcher parts, missile components
  • Fasteners and instruments: rivets, scientific and optical instrument parts

Compared with 7075, 2024 trades static strength for better fatigue and damage tolerance. For thick plate (above about 40 mm) where through-thickness toughness matters, consider the higher-purity variant 2124.

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) sheet and plate: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 2024-T3 and 2024-T351?

T3 is the sheet temper (solution treated, cold-worked, naturally aged); T351 is the plate temper, where the cold work is a controlled 1.5 – 3 % stretch that relieves quench stresses. Typical strengths are very similar.

Is 2024 stronger than 6061?

Yes. 2024-T3 has about 483 MPa tensile and 345 MPa yield, against 310 and 276 MPa for 6061-T6. 6061 is far easier to weld and resists corrosion better.

What is Alclad 2024?

Sheet with a thin layer of pure aluminium or 7072 metallurgically bonded to each side. The cladding protects the copper-bearing core against corrosion, at the cost of slightly lower strength.

Can 2024 aluminium be welded?

Not by conventional MIG or TIG for structural parts. It is riveted or bolted; friction-stir welding is used in specialised aerospace work.

2024 or 7075: which should I choose?

7075-T6 is stronger in static loading; 2024-T3 has better fatigue and fracture toughness. Use 2024 for tension-loaded, fatigue-critical parts and 7075 for compression or highly stressed fittings.

Do you supply 2024 plate with ultrasonic testing?

Yes. Plate can be supplied ultrasonically tested to AMS-STD-2154 class requirements with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate.
