7005 Aluminium Sheet and Plate

7005 is a weldable Al-Zn-Mg alloy: stronger than 6061, and unlike 7075 it can be fusion-welded, with the heat-affected zone recovering much of its strength by natural ageing after welding. It is best known for bicycle frames and extrusions, and 7005 sheet and plate are used for welded brackets, frames and transport parts. Metline supplies 7005 plate and sheet in T6 and T651, cut to size, worldwide.

Overview

What is 7005 aluminium?

7005 contains about 4.5 % zinc and 1.4 % magnesium with no significant copper. Copper-free Al-Zn-Mg alloys are the weldable branch of the 7xxx family: the strengthening precipitates dissolve in the weld heat but re-form at room temperature over the following weeks, so a welded 7005 joint regains a large share of its strength without post-weld heat treatment.

Zirconium, chromium and manganese control grain structure and improve resistance to stress corrosion. The alloy is also quench-insensitive: it reaches good properties after air or mild water quenching, which is why it extrudes well and why welded frames can be aged without a separate solution treatment.

7005 is primarily an extrusion and tube alloy. Rolled sheet and plate are made to order, usually in T6 or T651, for parts that must be welded to 7005 tubes or extrusions, or for welded structures that need more strength than 6061 or 6082 can give.

Working with 7005

Welding, machining and finishing

Welding

MIG and TIG weld with 5356 or 5183 filler. The heat-affected zone softens at first and recovers much of its strength by natural ageing over about 30 days at room temperature, or faster with an artificial ageing cycle.

Stress corrosion

Like all Al-Zn-Mg alloys, 7005 can be sensitive to stress corrosion where welds carry high residual or sustained tensile stress, especially in the short-transverse direction. Good joint design and a post-weld ageing step reduce the risk.

Machining

Machines well in T6/T651, with better chip control than 6xxx alloys. T651 plate stays flat during pocketing.

Anodising

Takes protective anodising and paint well. Zinc content can give a slightly yellow tint, so it is less suited to decorative clear finishes.

Applications

Where 7005 is used

  • Bicycles: frames, dropouts and gussets welded to 7005 tube
  • Transport: aircraft seat structure, cargo panels, truck and trailer frames, rail car parts
  • Medical and mobility: wheelchairs, walking aids and prosthetic structures
  • Sports equipment: archery risers, baseball bats and climbing hardware
  • Industrial frames: welded frames and brackets that need more strength than 6061

FAQ

7005 aluminium (aluminum): FAQ

Can 7005 aluminium be welded?

Yes. It is one of the weldable 7xxx alloys. Use 5356 or 5183 filler; the heat-affected zone recovers much of its strength by natural ageing after welding.

Is 7005 stronger than 6061?

Yes, by roughly 10 – 25 % depending on temper, and its welded joints retain more strength than welded 6061.

Why is 7005 used for bicycle frames?

It combines good strength with weldability and does not need full solution heat treatment after welding, so frames can be welded and simply aged.

Is 7005 as strong as 7075?

No. 7075-T6 is much stronger but cannot be fusion-welded reliably. 7005 is chosen when the part must be welded.

Is 7005 plate a stock item?

7005 is mainly an extrusion alloy, so plate and sheet are usually made to order.
