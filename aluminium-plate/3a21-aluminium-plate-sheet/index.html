Overview

What is 3A21 aluminium?

3A21 belongs to the group of older national designations that use a letter in the second position. It is the counterpart of the Russian AMts (АМц) and is functionally equivalent to AA 3003 and EN AW-3003: a manganese-strengthened aluminium alloy, often called "rust-proof aluminium" because of its corrosion resistance.

Like 3003, 3A21 cannot be heat-treated; strength comes from cold rolling. Annealed sheet is soft and very ductile; H14 and H24 give moderate stiffness for panels and tanks; as-rolled H112 plate is used for thicker parts. The alloy welds and brazes easily and is widely used for fuel tanks and lines, heat exchangers, containers and roofing.

Drawings from Chinese, Russian and CIS design offices frequently specify 3A21, while Western drawings specify 3003. In most applications the two are interchangeable, but the composition limits are not identical, so we always confirm which standard governs before supply.

3A21 in brief

  • Corrosion resistance close to commercially pure aluminium
  • Excellent ductility in O temper; good in H24
  • Easy to weld with Al-Mn or Al-Si fillers and to braze
  • Interchangeable with 3003 in most non-critical applications

Fabrication

Working with 3A21

Forming

O temper accepts deep drawing and tight bends; H24 handles moderate bends with less springback than H14.

Welding

TIG, MIG and gas welding with Al-Mn (such as the GB SAlMn type) or ER4043 filler. Brazing is straightforward.

Corrosion

Very good in the atmosphere, fresh water and petroleum products, which suits fuel tanks and lines.

Applications

Applications of 3A21 sheet and plate

  • Fuel and lubricant tanks and lines for vehicles, aircraft ground equipment and machinery
  • Heat exchangers and brazed plate exchangers
  • Liquid containers, cookware and washing-machine drums
  • Roofing, patterned and acoustic panels, vehicle body skins
  • Road signs and general sheet metal parts with light loads

FAQ

3A21 aluminium sheet: frequently asked questions

Is 3A21 the same as 3003?

They are very close and interchangeable in most uses. 3A21 allows manganese up to 1.6 % and has no minimum copper; 3003 requires 0.05 – 0.20 % copper. Confirm acceptance with the design authority for critical parts.

Can 3A21 be heat-treated?

No. It is an Al-Mn alloy strengthened only by cold work. Correct tempers are O, H14, H24, H18 and H112.

What is the old name for 3A21?

It was formerly designated LF21 in the Chinese system and corresponds to AMts in the Russian GOST system.

Can you supply 3003 instead of 3A21?

Yes, if your specification allows it. We can supply either and certify to the governing standard.

What is 3A21 used for?

Fuel tanks and lines, heat exchangers, containers, cookware, roofing and lightly loaded sheet metal parts.
