Overview

What is 6061 aluminium plate?

6061 belongs to the 6xxx family, where magnesium and silicon combine to form magnesium silicide (Mg2Si). Heat treatment dissolves and then precipitates these particles, which is why 6061-T6 is far stronger than non-heat-treatable grades: about twice the tensile strength of 3003 and about 40 % more than 5052.

The alloy was introduced in 1935 as "61S" and has been the default general-engineering aluminium ever since. A small copper addition raises strength, and chromium controls grain structure and improves resistance to stress corrosion. The result is a plate that machines cleanly, takes an excellent anodised finish, welds with standard MIG and TIG procedures and keeps its properties across a wide temperature range.

In practice, 6061 plate is the material engineers reach for when a part needs more strength than 5xxx alloys but does not justify the cost, corrosion sensitivity or poor weldability of 2xxx and 7xxx aerospace grades. Machine frames, jigs and fixtures, base plates, vacuum chambers, marine fittings, vehicle structures and semiconductor equipment are all routinely cut from 6061-T651.

Why buyers choose 6061

  • Best all-round balance of strength, machinability, weldability and corrosion resistance
  • T651 plate is stress-relieved by stretching, so it stays flat during heavy machining
  • Available from almost every rolling mill, which keeps lead times short and prices stable
  • Accepted under ASTM, AMS, EN, JIS and GB standards, so one grade works for global projects

Tempers

Which 6061 temper should you order?

6061-T651

Solution heat-treated, stretched 1.5 – 3 % to relieve quench stresses, then artificially aged. The standard choice for plate over 6 mm that will be machined, because it does not warp as material is removed.

6061-T6

Same strength as T651 without the stretching step. Normal for sheet up to about 6 mm, where residual stress is low and the sheet is used flat or formed lightly.

6061-T4

Solution treated and naturally aged. Softer and more formable; parts can be bent in T4 and then artificially aged to T6 after forming.

6061-O

Fully annealed for maximum formability: deep bends, spinning and drawing. Strength is restored by full heat treatment after forming.

6061-T6511 / T6510

Stretched tempers for extruded bar and profiles rather than rolled plate; listed here because buyers often compare them.

Cast tooling plate

If you need extreme flatness rather than strength, consider a cast and precision-milled tooling plate (for example 5083 cast) alongside 6061-T651.

Working with 6061

Machining, welding, forming and finishing

Machining

In T6/T651 temper 6061 produces short, broken chips and a bright finish. Use sharp carbide tools with high positive rake, high spindle speeds and flood coolant. T651 keeps parts flat when large amounts of material are removed from one side.

Welding

MIG and TIG weld readily. ER4043 filler resists hot cracking and suits parts that will be heat-treated after welding; ER5356 gives higher shear strength in fillet welds and a better colour match after anodising. Expect the heat-affected zone to fall to roughly T4-level strength (design codes use about 165 MPa / 24 ksi) unless the assembly is re-heat-treated.

Forming

T6 has limited ductility: allow inside bend radii of about 1.5 – 3 × thickness and bend across the rolling direction. For tight bends, form in O or T4 and age afterwards.

Anodising

Takes clear, coloured and hard (Type III) anodic coatings well, giving a uniform appearance and a wear-resistant surface. Machined faces anodise more evenly than mill-finish faces.

Corrosion

Good resistance to atmospheric and fresh-water corrosion and fair in seawater. For permanent immersion in seawater, 5083 or 5086 is the better choice; for 6061 in marine service, anodise or paint.

Temperature

Retains useful strength up to about 100 – 150 °C. Prolonged exposure above that over-ages the alloy and reduces strength. Performs well at cryogenic temperatures.

Applications

Where 6061 aluminium plate is used

  • Machinery and automation: base plates, gantries, linear-motion carriages, robot end-effectors, jigs and fixtures
  • Semiconductor and vacuum: chamber bodies, wafer-handling plates and frames, where clean machining and anodising matter
  • Transport: truck and trailer components, rail interiors, bus frames, EV battery trays and brackets
  • Marine: deck hardware, brackets and superstructure fittings that are anodised or painted
  • Aerospace and defence: ground-support equipment, secondary structures and fittings to AMS 4027
  • Construction: structural plates, connection plates, bridge decking components and architectural features
  • Moulds and tooling: prototype moulds, vacuum-forming tools and short-run injection moulds
  • Consumer and sports goods: bicycle parts, camera and drone frames, electronics housings

FAQ

6061 aluminium (aluminum) plate: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6061-T6 and 6061-T651?

Both are solution heat-treated and artificially aged and have the same strength. T651 plate is additionally stretched by 1.5 – 3 % after quenching to relieve internal stresses, so it stays flat when machined. Order T651 for plate that will be machined; T6 is normal for thinner sheet.

Is 6061 aluminium plate good for marine use?

It performs well above the waterline and in splash zones, especially when anodised or painted. For hulls or parts permanently immersed in seawater, 5083 or 5086 marine plate offers better corrosion resistance and keeps its strength after welding.

Can 6061-T651 be welded?

Yes, by MIG or TIG with ER4043 or ER5356 filler. The heat-affected zone loses part of its T6 strength, so welded structures are designed to reduced allowable stresses, or the assembly is heat-treated again after welding.

How much does a 6061 aluminium sheet weigh?

A 6061 sheet weighs 2.70 kg per m² for each millimetre of thickness. A 1220 × 2440 × 6 mm sheet weighs about 48.2 kg, and a 1250 × 2500 × 10 mm plate about 84.4 kg.

What is the European equivalent of 6061?

EN AW-6061 (AlMg1SiCu, Werkstoff 3.3211). In practice many European projects specify 6082, which has very similar properties and is more widely stocked there.

Do you ship 6061 plate internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies customers worldwide from Mumbai, with export packing, mill certificates and shipping by sea or air on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
