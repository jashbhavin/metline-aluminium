6062 Aluminium Sheet & Plate

6062 is a heat-treatable Al-Mg-Si-Cu alloy that is essentially 6061 with a tighter, lower chromium range. It still appears on older aircraft drawings, AMS tubing specifications, maintenance manuals and legacy mill certificates. Overview

What is 6062 aluminium?

6062 was registered alongside 6061 and 6063 in the early days of the four-digit system; under the older Alcoa naming it was 62S, next to 61S (6061) and 63S (6063). Chemically it is 6061 with chromium held to 0.04 – 0.14 % instead of 0.04 – 0.35 %. Everything else, including the magnesium, silicon and copper ranges, is the same.

The narrower chromium window mattered for drawn tube and extrusions. Chromium controls grain structure, but at higher levels it increases quench sensitivity, which makes it harder to reach full strength in some sections. Limiting it gave more consistent response for thin-wall hydraulic and oxygen tubing, and SAE still lists AMS 4092 and AMS 4093 for 6062-T6 tubing with a nominal 0.09 % chromium.

Today 6062 is not a routine mill grade for sheet and plate. Buyers meet it mainly on old drawings, legacy certificates, military maintenance documents and spare-part specifications. That makes sourcing a documentation exercise as much as a metallurgical one: the question is usually whether to have 6062 produced to order or to obtain written approval for a substitute.

Key points for buyers

  • 6062 is a genuine registered alloy, not a typing error for 6061, 6063 or 6262
  • Its chemistry falls entirely inside the 6061 limits, with a narrower chromium range
  • Mechanical properties in T6 are in the same class as 6061-T6
  • We never substitute 6061, 6082 or any other grade without the buyer's written approval

A practical consequence: a lot of 6061 whose certified chromium happens to fall between 0.04 and 0.14 % also meets the 6062 chemistry. Whether that lot can be certified as 6062 depends on the order specification and the customer's quality rules, so it must be agreed in writing, not assumed.

Tempers

6062 tempers you may see on documents

6062-T6

Solution heat-treated and artificially aged by the producer. The most common condition on drawings and in the AMS tubing specifications.

6062-T62

Solution heat-treated and artificially aged by the user (or from O / F by the producer to demonstrate response). Common in older records where parts were heat-treated after forming.

6062-T4

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged. Used where the part is formed before final ageing.

6062-O

Annealed. Maximum formability; strength is developed by full heat treatment afterwards.

T651 for plate

If plate will be heavily machined, a stretched T651 condition gives better flatness. Confirm it is acceptable when the drawing only says T6.

Trade listings sometimes attach European chemical designations or EN AW numbers to 6062. We have not found 6062 in EN 573-3, and the chemical names quoted in some listings belong to the free-machining alloy 6262. If your document gives a European designation, send it to us and we will check what material is actually meant before quoting.

Working with 6062

Fabrication and applications

Welding

Welds like 6061 by MIG and TIG with 4043 or 5356 filler. Heat-affected zones lose part of their T6 strength unless re-heat-treated.

Forming

Form in O or T4 and age afterwards for tight bends; T6 accepts moderate bends with radii of a few times thickness.

Machining

Good in T6, similar to 6061. For heavy machining of plate, request a stretched temper.

Corrosion

Good resistance in fresh water and marine atmospheres; anodise or paint for long-term seawater exposure.

  • Aircraft maintenance: replacement parts where historical documents list 6062 for oxygen and hydraulic lines, extrusions and sheet-metal parts
  • Vehicles and transport: legacy structural parts and brackets
  • Profiles and frames: trusses, glazing beads, connectors and frame parts originally specified in 6062
  • Precision parts: couplings, mounts and valve components made from 6062 plate or bar

FAQ

6062 aluminium (aluminum): frequently asked questions

Is 6062 the same as 6061?

Almost. 6062 has the same limits as 6061 for every element except chromium, which is restricted to 0.04 – 0.14 % instead of 0.04 – 0.35 %. Properties after heat treatment are essentially the same, but the two are separate designations.

Can I use 6061 instead of 6062?

Only if the drawing owner or customer approves it in writing. A 6061 lot with certified chromium within 0.04 – 0.14 % meets the 6062 chemistry, which can make approval straightforward.

Is 6062 the same as 6262?

No. 6262 adds lead and bismuth for free machining. It is a different alloy with different uses and should not be confused with 6062.

What is 6062-T62?

T62 means the material was solution heat-treated and artificially aged by the user, or by the producer from the O or F condition. Its properties are similar to T6.

Is 6062 still produced?

It is not a routine stock grade for sheet and plate, but it can be produced to order. Tube specifications for 6062-T6 are still listed by SAE.

Do you ship 6062 internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies 6062 and approved alternatives worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates, traceability and export packing.

Have a drawing that calls for 6062?
