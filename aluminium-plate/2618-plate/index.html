2618 / 2618A Aluminium Plate & Forged Plate

2618 and 2618A are aluminium-copper-magnesium alloys with nickel and iron additions that keep their strength at temperatures where most aluminium alloys soften, roughly 150 – 250 °C. Overview

What is 2618 aluminium?

2618 was developed in Britain as Hiduminium RR58 for aero-engine parts and later became the skin alloy of the Concorde supersonic airliner, whose surface heated to over 100 °C in cruise. Nickel and iron form thermally stable Al9FeNi particles that resist coarsening, so the alloy holds strength and creep resistance far better than 2024 or 7075 at elevated temperature.

Today 2618 is the classic material for forged pistons, turbocharger and compressor wheels, impellers and other rotating or hot-running components, as well as aircraft and engine structure that operates warm. Rolled and forged plate is used to machine prototype and low-volume parts that would otherwise be forged to shape, such as compressor wheels for motorsport and test engines.

Two versions exist. 2618 is the Aluminum Association grade used with AMS specifications (for example AMS 4132 for T61 die and hand forgings). 2618A is the European grade (EN AW-2618A, AlCu2Mg1.5Ni) with slightly wider limits, used for plate and clad sheet under EN and UK aerospace standards.

Where 2618 makes sense

  • Parts working continuously between about 150 and 250 °C
  • Rotating components that need creep and fatigue resistance when hot
  • Prototype pistons, impellers and compressor wheels machined from solid
  • Aircraft structure exposed to aerodynamic or engine heating

Tempers

2618 tempers and product forms

T61 forged plate and blocks

Solution treated, quenched in boiling water to limit residual stress, and artificially aged. The standard condition for forgings and forged plate for pistons and wheels.

T651 rolled plate

Solution treated, stretched and aged. Flat, low-stress plate for machining.

T851 rolled plate

Stretched before artificial ageing; used under EN aerospace standards for structural plate.

T6 / T62 clad sheet

Thin sheet clad for corrosion protection, historically used for supersonic airframe skins (UK DTD 5070 family).

Fabrication

Machining and processing 2618

Machining

Machines well in T61 with carbide tooling; iron-nickel particles are slightly abrasive, so expect somewhat shorter tool life than with 2024.

Welding

Not recommended for fusion welding; parts are machined from solid or forged to shape.

Corrosion

Moderate. Protect by anodising or coatings, or use clad sheet for skins.

Heat treatment

The boiling-water quench in T61 reduces residual stress in heavy sections. If re-heat-treating, follow the specification cycle closely.

Applications

Applications of 2618 plate and forgings

  • Forged and machined pistons for high-performance and racing engines
  • Turbocharger compressor wheels, impellers and fan components
  • Aero-engine casings, rings and components running warm
  • Supersonic and high-speed aircraft skins and structure
  • Prototype and low-volume rotating parts machined from forged plate

For room-temperature structural use, 2024 or 7075 is stronger and cheaper. Choose 2618 when the part runs hot for long periods, and compare with 2219 when welding is also required.

FAQ

2618 aluminium (aluminum) plate: frequently asked questions

What is 2618 aluminium used for?

Parts that must stay strong at elevated temperature: forged pistons, turbocharger and compressor wheels, aero-engine parts and supersonic aircraft skins.

What is the difference between 2618 and 2618A?

2618 is the Aluminum Association grade used in AMS forging specifications; 2618A is the European grade with slightly wider composition limits. Properties are essentially the same.

Is 2618 better than 4032 for pistons?

4032 has lower thermal expansion and better wear resistance due to its high silicon; 2618 is tougher and more fatigue resistant, so it is preferred for highly loaded racing and boosted engines.

What does the T61 temper mean for 2618?

Solution heat-treated, quenched in boiling water to limit residual stress, then artificially aged. It is the standard temper for 2618 forgings.

Can 2618 be welded?

It is not normally fusion welded; parts are machined from solid or forged to shape.
