1060 Aluminium Plate & Sheet

1060 is commercially pure aluminium with at least 99.6 % Al, giving slightly higher electrical and thermal conductivity than 1050 together with excellent formability and corrosion resistance. It is the usual choice for busbars, capacitor and battery parts, heat spreaders, chemical tanks and reflective products. Metline supplies 1060 plate and sheet to ASTM B209 and EN 485 in all common tempers, worldwide.

Overview

What is 1060 aluminium?

1060 sits one step above 1050 in purity: its 99.60 % aluminium minimum and tighter iron and manganese limits leave fewer impurity atoms to scatter electrons, so it conducts electricity and heat a little better. Like all 1xxx grades it cannot be heat-treated; it is strengthened only by cold rolling, and even full-hard 1060 is a low-strength material.

That combination makes 1060 an electrical and thermal grade first. Busbars and connector plates, capacitor housings, battery tabs and current collectors, transformer windings, heat sinks and heat-spreader plates all use it. Its chemical purity also suits tanks and equipment for chemicals and food, and its bright surface suits reflectors, nameplates and signs.

Unlike 1050, 1060 is listed in ASTM B209, so it is the common commercially pure grade in North American sheet and plate supply. In Europe and Asia it is registered as EN AW-1060 and in GB/T 3880, and is widely produced for the battery and electronics industries.

Where 1060 makes sense

  • You need high electrical or thermal conductivity in a sheet or plate form
  • The part is deep drawn, spun, stamped or bent tightly
  • Corrosion resistance and chemical purity matter more than strength
  • You need a grade listed in ASTM B209 for a pure-aluminium application

Tempers

Choosing a 1060 temper

1060-O

Fully annealed for deep drawing, spinning and severe forming, and for the highest conductivity.

1060-H12 / H22

Light cold work; still very formable, with a little more stiffness for flat panels.

1060-H14 / H24

The most common sheet tempers. H24 is partially annealed, giving better bendability than H14 at similar strength.

1060-H16 / H26

Harder, for parts that need to keep their shape with limited forming.

1060-H18

Full hard; used for stamped battery and electronic parts, and for thin strip that must stay flat.

1060-H112

As-rolled plate with minimum properties; common for thick busbar and conductor plate.

Working with 1060

Forming, joining and finishing 1060

Forming and stamping

Excellent in O and H2x tempers; H18 stamps cleanly for thin battery and electronic parts with little burr when tools are sharp.

Welding

Welds easily by TIG, MIG, ultrasonic, laser, resistance and friction stir; ultrasonic and laser welding are standard for battery tabs and connections.

Electrical joints

Clean the oxide and use contact grease or plating (tin, nickel or silver) at bolted joints to keep contact resistance low.

Machining

Soft and gummy; use sharp, high-rake tools with lubricant.

Anodising

Gives a bright, clear finish suitable for reflective and decorative use.

Corrosion

Excellent in atmosphere, water and many chemicals; avoid contact with copper or steel without isolation.

Applications

Where 1060 aluminium plate and sheet is used

  • Electrical: busbars, connector plates, transformer and reactor windings, switchgear parts
  • Batteries and capacitors: cell cases, tabs, lids, current-collector strip and capacitor shells
  • Thermal management: heat sinks, heat spreaders, cold plates and fin stock
  • Chemical and food: storage tanks, liners and process equipment
  • Lighting and signs: reflectors, lamp housings, nameplates and signage
  • Pharmaceutical packaging: vial seals and closures from 1060 strip

FAQ

1060 aluminium (aluminum) plate and sheet: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 1050 and 1060 aluminium?

1060 has 99.6 % minimum aluminium against 99.5 % for 1050, with tighter iron and manganese limits. It conducts slightly better and is slightly softer. 1060 is listed in ASTM B209; 1050 is mainly ordered to EN and JIS standards.

Is 1060 aluminium good for busbars?

Yes. Its conductivity of about 62 % IACS and good formability make it a common busbar and connector material. Some electrical specifications call for 1350 or 6101 instead, so check the standard your design follows.

Which temper of 1060 is used for battery parts?

Stamped tabs, lids and connectors often use H14, H18 or H24 for stiffness and clean blanking; deep-drawn cell cases and caps use O or H2x tempers for formability.

Can 1060 aluminium be welded?

Very easily, by TIG, MIG, ultrasonic, laser, resistance and friction stir welding. 1100 or 4043 filler is common for fusion welds; pure fillers preserve conductivity.

How strong is 1060-H14?

Typical tensile strength is about 97 MPa and yield about 90 MPa. It is a low-strength grade chosen for conductivity and formability, not load-bearing capacity.

Do you supply 1060 sheet and plate internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 1060 plate, sheet and strip worldwide with mill test certificates and export packing.
