5A03 Aluminium Plate & Sheet

5A03, formerly LF3, is a Chinese-standard aluminium-magnesium alloy with about 3.5 % magnesium and a deliberate silicon addition. It is stronger than 5A02, welds well and resists corrosion, and is used for welded structures, rail and transport panels and marine parts. Metline supplies 5A03 plate and sheet in O, H14, H24 and H112 to GB/T 3880, worldwide.

Overview

What is 5A03 aluminium?

5A03 contains 3.2 – 3.8 % magnesium, 0.30 – 0.6 % manganese and, unusually for a 5xxx alloy, 0.50 – 0.8 % silicon. It is non-heat-treatable and strengthened by cold work.

The higher magnesium gives clearly more strength than 5A02 while keeping good ductility and corrosion resistance. The silicon addition is intended to reduce hot cracking during welding, which is why 5A03 has long been used for welded structural parts, tanks and panels in Chinese-designed equipment.

There is no exact international equivalent. In magnesium content 5A03 sits between 5754 (2.6 – 3.6 %) and 5086 (3.5 – 4.5 %), but its silicon and manganese levels differ from both. Where a drawing permits substitution, 5754 or 5086 is usually reviewed as the alternative, depending on the strength needed.

Key properties of 5A03

  • Higher strength than 5A02 with good ductility
  • Good weldability; silicon addition helps resist weld cracking
  • Good corrosion resistance in atmospheric and marine conditions
  • Available as plate, sheet, bar and extruded profiles

Working with 5A03

Fabrication and applications

Welding

TIG and MIG weld well with 5xxx fillers such as ER5356 or ER5183. Weld zones soften towards O-temper strength.

Forming

O and H24 form well; allow larger bend radii in H14/H34.

Service temperature

With magnesium above 3 %, avoid long-term service above about 65 °C, where Al-Mg alloys can become sensitised.

  • Rail and transport: noise barrier panels, structural panels and vehicle bodies
  • Marine: deck fittings, superstructure parts and welded panels
  • Tanks and vessels: welded tanks and low-temperature equipment
  • General engineering: welded frames and covers to GB drawings

Export packing uses interleaving paper or film, moisture-barrier wrap with desiccant, edge protection and ISPM 15 pallets or cases.

FAQ

5A03 aluminium plate: frequently asked questions

What is the equivalent of 5A03?

There is no exact equivalent. Its magnesium (3.2 – 3.8 %) lies between 5754 and 5086, but its silicon (0.5 – 0.8 %) is higher than either. 5754 or 5086 is usually reviewed as an alternative.

Is 5A03 stronger than 5754?

In the annealed state it is broadly similar to slightly stronger, because of its higher magnesium. Compare the minimums in the specific standards for your temper and thickness.

Why does 5A03 contain silicon?

The silicon addition is intended to reduce hot cracking during welding of the alloy.

Can 5A03 be used above 65 °C?

Long-term service above about 65 °C is not recommended for Al-Mg alloys with more than 3 % magnesium because of sensitisation risk.

What does LF3 mean?

LF3 is the former Chinese designation for 5A03, meaning rust-proof aluminium number 3.
