Overview

What is 1070 aluminium?

1070 sits in the 1xxx series, where the last two digits give the purity above 99 %: 1070 means at least 99.70 % aluminium. Because almost nothing is dissolved in the metal to scatter electrons, it conducts electricity and heat better than any common alloy apart from the even purer 1080, 1085 and the electrical grade 1350.

The price of that purity is strength. Annealed 1070 has a tensile strength of only about 60 – 90 MPa, and even fully hard H18 sheet barely reaches 140 MPa. 1070 cannot be heat-treated; it is strengthened only by cold rolling, which is why it is sold in O, H12, H14, H16, H18 and as-rolled H112 tempers.

Buyers select 1070 for parts that carry current or heat, that must be drawn or spun into deep shapes, or that need a bright, uniform surface after polishing or anodising. Typical examples are busbars and connector plates, capacitor and battery components, heat-spreader plates, reflector blanks and chemical-plant linings. In Europe the grade is usually ordered as EN AW-1070A (Al99.7), which has very slightly different impurity limits but is used interchangeably for most purposes.

When 1070 is the right choice

  • You need conductivity close to electrical-grade aluminium in a sheet or plate form
  • The part will be deep drawn, spun, embossed or tightly bent
  • A bright anodised or polished finish with very few inclusions is important
  • Loads are low, or strength comes from the surrounding structure

Working with 1070

Forming, joining, machining and finishing

Forming

O-temper 1070 can be deep drawn, spun and bent flat on itself. Even H14 accepts bend radii of about 0–1 × thickness. Work hardening during forming is modest, so multi-stage draws rarely need intermediate annealing.

Welding

Welds easily by TIG and MIG with ER1100 or ER1188 filler when conductivity must be preserved, or ER4043 for better fluidity. Resistance and friction-stir welding also work well. The weld zone anneals, which matters little because the base metal is already soft.

Brazing and soldering

High purity and a solidus near 645 °C make 1070 easy to braze with Al-Si fillers. It is also a common partner in aluminium-to-copper transition joints for electrical work.

Machining

Soft and gummy: use sharp, polished, high-rake tools, high speeds and plenty of lubricant to avoid built-up edge. Harder tempers (H14, H18) machine more cleanly than O.

Anodising and polishing

Responds very well, giving a clear, bright film with good reflectance. For decorative mirror finishes, 1085 and 1090 give a slightly higher brightness, but 1070 is a cost-effective choice.

Corrosion

Excellent resistance to atmospheric, fresh-water and many chemical environments thanks to a stable oxide film. Avoid direct contact with copper or steel in wet conditions without insulation or plating.

Applications

Where 1070 sheet and plate is used

  • Power distribution: busbars, connector and terminal plates, earthing strips, switchgear and transformer parts
  • Batteries and capacitors: current collectors, tabs, cell connectors and capacitor cans
  • Thermal management: heat spreaders, cold plates, radiator and heat-exchanger components
  • Lighting and reflectors: lamp reflectors and housings, polished or anodised reflector sheet
  • Chemical and food equipment: tank linings and vessels for mild chemicals, foodstuffs and nitric acid service
  • Deep-drawn and spun parts: cookware blanks, lamp shades, nameplates and impact-extruded containers
  • Building and signage: embossed and decorative panels where strength demands are low

If the part also has to carry structural load, a better route is often a 1070 conductor bolted or clad to a stronger 6xxx alloy, or a switch to 6101 for busbars, which trades a few points of conductivity for much higher strength.

FAQ

1070 aluminium sheet: frequently asked questions

What is 1070 aluminium?

A commercially pure, non-heat-treatable grade with at least 99.70 % aluminium. It offers very high electrical and thermal conductivity, excellent formability and corrosion resistance, but low strength.

What is the difference between 1070 and 1070A?

1070 is the Aluminum Association grade used with ASTM standards; 1070A is the European EN AW grade. Both require 99.70 % aluminium minimum and differ only slightly in individual impurity limits, so they are interchangeable for most applications.

Is 1070 better than 1060 or 1050 for busbars?

It is marginally more conductive and is often specified for that reason. For busbars that also need strength, 6101-T6 or a hard-temper 1070 plate is used, depending on the current density and mechanical loads.

What is the electrical conductivity of 1070 aluminium?

About 61 – 62 % IACS in the annealed condition, falling slightly as the metal is cold-worked. If a guaranteed value is required, state it on the order so it can be tested and certified.

Can 1070 sheet be heat-treated to make it stronger?

No. 1070 has no strengthening phase to precipitate. Strength comes only from cold work (H tempers); heating anneals it back towards the soft O temper.

Do you supply 1070 plate cut to size for export?

Yes. Metline cuts 1070 sheet and plate to your sizes, packs it for sea or air freight and ships worldwide with an EN 10204 3.1 mill certificate.
