7A04 Aluminium Sheet & Plate

7A04, formerly designated LC4, is a high-strength Al-Zn-Mg-Cu alloy in the Chinese GB system, broadly comparable to 7075 but with a wider zinc range and a deliberate manganese addition. Metline supplies 7A04 sheet and plate in T6 and T651 tempers to GB/T 3880 for structural and aerospace-type parts, with mill certificates and export packing for customers worldwide.

Overview

What is 7A04 aluminium?

7A04 is one of the oldest high-strength aluminium alloys in the GB standard family. It gains strength from MgZn2 precipitates formed during artificial ageing, and in the T6 temper it reaches tensile strengths in the same region as 7075-T6.

Compared with 7075, 7A04 allows zinc from 5.0 % to 7.0 % and adds 0.20 – 0.6 % manganese alongside chromium. The manganese and chromium refine the grain structure, but the alloy is still sensitive to stress-corrosion cracking in the peak-aged T6 condition, and it has lower fracture toughness than modern high-purity grades such as 7475 or 7050. It is therefore used for parts that are loaded mainly in compression or in the rolling plane, or that are well protected by anodising and paint.

Buyers usually meet 7A04 on drawings that originate from Chinese aerospace or defence standards, or on general engineering drawings that need a strong, machinable 7xxx sheet. Where a drawing allows an international alternative, 7075-T6 or T651 is normally the direct substitute.

Working with 7A04

Machining, joining and corrosion protection

Machining

Machines well in T6 and T651, with short chips and a good finish. Use T651 for plate that will be pocketed heavily, so residual stress does not distort the part.

Joining

Spot and seam welding are workable, but fusion welding (gas, TIG, MIG) is not recommended: the alloy cracks and loses strength. Rivets and bolts are the normal joining method.

Corrosion

Stress-corrosion and exfoliation resistance in T6 are limited. Protect parts by anodising and priming, and avoid sustained tensile stress in the short-transverse direction.

Forming

Form in the O or freshly quenched condition and then age. T6 sheet tolerates only gentle bends.

Applications

Where 7A04 is used

  • Aircraft structural members: frames, ribs, stringers, bulkhead parts and skin panels on legacy designs
  • Highly loaded brackets, beams and truss members machined from plate
  • Defence equipment and fixtures that need high strength at low weight
  • Moulds, jigs and tooling plates where hardness and strength matter more than weldability
  • Sports and leisure equipment needing a strong, stiff aluminium sheet

FAQ

7A04 aluminium (aluminum) sheet: FAQs

What is the international equivalent of 7A04?

There is no exact equivalent. 7075 is the closest in strength and use; 7A04 differs by its wider zinc range and higher manganese content.

Can 7A04 sheet be welded?

Fusion welding is not recommended because of hot cracking and strength loss. Spot and seam resistance welding are possible; most structures are riveted or bolted.

Is 7A04 resistant to stress corrosion?

Not in the T6 temper. Protect it with anodising and paint and avoid sustained short-transverse tensile stress, or choose an over-aged grade such as 7075-T7351.

What does LC4 mean?

LC4 is the older Chinese designation for this alloy. GB/T 3190 renamed it 7A04 to fit the four-digit international system.

How much does a 7A04 plate weigh?

About 2.85 kg per m² per millimetre. A 1500 × 3000 × 10 mm plate weighs roughly 128 kg.
