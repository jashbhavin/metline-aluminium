Overview

What is 2014 aluminium plate?

2014 contains about 4.4 % copper plus silicon, manganese and magnesium. The higher silicon content distinguishes it from 2024: it allows 2014 to respond strongly to artificial ageing, so the T6 temper reaches higher yield strength than 2024-T3, though with lower fatigue resistance and toughness.

The alloy was developed for aircraft structures and heavy-duty forgings and remains common where high static strength and good machinability are needed in thick sections. Typical plate parts include aircraft fittings and frames, truck and trailer components under high load, hydraulic and pneumatic blocks, military equipment and tooling.

Two things limit where 2014 is used. First, copper lowers corrosion resistance, so unprotected 2014 should be anodised, painted or ordered as Alclad (clad with a thin layer of pure aluminium alloy) for exposed service. Second, fusion welding is difficult; 2014 parts are normally machined from solid and joined mechanically.

Where 2014 earns its place

  • Higher yield strength than 2024-T3 or 6061-T6 for statically loaded parts
  • Machines to a clean finish with short chips in T6/T651
  • Stretched T651 plate stays flat when heavily machined
  • Registered in AMS, ASTM and EN systems for aerospace and defence work

Tempers

2014 tempers explained

T651 plate

Solution heat-treated, stretched 1.5 – 3 % and artificially aged. The standard temper for machined plate parts; stretching removes quench stresses and keeps parts flat.

T6 sheet

Same heat treatment without stretching, used for thinner sheet. Highest strength, lowest formability.

T4 / T451

Naturally aged. Lower yield, higher elongation and better toughness; can be formed and then aged to T6.

O (annealed)

Softest condition for forming; must be fully heat-treated afterwards to regain strength.

Alclad 2014

Sheet clad on both sides with a thin aluminium layer that protects the core electrochemically. Choose it for exposed skins and panels.

T652 / forgings

2014 is also a major forging alloy; for complex shapes see our forging range rather than machining from plate.

Fabrication

Machining, joining and corrosion protection

Machining

Very good in T6/T651: short chips and a bright finish with carbide tooling. T4 is gummier. Leave stock on both faces when removing large volumes from one side.

Welding

Not recommended for fusion welding in structural parts because of hot cracking and loss of strength. Resistance spot welding and friction-stir welding are possible; mechanical fastening is the normal route.

Forming

Form in O or freshly quenched (W) condition, then age. Bending T6 needs large radii and risks cracking.

Corrosion

Lower resistance than 5xxx or 6xxx alloys and susceptible to intergranular and stress-corrosion attack in some tempers. Protect by anodising, chromate-free conversion coatings, primers or by specifying Alclad sheet.

Applications

Applications of 2014 plate

  • Aerospace: fittings, frames, bulkhead parts and landing-gear components machined from plate
  • Transport: heavily loaded truck frames, suspension and trailer components
  • Hydraulics: manifold and valve blocks where strength and machinability matter
  • Defence: structural parts for equipment and vehicles
  • Tooling and fixtures: jigs and dies needing more hardness than 6061

Compared with 2024, choose 2014-T651 when static strength and machinability matter most; choose 2024-T351 when fatigue and damage tolerance govern. Compared with 7075-T651, 2014 is weaker but holds strength better at moderately elevated temperatures.

FAQ

2014 aluminium (aluminum) plate: frequently asked questions

Is 2014 stronger than 2024?

In the T6/T651 temper 2014 has higher yield strength (about 414 MPa typical) than 2024-T3 or T351 (about 324 – 345 MPa). 2024 has better fatigue resistance and fracture toughness, which is why it dominates fatigue-critical airframe skins.

What is the difference between 2014 and 2014A?

2014A is the European grade with tighter limits on silicon and iron and a nickel limit; the mechanical properties are effectively the same. Many buyers accept either, but check your drawing.

Can 2014 aluminium be welded?

Fusion welding is not recommended for structural parts because of hot cracking and strength loss. Friction-stir and resistance welding are possible; most 2014 parts are machined and bolted or riveted.

Does 2014 need corrosion protection?

Yes, for most exposed service. Use anodising, conversion coating and paint, or specify Alclad 2014 sheet.

What does AMS 4029 cover?

2014-T6 sheet and 2014-T651 plate, solution and precipitation heat-treated, in thicknesses from 0.020 to 4.00 in (0.51 – 101.6 mm).

Can you cut 2014 plate to size?

Yes. We saw-cut and profile 2014 plate to your dimensions and supply it with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate.

Send the temper, size, quantity and specification. We will confirm availability, certification and a delivered price.
