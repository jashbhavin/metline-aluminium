5754 Aluminium Sheet & Plate

5754 (AlMg3) is Europe's most widely used Al-Mg sheet alloy: strong, very formable, weldable and highly resistant to seawater and industrial atmospheres. It is used for car body structures, tankers, boats, flooring and tread plate. Metline supplies 5754 sheet, plate and tread plate in O/H111, H22, H24, H32 and H34 to EN 485, worldwide.

Overview

What is 5754 aluminium?

5754 contains 2.6 – 3.6 % magnesium, with small, combined manganese and chromium additions (0.10 – 0.60 % in total). It is non-heat-treatable and gains strength from solid solution and cold work.

In Europe it fills the role that 5052 plays in North America: the general-purpose formable, weldable, corrosion-resistant sheet. It is slightly stronger than 5052 in the annealed condition and has become the main alloy for aluminium car body structures, where O-temper 5754 is pressed into inner panels, floor and crash structures and joined by spot welding, riveting and adhesive bonding.

With magnesium mostly below the sensitisation threshold, 5754 is regarded as suitable for elevated-temperature service in most designs, unlike 5083. It is also the common base alloy for tread and chequer plate in Europe, combining slip resistance with corrosion resistance on vehicle floors, steps and walkways.

Why choose 5754

  • High strength for an easily formed sheet: 190 – 240 MPa even in O/H111
  • Excellent resistance to seawater and industrial atmospheres
  • Welds well by MIG, TIG and resistance methods
  • Widely stocked in Europe; standard base for tread plate

Working with 5754

Fabrication notes

Forming

Excellent in O/H111. O-temper sheet can show stretcher-strain marks on visible surfaces; acceptable on hidden inner parts.

Welding

MIG and TIG with ER5356 or ER5183; for hot service ER5554. Resistance spot welding and bonding are standard in car bodies.

Corrosion

Excellent in seawater and industrial air. Use insulating washers with steel and stainless fasteners.

Machining

Fair; sharp tools and lubricant needed.

Anodising

Good for protective films; colour has a slight yellow tint.

Temperature

Generally suitable for elevated-temperature service; confirm magnesium content for critical hot applications.

Applications

Where 5754 is used

  • Automotive: body-in-white inner panels, floor and crash structures, heat shields
  • Transport: tanker shells, tipper bodies, trailer floors and sides
  • Marine: boat hulls, decks, fishing vessels and fittings
  • Flooring: tread plate for vans, steps, ramps and walkways
  • Industrial: chemical and food-processing equipment, welded structures

FAQ

5754 aluminium (aluminum) sheet and plate: FAQs

Is 5754 marine grade?

Yes. It has excellent seawater resistance and is widely used for boats and marine fittings. For large welded hulls built to class, 5083 in H116 or H321 is the usual choice.

What is the difference between 5754 and 5052?

5754 has more magnesium (2.6 – 3.6 % against 2.2 – 2.8 %) and some manganese, so it is slightly stronger. 5052 is the North American standard and 5754 the European one.

What are the properties of 5754-H111?

Under EN 485-2, tensile strength 190 – 240 MPa, minimum proof strength 80 MPa and elongation 12 – 18 % depending on thickness.

Is 5754 used in cars?

Yes. 5754-O is a leading alloy for aluminium car body structures, inner panels and crash members.

Can 5754 be used for tread plate?

Yes; it is the standard European tread plate alloy, combining slip resistance and corrosion resistance.
