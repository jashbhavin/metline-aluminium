4047 Aluminium Coil, Strip and Sheet

4047 is an aluminium-silicon alloy with 11 – 13 % silicon, close to the eutectic composition, so it melts over a narrow range of about 577 – 582 °C, roughly 50 – 70 °C below the 3xxx and 6xxx alloys it joins. In coil and strip form it is used as brazing filler foil, as the cladding layer on brazing sheet and as feedstock for stamped brazing preforms. Overview

What 4047 strip is used for

4047 is not a structural alloy. Its value is its melting behaviour: with about 12 % silicon it sits almost exactly at the aluminium-silicon eutectic (577 °C), so it turns from solid to liquid over just a few degrees and flows freely into capillary joints.

That makes 4047 the filler of choice for brazing aluminium heat exchangers, where thousands of fin-to-tube joints must form at once in a furnace. It is supplied in three ways: as thin foil or strip placed between parts, as a cladding layer roll-bonded onto a 3003 or similar core to make brazing sheet, and as strip stamped into washers, rings and other preforms.

Because 4047 is also the composition of ER4047 welding wire, the alloy is familiar to welders: it gives fluid weld pools and low shrinkage, which reduces hot cracking in castings and 6xxx joints. For brazing, though, the key property is the narrow melting range and the large margin below the melting point of the parts being joined.

Typical 4047 strip applications

  • Filler foil interleaved between fins, tubes and plates in vacuum or controlled-atmosphere brazing
  • Braze cladding on 3003, 3005 or 6951 core sheet for radiator, condenser and evaporator production
  • Stamped brazing washers, discs and rings for automated joints
  • Feedstock for drawing ER4047 welding wire and brazing rod

Comparison

4047 compared with other aluminium brazing alloys

AlloySi %Melting rangeMain use
4343 (BAlSi-2)6.8 – 8.2About 577 – 613 °CCladding on brazing sheet for controlled-atmosphere brazing; less erosion of thin fins
4045 (BAlSi-5)9.0 – 11.0About 577 – 591 °CCladding and filler; more fluid than 4343
4047 (BAlSi-4)11.0 – 13.0About 577 – 582 °CMost fluid; filler foil, preforms and cladding where large fillets are needed
4004 / 41049 – 10.5, with 1 – 2 % Mg (+Bi in 4104)About 559 – 591 °CVacuum brazing; magnesium acts as a getter

The trade-off is fluidity against erosion. 4047 flows most freely and builds the largest fillets, but the extra silicon also dissolves more of the parent metal, so very thin fins (below about 0.1 mm) are usually clad with 4343 instead. For vacuum brazing, a magnesium-bearing filler such as 4004 or 4104 is used because there is no flux to break the oxide film.

Process

Using 4047 strip in brazing

  1. Choose the processControlled-atmosphere brazing (CAB) with non-corrosive fluoride flux, or flame and induction brazing with flux. For vacuum furnaces use a magnesium-bearing filler instead.
  2. Check the parent metalParts should have a solidus well above 600 °C: 1xxx, 3xxx and low-magnesium 6xxx alloys (e.g. 6063, 6951). 6061 and 5xxx alloys are difficult.
  3. Place the fillerInterleave foil, place preforms or use clad sheet so filler sits next to every joint. A gap of about 0.05 – 0.25 mm suits capillary flow.
  4. BrazeHeat to about 590 – 605 °C, hold just long enough for the filler to flow, and cool. Over-temperature or long holds erode thin parts.

FAQ

4047 aluminium strip: frequently asked questions

What is the melting point of 4047 aluminium?

About 577 – 582 °C. It is close to the aluminium-silicon eutectic, so it melts over a very narrow range, which is why it flows so well as a brazing filler.

What is the difference between 4047 and 4045?

4047 has 11 – 13 % silicon and 4045 has 9 – 11 %. 4047 melts over a narrower range and is more fluid; 4045 melts up to about 591 °C and erodes thin parent metal slightly less.

Can 4047 strip be used for structural parts?

Not normally. It is a filler and cladding alloy. For structural aluminium sheet use 3xxx, 5xxx or 6xxx alloys.

Is 4047 suitable for vacuum brazing?

Not on its own. Vacuum brazing needs a magnesium-bearing filler such as 4004 or 4104 to break the oxide film. 4047 is used with flux, in CAB furnaces or for flame and induction brazing.

Can you supply 4047 as discs or preforms?

Yes. 4047 discs and stamped preforms are available, as are rod, wire and clad brazing sheet in the same alloy.
