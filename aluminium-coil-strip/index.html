Overview

Why buy aluminium as coil

Coil is the most efficient way to buy thin aluminium in volume. Instead of handling individual sheets, a press line, roll former or slitter feeds directly from a wound coil, cutting set-up time, scrap and handling damage. Strip is simply coil that has been slit to a narrower width so it matches the exact blank or profile you are producing.

The right coil depends on what happens next on your line. Roll forming roofing sheet needs a different temper from deep drawing a cooking vessel, and a transformer winding needs high conductivity rather than strength. Tell us the process and the finished part, and we will match the alloy, temper, thickness and coil dimensions to it.

Coil or sheet?

  • Coil suits continuous lines: roll forming, stamping, slitting and cut-to-length.
  • Sheet suits shops that cut, bend and fabricate individual pieces.
  • Strip is slit coil sized to your blank or profile width.

Tempers

Choosing the temper for your process

Temper decides how far the coil can be bent or drawn before it cracks, and how stiff the finished part will be.

O (annealed)

Fully soft. The choice for deep drawing, spinning and severe bending, such as cookware and lamp reflectors.

H14 / H24

Half hard. A balance of stiffness and formability for roll-formed profiles, ducting and general fabrication.

H16 / H26

Three-quarter hard. Stiffer panels and roofing that must hold shape across wide spans.

H18 / H19

Full hard. Maximum stiffness for flat products, closures and parts that see little forming.

H2x vs H1x

H2x tempers are partially annealed after rolling, giving better ductility than H1x at the same strength.

Tell us the bend

Share the bend radius or draw depth and we will recommend the temper that will not crack on your tooling.

FAQ

Coil and strip questions

What is the difference between aluminium coil and strip?

Coil is wound aluminium at mill width. Strip is coil that has been slit to a narrower width, usually to match the blank or profile size on your production line.

Which aluminium coil is best for roofing?

3003 and 3105 in H14 to H26 tempers are the most common choices. They roll form well, resist weathering and take paint coatings reliably.

Which coil is used for transformer windings?

Commercially pure grades such as 1050, 1060 and 1070 in the annealed condition, because they offer the highest electrical conductivity.

Can you slit coil to a custom width?

Yes. Send the width, thickness and quantity and we will confirm availability and lead time for slit strip.

How do I calculate the length of material in a coil?

Length is coil weight divided by the product of thickness, width and density (2.70 g/cm³ for most coil alloys). Our coil calculator does this for you.

Need aluminium coil or slit strip?

Send us the alloy, temper, thickness, width and coil weight. Our Mumbai team will reply with availability, lead time and a competitive quote.
