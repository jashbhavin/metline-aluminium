What Is the GOST Standard? A Buyer’s Guide to Russian Aluminium Alloys

GOST (ГОСТ, short for Gosudarstvennyy Standart, “state standard”) is the standards system inherited from the Soviet Union and still used across Russia and much of the CIS. For aluminium buyers it governs alloy names such as Д16, АМг6 and В95, temper letters like Т1 and Н2, and product standards for sheet, plate, foil, bar, tube and profiles. Reading those codes correctly, and knowing when a Western grade is an acceptable substitute, is the difference between a smooth order and a rejected shipment.

Definition

What does GOST mean?

GOST began as the national standards system of the USSR in the 1920s. After 1991 it became an interstate system maintained by the Euro-Asian Council for Standardization, Metrology and Certification (EASC), with each member state adopting the standards nationally. In Russia the national body is Rosstandart, which also issues purely national standards marked GOST R.

A GOST reference always has a number and a year, for example GOST 4784-2019 for wrought aluminium grades. The year matters: standards are revised, and a drawing that cites GOST 4784-97 may carry slightly different composition limits or grade lists from the current edition. When a buyer sends only “GOST aluminium” or a grade name without the standard, the first job is to recover the full reference.

For the aluminium trade, GOST plays the same role that ASTM and EN play elsewhere: it defines alloy chemistry, delivery conditions (tempers), dimensional tolerances, test methods and marking. The difference is that its alloy naming is unique, written in Cyrillic, and only partly aligned with the international four-digit system.

Geography

Which countries use or reference GOST?

GOST standards are adopted or referenced in Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan and Moldova, and they remain embedded in Soviet-era equipment wherever it operates. Ukraine began withdrawing GOST in favour of its own DSTU and European standards from 2015, and Georgia has moved largely to EN and ISO, but GOST references still appear in legacy drawings, maintenance manuals and spare-parts orders in both countries.

Outside the CIS you meet GOST mainly in three situations: maintenance of Russian-built aircraft, helicopters, ships and power plants; projects funded or engineered by Russian or CIS contractors; and reverse-engineering of older equipment where the only documentation is in Russian. In each case the buyer often needs either a true GOST grade or a carefully justified Western substitute.

Document types

GOST, GOST R, OST and TU are not the same thing

DocumentWhat it isWhat it means for a buyer
GOSTInterstate standard adopted by CIS member statesPublicly available; the common technical language for most commercial aluminium products
GOST RRussian national standardApplies in Russia; may cover topics not yet harmonised at interstate level
OSTIndustry (branch) standard; OST 1 is the aviation industry seriesOften required for aerospace alloys and legacy military or shipbuilding parts; harder to obtain
TU (ТУ)Technical conditions written by a producer or for a projectThe real requirements may sit in a document the buyer must supply; never quote a TU order without it
STO (СТО)Company standardInternal to one organisation; treat like a customer specification

A useful rule: the further down this table a reference sits, the less you can assume. A GOST sheet order can be quoted from public documents; an OST or TU order cannot be quoted responsibly until the buyer provides the text, or at least the composition, condition, properties and test requirements it contains.

Designations

How to read Russian aluminium alloy designations

Russian wrought alloys carry two kinds of name. The traditional letter-number names are abbreviations of Russian words: Д for duralumin (Д16), АМг for aluminium-magnesium (АМг6), АМц for aluminium-manganese, АД for wrought aluminium (АД31, АД33), АК for forging alloys (АК4-1), and В for alloys developed at the VIAM aviation institute (В95, В93). Many alloys also have a four-digit numerical name, in which the first digit 1 means aluminium-based and the second digit indicates the alloy system.

Second digitAlloy systemExamples (numeric = letter name)
1Al-Cu-Mg (duralumins)1160 = Д16, 1110 = Д1, 1163 (higher-purity Д16 variant)
3Al-Mg-Si1310 = АД31, 1330 = АД33
4Al-Mn and Al-Li1400 = АМц, 1420 (Al-Mg-Li)
5Al-Mg1530 = АМг3, 1550 = АМг5, 1560 = АМг6
9Al-Zn-Mg(-Cu)1950 = В95, 1901, 1920, 1980

The numeric scheme is not applied uniformly: some pure aluminium and foil grades (for example 1145 and 8111) follow international-style numbers instead. Always confirm the grade against the cited standard.

Suffixes add meaning. ч (chistyi) marks a higher-purity version with tighter iron and silicon limits, as in Д16ч; пч and оч mark increased and extra purity. These versions exist because lower impurity levels improve fracture toughness and fatigue life, so a substitute for Д16ч must match the purity, not just the main alloying elements.

One letter can also mean different things in different material systems. In Russian steel grades М can denote molybdenum, while in aluminium alloy names it often refers to copper (from med) and in temper codes it means annealed. Translate designations with the material family in mind, and keep the original Cyrillic on every purchase order, certificate and label alongside the transliteration.

Compliance

GOST standards and “GOST certification” are different things

Meeting a GOST material standard means the product conforms to that document’s chemistry, properties and tolerances, shown on a mill test certificate. “GOST certification” is a separate, regulatory matter. Since the creation of the Eurasian Economic Union, mandatory conformity assessment for many products is carried out under EAEU technical regulations (TR CU / TR EAEU) and shown by the EAC mark, while voluntary GOST R certificates remain in use for some goods in Russia.

Most semi-finished aluminium (sheet, plate, bar, tube) is not itself subject to an EAC technical regulation, but the equipment it goes into may be. Clarify early who needs what: the material supplier provides conformity to the material standard with test evidence; any declaration or certificate for the importing market is normally arranged by the importer through an accredited body.

Before you order

How to read GOST aluminium requirements on a drawing

  1. GradeRecord the grade exactly as written in Cyrillic, plus the transliteration (for example АМг6 / AMg6) and any purity suffix such as ч.
  2. Standard and yearIdentify the exact document: GOST 4784-2019, GOST 21631, OST 1 or a TU number. Ask for a copy of any OST or TU.
  3. Form and conditionSheet, plate, strip, foil, bar, tube or profile; temper code (М, Н2, Т, Т1) and cladding letter (А, Б, У).
  4. Sizes and tolerancesThickness, width, length, diameter, wall, profile drawing, accuracy class and machining allowance.
  5. Properties and testsComposition, tensile and yield strength, elongation, hardness, ultrasonic or corrosion tests demanded by the standard.
  6. Documents and substitutionMill test certificate content, traceability, third-party inspection, and whether ASTM, EN or other equivalents are allowed.

Common mistakes on GOST orders

  • Quoting a grade without the standard number and year
  • Treating В95 as Latin “B95” and searching the wrong grade
  • Substituting 2024 for Д16ч without matching the purity limits
  • Ignoring cladding letters on Д16 and В95 sheet
  • Confusing material conformity with EAC or GOST R certification

FAQ

GOST aluminium standards: frequently asked questions

What does GOST stand for?

GOST is the transliteration of ГОСТ, short for Gosudarstvennyy Standart, meaning “state standard”. Today most GOST documents are interstate standards adopted across CIS countries.

What is the GOST equivalent of 6061 or 2024 aluminum?

The nearest Russian grade to 6061 is АД33 (1330), and the nearest to 2024 is Д16 (1160). They are close in chemistry but not identical, so substitution needs engineering approval against the specific product standard.

What does the code Т1 mean on a GOST alloy?

Т1 means solution heat-treated and artificially aged, roughly equivalent to T6. Т means naturally aged (about T4), М means annealed (O) and Н2 means half-hard.

Is GOST compliance the same as GOST certification?

No. Compliance means the material meets a GOST standard, proven by test results on the mill certificate. Certification for market access in Russia and the EAEU is a regulatory process, now usually under EAEU technical regulations with the EAC mark.

Is a grade name alone enough to get a quotation?

Usually not. Send the grade in Cyrillic, the standard number and year, product form, condition, dimensions, quantity and the documents you need. For OST or TU references, include a copy of the document.

Which documents should come with GOST aluminium?

At minimum a mill test certificate with chemical composition and mechanical properties for the lot, plus dimensional inspection, heat or batch traceability and a packing list. Third-party inspection can be added where the contract requires it.

Need aluminium to a GOST or OST specification?

Send the Cyrillic grade, standard, condition, sizes and destination. We will confirm GOST supply or propose a documented international equivalent.
