2024 Aluminium Forgings

2024 aluminium forgings are open-die and closed-die parts, rolled rings and forged bar in the aluminium-copper-magnesium alloy best known for fatigue resistance and high strength at moderate temperatures. Forging refines the cast structure and aligns the grain flow with the part, so a 2024 forging is tougher and more reliable than the same shape machined from plate. Metline supplies 2024 forgings in T4, T6 and T852, made to drawing and certified, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is a 2024 aluminium forging?

2024 contains about 4.4 % copper and 1.5 % magnesium. After solution treatment, these elements precipitate as fine Al-Cu-Mg particles that give the alloy high strength even in the naturally aged T4 condition, together with excellent resistance to fatigue crack growth.

Forging starts with a cast or extruded billet of 2024 that is heated to roughly 420 – 460 °C and shaped under a press or hammer. The working breaks up the coarse cast structure, closes internal porosity and makes the grains flow along the contour of the part. A forged ring, disc or fitting therefore has better ductility, fatigue life and toughness in the critical directions than the same shape cut from thick plate, where the grain runs only one way.

2024 is not the most common forging alloy; 2014, 2618, 6061 and 7075 are forged in larger volumes. It is chosen when a design was qualified on 2024 (often aircraft parts carried over from plate or bar), when fatigue life is the governing requirement, or when parts must keep strength at temperatures up to about 120 – 150 °C, where 7xxx alloys begin to soften. Most 2024 forgings are open-die hand forgings, rolled rings, forged discs and forged bar, which are then machined to the final shape.

Before specifying a 2024 forging

  • Check whether your drawing really needs 2024: 2014 is the more usual forging alloy with similar strength
  • Corrosion resistance is low; plan anodising, chromate conversion or paint
  • Fusion welding is not recommended; design for mechanical fastening
  • State the governing specification and test directions (L, LT, ST) on the order

Tempers & heat treatment

Which temper for a 2024 forging?

T4

Solution heat-treated at about 495 °C, quenched and naturally aged at room temperature. High strength with the best toughness and fatigue crack resistance. Quench stresses remain, so large forgings can move during machining.

T6

Solution treated and artificially aged. Higher yield strength than T4 and better resistance to intergranular corrosion, with lower elongation.

T852

Solution treated, cold-compressed 1 – 5 % to relieve quench stress, then artificially aged. Chosen for hand forgings and rings that will be heavily machined, because parts stay dimensionally stable.

O

Annealed for further forming or as stock for later heat treatment by the customer.

Artificially aged tempers (T6, T852) keep their properties better at elevated temperature and resist stress-corrosion cracking better than T4. Naturally aged T4 is preferred where toughness and fatigue crack growth resistance govern. Heat treatment practice for aerospace forgings is controlled by AMS 2770 or the customer's process specification.

Supply range

2024 forging forms we supply

Open-die (hand) forgings

Blocks, rectangles and stepped shapes forged between flat dies, ideal for prototypes and low volumes where tooling for closed dies is not justified.

Rolled rings

Seamless rings formed by piercing a forged disc and rolling it on a ring mill; grain runs around the circumference.

Forged discs

Upset pancakes and discs for wheels, flanges and housings, with radial grain flow.

Forged bar

Round, square and rectangular bar forged from large ingots, used where extruded bar is not available in the size or quality needed.

Closed-die forgings

Near-net shapes for repeat production; justified at higher volumes because of die cost.

Machined forgings

Rough- or finish-machined to drawing, with inspection reports.

Working with 2024

Machining, joining and protecting 2024 forgings

Machining

Good in T4, T6 and T852 tempers; chips break well and surface finish is good. T852 forgings move least when large volumes are removed.

Joining

Fusion welding is not recommended because of hot cracking and strength loss. Use bolts, rivets or adhesive bonding; friction stir welding is possible with qualified procedures.

Corrosion protection

The high copper content lowers corrosion resistance. Sulphuric or chromic acid anodising, chromate conversion coatings and primers are standard on aircraft parts.

Temperature

Keeps useful strength to about 120 – 150 °C, better than 7075; for higher temperatures 2618 or 2219 are the usual forging alloys.

Applications

Where 2024 forgings are used

  • Aircraft structures: fittings, brackets, hinge parts and frames carried over from 2024 plate or bar designs
  • Rotating parts: rings, flanges and wheels where fatigue life governs
  • Defence: missile and munition components, launcher parts
  • Transport: high-performance vehicle and rail parts needing strength at moderate temperature
  • Fasteners and hardware: forged blanks for bolts and couplings

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) forgings: frequently asked questions

Why choose a 2024 forging instead of machining from 2024 plate?

Forging aligns the grain flow with the part shape and closes internal porosity, which improves fatigue life, ductility and toughness in the directions that matter. It also saves material for thick, round or ring-shaped parts.

What does T852 mean on a 2024 forging?

The forging was solution treated, stress-relieved by cold compression of about 1 – 5 %, then artificially aged. It keeps parts stable during machining, much as T851 does for stretched plate.

Is 2014 an acceptable substitute for 2024 forgings?

Often, because 2014 forges more easily and has similar strength, but the two alloys differ in chemistry, toughness and corrosion behaviour. A substitution needs approval from the design authority.

Can 2024 forgings be welded?

Conventional MIG and TIG welding is not recommended because the alloy is prone to hot cracking and loses strength. Use mechanical fastening or qualified friction stir welding.

What testing is supplied with 2024 forgings?

Chemical analysis, tensile tests in the specified directions, hardness and conductivity, dimensional inspection and, where ordered, ultrasonic testing, all reported on an EN 10204 3.1 certificate.

Do you ship 2024 forgings internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies forgings worldwide from Mumbai, with export packing and full documentation.
