6101 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A heat-treatable electrical extrusion alloy for bus conductors. Temper selection balances strength, bending behaviour and conductivity.

Specify electrical conductivity as well as strength. T61, T63 and T64 have alloy-specific processing objectives. Check joint design and operating temperature independently of a simple resistance calculation.
