7075 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A high-strength aluminium alloy with separate T6 and T73 family reference conditions. Rod and bar values must retain their form and test basis.

The source distinguishes the corrosion response of T6 and T73 family tempers. A substitution needs assessment of strength, environment and fabrication. No exact equivalence to 2024 or other aerospace grades is asserted.
