6063 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A heat-treatable extrusion alloy selected for architectural shapes, visible finishes and heat sinks. Surface requirements should be specified separately from mechanical properties.

State the exposed faces, finish and protective packaging in the enquiry. Weld regions may lose strength. T5 and T6 denote different processing histories; use the exact supplied condition.
