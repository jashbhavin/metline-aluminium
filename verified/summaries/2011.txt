2011 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A free-machining aluminium rod and bar alloy referenced in T3 and T8 conditions. Machining benefits do not establish suitability for welded assemblies.

The supplier cautions against most welding methods. Review corrosion exposure and the complete chemistry against end-use substance requirements; do not copy a historical product compliance statement as a guarantee for all 2011 stock.
