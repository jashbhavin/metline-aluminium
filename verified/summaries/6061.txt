6061 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A heat-treatable structural alloy used for profiles, rod, bar and tube. Its strength and forming behaviour depend on the supplied temper.

Consider softer tempers before severe forming. Welding can lower strength near the joint; use properties appropriate to the welded condition. Compare 6063 when exposed surface appearance drives selection.
