4045 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

An aluminium-silicon braze-cladding option listed by Gränges for controlled-atmosphere brazing of rolled heat-exchanger products.

Treat this as a clad-layer material, with core alloy, layer ratio and brazing cycle specified separately. The brochure lists iron variants and permits zinc modification; this entry retains only the shared silicon interval.
