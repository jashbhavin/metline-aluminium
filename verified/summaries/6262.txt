6262 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A machining-oriented aluminium extrusion alloy referenced for rod, bar and hexagonal stock. Material composition and the finished use require separate review.

The published chemistry includes lead and bismuth additions. Check the specified composition against end-use substance requirements rather than assuming compliance. Weld heat can reduce strength in the heat-treated condition.
