6060 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

An extrusion grade selected for visible architectural profiles and heat sinks, with decorative anodizing response and strength below 6063 in Hydro’s comparison.

Specify wall thickness and temper together. Hydro applies the lowest applicable mechanical requirement to a profile containing multiple thickness bands; T64 is identified as bending quality.
