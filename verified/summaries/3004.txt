3004 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A moderate-strength rolled alloy documented by Hulamin for roofing, cladding and signage, with mill, painted and embossed finishes.

Select the finish and temper for the fabrication route. Hulamin identifies suitability for roll forming and coating; its strength table does not state a thickness band, so confirm the ordered gauge and certificate.
