6082 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A structural extrusion alloy used for machining stock, tubing and profiles. It is a comparison candidate for 6061, not an automatic equivalent.

Check section thickness and quench capability before specifying T6 for thin walls. Welding reduces local strength. Softer O or T4 conditions may be appropriate for forming.
