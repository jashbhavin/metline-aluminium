4015 ALUMINIUM — ORIGINAL REFERENCE SUMMARY

A silicon-containing aluminium sheet alloy referenced in the H14 condition. Surface-treatment appearance can differ from other sheet grades.

The supplier cautions that anodized appearance is darker than some other grades. Agree visible-surface expectations with the finisher. No general interchangeability with 3103 or 1050 is asserted.
