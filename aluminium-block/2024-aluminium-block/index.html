2024 Aluminium Block

2024 aluminium block is cut from thick 2024-T351 plate or forged block and machined into parts that must carry high, repeated loads: aircraft fittings, actuator housings, racing components and high-strength fixtures. It combines about 470 MPa typical tensile strength with excellent fatigue resistance and good machinability. Metline supplies 2024 block cut to size in T351 and other tempers, certified to ASTM, AMS and EN, worldwide.

Overview

What is 2024 aluminium block?

"Block" is a buyer’s term rather than a separate product standard. In practice, 2024 block is either thick rolled plate (to ASTM B209 or AMS specifications) saw-cut into rectangular pieces, or an open-die forged block for sizes beyond the plate range. Either way, the material is 2024: the original high-strength aluminium-copper-magnesium aircraft alloy.

2024 is chosen when fatigue strength and damage tolerance matter more than peak static strength. It is weaker than 7075 but resists crack growth better, which is why it remains standard for tension-loaded aircraft structure and many machined fittings. In the T351 temper the plate is solution treated, stretched to relieve quench stress and naturally aged, so blocks stay stable when large amounts of material are removed.

Its weak points are corrosion resistance and weldability. The high copper content makes bare 2024 susceptible to pitting and intergranular attack, so finished parts are normally anodised, conversion coated and painted. Arc welding is not recommended.

Why choose 2024 block

  • Excellent fatigue strength and crack-growth resistance
  • High strength (typical 470 MPa tensile) with good toughness
  • Stretched T351 temper keeps machined parts flat and stable
  • Machines with short chips and a good surface finish

Tempers

Which temper for 2024 block?

T351

Solution treated, stretched 1.5 – 3 %, naturally aged. The standard temper for machining block: high toughness and fatigue strength, stable in machining.

T851

T351 material artificially aged. Higher yield strength and better resistance to intergranular and stress corrosion, with lower elongation and toughness. Useful for parts in warm service.

T352 / forged

Forged blocks are stress-relieved by compression (T352) rather than stretching, for sizes beyond the plate range.

O

Annealed. Bought only when the part will be formed or heat-treated after machining.

Quality

Tolerances and inspection for 2024 block

  • Saw-cut blocks are typically supplied with a small positive allowance (for example +1 to +3 mm) so all faces can be cleaned up; tighter milled tolerances on request
  • Thickness tolerance of the parent plate per ASTM B209 / ANSI H35.2 or EN 485-3
  • Ultrasonic inspection to AMS-STD-2154 classes for aerospace orders, on request
  • Electrical conductivity and hardness checks as temper verification
  • EN 10204 3.1 mill certificate with chemistry and mechanical test results, traceable to the parent plate lot
Working with 2024

Machining, joining and protecting 2024

Machining

Machines very well in T351: chips break cleanly and surfaces are bright. Use sharp carbide, high speeds and coolant. Balance material removal from both sides of thick blocks to limit distortion.

Joining

Not suited to arc welding. Assemble with rivets or bolts; friction stir welding is used in some aerospace structures.

Corrosion protection

Anodise (sulphuric or chromic), apply conversion coating and prime. Isolate from carbon fibre and stainless steel to avoid galvanic attack.

Temperature

Keeps strength better than 7075 up to about 120 – 150 °C; T851 is preferred for sustained warm service.

Applications

Where 2024 block is used

  • Aerospace: wing and fuselage fittings, brackets, hinges and actuator parts
  • Defence: missile and weapon components needing fatigue strength
  • Motorsport: uprights, suspension mounts and brake components
  • Tooling: high-strength fixtures and gauges
  • Industrial: hydraulic manifolds, gear housings and screw-machine parts

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) block: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 2024 block and 2024 plate?

Usually none in material terms: block is thick plate cut into rectangular pieces. For sizes beyond the plate range, forged block is used and is stress-relieved by compression (T352) instead of stretching.

How strong is 2024-T351 block?

Typical values are about 470 MPa tensile and 325 MPa yield. Specified minimums fall with thickness, so check the band that matches your block on the certificate.

Is 2024 better than 7075 for machined parts?

7075 is stronger and harder, but 2024 has better fatigue and crack-growth resistance. Choose by the load case: static strength favours 7075, cyclic tension favours 2024.

Does 2024 block need corrosion protection?

Yes. Bare 2024 is prone to pitting and intergranular corrosion. Anodise or conversion coat and paint finished parts.

How do I calculate the weight of a 2024 block?

Multiply thickness × width × length in mm by 2.78 and divide by 1,000,000 to get kg. A 100 × 200 × 300 mm block weighs about 16.7 kg.

Can you cut blocks to my drawing?

Yes. Blocks are saw-cut to size with a clean-up allowance, or milled to tighter tolerances on request, and shipped worldwide.
