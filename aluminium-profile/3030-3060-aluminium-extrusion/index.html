3030 & 3060 Aluminium Extrusion

3030 and 3060 are metric T-slot profiles on a 30 mm module, 30 × 30 mm and 30 × 60 mm, usually with an 8 mm slot for M6 fasteners. They sit between light 20 mm and heavy 40/45 mm systems, and suit workbenches, machine guards, small machine frames and enclosures. Metline supplies 3030, 3045 and 3060 profiles in 6063 alloy, cut, tapped and anodised, with fittings, worldwide.

Overview

What are 3030 and 3060 extrusions?

A 3030 is a 30 mm square T-slot profile with a slot on each face; a 3060 is a 30 × 60 mm profile with two slots on each long face. 3045 profiles, 30 × 45 mm, fill the gap between them. All share a 30 mm grid, so fittings and hole patterns repeat across the range.

The 30 mm system is a practical middle size. Compared with 2020, a 3030 is roughly three to four times stiffer and takes M6 bolts, so joints clamp harder and resist loosening. Compared with 4040, it is lighter and cheaper while still strong enough for most benches, guards and medium frames.

Slot geometry varies between systems. The most common 30 mm profiles have an 8 mm slot opening, but some catalogue systems use a 6 mm slot on a 30 mm profile. T-nuts and brackets made for one may not seat properly in the other, so confirm the slot width and profile drawing before ordering mixed parts.

Why choose the 30 mm system

  • Clear step up in stiffness from 2020 without the weight of 40 mm profiles
  • M6 hardware: strong, inexpensive and widely available
  • Good size for panels, doors and guarding
  • Compact enough for benchtop and mobile equipment

For a simply supported 1 m beam carrying 200 N at mid-span, a 3030 with I ≈ 2.8 cm⁴ deflects about 2 mm (δ = PL³ / 48EI, E = 69 GPa). A 3060 standing on edge reduces that to well under half a millimetre. Where a bench top or shelf must stay flat, use 3060 on edge for the long members and 3030 for posts.

30 mm profiles are commonly extruded in 6063-T5 or T6 for clean surfaces and good anodising. 6105-T5 or 6061-T6 can be quoted for more highly stressed frames.

Minimum values from ASTM B221 for sections up to about 12.7 mm wall; EN 755-2 gives similar figures for EN AW-6063 and EN AW-6005A. Stiffness (E ≈ 69 GPa) is the same for all four alloys, so alloy choice changes strength but not deflection.

Surface finish

Finishes for 3030 and 3060

Clear anodising

The standard finish: a hard, transparent oxide layer (typically 10 – 15 µm) that resists corrosion and scuffing and keeps a clean satin look.

Black and colour anodising

Black is popular for optical, laser and display equipment because it cuts reflections. Other dyed colours can be quoted for safety or brand colour coding.

Powder coating

A thicker, coloured coating for washdown areas or where a specific RAL colour is required. Slots may need masking so T-nuts still slide freely.

Cut ends and machined faces

Any cut, drilled or tapped surface made after anodising shows bare aluminium. It is cosmetic only, as aluminium forms its own thin oxide film, but specify machining before finishing if appearance matters.

Matching 3030 and 3060 fittings (brackets, gussets, joining plates and T-nuts) can be supplied in the same finish so the assembled frame looks uniform.

Applications

Where 3030 and 3060 are used

  • Workbenches and tool stands: light industrial and laboratory benches
  • Machine guards: guarding and fencing with mesh or polycarbonate panels
  • Small machine frames: CNC routers, laser cutters and inspection stations
  • Enclosures: acoustic and dust enclosures, control cabinets
  • Racks and carts: shelving, material carts and flow racks
  • Conveyors: frames and stands for light conveyors

FAQ

3030 and 3060 aluminium (aluminum) extrusion: FAQs

What slot size does 3030 extrusion have?

Most 3030 profiles have an 8 mm slot for M6 hardware; some systems use a 6 mm slot on a 30 mm profile. Check before mixing fittings.

How much does 3030 weigh?

Typically 0.8 – 1.1 kg per metre depending on design; 3060 weighs about 1.5 – 2.0 kg/m.

When should I use 3060 instead of 3030?

For beams and long members, especially bench tops and shelves. Standing on edge, 3060 is several times stiffer than 3030.

Which bolts fit 3030 profiles?

M6 bolts with matching T-nuts are standard for 8 mm slots.

Can 3030 profiles be supplied black?

Yes. Black anodising is available, as are clear anodising and powder coating.

Do you supply cut and tapped kits?

Yes. Profiles can be cut, end-tapped and drilled for connectors, then packed as labelled kits with fittings.

Building with 3030 or 3060?

Send your cut list or drawing, slot width, finish and fittings. We will quote the complete package.
