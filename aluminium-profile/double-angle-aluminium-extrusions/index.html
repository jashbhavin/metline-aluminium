Double Angle Aluminium Extrusions

A double angle extrusion is an aluminium corner profile with two slots set at 90°, so that two panels slide or click into it and form a square, reinforced edge. It is the standard framing member for flight cases, road cases, display cabinets and panel enclosures. Metline supplies double angle profiles in 6063 and 6061 with slots for common panel thicknesses, cut to length and finished as required, worldwide.

Overview

What a double angle extrusion does

Two panels meeting at a right angle are the weakest point of any box. A double angle extrusion wraps that corner in metal: each panel edge sits in its own channel, the outer legs lie flat on the panel faces and are riveted or screwed through, and the rounded or squared nose takes knocks that would otherwise split the board.

The profile is essentially two U channels joined back to back at 90°, sharing a common corner. Because the panels are captured on both faces, the joint resists racking and impact far better than a glued butt joint or a single external angle. Ball corners, brackets and catches are then fitted over the ends where three extrusions meet.

Double angles are made in several variants: with standard or long legs (more leg gives more rivet edge distance and stiffness), with smooth or ribbed outer faces, as a slide-on profile that accepts a range of panel thicknesses, or as a click-on profile with internal barbs that grip a grooved panel edge. Click-on types need the panel thickness held closely; slide-on types are more forgiving but rely on rivets to hold the panel.

The same idea appears in other industries under other names: corner trims for partition and cleanroom panels, glazing corner profiles, and aerospace "double angle" clips that join a frame to a skin. The engineering is the same: two perpendicular surfaces in one extrusion.

What to tell us

  • Panel thickness and type (plywood, ABS-laminated ply, honeycomb, polycarbonate, MDF)
  • Leg length and whether outer faces should be smooth or ribbed

Material

Alloys, tempers and properties

6063-T5 or T6 is the standard alloy. It extrudes the thin, intricate slot walls and barbs cleanly, takes a uniform anodised colour and is soft enough to rivet without cracking. 6061-T6 or 6082-T6 is used for heavy-duty crates and frames where the extrusion contributes real structural strength, or where a thicker section will be machined.

Density is about 2.70 g/cm³ for all of these alloys, so the extrusion adds very little weight to a case compared with steel edging, while giving it a rigid, impact-resistant frame.

Design & assembly

Designing and assembling with double angles

  1. Measure panelsMeasure actual panel thickness at several points and choose a slot that gives a snug sliding fit; for click-on profiles, groove the panel edge to the profile drawing.
  2. Cut and mitreCut extrusions to the panel length; where profiles meet at corners, cut square ends for ball corners or mitre for a seamless frame.
  3. Fit the panelsSlide or press the panels fully home. A bead of adhesive in the slot reduces rattle and improves water resistance.
  4. RivetRivet through the outer legs into the panel at an even pitch, keeping holes a safe distance from the leg edge. Aluminium rivets avoid galvanic corrosion; steel rivets give higher pull-out strength.
  5. Fit hardwareAdd ball corners, brackets, hinges, catches and handles, which are riveted over the extrusion ends.

Lid and base joints

For a case that opens, pair the double angle with a mating lid profile (tongue-and-groove or "hybrid" section) so the lid locates and seals on closing.

Long-leg profiles

Choose long legs on heavy or large cases: more leg gives more rivet edge distance and better resistance to panel pull-out.

Honeycomb panels

For lightweight cases, aluminium honeycomb or composite panels can be used in place of ply; check slot size and use inserts at rivet points.

Surface finishes

Finishes and appearance

  • Mill finish: the economical industrial look; tarnishes to a dull grey in use
  • Clear anodised: bright, hard-wearing, does not mark hands or equipment; the standard for touring cases
  • Black anodised: popular for stage and broadcast equipment where a low-visibility finish is wanted
  • Gold or bronze anodised: premium display, retail and presentation cases
  • Powder coated: any colour to match branding; thicker film, good on cases that will be cleaned often

Anodising can be specified to MIL-A-8625 (Type II) or to a film thickness class; for cases that see heavy handling, 15 – 20 µm gives a good balance of wear resistance and cost.

Applications

Where double angle extrusions are used

  • Flight and road cases: audio, lighting, instrument, camera and broadcast equipment cases, rack cases
  • Transport and storage crates: reusable crates for tools, medical and test equipment
  • Display and exhibition: trade-show cases, plinths and showcases
  • Enclosures and cabinets: electrical and electronic enclosures, kiosks and machine guards
  • Furniture and partitions: panel furniture, partition corners and cleanroom wall corners
  • Doors and windows: corner frames that join two glazed or panelled faces

FAQ

Double angle aluminium (aluminum) extrusions: FAQs

What is a double angle extrusion used for?

It joins two panels at a right angle and protects the edge. Its main use is framing flight cases and road cases, but it is also used for display cases, enclosures, crates and partition corners.

How do I choose the slot size?

Match the slot to the measured thickness of your panels, not the nominal size. For example, "3/8 in" plywood often measures 9 – 10 mm. Send a panel sample if you are unsure.

What is the difference between slide-on and click-on profiles?

Slide-on profiles accept a range of panel thicknesses and rely on rivets to hold the panel. Click-on profiles have internal barbs that lock into a groove routed in the panel edge, which needs a closely controlled panel thickness.

Which alloy is used for double angle extrusions?

6063 in T5 or T6 temper is standard because it extrudes the thin slot walls cleanly and anodises well. 6061-T6 or 6082-T6 is used for heavy-duty frames.

Can you supply black anodised double angle in cut lengths?

Yes. Profiles can be anodised black, clear, gold or bronze, or powder coated, and cut and drilled to your lengths.

Building cases or panel frames?

Send your panel thickness, finish and lengths. We will confirm the right double angle profile and a delivered price.
