15 Series T-Slot Aluminium Extrusion

15 series is the heavier inch T-slot system, built on a 1.5-inch module with a 0.320 in slot opening on 1.5-inch centres. Its base 1515 profile is about six times stiffer than a 1010, so it is the usual choice for machine bases, workstations, robot cells and guarding. Metline supplies 15 series profiles in 6105-T5, cut, machined and anodised, with matching fittings, worldwide.

Overview

What is 15 series aluminium extrusion?

15 series profiles are built on a 1.5-inch square. A 1515 is 1.5 × 1.5 in, a 1530 is 1.5 × 3 in, a 1545 is 1.5 × 4.5 in and an inch 3030 is 3 × 3 in. Slot centrelines sit 3/4 inch from each edge and 1.5 inches apart, so every profile and fitting in the series lines up on the same grid.

The larger section brings a step change in stiffness. Moment of inertia grows roughly with the fourth power of section size, so going from 1 inch to 1.5 inch multiplies stiffness by about six while weight rises by about two and a half. Larger slots also take 5/16-18 bolts, so joints carry more clamping force and resist slipping under vibration.

That is why 15 series is the default for frames that people lean on, that carry motors and moving axes, or that span more than about a metre. 10 series remains the lighter choice for desktop equipment and small enclosures; many plants use both, with 15 series for the base and posts and 10 series for light panel framing.

Where 15 series earns its place

  • Machine bases and gantries with moving loads
  • Workstations, assembly benches and production cells
  • Robot cell guarding and safety fencing
  • Conveyors and long spans where deflection must stay small

Light and grooved variants have lower values; ask for the data sheet for the exact profile.

As a worked illustration, a 1515 beam simply supported over 1 m with a 500 N central load deflects about 1.4 mm (δ = PL³ / 48EI). Over 1.5 m the same load gives about 4.8 mm, because deflection rises with the cube of span. Turning to a 1530 on edge cuts deflection by roughly a factor of five to six, at about double the weight.

15 series profiles are normally extruded in 6105-T5. Its strength margin over 6063 matters more here, where bolted joints and long beams carry higher stresses.

Minimum values from ASTM B221 for sections up to about 12.7 mm wall; EN 755-2 gives similar figures for EN AW-6063 and EN AW-6005A. Stiffness (E ≈ 69 GPa) is the same for all four alloys, so alloy choice changes strength but not deflection.

Surface finish

Finishes for 15 series profiles

Clear anodising

The standard finish: a hard, transparent oxide layer (typically 10 – 15 µm) that resists corrosion and scuffing and keeps a clean satin look.

Black and colour anodising

Black is popular for optical, laser and display equipment because it cuts reflections. Other dyed colours can be quoted for safety or brand colour coding.

Powder coating

A thicker, coloured coating for washdown areas or where a specific RAL colour is required. Slots may need masking so T-nuts still slide freely.

Cut ends and machined faces

Any cut, drilled or tapped surface made after anodising shows bare aluminium. It is cosmetic only, as aluminium forms its own thin oxide film, but specify machining before finishing if appearance matters.

Matching 15 series fittings (brackets, gussets, joining plates and T-nuts) can be supplied in the same finish so the assembled frame looks uniform.

Applications

Where 15 series is used

  • Machine frames: bases, columns and gantries for automation and test equipment
  • Workstations: adjustable assembly benches, lean manufacturing carts and flow racks
  • Safety guarding: robot cell fencing and machine enclosures with mesh or polycarbonate panels
  • Conveyors: side frames and support stands for longer runs
  • Clean rooms and laboratories: equipment frames that can be anodised and wiped down
  • Control cabinets: panel mounting and cable management using the slots

FAQ

15 series T-slot aluminium (aluminum) extrusion: FAQs

What is the difference between 10 series and 15 series?

10 series uses a 1-inch module with 0.256 in slots on 1-inch centres; 15 series uses a 1.5-inch module with 0.320 in slots on 1.5-inch centres. 15 series is heavier and roughly six times stiffer in the base profile.

What bolts are used with 15 series?

Typically 5/16-18 bolts with matching T-nuts; M8 nuts made for the 15 series slot are also available.

How much does 1515 weigh?

The standard 1515 weighs about 1.35 lb/ft (2.0 kg/m). Light versions weigh less.

Can 15 series carry a person or heavy machine?

Yes, when the frame is designed for it. Size members on deflection, brace frames against racking, use gussets at load-bearing joints and check fastener capacity.

Is 15 series anodised?

Clear anodising is standard; black anodising and powder coating are available.

Can you supply kits with fittings?

Yes. We cut and machine profiles to your list and pack them as labelled kits with brackets, T-nuts and feet.

Planning a 15 series frame?

Send your drawing, cut list and fittings. We will quote profiles, machining and hardware together.
