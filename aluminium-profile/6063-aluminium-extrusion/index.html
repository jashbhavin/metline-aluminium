Overview

What makes 6063 the standard extrusion alloy?

6063 is a lean aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloy. Its low alloy content keeps the billet soft at extrusion temperature, so it can be pushed fast through intricate hollow dies with thin walls and sharp detail. The result is a smooth, uniform surface that needs little preparation before finishing.

Strength comes from ageing after extrusion. Because 6063 is not very quench-sensitive, it can be solution treated simply by cooling with air or a light water mist as it leaves the press, then aged in an oven to T5 or T6. That short route is a big reason why 6063 profiles are economical: no separate furnace solution treatment is needed for most sections.

The trade-off is strength. 6063-T6 has roughly 80 % of the yield strength of 6061-T6 and about two-thirds that of 6082-T6, so it is the right alloy for window frames, curtain wall mullions with modest spans, trims, furniture and heat sinks, but not usually for heavy structural members. Its close relative 6060 is even easier to extrude and is common in Europe for the same applications.

Tempers

6063-T5, T6 and T66: which one?

6063-T5

Cooled at the press and artificially aged. The economical standard for window and door frames, trims and furniture where moderate strength is enough.

6063-T6

Fully solution treated (usually by intensive press quench) and aged. About 20 – 25 % stronger than T5; used for curtain wall, handrails, ladders and load-carrying frames.

6063-T66

A higher-strength T6 variant from controlled chemistry and processing. Used where 6063 finish is wanted with strength closer to 6005A.

6063-T4

Naturally aged and soft. For bending and forming, followed by ageing if strength is needed.

6063-T52

Lower-strength temper with stress relief, used for some architectural and electrical sections.

6063-O

Annealed for severe forming such as tight bends and flaring.

Order against the exact temper: T5 and T6 profiles look identical, and only a hardness check or tensile test separates them. Webster or Barcol hardness testing on site is a quick way to confirm that a delivered lot has been aged properly.

Applications

Where 6063 extrusions are used

  • Windows and doors: sliding, casement and thermal-break frames, sashes and beads
  • Curtain wall and facades: mullions, transoms, pressure plates and cover caps
  • Interiors and furniture: partitions, shelving, cabinet handles, wardrobe and kitchen profiles
  • Lighting: LED strip channels, linear luminaire housings and diffusers
  • Electronics: heat sinks and enclosures, where 6063 conducts heat about 20 % better than 6061
  • Transport interiors: trims, luggage racks and panel joiners
  • Handrails, balustrades and ladders: in T6 for extra strength
  • Irrigation and general tube: drawn or extruded 6063 tube for frames and fluids

FAQ

6063 aluminium (aluminum) extrusion: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6063-T5 and 6063-T6?

T5 is cooled at the press and aged; T6 is fully solution treated, quenched and aged. Under EN 755-2, T6 profiles (walls up to 10 mm) have a minimum of 215 MPa tensile and 170 MPa yield, against 175 MPa and 130 MPa for T5 in thin walls. T5 is fine for windows and trims; T6 suits load-carrying frames.

Is 6063 strong enough for structural use?

For light structures such as window frames, curtain wall with moderate spans, handrails and furniture, yes. For beams, platforms and heavily loaded frames, 6061 or 6082 is the better choice.

Why is 6063 used for heat sinks?

It extrudes into thin, closely spaced fins and conducts heat at about 200 – 210 W/m·K, better than 6061. It also anodises well for improved emissivity.

Can 6063 be welded and bent?

Yes. It welds well with ER4043 or ER5356 filler, though the weld zone loses strength. For bending, T4 or O temper gives the tightest radii; T5 and T6 can be bent to moderate radii.

What is the Indian equivalent of 6063?

IS 63400 (formerly HE9) under IS 733 is the commonly used close equivalent. Confirm the governing standard on your drawing.

Do you supply 6063 profiles finished and cut to length?

Yes. Metline supplies 6063 profiles anodised, powder coated or mill finish, cut to length and protected with film for transport, worldwide.
