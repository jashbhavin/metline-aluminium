Overview

What makes a good circle

A circle is judged by how it behaves in the press. It needs a fine, even grain so the drawn wall does not show orange peel, consistent mechanical properties around the whole blank so the part does not form unevenly ("earing"), and a clean surface so the finished product looks good after polishing or coating.

Hot-rolled circles generally offer better deep-drawing performance, while cold-rolled circles give a brighter surface and tighter thickness control.

FAQ

Circle questions

Which aluminium circle is best for cookware?

1100 and 3003 in O or H12 temper are the usual choices. 3003 gives a stiffer pan; 1100 draws more easily.

How do I specify circles?

Give the alloy, temper, thickness, diameter, quantity and whether you need hot- or cold-rolled material.

Ready to discuss your requirement?

Send us the alloy, form, size and quantity. Our Mumbai team will reply with availability, lead time and a competitive quote.
