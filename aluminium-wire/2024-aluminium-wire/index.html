2024 Aluminium Wire

2024 aluminium wire is a high-strength copper alloy wire used mainly to make solid rivets, cold-headed bolts and screws, pins and small machined parts for aircraft and engineering. In the T4 temper it reaches a typical shear strength of about 283 MPa, far above general-purpose aluminium wire. Metline supplies 2024 wire and rod in coils and straight lengths, drawn to size and certified, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is 2024 aluminium wire?

2024 is a heat-treatable aluminium-copper-magnesium alloy, one of the original high-strength aircraft alloys. As wire, it is drawn from extruded or rolled rod and supplied for cold heading, mainly into rivets and fasteners.

In aerospace, solid rivets made from 2024 are known as "DD" rivets. They are driven in the freshly solution-treated condition because 2024 age-hardens quickly at room temperature: once quenched, the rivets must be driven within a short time or kept refrigerated to delay ageing, which is why they are often called "ice-box" rivets. After driving they age naturally at room temperature and reach their full shear strength.

Outside aircraft, 2024 wire is cold-headed into high-strength bolts, screws and pins, and machined into small turned parts on automatic lathes. It is chosen when 5xxx or 6xxx wire is not strong enough and steel would be too heavy or cause galvanic problems in an aluminium assembly. Its main limitation is corrosion resistance: the copper content means parts are usually anodised, chromate-converted or primed.

Why buyers choose 2024 wire

  • High shear and tensile strength for structural rivets and fasteners
  • Good cold-heading behaviour in the annealed or freshly quenched state
  • Good fatigue performance, important in riveted aircraft joints
  • Recognised in ASTM, AMS and EN specifications for traceable supply

Tempers

2024 wire tempers

O (annealed)

Softest condition for severe cold heading. The fastener maker solution-treats and ages the finished parts.

H13

Lightly drawn condition used as rivet and cold-heading stock, giving better straightness and feeding than O.

T4 / T42

Solution treated and naturally aged. T4 is heat-treated by the mill; T42 is heat-treated from O or F by the user.

Driven rivets

Rivets driven freshly quenched and then naturally aged reach full T4-level shear strength in service.

Quality & inspection

Inspection and certification

Rivet and cold-heading wire must be free of seams, laps and surface defects that would split during heading. Typical testing includes diameter and ovality checks along the coil, tensile tests, and for rivet wire, a heading or upset test and a driven-rivet shear test on samples after heat treatment.

Typical checks

Dimensional
Diameter and ovality at both coil ends and along the length
Mechanical
Tensile test; shear test after heat treatment for rivet wire
Formability
Upset (heading) test to the specification ratio
Surface
Visual and, for aerospace orders, eddy-current inspection
Certificate
EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate with lot traceability
Applications

Applications of 2024 wire

  • Aircraft rivets: DD solid rivets for highly loaded structural joints
  • Cold-headed fasteners: bolts, screws, studs and pins for aluminium assemblies
  • Turned parts: small high-strength components made on automatic lathes
  • Transport and defence: fasteners where weight and galvanic compatibility matter
  • Sport and precision equipment: pins, axles and spoke nipples where strength is needed

  2. QuoteWe confirm availability, tolerances, certification and delivery terms (EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP) for your destination.
  3. ProcessMaterial is cut to length, de-burred and, if you ask, bent, machined or finished before packing.
  4. DocumentEach shipment carries a mill test certificate to EN 10204 3.1 with chemistry and tensile results, plus the packing list. Third-party inspection can be arranged.
  5. ShipBundled with end caps and interleaving or packed in seaworthy wooden cases, then shipped by sea or air.

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) wire: frequently asked questions

What is 2024 aluminium wire used for?

Mainly for high-strength solid rivets (DD rivets) and cold-headed fasteners such as bolts, screws and pins, plus small turned parts.

Why are 2024 rivets called ice-box rivets?

After solution heat treatment, 2024 hardens at room temperature within a short time. Keeping rivets refrigerated delays this ageing so they stay soft enough to drive.

What is the shear strength of 2024-T4 rivet wire?

Typical shear strength is about 283 MPa (41 ksi), the highest of the common aluminium rivet alloys.

Is 2024 wire corrosion resistant?

Only moderately. The copper content lowers resistance, so 2024 fasteners are normally anodised, conversion coated or wet-installed with sealant.

In what form is 2024 wire supplied?

In coils, spools or straight lengths, in O, H13 or T4 temper, drawn to diameter and certified to ASTM, AMS or EN specifications.
