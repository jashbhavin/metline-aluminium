8111 Aluminium Alloy: Foil and Strip for GOST 618 and GOST 745 Orders

8111 is an aluminium-iron-silicon alloy, close to 8011 in composition but with wider silicon limits, used mainly for packaging, household and technical foil and thin strip. Buyers in Russia and the CIS often order it against ГОСТ 745 (packaging foil) or ГОСТ 618 (technical foil), while ASTM B479 lists it for flexible barrier foil elsewhere. This guide explains its chemistry, tempers, products, how it compares with 8011 and how to order it.

Overview

What 8111 aluminium alloy is

8111 belongs to the 8xxx family of "other element" alloys. Like 8011 and 8079, it relies on iron and silicon rather than magnesium or copper. These elements form fine intermetallic particles that strengthen thin foil, refine grain and reduce the risk of pinholes and tearing during rolling, without any heat treatment.

The practical result is a foil alloy that can be rolled thin, annealed soft for wrapping and lamination, or left hard for lids, caps and fin-type uses. Its silicon range is wider than that of 8011, which gives mills flexibility in raw material while keeping very similar properties in the finished foil.

In Russian and CIS documentation, 8111 appears in contracts for packaging and technical foil, where buyers specify the ГОСТ product standard and the alloy together. Outside that region the same alloy is named in ASTM B479 for annealed flexible-barrier foil, alongside 1100, 1145, 1235, 8011 and 8079.

Key points

  • Non-heat-treatable; strength comes from alloying and cold rolling
  • Rolls to thin gauges with good pinhole performance
  • Food-contact suitable when produced to the relevant packaging standard
  • Interchangeable with 8011 for many foil uses, subject to the customer's specification

Tempers

Tempers and mechanical behaviour

TemperConditionBehaviourTypical uses
OFully annealed, often dry (oil-free)Softest and most ductile; folds, wraps and laminates easilyHousehold foil, food wrapping, laminates, insulation facings
H22 / H24Strain-hardened and partly annealedModerate strength with useful elongationContainers, lids, semi-rigid packaging
H18 / H19Fully hard as rolledHighest strength, little elongationLidding, technical foil, fin and strip uses

Tensile strength and elongation for foil are specified by gauge and temper in the product standard (ГОСТ 745, ГОСТ 618, ASTM B479 or EN 546-2). Elongation rises with thickness, so a 9 µm household foil and a 100 µm container foil in the same temper have quite different values. Rather than rely on a single generic figure, check the limits for your gauge in the contract standard and the results on the mill certificate.

Supply range

8111 products and typical sizes

Household and food foil

Soft O-temper foil around 9 – 25 µm for wrapping, baking and food service rolls.

Container and lid foil

Thicker semi-rigid foil, typically about 30 – 200 µm in H22/H24, for trays, containers and lids.

Laminating foil

Clean, wettable soft foil for paper and film laminates in flexible packaging and insulation.

Technical foil

Foil for heat, moisture and sound insulation facings and industrial wrapping, ordered to ГОСТ 618 where required.

Thin strip

Slit strip for caps, closures and stamped parts, in hard or partly annealed tempers.

Jumbo rolls

Large rolls for converters who slit and rewind into smaller consumer or industrial rolls.

Comparison

8111 or 8011: which should you specify?

Chemically the two alloys overlap heavily: 8111 allows a wider silicon range and a slightly lower minimum iron, while 8011 allows a little more manganese. In finished foil of the same gauge and temper, their properties are very similar, and many converters accept either.

A common mistake is to treat the alloy name as the specification. What matters is that gauge, temper, mechanical limits, surface and food-contact requirements are written into the order. If your drawing or tender names 8111 specifically, supply must be certified as 8111; if it allows equivalents, 8011 may widen the choice.

Ordering

How to order 8111 aluminium foil and strip

  1. SpecifyAlloy 8111, temper, thickness and width, roll inner and outer diameter or weight, and the product standard (ГОСТ 745, ГОСТ 618 or ASTM B479).
  2. Add requirementsSurface (one side matt, both bright), wettability, pinhole limits for thin gauges, food-contact declarations.
  3. QuoteWe confirm availability, certification and delivery terms (EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP).
  4. DocumentAn EN 10204 3.1 mill certificate with chemistry and mechanical results ships with every lot; certificates can reference the ГОСТ standard named in the order.
  5. ShipRolls packed in moisture-proof wrap with desiccant, on pallets or in wooden cases, eye-to-sky or eye-to-wall.

FAQ

8111 aluminium (aluminum) alloy: frequently asked questions

Is 8111 the same as 8011?

No, but they are close. Both are Al-Fe-Si foil alloys; 8111 allows a wider silicon range (0.30 – 1.10 %) and slightly lower iron. In the same gauge and temper they behave very similarly, and many buyers accept either where the specification allows.

Which ГОСТ standard covers 8111 foil?

Packaging foil is ordered to ГОСТ 745 and technical (insulation and industrial) foil to ГОСТ 618. The contract should state the edition and confirm the alloy is listed for the product.

Is 8111 foil safe for food contact?

Yes, when produced and certified to a packaging-foil standard such as ГОСТ 745 or ASTM B479 and the food-contact rules of the destination market.

What tempers is 8111 foil supplied in?

Soft O for wrapping and laminating, H22/H24 for containers and semi-rigid packaging, and H18/H19 for hard lidding and technical uses.

Can you certify 8111 to ГОСТ requirements?

Each lot ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill certificate showing chemistry and mechanical results; it can reference the ГОСТ product standard named in your order, and third-party inspection can be arranged.
