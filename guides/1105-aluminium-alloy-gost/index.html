GOST alloy guide

1105 Aluminium Alloy (GOST System)

1105 is a Russian wrought aluminium alloy of the aluminium-copper-magnesium family, listed in GOST 4784 with unusually wide composition limits: 2.0–5.0 % copper, 0.4–2.0 % magnesium and up to 3 % silicon. It is supplied as ingot, sheet and strip for general engineering parts of moderate strength. This guide explains what the grade really is, how to read its temper codes and how to choose a substitute outside the GOST system.

Overview

What is 1105 aluminium alloy?

Russian wrought aluminium grades carry a four-digit number in which the first digit "1" means aluminium and the second digit identifies the alloy system. Grades beginning 11 belong to the Al-Cu-Mg group that also contains Д1 (1110) and Д16 (1160). 1105 sits in that group, but unlike the classic duralumins it has no letter name and a composition window that is several times wider.

That wide window is the most important fact about the alloy. Copper may lie anywhere between 2.0 and 5.0 %, magnesium between 0.4 and 2.0 % and manganese between 0.3 and 1.0 %, while silicon is allowed up to 3.0 %, iron up to 1.5 % and zinc up to 1.0 %. Two lots that both comply with the standard can therefore behave quite differently. A lot near the top of the copper and magnesium ranges responds to heat treatment like a light duralumin; a lot near the bottom behaves more like a lean, work-hardened alloy.

The permissive limits make 1105 a practical outlet for mixed or recycled charge, and the grade also appears in GOST 1131, the standard for wrought-alloy pigs that are remelted to make rolling ingots or used to adjust the composition of other melts. For the buyer, this means the grade name alone does not define the material: the actual analysis on the mill certificate does.

Key points for buyers

  • Al-Cu-Mg alloy with wide limits; always read the certified analysis, not just the grade
  • Moderate strength; not a substitute for Д16 / 2024 in critical structures
  • Copper content means lower corrosion resistance than 1xxx, 3xxx or 5xxx grades; cladding or coating helps
  • No direct ASTM or EN equivalent; substitutes are chosen by property, not by name

GOST lists a nominal density of 2.80 g/cm³ for 1105, higher than pure aluminium (2.70) because of the copper. Use 2.80 for weight estimates: a 1500 × 3000 × 2 mm sheet weighs about 25.2 kg.

Temper designations

How to read 1105 sheet codes such as 1105АМ

Russian sheet designations combine the alloy number, a cladding letter and a temper letter. This is a frequent source of confusion: in "1105АМ" the letter А is not part of the temper but means normal cladding, and М means annealed. The table summarises the codes used in GOST 21631 and related sheet standards.

CodeMeaningNotes
АNormal claddingThin layer of purer aluminium on both faces for corrosion protection
УThickened claddingHeavier clad layer for more aggressive environments
БTechnological claddingThin layer applied mainly to aid rolling, not for service protection
(no letter)UncladBare alloy on both faces
МAnnealed (soft)Maximum formability for bending, drawing and stamping
Н2Semi-hard (half work-hardened)Balance of stiffness and formability
НWork-hardened (hard)Highest strength from cold rolling alone
ТQuenched and naturally agedHeat-treated condition, where the lot chemistry supports it
Т1Quenched and artificially agedHigher strength, lower elongation

So 1105АМ is normally clad, annealed sheet, 1105АН2 is clad semi-hard sheet, and 1105Н would be bare hard sheet. The exact list of codes a mill can supply for 1105 depends on its product standard or technical conditions (ТУ), so confirm the code on the quotation.

Properties

Mechanical and physical properties

Because the composition range is so wide, there is no single "typical" property set for 1105, and published tables that quote one should be treated with caution. Guaranteed values come from the product standard or ТУ for the specific form and temper, and the actual values come from the lot certificate. Some general rules still help with design:

  • Strength class: moderate, between the soft 3xxx alloys and the duralumins. It is not a structural substitute for Д16Т or 2024-T3.
  • Temper effect: annealed (М) sheet has the highest elongation; Н2 and Н tempers trade ductility for strength.
  • Electrical conductivity: low compared with pure aluminium. With several per cent of copper and silicon in solution, 1105 conducts far less than the 55–62 % IACS of 1xxx grades, so it is not an electrical alloy.
  • Thermal properties: expansion coefficient around 23 × 10⁻⁶ /K and elastic modulus around 70 GPa, as for most wrought aluminium alloys.
  • Corrosion: copper-bearing alloys pit more readily than Al-Mn or Al-Mg alloys, which is why clad (А) sheet is common.

Working with 1105

Forming, machining, welding and finishing

Forming

Order annealed (М) sheet for deep bends and stamping. Inside bend radii of about 1 × thickness are usually achievable in М temper; hard tempers need larger radii and bending across the rolling direction.

Machining

Copper improves chip breaking compared with pure aluminium, so 1105 machines cleanly with sharp carbide or HSS tools and coolant. Annealed material can be gummy.

Welding

Copper-bearing alloys are prone to hot cracking in fusion welds. Use 4043-type filler, control heat input and clean off oxide and oil. Qualify the procedure with a crack test before production. Resistance spot welding of thin sheet is common.

Heat treatment

Annealing relieves work hardening and restores ductility between forming stages. Whether quenching and ageing give a useful strength gain depends on the copper and magnesium level of the lot.

Corrosion protection

Use clad sheet, chromate-free conversion coating, primer or paint for outdoor or humid service. Avoid contact with copper and carbon steel without insulation.

Anodising

Possible, but copper gives a duller, less uniform film than 5xxx or 6xxx alloys. Not a first choice for decorative anodised parts.

Applications

Where 1105 is used

  • Building products: roofing and profiled sheet, cladding panels, trims and interior sections where moderate strength is enough
  • Transport and machinery: body panels, covers, brackets, enclosures and lightly loaded equipment parts
  • General engineering: housings, guards, stamped parts and fabricated frames
  • Semi-finished feedstock: ingot and slab for further rolling, drawing and pressing
  • Charge make-up: pigs to GOST 1131 used to correct copper and magnesium in other melts

Avoid 1105 for primary aircraft structure, pressure equipment, marine immersion and electrical conductors. Those duties have dedicated alloys with tighter composition control.

Equivalents

Equivalents and how to choose a substitute

1105 has no one-for-one ASTM, EN, ISO or JIS equivalent. Choose a replacement by matching the function of the part rather than the chemistry:

FAQ

1105 aluminium alloy: frequently asked questions

What type of alloy is 1105?

An aluminium-copper-magnesium wrought alloy in the Russian GOST 4784 system, with 2.0–5.0 % Cu, 0.4–2.0 % Mg and 0.3–1.0 % Mn and wide limits on silicon, iron and zinc. It is not a pure aluminium grade despite the leading "1", which in GOST numbering simply means aluminium-based.

What does 1105АМ mean?

Alloy 1105, normally clad (А), in the annealed temper (М). The cladding letter comes before the temper letter in Russian sheet codes.

Is 1105 the same as AA 1105 or AA 1100?

No. The Russian digital number is unrelated to Aluminum Association numbers. AA 1100 is 99 % pure aluminium, while GOST 1105 contains several per cent of copper and magnesium.

Can 1105 be welded?

Yes, but copper raises the risk of hot cracking. Use a silicon-bearing filler such as 4043, control heat input and qualify the procedure. For welded structures, a 5xxx or 6xxx alloy is usually the better choice.

What is a good international substitute for 1105?

It depends on the duty: 3003 or 5052 for formed sheet, 5083 or 6061 for stronger welded parts, and 2017A or 2024 where duralumin-type strength is needed. Compare guaranteed properties before approving the change.

Can Metline supply 1105 with certificates?

Yes. We supply GOST grades or approved substitutes with mill test certificates showing the actual chemical analysis and mechanical results, packed for export and shipped worldwide.

Sourcing 1105 or a replacement?

Send the GOST designation, form, size, quantity and destination. We will reply with availability, certification and a proposed substitute if it helps.
