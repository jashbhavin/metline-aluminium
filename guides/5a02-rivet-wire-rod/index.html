Rivet wire guide

5A02 Aluminium Wire for Solid Rivets

5A02 is a Chinese-standard aluminium-magnesium alloy with about 2.5 % magnesium, close in character to 5052. Drawn into wire, usually in the H14 temper, it is cold-headed into solid rivets that are driven as received, need no heat treatment and resist corrosion well. This guide covers its chemistry, strength, equivalents, how it behaves on a heading machine and where 5A02 rivets make sense.

Overview

What is 5A02 rivet wire?

5A02 is listed in the Chinese wrought-alloy standard GB/T 3190, where it replaced the older designation LF2 (Russian AMg2 is a close relative). It is a non-heat-treatable Al-Mg alloy: strength comes from magnesium in solid solution and from cold drawing, not from ageing.

For rivet makers that matters in two ways. First, a 5A02 rivet is installed in the condition it is delivered in. There is no solution treatment before driving and no "icebox" storage, unlike 2017 or 2024 rivets, which must be driven soon after quenching or kept refrigerated. Second, the alloy sits close to structural aluminium in the galvanic series and contains almost no copper, so a 5A02 rivet does not become the weak point for corrosion in a marine or humid structure.

The trade-off is strength. 5A02 rivets carry roughly half the shear load of a 2A10 or 2024 rivet of the same diameter, so they are chosen for joints where corrosion resistance, easy driving and low cost matter more than load: lightly loaded aircraft fairings and interiors, boat fittings, electrical enclosures, vehicle trim and sheet-metal assemblies in Al-Mg alloys or magnesium sheet.

Properties

Shear strength and riveting performance

Rivet wire is judged mainly on two tests rather than on tensile strength alone. A double-shear test on a length of wire shows the load a finished rivet shank can carry, and an upsetting (riveting) test checks that a sample can be headed without cracking.

Property5A02-H14 wireComment
Shear strength≥ 115 MPa (requirement commonly quoted for 1.6 – 10 mm)Tested in double shear, e.g. to the method of ASTM B565
Upsetting testSample upset to a head height of about 1.5 × diameter without cracksConfirms cold-heading quality
Tensile strength (indicative)about 225 – 275 MPa in H14Rises with drawing reduction
Density2.68 g/cm³About one third of steel

For comparison, 1035 and 3A21 rivet wire offer lower shear strength, 5B05 and 5A06 more, and the heat-treatable 2A01, 2A10 and 7A03 grades much more. See the 5A06 rivet wire guide for the stronger Al-Mg option.

Where a drawing written to ASTM or EN standards asks for 5052 rivets, 5A02 is usually a technical match, but substitution should be agreed with the design authority because the standards differ in test methods and permitted manganese.

Manufacturing

Cold heading 5A02 and what to order

Temper choice

H14 gives a firmer shank and is standard for solid rivets. Annealed (O) wire heads with less force and is used for large-diameter rivets or semi-tubular rivets that are rolled over.

Diameter tolerance

Rivet wire is drawn to tight tolerance, typically within a few hundredths of a millimetre, so that rivets fit their holes and heading dies fill consistently.

Surface

Free of cracks, laps, seams and pick-up; light oil is acceptable. Shallow scratches must stay within half the negative tolerance.

Coil form

Level-wound coils, typically 50 kg and above, on pallets or in cases, sized to the pay-off of the heading machine.

Lubrication

A light heading lubricant or soap coating reduces die wear and galling.

Testing

Chemistry, diameter, shear strength and upsetting tests per lot, reported on a 3.1 certificate.

Applications

Where 5A02 rivets are used

  • Light aircraft and aerospace secondary parts: fairings, interior panels and equipment brackets where corrosion resistance outweighs shear strength
  • Marine: joining 5xxx sheet in boat fittings, hatches and superstructure trim
  • Electrical and electronics: enclosures, cabinets and heat-sink assemblies
  • Transport: vehicle body trim, trailer panels and luggage racks
  • Magnesium-alloy structures: low-copper Al-Mg rivets limit galvanic attack in magnesium sheet
  • General sheet metal: ducting, signage and consumer goods

FAQ

5A02 rivet wire: frequently asked questions

Is 5A02 the same as 5052 aluminum?

They are close but not identical. Both contain about 2.5 % magnesium; 5A02 allows manganese or chromium for grain control, while 5052 specifies chromium. Many designs accept either, but substitution should be approved.

Do 5A02 rivets need heat treatment before driving?

No. 5A02 is not heat-treatable, so rivets are driven as delivered. That avoids the quench and refrigerated storage needed for 2017 and 2024 rivets.

What shear strength does 5A02 rivet wire have?

Rivet wire in H14 is commonly specified at a minimum of about 115 MPa in shear for diameters up to 10 mm. Check the edition of the standard on your purchase order for the exact requirement.

Which diameters are available?

Typically 1.6 – 10 mm for rivet wire, supplied in level-wound coils. Larger rod for machined fasteners can be supplied on request.

When should I choose 5A06 instead of 5A02?

Choose 5A06 when the joint needs more shear strength, or when riveting high-magnesium sheet such as 5A06 or 5083, while keeping an all-Al-Mg, corrosion-resistant joint.
