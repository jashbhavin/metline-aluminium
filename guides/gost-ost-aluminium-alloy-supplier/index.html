GOST / OST Aluminium Alloy Supply: Russian Grades to Drawing

If your drawing calls for a Russian or Soviet aluminium grade such as Д16ч, 1163, В93, 1420 or 1980, the safest route is to source that grade to its own GOST, OST or TU chemistry rather than substitute a Western alloy. Metline supplies flat-rolled Russian-standard aluminium (sheet, plate, coil, strip, foil and circles) and checks the designation, composition, temper and paperwork before quoting.

Overview

Why Russian-standard aluminium is still specified

A large installed base of aircraft, ships, rail vehicles, military hardware and industrial plant was designed in the Soviet Union and its successor states. Every part on those drawings refers to a Russian grade, a GOST or OST product standard and a Russian temper code. When that equipment is repaired, overhauled or rebuilt, the material on the drawing still governs.

Swapping in a "near equivalent" such as 2024 for Д16 or 7075 for В95 is tempting, and sometimes acceptable for non-critical parts. For certified, fatigue-loaded or welded structures it usually is not. Composition windows differ, impurity limits (iron and silicon in particular) differ, temper definitions differ, and the guaranteed properties in a GOST sheet or plate standard are not the same numbers as ASTM B209 or EN 485-2. A maintenance organisation or design authority normally needs the original grade, or a formal concession, before a substitute can be fitted.

Metline approaches these enquiries as a materials-matching job first and a supply job second. We confirm the exact designation (including Cyrillic suffixes), the governing document and edition, the temper and any cladding, then propose a production route for the flat-rolled form you need. Where a grade cannot be produced economically in a small lot, we say so and set out the closest documented alternative so your engineers can decide.

Typical buyers of GOST / OST aluminium

  • Aircraft MRO shops and operators maintaining Soviet- and Russian-designed fleets
  • Shipyards and repair yards working on vessels built to Russian register rules
  • Defence and rail maintenance organisations with legacy drawings
  • Plant owners in CIS and partner markets replacing equipment parts like-for-like
  • Engineering firms reverse-engineering parts where only a Russian material callout survives
Designation system

How Russian aluminium grade names work

Russian wrought aluminium grades are listed in GOST 4784 (the 1997 edition was replaced by GOST 4784-2019). Most grades carry two names: an older letter-and-number name and a four-digit numeric code. Both appear on drawings, often side by side, and you must be able to read either.

The Western grades in the right-hand column are the usual cross-references, not certified equivalents. Composition limits overlap but are not identical.

In the numeric system the leading 1 marks an aluminium base, and the second digit groups the alloy system (for example 11xx Al-Cu-Mg, 13xx Al-Mg-Si, 15xx Al-Mg, 19xx Al-Zn-Mg). Newer alloys developed from the 1960s onward, such as 1163, 1420, 1920 and 1980, were registered with numeric names only, which is why many aerospace and marine drawings show nothing but four digits.

Documents

GOST, OST and TU documents that govern supply

A Russian material callout usually names two things: the grade (chemistry from GOST 4784 or a specific OST/TU) and the product standard that fixes dimensions, tolerances, tempers and guaranteed properties. Both must be agreed before production.

DocumentScopeWhy it matters on an order
GOST 4784-2019 (formerly 4784-97)Grades and chemical composition of wrought aluminium and alloysFixes the melt chemistry; certificate analysis is checked against it
GOST 1131Wrought alloy ingots for further processingRelevant when a custom cast or remelt stock is needed
GOST 21631Sheet in aluminium and aluminium alloysThickness bands, tolerances, cladding and sheet properties
GOST 17232Plate in aluminium and aluminium alloysPlate thickness, flatness and guaranteed properties
GOST 13726Strip in aluminium and aluminium alloysStrip and coil width and thickness tolerances
GOST 618-2014Technical foilFoil thickness, width and mechanical requirements
GOST 745-2014Packaging foilFood-contact and converting foil grades
OST 1 (aviation industry) and other OSTsIndustry standards for semi-finished productsOften stricter inspection or test schedules for aerospace parts
TU (технические условия)Producer- or project-specific technical conditionsMust be supplied by the customer or agreed in writing

GOST documents are interstate standards adopted across many CIS countries; GOST R documents are Russian national standards. OSTs are industry standards from the Soviet ministries (OST 1 for aviation, OST 5 for shipbuilding), and TUs are technical conditions. Older OSTs and TUs can be hard to obtain; if you hold a copy, send it with your enquiry. Our guide to the GOST system explains the hierarchy in more detail.

Engineering support

Working from old Russian drawings

Many enquiries arrive with nothing more than a photocopied title block. A careful review at the start avoids the most expensive mistake in this field: producing the wrong alloy.

  1. Identify the gradeConfirm the Cyrillic designation, its Latin transliteration and any suffix. В93 may appear as V93 or B93 in export papers; Д19ч as D19ch; 1985ч as 1985ch.
  2. Identify the documentEstablish which GOST, OST or TU, and which edition, governs the material. Editions change composition limits and property tables.
  3. Confirm chemistryCheck composition limits from the document or from a customer-supplied table, including impurity limits that distinguish ч and пч grades.
  4. Choose the production routeDecide whether standard rolling stock can be used or whether a dedicated melt and slab is needed before rolling.
  5. Fix temper and formAgree temper (М, Н, Т, Т1 and so on), cladding (А, Б, У), thickness, width, length or coil dimensions and surface condition.
  6. Agree documentationSet the certificate content, test schedule, batch marking, traceability and any third-party inspection before production.

Common transcription errors on old drawings

  • Cyrillic В read as Latin B instead of V (В93 is V93)
  • The suffix ч dropped, turning a clean grade into a standard one
  • Letter З confused with digit 3 and letter О with digit 0 in typewritten documents
  • Сил1С and Сил2С misread because the digit sits between Cyrillic letters
  • Cladding letter А mistaken for part of the grade name (Д16АТ is clad Д16 in temper Т)
Substitution

When can an ASTM or EN grade replace a GOST grade?

Substitution is an engineering decision for the design authority, not the supplier. Our role is to give accurate information on how close two materials are. In general:

Often acceptable

Non-structural parts, brackets, covers and general machinery where the drawing intent is "commercial Al-Mg-Si" or "commercial Al-Mn". АД31 and 6063, or АМц and 3003, are routinely interchanged in non-critical use.

Needs a concession

Structural parts in Д16 or В95 where 2024 or 7075 is proposed. Properties are close, but impurity limits, cladding and guaranteed minimums differ, so approval is needed.

Usually not acceptable

Grades with no Western twin: 1420 (Al-Mg-Li), 1163, 1980, 1985ч, the Сил brazing alloys and most aerospace Al-Li grades. These are supplied to their own chemistry.

Where a substitute is acceptable, we can supply the Western alloy instead, for example 2024 sheet and plate or 7075 plate, and set out the differences in writing for your file.

Quality

Certificates, traceability and inspection

Russian-standard orders are normally inspection-heavy. We agree the document set before production and supply the following as standard or on request:

  • Mill test certificate (EN 10204 3.1) showing the GOST grade, governing standard, heat or batch number, chemical analysis and mechanical test results
  • Certificate wording in English, with Russian designations reproduced exactly; bilingual layouts on request
  • Batch marking on every plate, sheet pack or coil so each piece traces back to the certificate
  • Dimensional and visual inspection records; ultrasonic testing of plate where the order calls for it
  • Third-party inspection by an agency you nominate, before shipment
  • Export packing in seaworthy wooden cases or pallets with moisture protection

Metline holds ISO 9001 certification. We do not issue Russian state conformity certificates (EAC or GOST R certification); where your import process needs them, tell us early so the documentation can be planned with your customs broker.

FAQ

Russian GOST / OST aluminium: frequently asked questions

Can you supply a Russian alloy if we only have an old drawing?

Usually, yes. Send the drawing extract showing the material callout. We identify the grade and governing document, confirm the chemistry and temper, and propose a production route. If key information is missing, we tell you exactly what needs confirming before quotation.

Is Д16 the same as 2024 aluminum?

They belong to the same Al-Cu-Mg family and are often cross-referenced, but they are not identical. Composition limits, impurity limits and guaranteed properties differ, and Д16 sheet is commonly clad. Use 2024 in place of Д16 only with approval from the design authority.

What does the letter ч mean after a grade such as Д16ч or 1985ч?

It marks a "clean" version of the alloy with lower permitted iron and silicon. Lower impurities improve fracture toughness and fatigue behaviour, which is why clean grades are specified for airframe and marine structures. Do not drop the suffix when ordering.

What is the difference between GOST and OST?

GOST documents are interstate standards adopted across CIS countries. OSTs are industry standards from former Soviet ministries, for example OST 1 for the aviation industry, and often add tighter inspection rules. TU documents are technical conditions agreed for a producer or project.

Which product forms can you supply in Russian grades?

Flat-rolled forms: sheet, plate, coil, strip, foil and circles or blanks cut from sheet and plate. Ingot or slab to a Russian chemistry can be considered as a step towards rolled product. Extruded and forged Russian-grade parts are evaluated case by case.

Do you provide Russian conformity certificates?

We provide mill test certificates to EN 10204 3.1 that state the Russian grade, standard and batch results, under our ISO 9001 system. We do not issue EAC or GOST R certificates; your importer or a certification body handles those where they are required.

Need aluminium to a Russian grade?

Send the grade, governing GOST, OST or TU, temper, size and quantity. We confirm the designation and reply with a production route, certificate scope and delivered price.
