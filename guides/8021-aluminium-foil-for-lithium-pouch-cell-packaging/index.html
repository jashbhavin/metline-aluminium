8021 Aluminium Foil for Lithium Pouch Cell Packaging

8021 aluminium foil is the barrier layer inside the aluminium-plastic laminated film that encloses lithium-ion pouch cells. Supplied in soft O temper, typically 25 – 60 µm thick, it keeps moisture and oxygen out of the cell while stretching enough to be cold-formed into deep cell pockets without cracking. This guide explains why 8021 is used, how the laminate is built, which properties matter and how to specify the foil.

Overview

The role of aluminium foil in a pouch cell

A pouch cell has no rigid can. Its electrodes and electrolyte are sealed inside a flexible laminate, so that laminate alone must keep water vapour out for the life of the battery. Even a few hundred parts per million of water reacts with the LiPF6 salt in the electrolyte to form hydrofluoric acid, which degrades the cell. Polymer films cannot provide that barrier; a continuous aluminium foil can.

The foil also has to survive the way pouches are made. Cell makers cold-form, or deep-draw, a pocket into the flat laminate to hold the electrode stack. Pocket depths of several millimetres stretch the foil hard, especially at the corners. If the foil necks or cracks there, the barrier is lost and the cell fails, often only after months in service.

8021 meets both needs. It contains about 1.2 – 1.7 % iron and very little silicon, which produces many fine Al-Fe intermetallic particles. After full annealing these particles pin a very fine, uniform grain structure, giving high elongation and isotropic deformation: the foil stretches evenly in all directions instead of thinning locally. That is why 8021 and its close relative 8079 dominate battery pouch and cold-form pharmaceutical foil.

What the foil must deliver

  • A pinhole-free barrier at 25 – 60 µm gauge
  • High, uniform elongation for deep cold-forming without corner cracks
  • A very clean, oil-free surface that accepts chemical conversion treatment and adhesive
  • Tight gauge tolerance and flatness for high-speed laminating

Finished laminates are usually sold by total thickness: around 88 µm for thin consumer cells, about 113 µm as a common general grade (for example 25 µm nylon, 40 µm foil and 40 µm polypropylene plus adhesives), and about 150 µm or more for large EV and energy-storage cells that need deeper pockets and more puncture resistance. The foil gauge rises with pocket depth: a thicker foil spreads strain over more metal and resists cracking.

Performance

Properties that decide forming depth and reliability

Elongation

High, uniform elongation in rolling, transverse and 45° directions lets the foil follow the punch into the pocket corners. Laminators test this as a minimum elongation and by forming trial pockets to a target depth.

Grain size

A fine, even grain (helped by 8021's iron particles and controlled final annealing) prevents orange-peel and local necking during deep forming.

Pinholes

At 35 – 40 µm, good battery foil is essentially pinhole-free. Pinholes come from inclusions and rolling defects, so melt cleanliness and filtration matter.

Surface cleanliness

Residual rolling oil weakens the conversion coating and adhesive bond. Battery foil is annealed to burn off oil and checked by wettability (brush or dyne) tests.

Gauge and flatness

Thickness variation across the width causes uneven lamination and inconsistent forming. Tight tolerance and good shape keep the laminating line stable.

Mechanical strength

Tensile strength in O temper is modest; what matters is the combination with nylon, which carries much of the load during forming.

8021 generally gives the highest and most uniform elongation of the group, which is why it is preferred for the deepest pockets. 8079 is used where its forming is sufficient and supply or cost favours it. Pouch laminate foil and cathode current-collector foil are completely different products: the collector is a thin, hard, high-purity foil coated with cathode material inside the cell, whereas 8021 is a soft barrier layer outside it. How to specify 8021 pouch foil

  1. Define the laminateTotal laminate thickness, foil gauge and the deepest pocket you plan to form.
  2. Set foil propertiesMinimum elongation (and directions), tensile range, pinhole limit and wettability.
  3. Give dimensionsWidth and tolerance, core ID, maximum coil OD or weight, splice rules.
  4. Agree surfaceBright or matt faces, oil residue limit, and whether conversion coating is applied by you or upstream.
  5. Confirm documents and packingCertificates, inspection reports, and moisture-proof, dust-free packing with desiccant on export pallets or in cases.

Beyond batteries, the same 8021-O foil is used for cold-form (Alu-Alu) pharmaceutical blisters and other high-barrier laminates where deep drawing is needed.

FAQ

8021 pouch cell foil: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 8021 and 8011 aluminium foil?

8021 has more iron (1.2 – 1.7 %) and very little silicon, giving a finer grain and higher, more uniform elongation in O temper. That suits deep cold-forming in pouch laminates and Alu-Alu blisters. 8011 contains both iron and silicon and is the general packaging foil.

What is the difference between 8021 and 8079 foil?

Both are soft Al-Fe foils used as barrier layers. 8021 has higher iron and usually forms deeper pockets; 8079 has a little less iron, some silicon and is widely used in flexible packaging and some pouch laminates.

Is 8021 aluminum foil used as cathode foil?

No. Cathode current collectors use thin, hard, high-purity foil such as 1060, 1070 or 1235-H18. 8021-O is the soft barrier layer in the outer pouch laminate.

What thickness of 8021 foil is used in pouch cells?

Most laminates use 35 or 40 µm foil. Thinner gauges suit small consumer cells; thicker foil, up to about 60 µm, is used for large cells with deep pockets.

Why must the foil surface be treated?

Electrolyte that reaches the inner face can form hydrofluoric acid, which attacks untreated aluminium and causes the sealant to delaminate. A conversion coating on the inner face protects the metal and keeps the bond.

Can Metline provide test documents?

Yes. Each lot comes with an EN 10204 3.1 mill certificate showing chemistry and mechanical properties, and inspection or third-party test reports can be added on request.
