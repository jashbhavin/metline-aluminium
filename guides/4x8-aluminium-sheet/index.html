Sheet size guide

4x8 Aluminium Sheet: Sizes, Weights and Grade Selection

A 4x8 aluminium sheet measures 4 × 8 feet, which is 48 × 96 inches or 1219 × 2438 mm, and is the standard panel size for fabrication, signage, vehicle bodies and cladding. A 1/8 inch (3.2 mm) 4x8 sheet in 6061 weighs about 56 lb (25.5 kg); in 5052 it is slightly lighter. This guide gives weights for every common thickness, explains how to pick the alloy and temper, and lists what to state when you order.

Dimensions

What exactly is a 4x8 sheet?

The 4x8 format comes from North American building practice, where plywood, drywall and metal panels are made to fit framing at 16 or 24 inch centres. Aluminium mills and service centres roll and shear to the same 48 × 96 inch size so that sheets drop straight into fabrication jigs, CNC routers and vehicle body designs built around it.

Outside North America many mills cut to 1220 × 2440 mm, which is within a millimetre of true 4x8, or to the European standard 1250 × 2500 mm, which is about 5 % larger. If your drawing, router bed or shipping crate is designed tightly around 48 × 96 inches, say so on the order: a 1250 × 2500 sheet will not fit a 4x8 rack, and nesting programs built for one size waste material on the other.

Larger formats follow the same logic: 4x10 (48 × 120 in), 4x12 (48 × 144 in), 5x10 (60 × 120 in) and 5x12 (60 × 144 in). They reduce seams on trailer sides and facades, but they are heavier to handle and cost more to ship in containers.

Gauge

Aluminium sheet gauge to inches and millimetres

In the US, aluminium sheet is often called out by gauge. Aluminium uses the Brown & Sharpe (American Wire Gauge) series, not the Manufacturers' Standard Gauge used for steel, so a 16 gauge aluminium sheet is thinner than 16 gauge steel. Avoid confusion by ordering in decimal inches or millimetres.

Alloy selection

Which alloy and temper should a 4x8 sheet be?

Any wrought alloy can be cut to 4x8, but a handful cover almost all orders. Choose the alloy for what the part must do: bend, weld, resist the weather, carry load or take a decorative finish.

Quick selection rules

  • Bending tightly or forming? Start with 3003-H14 or 5052-H32; 6061-T6 cracks on tight bends.
  • Outdoor or salt exposure? 5052 or 5083 are the safe defaults.
  • Machining and structural loads? 6061-T6 (T651 at 1/4 in and over).
  • Anodised appearance panels? 5005 or anodising-quality 5052 and 6061.
  • Lowest cost for a non-structural panel? 3003 or 1100.

Definitions

When does a 4x8 sheet become plate?

Under ASTM B209 and the ANSI H35.2 dimensional standard, flat-rolled aluminium 0.006 to 0.249 in thick is sheet, and 0.250 in (6.35 mm) and over is plate. European practice under EN 485 uses 6 mm as the usual dividing line. The distinction is not just vocabulary: plate has its own tolerance tables, is often produced on different lines and is available in stress-relieved tempers such as 6061-T651.

A 4x8 sheet at 1/4 inch in 6061 weighs about 112 lb (51 kg), which is about the limit of comfortable two-person handling. Above that thickness, plate is often bought in larger formats and saw-cut to finished blanks rather than ordered as 4x8 sheets. Our 1/4 inch plate guide covers that thickness in detail.

Fabrication

Getting the most out of a 4x8 sheet

  • Plan the nest first: a 4x8 sheet yields eight 24 × 24 in blanks or eighteen 16 × 16 in blanks only if the saw kerf (about 3 mm for a blade, under 1 mm for a laser) is allowed for. Design parts to 23.875 in rather than 24 in if you need exactly eight.
  • Respect grain direction: bend across the rolling direction where possible. The grain runs along the 96 in length on a standard sheet.
  • Use the right method: shears for straight cuts up to about 6 mm, CNC routing and waterjet for profiles, fibre laser for thin sheet. See our guide to cutting aluminium sheet.
  • Keep the film on: PVC film protects decorative faces during cutting and bending; remove it within the time the film supplier states to avoid adhesive residue.
  • Allow bend radii: 3003-H14 and 5052-H32 bend around about 1 × thickness; 6061-T6 needs closer to 1.5 – 3 × thickness.
  • Store flat and dry: water trapped between stacked sheets causes white staining that will not polish out.
Ordering

What to specify when ordering 4x8 sheet

  1. Alloy and temperFor example 5052-H32 or 6061-T6, with the standard (ASTM B209, EN 485, JIS H4000).
  2. Size and thicknessTrue 48 × 96 in or metric 1220 × 2440 mm, and thickness in inches or millimetres rather than gauge.
  3. FinishMill, PVC-film one or both sides, brushed, tread pattern, painted colour or anodised.
  4. Quantity and packingNumber of sheets, maximum bundle weight for your handling equipment, interleaving if needed.
  5. Documents and deliveryMill test certificate (EN 10204 3.1), destination and terms: EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP.

Price depends mainly on the aluminium market on the order date, the alloy and temper, the thickness (very thin sheet costs more per kilogram), the finish and the freight route. We quote each enquiry against current market conditions.

FAQ

4x8 aluminium (aluminum) sheet: FAQs

How much does a 4x8 sheet of aluminium weigh?

It depends on thickness and alloy. In 6061, a 0.040 in sheet weighs about 18 lb (8.2 kg), 1/8 in about 56 lb (25.5 kg) and 1/4 in about 112 lb (51 kg). 5052 is about 1 % lighter.

Is 1220 × 2440 mm the same as 4x8?

Almost. True 4x8 is 1219.2 × 2438.4 mm, so 1220 × 2440 is less than 2 mm larger and is accepted as 4x8 in most markets. 1250 × 2500 mm is a different, larger European size.

What is the best aluminium for a 4x8 sheet that must be bent?

5052-H32 is the usual choice: stronger than 3003 yet still bendable to about 1 × thickness. 3003-H14 is cheaper and bends even more easily. Avoid 6061-T6 for tight bends.

What thickness is 16 gauge aluminium sheet?

Aluminium uses the Brown & Sharpe series, so 16 gauge is 0.0508 in (1.29 mm). A 4x8 sheet of it in 6061 weighs about 22.8 lb (10.3 kg).

Can you cut 4x8 sheets to size?

Yes. Sheets can be sheared, sawn, routed or waterjet cut to blanks before shipping, which saves freight on offcuts and handling at your end.

Do you ship 4x8 aluminium sheet internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies sheet worldwide from Mumbai, packed on seaworthy pallets or in wooden cases, with mill test certificates.
