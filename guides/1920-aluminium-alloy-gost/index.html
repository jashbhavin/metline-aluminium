1920 (В92) Aluminium Alloy (GOST)

1920, known by its letter name В92 (V92 or B92 in Latin script), is a Russian weldable aluminium alloy with about 4.3 % magnesium, 3.2 % zinc and 0.8 % manganese. It sits between the non-heat-treatable АМг (5xxx-type) alloys and the high-zinc 7xxx grades: it can be aged to higher strength than АМг5 or АМг6 while staying weldable. It is supplied as plate, sheet, extrusions and forgings for welded structures.

Overview

What is 1920 (В92) aluminium alloy?

GOST 4784 lists 1920 in its table of Al-Zn-Mg alloys, together with 1901 and 1915, although in 1920 magnesium is actually the larger addition. Its magnesium level of 3.9–4.6 % is similar to 5083, while 2.9–3.6 % zinc allows a precipitation-hardening response that pure Al-Mg alloys do not have.

The result is an alloy that behaves in welding much like a 5xxx grade but can be quenched and aged for extra strength. The welded heat-affected zone, briefly heated to solution temperature, regains part of its strength by natural ageing afterwards, a property shared with other weldable Al-Zn-Mg alloys such as 7020 and 7005.

In Western systems there is no registered alloy with the same composition. Its nearest relatives in purpose are 7020 and 7005 (weldable Al-Zn-Mg) and 5083 / 5456 (high-Mg Al-Mg). A substitute must be chosen by properties, not by name.

Key points for buyers

  • Weldable alloy stronger than АМг5 / АМг6 after ageing
  • Moderate zinc keeps stress-corrosion risk lower than in high-zinc 7xxx alloys, but heat treatment still matters
  • Long-term service above about 150 °C is not advised
  • No direct ASTM / EN equivalent; compare 7020, 7005 and 5083

Properties

Properties and supply conditions

Properties depend on product form and condition, and guaranteed values are those of the product standard or ТУ. In general terms:

  • Annealed (М): softest, for forming; strength similar to work-hardened АМг alloys
  • Quenched and naturally aged (Т): higher strength with good ductility; the normal condition for welded structures because the HAZ recovers in the same way
  • Quenched and artificially aged (Т1): highest strength, with more attention needed to stress-corrosion resistance
  • Physical properties: elastic modulus about 70 GPa, expansion about 23–24 × 10⁻⁶ /K, thermal and electrical conductivity well below pure aluminium because of the alloy content
  • Temperature limit: precipitates coarsen with long exposure above roughly 150 °C, reducing strength

Fabrication

Welding, forming and finishing

Arc welding

TIG and MIG with Al-Mg fillers such as ER5356 or ER5183 (or the Russian Св-АМг5 / Св-АМг6 types). Clean oxide and keep heat input moderate.

Resistance welding

Spot and seam welding of sheet are practical with proper surface preparation.

After welding

Allow natural ageing of the HAZ before final strength testing; protect weld toes from corrosive exposure.

Forming

Form in the annealed or freshly quenched condition, then age.

Machining

Machines well in aged condition with sharp tools; annealed material is softer and can smear.

Anodising

Anodises satisfactorily for protection; colour may be less uniform than on 6xxx alloys.

Applications

Where 1920 is used

  • Welded load-bearing frames and structures in transport and special vehicles
  • Marine superstructures and deck structures where more strength than АМг5 is wanted
  • Aircraft and aerospace secondary structure built to GOST / OST materials
  • Machine frames and welded equipment parts
  • Replacement material for equipment originally designed with В92

FAQ

1920 (В92) aluminium alloy: frequently asked questions

Is 1920 the same as В92?

Yes. 1920 is the digital designation and В92 the letter designation of the same GOST alloy; in Latin script it is often written V92 or B92.

Can 1920 be welded?

Yes. It is welded by TIG, MIG and resistance methods, using Al-Mg fillers such as 5356 or 5183, and the HAZ partly recovers strength by natural ageing.

Which heat treatment gives the highest strength?

Quenching followed by artificial ageing (Т1). For welded structures the naturally aged condition (Т) is often preferred.

What is the international equivalent of 1920?

There is no exact equivalent. 7020 and 7005 are the closest weldable heat-treatable alloys; 5083 and 5456 are non-heat-treatable alternatives.

What is the density of 1920?

2.72 g/cm³ according to GOST 4784.

Can Metline supply 1920 with certificates?

Yes. We supply GOST grades or agreed substitutes with mill test certificates and export packing to destinations worldwide.
