6063 Aluminium U Channel: Sizes, Weights, Finishes and Uses

6063 aluminium U channel is an extruded three-sided section, a flat base with two parallel legs, made in the architectural alloy 6063. Usually supplied in T52 or T5 temper, it is the standard section for glass and panel edging, LED strip housings, trims, slide tracks and light frames because it extrudes accurately and anodises evenly. Below are size and weight tables, guaranteed properties, finishing options and the checks that prevent ordering mistakes.

Overview

What is 6063 U channel?

A U channel has a base (the web) and two legs of equal height standing at right angles to it, with square or slightly radiused inside corners. Dimensions are given as outside base width A × outside leg height B × thickness t.

The U shape is a natural carrier: a sheet of glass, acrylic, plywood or composite panel slides into it; an LED strip sits on its base under a diffuser; a drawer runner or sliding panel runs along it. In 6063 the channel comes off the extrusion press with accurate internal dimensions and a smooth, uniform surface, so the fit stays consistent and visible faces look the same after anodising.

U channel differs from structural C channel. Structural channels (for example the Aluminum Association and American Standard series in 6061-T6) have wide, thick flanges and tapered or radiused sections designed for bending loads. Architectural U channel in 6063 is lighter, usually of uniform thickness, and designed for fit and appearance. If your channel is a beam, see the aluminium channel guide.

Getting the size right

  • Specify outside dimensions: the internal slot width is A − 2t, and the internal depth is B − t
  • For panel edging, the slot should be the panel thickness plus a gasket or tape allowance
  • Equal-leg channels have A = B; "deep" channels have legs taller than the base; "shallow" channels the reverse
Product types

Types of 6063 channel

Equal-leg U channel

Base and legs the same size, such as 1 × 1 × 1/8 in or 25 × 25 × 2 mm. General trim, edging and light framing.

Unequal or deep U channel

Legs taller than the base for holding panels deeply, or a wide base with short legs for capping and slide tracks.

Architectural channel (sharp corner)

Square inside and outside corners, uniform thickness. The usual 6063 stock form.

LED channel

U channels with diffuser grooves and end-cap features. 6063 conducts heat well (about 200 W/m·K), helping LED strips run cooler.

Glazing and panel channel

U channels sized to hold glass, polycarbonate or composite panels with gaskets, used in balustrades, partitions and display cases.

Custom channel

Lips, inward-turned flanges, screw bosses or snap features extruded to drawing for system suppliers.

On U channel, the dimension that matters most is usually the slot width A − 2t. Leg spread (legs splaying outwards) or closing inwards is the most common fit problem; it is controlled by the angularity tolerance and by the stretching and straightening practice of the extruder. For channels that must hold glass or panels with a set gasket, state the slot width and its tolerance on the drawing rather than relying on the general tables.

Typical stock-length tolerances are about ±6 mm (1/4 in) on mill lengths and about ±1.5 mm (1/16 in) on saw-cut lengths; tighter cut-length tolerances are available on request.

Finishing

Finishes and colours

Mill finish

As extruded; for hidden use or parts finished after fabrication.

Anodised

Clear, champagne, bronze, black and gold; 6063 gives the most even anodised colour of the common alloys.

Powder coated

RAL colours, matt or gloss, and wood-grain effects for furniture, windows and outdoor use.

Electrophoretic and PVDF

Electrophoretic lacquer over anodising, or PVDF coating, for demanding façade and coastal exposure.

Brushed, polished, sandblasted

Mechanical surface textures before anodising for decorative trim and shopfitting.

Protective film

Peel-off film on visible faces for transport and installation.

Working with 6063 channel

Cutting, bending, joining and machining

  • Cutting: carbide-tipped fine-pitch blade with lubricant; clamp the channel on its base and support both legs to stop them vibrating
  • Drilling and machining: sharp, high-rake tools and high speeds; T6 machines more cleanly than T5 or T52
  • Bending: bend T52 or T4 for curves; legs on the inside of a bend tend to buckle, so use filled or formed tooling and generous radii
  • Joining: end caps, corner keys and screws are usual; TIG or MIG welding with 4043 or 5356 filler works but softens the metal locally and damages finishes
  • Galvanic corrosion: separate the channel from steel fixings with washers or tape in damp locations

FAQ

6063 aluminium U channel: frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the weight of 6063 U channel?

Area = t × (A + 2B − 2t). In millimetres, multiply the area by 0.0027 for kg/m. A 25 × 25 × 2 mm channel weighs about 0.38 kg/m; a 1 × 1 × 1/8 in channel about 0.40 lb/ft.

What is the inside width of a U channel?

Outside base width minus twice the thickness. A 1 in channel with 1/8 in walls has a 3/4 in slot. Allow for gaskets or tape when sizing for panels.

Which temper should I choose for 6063 channel?

T52 or T5 for trims, edging and LED channels; T52 bends most predictably. T6 for channels that span further or carry more load.

Can 6063 aluminum U channel be bent into curves?

Yes, preferably in T52 or T4 with formed tooling and generous radii. Bend before anodising or coating.

Is 6063 U channel suitable for LED strips?

Yes. Its thermal conductivity of about 200 W/m·K spreads heat from the strip, and it anodises to clean silver or black finishes.

What lengths are available?

Usually 12 or 20 ft, or 6 m mill lengths, and cut lengths to order.
