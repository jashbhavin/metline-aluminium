PTP Aluminium Foil for Blister Packs

PTP (press-through pack) foil is the hard aluminium lidding that seals a pharmaceutical blister and lets the patient push a tablet straight through it. It is usually 8011 or 1235 in H18 temper, 20 – 25 µm thick, primed and printed on the outside and coated with a heat-seal lacquer on the inside. This guide explains the alloys, properties and coating choices, and Metline supplies PTP-grade foil worldwide.

Overview

What PTP foil does in a blister pack

A blister pack has two parts: a thermoformed cavity web in PVC, PVC/PVDC, PET or PP, and a flat lidding foil sealed over it. The lid must keep moisture, oxygen and light away from the drug for the full shelf life, then split cleanly when the tablet is pressed. Hard-temper aluminium foil is one of the few materials that does both.

Aluminium foil above about 17 µm is effectively a complete barrier to water vapour, gases and light, provided it is free of pinholes. In H18 temper it is stiff and has low elongation, so it ruptures under thumb pressure rather than stretching. Soft O-temper foil is used for child-resistant peel-push and peelable lids, where the foil is laminated with paper or PET.

Typical PTP lidding structure

  • Outer print primer or overlacquer (protects the print, about 1 g/m²)
  • Printing: product name, batch area, anti-counterfeit features
  • Aluminium foil, 8011 or 1235 H18, 20 – 25 µm
  • Heat-seal lacquer matched to the cavity film, typically a few g/m²

Quality

Pinholes, cleanliness and seal performance

  • Pinholes: unavoidable at very thin gauges, they fall sharply as thickness rises. Agree a maximum count per square metre and a maximum diameter, measured on a light table.
  • Surface cleanliness: residual rolling oil impairs primer and lacquer adhesion. Plate makers specify a wettability (dyne or water-brush) test.
  • Seal strength: the lacquer must bond to the cavity film; a common target is above about 10 N per 15 mm, verified on the customer's blister machine.
  • Flatness and winding: tight, even winding without telescoping avoids wrinkles on high-speed lines.
  • Food and drug contact: confirm compliance of lacquers and inks with the regulations in your market.

Plain

Printed

Coloured and gold

Tinted lacquers on one or both sides for brand identity and product differentiation.

Anti-counterfeit

Hidden images, fine-line patterns and positive/negative printing to deter copying.

FAQ

PTP aluminium (aluminum) blister foil: FAQs

What alloy and thickness is used for PTP blister foil?

Most push-through lids use 8011 or 1235 in H18 hard temper, 20 to 25 µm thick. Hard temper lets the foil break cleanly when the tablet is pressed.

What is the difference between PTP foil and cold-form (Alu-Alu) foil?

PTP foil is the hard, flat lid. Cold-form foil is soft O-temper foil, usually 45 – 60 µm, laminated with nylon and PVC and pressed into cavities to make a fully aluminium blister.

How are pinholes controlled?

By clean casting and rolling and by thicker gauges: pinholes drop sharply above about 20 µm. Buyers set a maximum number and size per square metre in the specification.

Which heat-seal lacquer do I need?

It must match the cavity film. PVC and PVDC-coated PVC are the most common; PET and PP cavities need dedicated lacquers. Coat weight depends on the sealing conditions of your blister machine.

Can PTP foil be supplied in narrow slit widths?

Yes. Foil can be slit to the web width of your blister line, on 76 mm or 152 mm cores, with joint limits per roll agreed in advance.
