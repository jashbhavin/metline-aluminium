7021 Aluminium Sheet for 3C Electronics Housings

7021 is a copper-free Al-Zn-Mg alloy that sits between 6xxx housing alloys and 7075 in strength, while anodising far more evenly than copper-bearing 7xxx grades. That combination makes 7021 sheet and plate in O, T6 and T651 a popular choice for phone middle frames, wearable cases, buttons and handheld device shells that must be both stiff and cosmetically perfect.

Overview

Why device makers use 7021

3C products (computers, communications and consumer electronics) push aluminium in two directions at once. Frames keep getting thinner, so they need more strength and dent resistance; and the finish is the product, so the alloy must anodise to a uniform, bright colour with no streaks.

6063 and 6013 anodise beautifully but are limited to roughly 250 – 400 MPa tensile. 7075 is far stronger, but its 1.2 – 2.0 % copper gives a yellowish, less consistent anodic film and makes colour matching difficult across batches. 7021 removes most of the copper (0.25 % maximum) and relies on zinc and magnesium for strength, with zirconium for grain control. The result is roughly 7075-T73 level strength with an anodising response much closer to the 6xxx alloys.

Properties

Mechanical properties by temper

TemperTensile strengthYield strengthElongationUse

T6 / T651, typicalAbout 460 MPaAbout 390 MPaAbout 9 %Machined frames and housings

Values for T6 are typical figures from published data; supplier and customer specifications for electronics sheet often set their own minimums, which can be higher when the ageing practice is optimised. Always design to the minimums written into your purchase specification.

T651 is stretched after quenching to relieve residual stress. For a CNC-machined middle frame, where most of the plate is cut away, T651 is the right temper, because it keeps the frame flat and the wall thickness consistent. Thin stamped parts are usually made from T6 or formed in O and then aged.

Physical properties such as density, modulus and thermal conductivity are in the normal range for Al-Zn-Mg alloys: slightly denser than 6xxx housing alloys and with lower thermal conductivity. Where heat spreading in a device matters, ask for measured values on the certificate rather than relying on generic figures.

Finishing

Anodising, blasting and polishing 7021

Colour anodising

Low copper lets 7021 take clear, black and dyed colours evenly. Zr grain control helps avoid the streaks that appear when coarse grains are exposed by machining.

Sand blasting

Fine bead or sand blasting before anodising gives the soft matte texture common on phones and laptops, and hides fine machining marks.

Polishing and diamond cut

Chamfers cut with diamond tools show a bright edge after anodising; clean material with few inclusions is essential here.

Hard anodising

Possible for wear parts such as buttons and SIM trays, with some loss of colour brightness.

Even with 7021, anodising quality depends on the plate. Segregation, coarse intermetallic particles and inconsistent grain structure appear as spots, bands or colour variation after anodising. That is why 3C buyers often ask for an anodising trial panel and grain-structure check from each new lot.

Applications

Typical 3C applications

  • Smartphones: middle frames, SIM trays, side buttons and camera rings
  • Wearables: smartwatch cases, fitness-band housings, earbud cases and smart-glasses frames
  • Handheld consoles and tablets: shells and internal stiffening frames
  • Laptops: hinges, stiffeners and premium lid or base parts

FAQ

7021 aluminium (aluminum) sheet: FAQs

Is 7021 stronger than 6061 and 6013?

Yes. Typical 7021-T6 tensile strength is around 460 MPa, compared with about 310 MPa for 6061-T6 and about 400 MPa for 6013-T6. That extra strength lets designers thin frame walls without losing dent resistance.

Why does 7021 anodise better than 7075?

Copper in 7075 (1.2 – 2.0 %) gives the anodic film a yellow tint and makes colours vary. 7021 limits copper to 0.25 %, so clear and dyed finishes are more even and easier to match.

Should I order T6 or T651?

For parts machined from plate, order T651: it is stretched to relieve stress and stays flat when most of the material is removed. T6 suits thin sheet used flat or lightly formed.

Can 7021 be welded?

It is weldable by MIG and TIG with 5xxx filler and recovers part of its strength by natural ageing, which is one reason it is used for automotive structures. In electronics it is normally machined rather than welded.

What should a 3C purchase specification include?

Temper, thickness and flatness tolerance, blank size, grain structure or anodising trial requirement, permitted surface defects, and the mill certificate with chemistry and mechanical results.
