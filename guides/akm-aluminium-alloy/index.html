AKM Aluminium Alloy to GOST Standards

AKM is a Russian GOST designation in the heat-treatable aluminium-copper-magnesium-silicon family, the same group as the forging alloys AK6 and AK8. Buyers working to GOST drawings use it for strong, machinable sheet, plate and strip. This guide explains how the GOST system names and certifies these alloys, how AKM relates to better-known grades, and what to specify when ordering.

Overview

What AKM aluminium alloy is

In the Russian and CIS designation system, the letters of a grade describe its alloying elements. "A" stands for aluminium, "K" (from kremniy) for silicon and "M" (from med') for copper, so AKM signals an aluminium alloy containing silicon and copper, with magnesium and manganese as further additions. Alloys of this type are heat-treatable: they gain most of their strength from solution treatment, quenching and ageing.

The closest widely documented relatives are AK6 (GOST number 1360) and AK8 (1380), the classic Russian forging alloys. AK8 corresponds closely to the international alloy 2014, while AK6 has a lower copper content and sits between 2014 and the 6xxx alloys. The copper and magnesium form strengthening precipitates on ageing, silicon promotes the Mg2Si phase and improves fluidity during hot working, and manganese refines grain and adds strength.

AKM appears on drawings and specifications from Russian, Kazakh, Belarusian and Ukrainian engineering practice, and on spare parts for equipment designed to those standards. Buyers outside the CIS often meet it when sourcing replacement parts or when a project requires GOST certification. Because GOST editions have changed over time and some grade names are used inconsistently in trade, the first step on any AKM order is to confirm which standard and edition defines the grade on your drawing.

Before you order AKM

  • Confirm the defining standard and edition (for example GOST 4784, GOST 1131 or an industry OST) shown on your drawing
  • Check the required chemical limits and temper against that edition; do not rely on a generic datasheet
  • Decide whether you need GOST certification itself or will accept an equivalent EN or ASTM grade
  • State the semi-finished product standard (sheet, plate or strip) separately from the grade standard

Properties and tempers

Properties and GOST temper designations

GOST uses letter suffixes rather than the T-codes of the Aluminum Association. Knowing them avoids most ordering errors, because a sheet ordered "AKM" without a temper letter may be supplied in a different condition from the one on the drawing.

GOST suffixMeaningNearest EN / AA temper
MAnnealed (soft)O
TSolution treated and naturally agedT4
T1Solution treated and artificially agedT6
T2, T3Over-aged variants for corrosion or stress resistanceT7x

No suffix / GKAs fabricated or hot rolled, without heat treatmentF, H112

In the annealed condition, alloys of the AKM type are soft and formable. After solution treatment and artificial ageing they reach strengths comparable to the 2xxx forging alloys, well above 6061-T6, while retaining reasonable elongation. The guaranteed tensile strength, proof stress and elongation for each thickness band are set by the sheet or plate standard and printed on the certificate; ask for those values for the exact thickness you need rather than using a single figure for all sizes.

Like other copper-bearing alloys, AKM has lower general corrosion resistance than 5xxx and 6xxx grades. Parts for humid or marine service are usually anodised, painted or ordered with cladding.

Substitution

Equivalents and when substitution is acceptable

There is no single official cross-reference for AKM. The right substitute depends on what the part needs, and any substitution should be approved by the design authority.

Common mistakes with GOST grades

  • Assuming a grade name alone defines chemistry when the edition of the standard has changed
  • Ordering by grade without the GOST temper suffix, which leaves the condition undefined
  • Accepting an "equivalent" without checking mechanical minimums for the actual thickness
  • Forgetting that GOST tolerance tables differ from EN 485 and ASTM B209
Applications

Where AKM-type alloys are used

  • Machinery and equipment: strong machined parts, frames and housings built to GOST drawings
  • Transport: brackets, structural components and spare parts for vehicles and rolling stock designed to CIS standards
  • Aerospace and defence: forged and machined fittings, where alloys of the AK6 and AK8 type are traditional choices
  • Forgings and stampings: the Al-Cu-Mg-Si family hot-works well into complex die forgings
  • Replacement parts: maintenance of equipment originally manufactured in the CIS, where certified like-for-like material is required

FAQ

AKM aluminium (aluminum) alloy: frequently asked questions

What does AKM mean in a GOST aluminium grade?

In the Russian designation system, A stands for aluminium, K for silicon and M for copper. AKM therefore indicates an aluminium-silicon-copper alloy, with magnesium and manganese, in the heat-treatable family.

What is the equivalent of AKM aluminium alloy?

There is no official one-to-one equivalent. Depending on the strength and corrosion resistance needed, 2014, 2014A, 6061 or 6151 may be suitable, but the design authority should approve any substitution.

Is AKM heat-treatable?

Yes. Like AK6 and AK8, it is strengthened by solution treatment, quenching and ageing. The temper is shown by GOST suffixes such as M (annealed), T (naturally aged) and T1 (artificially aged).

Which GOST standards apply to AKM sheet and plate?

The grade is defined in a composition standard such as GOST 4784, while sheet is supplied to GOST 21631, plate to GOST 17232 and strip to GOST 13726. Always quote the edition.

Can AKM be welded?

Copper-bearing heat-treatable alloys of this type are difficult to fusion weld and lose strength near the weld. Mechanical fastening is usually preferred; if welding is essential, consider a weldable 6xxx substitute.

Do you ship GOST aluminum grades internationally?

Yes. Metline supplies GOST and international aluminium grades worldwide, with mill certificates, cutting to size and export packing.

Sourcing AKM or another GOST alloy?

Send the grade, temper, standards, sizes and quantity. We will confirm availability or a documented equivalent, with certification and a delivered price.
