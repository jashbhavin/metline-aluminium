Сплав АКЦМ Aluminium Alloy

"Сплав АКЦМ" means "АКЦМ alloy": an aluminium alloy whose letters, in Russian practice, signal silicon (К), zinc (Ц) and copper (М) additions. It is not among the grades in the GOST 4784 composition tables we have reviewed, so it should be treated as a trade or works designation that must be pinned to a written specification. This guide decodes the name, explains what its quoted chemistry implies, and shows how to order it or a standard substitute safely.

Overview

Decoding the АКЦМ name

Russian aluminium designations use letters for alloying elements: А for aluminium, К (kremniy) for silicon, Ц (tsink) for zinc, М (med') for copper and Мг for magnesium. Read that way, АКЦМ describes an aluminium alloy containing silicon, zinc and copper. "Сплав" simply means "alloy". The Latin transliteration is AKTsM, and the grade is sometimes confused with АКМ, a different Al-Si-Cu designation.

Standard Russian wrought alloys are listed in GOST 4784 with a four-digit number (for example В95, number 1950) and cast alloys in GOST 1583. АКЦМ does not appear in the GOST 4784 tables we have checked, and we have not found it in an openly published standard. That does not make the material fictitious; works and trade names are common in the CIS market. It does mean the name alone does not define the chemistry, the temper or the guaranteed properties, so a buyer must obtain the specification behind it before ordering or accepting a substitute.

Chemical composition

What the quoted chemistry tells you

Trade literature for АКЦМ quotes roughly 3.5 – 6 % zinc, 1.4 – 3 % copper, 1.2 – 2.5 % magnesium and 0.8 – 1.6 % silicon, with manganese, chromium and small nickel and zirconium additions and iron up to about 1 %. Zinc, magnesium and copper in these proportions place the alloy in the high-strength, heat-treatable Al-Zn-Mg-Cu family, the same group as В95 and 7075. The silicon and iron levels are the surprise: registered high-strength wrought grades keep both low, because coarse iron- and silicon-bearing particles reduce toughness, fatigue life and ductility. A deliberate silicon addition of around 1 % and iron up to 1 % is more typical of casting alloys or of alloys made from recycled metal. That is a strong reason to ask for the full specification and certified test results before relying on any strength figure.

Properties

Expected behaviour and honest limits

Strength

Alloys of this family reach high strength after solution treatment and ageing. Actual minimums depend on the specification and temper and must come from the certificate.

Corrosion

Copper-bearing Al-Zn-Mg alloys have only moderate general corrosion resistance and can be prone to exfoliation and stress-corrosion cracking. Claims of seawater resistance should be treated with caution; protect parts by anodising, painting or cladding.

Weldability

Copper-containing 7xxx-type alloys are generally regarded as unsuitable for fusion welding because of hot cracking. Use mechanical fastening or a weldable grade such as 7020.

Machinability

High-strength tempers of this family machine well, producing short chips and good finishes with sharp carbide tools.

Toughness

High silicon and iron are expected to lower toughness compared with В95 or 7075; verify with impact or fracture data if the part is critical.

Heat treatment

Solution treatment, quench and artificial ageing; over-aged tempers improve stress-corrosion resistance at some cost in strength.

Ordering

How to order АКЦМ material safely

  1. Find the specificationAsk the designer or original supplier for the document behind "АКЦМ": a works standard (ТУ), drawing note or contract annex with chemistry, temper and properties.
  2. Check the nameConfirm the grade is АКЦМ and not АКМ, АК6 or a cast alloy; misreadings between Cyrillic and Latin letters are common.
  3. Decide on equivalenceIf the specification cannot be found, select a registered grade (В95, 7075, 7020) and get it approved.
  4. QuoteWe confirm form, size, temper, certification and delivery terms (EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP).
  5. ShipExport-packed with mill certificate (EN 10204 3.1) and packing list.

FAQ

АКЦМ aluminium (aluminum) alloy: frequently asked questions

What does АКЦМ mean?

In Russian alloy naming, А is aluminium, К silicon, Ц zinc and М copper. АКЦМ therefore indicates an aluminium alloy with silicon, zinc and copper; magnesium is also present in the quoted chemistry.

Is АКЦМ a GOST standard grade?

We have not found it in the GOST 4784 composition tables or another openly published standard. Treat it as a trade or works designation and obtain the specification that defines it.

What is the equivalent of АКЦМ?

There is no official one. By its zinc, magnesium and copper content it belongs with В95 and 7075; if weldability matters, 7020 or GOST 1915 are copper-free alternatives. Any substitute needs design approval.

Is АКЦМ corrosion resistant in seawater?

Copper-bearing Al-Zn-Mg alloys are not marine alloys. They need protective finishes in humid or salt environments. For seawater service, 5083 or 5086 are the usual choices.

Can АКЦМ be welded?

Alloys of this type are generally not fusion welded because they crack and lose strength near the weld. Use bolting or riveting, or choose a weldable grade such as 7020.
