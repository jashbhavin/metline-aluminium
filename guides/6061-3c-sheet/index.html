3C electronics sheet guide

6061 Aluminium Sheet and Plate for 3C Electronics

6061 is the default aluminium for machined electronics parts: CNC-milled phone mid-frames, tablet and laptop shells, camera bodies, SIM trays and the fixtures used to make them. In T6 it gives a reliable 276 MPa typical yield strength, machines cleanly and takes a uniform anodised finish, and it is available everywhere. This guide explains which temper to buy, what finish quality to expect and where 6061 stops being the best choice.

Overview

Why 6061 is used in 3C products

3C stands for computers, communications and consumer electronics. Aluminium parts in these products have to be light, stiff, precise and good-looking, and most are produced by CNC machining, stamping or a combination of the two followed by anodising.

6061 meets that brief at a low cost. Its magnesium and silicon give a heat-treatable strength that is about twice that of 5052, a small copper addition raises strength further, and chromium keeps the grain structure fine. In T6 or T651 it cuts with short chips and a bright surface, holds tolerance during heavy machining, and anodises to a hard, even film suitable for dyed colours.

For thin, drawn or bent housings, 6061 can be formed in O or T4 and then aged; for milled unibody parts, T6 or stress-relieved T651 plate is machined directly. Where higher dent resistance is wanted, designers move up to 6013 or 7xxx alloys; where the finest cosmetic finish matters more than strength, 6063 is an option.

Processing

Machining, finishing and anodising 6061

CNC machining

Use sharp carbide with high positive rake and high spindle speeds. T651 plate stays flat when frames are pocketed from one side.

Stamping and bending

Form in T4 with radii of about 1 × thickness, then age. T6 needs roughly 1.5 – 3 × thickness for bends.

Surface prep

Bead blasting, brushing or diamond cutting create the texture before anodising. Even grain gives a uniform look.

Decorative anodising

Sulphuric (Type II) films of about 10 – 25 µm, dyed in black, grey, blue and other colours, then sealed.

Hard anodising

Type III films of about 25 – 50 µm for wear-prone parts such as buttons and trays.

Watch points

Coarse grains, iron-rich particles and rolling streaks show after anodising; specify a cosmetic or anodising-quality surface.

Applications

3C parts made from 6061

  • Phone mid-frames and housings machined from plate, combining stiffness with low weight
  • SIM trays, internal brackets and side buttons, often hard-anodised
  • Tablet and laptop shells milled or formed and anodised
  • Camera parts: lens housings, body shells and mounts
  • Production fixtures and test jigs used on electronics assembly lines, usually from T651 plate
  • Heat spreaders and brackets where moderate conductivity is adequate

FAQ

6061 3C sheet: frequently asked questions

Is 6061 good for phone housings?

Yes. It is strong enough for most frames, machines well and anodises evenly. For thinner, more dent-resistant housings, 6013 or 7xxx alloys are used.

Which temper of 6061 aluminum is used for CNC-machined electronics parts?

T6 for thin sheet and T651 for plate. T651 is stress-relieved by stretching, so parts stay flat when pockets are milled.

Can 6061 sheet be bent before anodising?

Yes. Bend in T4 and age to T6 afterwards, or bend T6 using generous radii of about 1.5 – 3 × thickness.

What anodised colours work on 6061?

Clear, black, grey, blue, red, gold and most dyed colours. Production trials on the supplied sheet are recommended for colour matching.

Can you supply 6061 in coil for stamping?

Yes, as coil or slit strip, as well as cut sheet and plate.
