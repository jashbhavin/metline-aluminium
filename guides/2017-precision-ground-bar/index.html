2017 T4 / T451 Precision Ground Aluminium Bar

2017 precision ground bar is Al-Cu-Mg round bar in T4 or T451 temper that has been centreless ground to a tight ISO tolerance such as h8 or h9, ready for bar feeders on CNC and Swiss-type lathes. It combines about 380 – 430 MPa tensile strength with good machinability and a consistent diameter that keeps collets gripping and parts in size. This guide covers tolerances, properties, sizes and machining practice.

Overview

What is precision ground bar, and why 2017?

Ordinary extruded or drawn bar has a diameter tolerance of several hundredths of a millimetre and may be slightly oval or bent. Precision ground bar is passed through a centreless grinder after straightening, which brings the diameter into a narrow ISO tolerance band, improves roundness and leaves a fine, uniform surface.

That matters on automatic lathes. A Swiss-type (sliding headstock) machine supports the bar in a guide bush close to the cutting tool; if the bar diameter varies, the bush either binds or lets the bar chatter. Ground bar feeds smoothly at high speed, so turned parts hold size and surface finish over long runs.

2017 is chosen for ground bar because it is a strong, free-cutting Al-Cu-Mg alloy that produces short chips in T4, holds threads well and costs less than 2024 or 7075. It is popular for screws, pins, connectors, pneumatic fittings and small precision parts. T451 is the stress-relieved (stretched) version, which stays straighter when a large share of material is removed.

Quality

Coarse grain rings, surface and inspection

Extruded 2xxx bar can form a peripheral coarse-grain ring during solution treatment. On a ground or turned surface it may show up as white spots or a mottled appearance after anodising, and it lowers fatigue strength at the surface. For precision parts, buyers often limit the ring depth (for example to 0.5 mm) and ask for a macro-etch check.

  • Diameter measured at several points along the bar and around its circumference
  • Straightness checked on a reference table
  • Surface inspected for chatter, scratches and grinding burn
  • Coarse grain ring depth checked by macro-etch when specified
  • Chemistry and tensile tests on each lot

Machining

Machining 2017 ground bar

Tooling

Sharp carbide or coated HSS with high positive rake. Polished rake faces reduce built-up edge.

Chips

T4 gives short, broken chips; chip breakers help on long turning passes.

Threads

Cuts and rolls clean threads; 2017 holds thread form better than soft 6xxx alloys.

Stability

T451 limits distortion of long, slender parts; use light finishing passes.

Anodising

Takes decorative and hard anodising, but colour may be less even than on 6xxx alloys; control the process.

Joining

Not suited to fusion welding; parts are threaded, pressed or bonded.

FAQ

2017 precision ground aluminium (aluminum) bar: FAQ

Is there a difference between 2017 and 2017A?

They are very close. 2017A is the European grade with a wider magnesium range and a combined Zr + Ti limit; properties are similar and many drawings accept either.

Why choose precision ground bar instead of regular bar?

Grinding gives a tight, consistent diameter and roundness, so the bar feeds smoothly through guide bushes and collets on automatic lathes and parts hold size.

What tolerance does ground bar have?

Usually h9 to ISO 286, with h8 or h7 on request. For a 13 mm bar, h9 is 0 / −0.043 mm and h8 is 0 / −0.027 mm.

Can 2017 aluminium be welded?

Fusion welding is not recommended because of hot cracking. Resistance or friction welding is possible in some cases, but most parts are joined mechanically.

Is 2017 suitable for anodising?

Yes, but copper makes the colour less uniform than on 6xxx alloys, so process control matters.

Are samples available?

Short sample bars can be arranged for trial machining; tell us the diameter and tolerance you need.
