2024 Aluminium Alloy: Tempers, Properties and Product Forms

2024 is the classic high-strength aluminium-copper-magnesium alloy of aircraft structures, valued for its fatigue resistance and damage tolerance. In the common T3 and T351 tempers it reaches about 470 – 485 MPa tensile strength, roughly 50 % more than 6061-T6, but it corrodes more easily and is not fusion welded. This guide covers its composition, tempers, standards, forms and how to work with it.

Overview

What is 2024 aluminium?

2024 was introduced in the early 1930s as a stronger successor to 2017 duralumin, with more magnesium. It became the standard material for aircraft fuselage skins, wing lower surfaces and other tension-loaded structure, and it is still one of the most widely specified aerospace aluminium alloys.

Its strength comes from copper and magnesium, which form fine precipitates during natural or artificial ageing. In the naturally aged T3 and T4 tempers the alloy combines high strength with excellent resistance to fatigue crack growth, which is why it is preferred for parts that see repeated loading, such as pressurised fuselage skins.

The copper that gives 2024 its strength also lowers its corrosion resistance. Sheet is therefore often supplied as Alclad, with a thin layer of pure aluminium bonded to each face, and bare parts are anodised, conversion coated and painted. Fusion welding causes hot cracking, so 2024 structures are riveted, bolted, bonded or friction stir welded.

Choose 2024 when

  • Fatigue life and damage tolerance matter more than peak static strength
  • Parts are joined by riveting or bolting rather than welding
  • You need a strong, free-machining bar or plate for machined parts
  • Corrosion protection (cladding, anodising, paint) is part of the design

Working with 2024

Machining, forming, joining and corrosion protection

Machining

Machines very well in T3, T351 and T4: short chips and a good finish. Use sharp carbide tools and coolant. The O temper is gummy.

Forming

Form in O or freshly quenched (W) condition, then age. T3 allows only moderate bends; check minimum bend radii for thickness.

Welding

Fusion welding causes hot cracking and weak joints. Use riveting, bolting, adhesive bonding or friction stir welding.

Corrosion

Use Alclad sheet, anodising (often sulphuric or boric-sulphuric), conversion coatings and primer. Avoid contact with carbon fibre or copper without isolation.

Stress corrosion

T3/T351 is susceptible in the short-transverse direction of thick sections; T8 tempers are much more resistant.

Temperature

Loses strength progressively above about 120 °C; T8x tempers hold up better than T3.

A common airframe rule of thumb uses 2024 for tension-dominated structure, such as lower wing skins and fuselage skins, and 7075 for compression-dominated structure, such as upper wing skins and stringers. 6061 is the economical, weldable option where 2024 strength is not needed.

Applications

Applications of 2024 aluminium

  • Aerospace: fuselage skins, wing tension members, bulkheads, fittings and rivets
  • Defence: airframe parts, missile structures and military vehicle components
  • Transport: truck wheels, high-performance vehicle parts and rail interiors
  • Machinery: gears, shafts, fixtures and machined parts from bar and plate
  • Fasteners: DD rivets, screws and bolts from cold-heading wire
  • Sports equipment: components where high strength-to-weight is needed

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum): frequently asked questions

What is 2024 aluminium used for?

Mainly aircraft structure (fuselage skins, wing tension members, fittings and rivets), plus defence hardware, truck wheels, fasteners and highly stressed machined parts.

Is 2024 stronger than 6061?

Yes. 2024-T3 has about 483 MPa typical tensile strength versus about 310 MPa for 6061-T6, and much better fatigue resistance. 6061 is easier to weld and more corrosion resistant.

Can 2024 aluminium be welded?

Not by conventional fusion welding, which causes cracking. Friction stir welding works; otherwise use riveting, bolting or bonding.

What is the difference between 2024-T3 and T351?

Both are solution treated and naturally aged. T3 (mostly sheet) includes cold work for flatness; T351 (plate and bar) is stretched to relieve residual stress so it stays stable when machined.

What is Alclad 2024?

2024 sheet with a thin layer of high-purity aluminium on each face. The cladding protects the core from corrosion sacrificially, at the cost of slightly lower strength.

What is the Russian equivalent of 2024?

Д16 (1160). China's 2A12 is a close national grade with a similar composition.
