Overview

Extruded or cold-finished bar

Most aluminium bar starts life as an extrusion. Extruded bar is cost-effective, available in a wide range of sizes and suitable for most machining and fabrication. Cold-finished bar is drawn through a die after extrusion, which tightens the diameter tolerance, improves straightness and leaves a cleaner surface, so it is preferred for automatic lathes and precision turned parts.

Choosing bar is about balancing machinability, strength and corrosion resistance against cost. A free-machining grade can double the output of a CNC lathe, while a high-strength 7075 bar lets you make a lighter part that still carries the load.

Shapes

Bar shapes

For turned parts, shafts, pins and fasteners. Rod usually refers to smaller diameters.

Square bar

For milled blocks, keys and components with flat faces.

Hexagonal bar

For nuts, fittings and parts that need spanner flats without extra machining.

Flat bar

For brackets, busbars, frames and base plates.
Machining

Getting the best out of bar on the machine

  • Choose 2011 for maximum output on automatic lathes where strength demands are modest
  • Use 6061-T6 or 6082-T6 when parts need welding or good corrosion resistance
  • Specify T651 or T6511 stress-relieved tempers to reduce distortion on long or heavily machined parts
  • Select cold-finished bar when you need tight diameter tolerance straight off the bar
  • Ask for lead-free grades where RoHS or drinking-water rules apply

FAQ

Bar and rod questions

What is the easiest aluminium bar to machine?

2011 is the classic free-machining grade. It produces small, broken chips and a bright finish at high cutting speeds.

What is the difference between 6061 and 6082 bar?

Both are heat-treatable Al-Mg-Si alloys. 6082 is slightly stronger and is common in European specifications; 6061 is more widely used in American standards. Both weld and machine well.

Which aluminium bar is the strongest?

7075-T6 and 7075-T651 offer the highest strength among common bar grades, followed by 2024 and 2014.

What is cold-finished bar?

Extruded bar that has been drawn through a die to tighten tolerances, straighten it and improve the surface finish.

Can you cut bar to length?

Yes. Include the lengths and quantities you need in your enquiry.
