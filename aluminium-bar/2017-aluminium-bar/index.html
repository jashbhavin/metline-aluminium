2017 T4 T451 Aluminium Bar

2017 aluminium bar is the original duralumin: an aluminium-copper-magnesium alloy that is used in the naturally aged T4 or stress-relieved T451 temper, giving about 427 MPa tensile strength with good machinability. It is a standard stock for precision machined parts, fittings and screws, and in Europe its 2017A variant is one of the most common general engineering bars. Overview

What is 2017 aluminium bar?

2017 descends directly from the duralumin discovered by Alfred Wilm in 1906, when he found that an aluminium-copper-magnesium alloy kept getting harder for days after quenching. That effect, natural age hardening, is still how 2017 bar gets its strength today.

The alloy contains around 4 % copper, 0.6 % magnesium and 0.7 % manganese. After solution heat treatment and quenching it is left to age at room temperature, which produces the T4 temper. Bar that is also stretched to relieve residual stress is designated T451. In these conditions 2017 has a tensile strength around 427 MPa, a yield of about 276 MPa and good elongation, so it is both strong and tough enough for threaded and highly loaded small parts.

In North America 2017 has largely been replaced by 2024 for structural uses, but it remains a standard bar for screw-machine products and rivets. In Europe and Asia the 2017A variant (French AU4G, German AlCuMg1) is one of the most widely stocked machining bars, valued for its predictable chip behaviour and dimensional stability.

Strengths of 2017 bar

  • High strength for an alloy used in the naturally aged condition
  • Good machinability and stable dimensions in T451
  • Good fatigue and impact performance for fittings and fasteners
  • Widely accepted in ASTM, AMS, EN and JIS specifications

Tempers

2017 tempers: T4, T451 and O

2017-T4

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged to a stable condition. The standard temper for cold-finished rod and wire, including rivet and screw stock.

2017-T451

T4 plus stretching by about 1 – 3 % to relieve quench stresses. Order T451 for larger bar that will be machined asymmetrically or to tight tolerances.

2017-T42

Solution treated by the user from O or F temper and naturally aged; properties are similar to T4 and certified on the user's test results.

2017-O

Annealed for cold heading and forming. Parts are then solution treated and aged to regain strength.

2017 is rarely artificially aged. Ageing at elevated temperature raises yield strength only modestly and reduces toughness and corrosion performance, so T4 / T451 remain the normal supply conditions.

Working with 2017

Machining, forming, joining and protection

Machining

Good machinability in T4 / T451; chips are longer than with 2011 but well controlled with chip-breaker inserts. Use carbide or HSS tools, high speeds and coolant.

Cold heading

Used for rivets and screws. Rivets are often driven soon after solution treatment, or kept refrigerated, while the alloy is still soft.

Welding

Not recommended for fusion welding because of hot-cracking risk and strength loss. Mechanical joints are standard.

Heat treatment

Solution treat at about 500 – 510 °C, quench in water and age naturally for at least four days to reach stable T4 properties.

Corrosion

Moderate. Protect by anodising, conversion coating or paint, especially in humid or marine air.

Anodising

Accepts protective and hard anodising; the film is greyer than on 6xxx alloys.

Applications

Where 2017 bar is used

  • Precision engineering: machined components, bushes, spindles and instrument parts
  • Fasteners: screws, bolts, rivets and threaded inserts
  • Aerospace and defence: secondary fittings and hardware to AMS 4118
  • Hydraulics and pneumatics: fittings, couplings and valve components
  • Machinery and tooling: jigs, fixtures, mould parts and mechanical linkages
  • Transport: vehicle fittings and components where strength-to-weight matters

FAQ

2017 aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

Is 2017 the same as duralumin?

Yes, 2017 is the modern registered form of the original duralumin alloy. In Europe the term usually refers to 2017A (AlCu4MgSi(A), AU4G).

What is the difference between 2017-T4 and 2017-T451?

Both are solution treated and naturally aged with the same strength. T451 bar has also been stretched to relieve residual stress, so it distorts less during machining.

Is 2017A the same as 2017?

They are close. 2017A allows up to 1.0 % magnesium and a combined Zr + Ti limit. Properties are similar, and the grades are often dual-certified, but follow the designation on your drawing.

Can 2017 aluminium be welded?

Fusion welding is not recommended because of hot cracking and loss of strength. Use mechanical fastening or adhesive bonding.

What is 2017 aluminium used for?

Machined parts, fittings, screws, rivets, hydraulic components and precision instrument parts where good strength and machinability are needed.

How much does 2017 bar weigh?

About 2.79 g/cm³. A 1 m length of 50 mm round bar weighs about 5.48 kg.
