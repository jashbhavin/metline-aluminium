1100 Aluminium Rod & Bar

1100 aluminium rod is commercially pure aluminium (at least 99.0 % Al) with a small, controlled copper addition. It is soft, very ductile, highly conductive and resistant to corrosion, but not strong, so it is chosen for formed, spun, cold-headed and electrical or chemical-service parts rather than load-bearing components. Metline supplies 1100 rod and bar in annealed, strain-hardened and as-extruded tempers to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is 1100 aluminium rod?

1100 is the most common grade in the 1xxx series of commercially pure aluminium. Unlike 1050 or 1060, it carries a deliberate 0.05 – 0.20 % copper addition, which gives slightly higher strength and a finer, more even response to cold working while keeping conductivity and corrosion resistance high.

Because 1100 contains almost no alloying elements, it cannot be strengthened by heat treatment. Its strength comes only from cold work: the bar is drawn or rolled, and the amount of reduction sets the temper (H12, H14, H16, H18). Annealing returns it to the soft O temper. Even fully hard 1100 is weaker than annealed 6061, so the grade is used where formability, purity, conductivity or appearance matter more than load-carrying capacity.

In rod form, 1100 is typically supplied as extruded bar (H112 or F), cold-finished drawn rod in H12 to H18, or annealed rod for heavy cold forming. It is a common feedstock for cold heading, spinning, impact extrusion, rivets for non-structural use, electrical connectors, chemical equipment fittings, reflectors and decorative turned parts.

Choose 1100 rod when you need

  • Very high ductility for heading, upsetting, bending and spinning
  • Electrical conductivity around 57 – 59 % IACS and thermal conductivity above 200 W/m·K
  • Excellent resistance to atmospheric, food and many chemical environments
  • A clean, bright surface that anodises to a clear, even finish

Tempers

Which 1100 temper should you order?

1100-O

Fully annealed, the softest condition. Order O for cold heading, upsetting, deep bending, spinning or swaging, where the rod must accept heavy deformation without cracking.

1100-H12 / H14

Quarter-hard and half-hard. A good balance for turned parts, light bending and general fabrication; H14 is the most commonly stocked drawn temper.

1100-H16 / H18

Three-quarter hard and full hard. Highest strength and stiffness the grade can reach, used for straight rod, pins and parts that must resist bending in handling.

1100-H112 / F

As-extruded bar with only incidental work hardening. Economical for larger diameters, flats and shapes that will be machined or formed further.

Strain-hardened tempers soften if heated. Welding, brazing or service above roughly 200 °C will anneal the affected area towards O temper, so do not rely on H-temper strength near heat-affected zones.

Working with 1100

Forming, machining, joining and finishing

Forming

Outstanding cold formability in O temper. Can be headed, upset, swaged, spun and bent to very tight radii. Strain hardens as it is worked, so intermediate anneals help on severe parts.

Machining

Soft and gummy; produces long, stringy chips. Use sharp, polished high-rake tools, high speeds and lubricant. Harder tempers (H14 – H18) machine more cleanly than O.

Welding

Excellent weldability by TIG, MIG, resistance and gas methods. ER1100 filler matches the base metal; ER4043 is used where better fluidity is needed.

Brazing and soldering

Readily brazed and soldered, which is why 1100 appears in heat-exchanger and electrical assemblies.

Anodising

Gives a clear, bright protective anodic film; suitable for decorative and reflective parts.

Corrosion

Very good resistance in the atmosphere, fresh water, food products and many chemicals, owing to the stable oxide film on high-purity aluminium.

Applications

Where 1100 aluminium rod is used

  • Cold-headed parts: non-structural rivets, pins, studs and blanks made from O-temper rod
  • Electrical: connectors, terminals, grounding parts and conductors where moderate conductivity and good formability are needed
  • Chemical and food equipment: fittings, agitator parts and components in contact with food or mild chemicals
  • Heat transfer: fin and header components, heat-sink pins and parts for brazed assemblies
  • Spun and pressed parts: reflectors, lamp parts and decorative trim from forged or upset rod slugs
  • Signage and architecture: decorative turned parts and anodised accents

FAQ

1100 aluminium (aluminum) rod: frequently asked questions

Is 1100 aluminium rod pure aluminium?

It is commercially pure: at least 99.0 % aluminium, with 0.05 – 0.20 % copper added and limits on iron, silicon and other impurities. For higher purity, 1050A (99.5 %) or 1070 / 1350 electrical grades are used.

Can 1100 rod be heat treated to make it stronger?

No. 1100 is non-heat-treatable. Strength is raised only by cold work (H12 to H18). Heating it softens the bar back towards O temper.

What is the difference between 1100-O and 1100-H14 rod?

O is fully annealed, with about 90 MPa tensile strength and very high elongation, ideal for heading and bending. H14 is half-hard, at about 125 MPa, stiffer and easier to machine, but less formable.

Is 1100 good for electrical use?

Yes for connectors, terminals and grounding parts, with conductivity around 57 – 59 % IACS. For busbars and conductors where maximum conductivity is specified, 1350 or 6101 are the usual grades.

How much does 1100 aluminium rod weigh?

A 1 m length of 25 mm diameter rod weighs about 1.33 kg, and 50 mm rod about 5.32 kg, based on a density of 2.71 g/cm³.

Do you supply 1100 rod internationally?

Yes. Metline ships 1100 rod and bar worldwide from Mumbai with mill test certificates, export packing and EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms.
