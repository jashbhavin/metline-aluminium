Overview

What is 2024 aluminium bar?

2024 was introduced in the early 1930s as a stronger successor to 2017, by raising the magnesium content to about 1.5 %. It quickly became the standard aircraft alloy and is still specified wherever high strength must be combined with good fatigue performance.

The alloy gets its strength from natural ageing: after solution heat treatment and quenching, copper and magnesium form fine clusters and precipitates at room temperature. Bar is normally supplied in T4 (naturally aged) or T351 (also stretched to relieve stress). In these tempers 2024 offers a yield strength of around 325 MPa and a tensile strength around 470 MPa, plus a combination of fatigue resistance and fracture toughness that 7075 does not match.

Bar is the starting stock for many machined parts: aircraft fittings, shafts, gears, fasteners, hydraulic pistons and high-stress components in motorsport and defence. Because 2024 has limited corrosion resistance and is not suited to fusion welding, parts are protected by anodising or conversion coating and joined mechanically.

Why 2024 bar is chosen

  • High strength combined with excellent fatigue resistance
  • Good machinability in T4, T351 and T851 tempers
  • Covered by ASTM, AMS, federal and EN specifications for aerospace traceability
  • Better damage tolerance than 7075 for cyclically loaded parts

Tempers

Which 2024 bar temper should you order?

2024-T4

Solution heat-treated and naturally aged. Standard for cold-finished rod and wire, including rivet and screw stock.

2024-T351

Solution treated, stretched to relieve stress and naturally aged. The usual temper for rolled bar and the best choice for heavily machined parts.

2024-T3511 / T3510

Stress-relieved tempers for extruded bar and shapes. T3511 permits minor straightening after stretching.

2024-T851 / T8511

Stretched and artificially aged. Higher yield strength, lower elongation and better resistance to stress-corrosion cracking; suited to service at moderately elevated temperature.

2024-T6 / T62

Artificially aged without stretching, or heat treated by the user from O temper.

2024-O

Annealed for cold forming and heading before heat treatment.

Working with 2024

Machining, joining, forming and corrosion protection

Machining

Machines well in T4, T351 and T851, producing manageable chips and a good finish. Use carbide tools, positive rake and flood coolant; T351 holds dimensions on asymmetric parts.

Welding

Not suitable for conventional arc welding because of hot cracking and loss of strength. Friction-stir welding and mechanical fastening are used instead.

Forming

Form in O or freshly quenched W condition, then age. T4 can take moderate bends with generous radii.

Corrosion

Lower resistance than 5xxx and 6xxx alloys. Anodise (sulphuric or boric-sulphuric), conversion-coat or prime and paint. T8 tempers resist stress-corrosion cracking better than T3 / T4.

Heat treatment

Solution treat at about 495 °C, quench, then age naturally (T4) or artificially at around 190 °C (T8). Follow AMS 2770 for aerospace parts.

Temperature

Keeps useful strength up to about 120 – 150 °C in T4; T8 tempers are preferred for longer exposure at elevated temperature.

Applications

Where 2024 bar is used

  • Aircraft: fittings, hinges, actuator parts, bolts, rivets and machined structural components
  • Defence: missile parts, ordnance components and military vehicle fittings
  • Motorsport and automotive: suspension and drivetrain parts, wheel hubs and high-load brackets
  • Hydraulics: pistons, valve parts and high-pressure fittings
  • Fasteners: screws, bolts, nuts and rivets made from T4 rod and wire
  • Machinery: gears, shafts, couplings and orthopaedic or instrument components

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 2024-T4 and 2024-T351 bar?

Both are solution treated and naturally aged with similar strength. T351 bar is also stretched about 1 – 3 % to relieve quench stresses, so it moves less during machining.

Is 2024 stronger than 6061?

Yes. 2024-T351 has about 469 MPa tensile and 324 MPa yield strength, against about 310 and 276 MPa for 6061-T6, and a much higher fatigue strength. 6061 is easier to weld and more corrosion resistant.

Can 2024 aluminium bar be welded?

Not by conventional MIG or TIG welding; the alloy is prone to hot cracking and loses strength. Friction-stir welding and mechanical fastening are the usual joining methods.

Does 2024 bar need corrosion protection?

Generally yes. Its copper content lowers corrosion resistance, so parts are anodised, conversion-coated or painted, particularly in marine or humid conditions.

What is the European equivalent of 2024?

EN AW-2024 (AlCu4Mg1, Werkstoff 3.1355). The Chinese grade 2A12 is similar but not identical.

Do you supply 2024 bar with aerospace certification?

Yes, 2024 bar can be supplied certified to AMS 4120 or AMS-QQ-A-225/6 with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate; ultrasonic test reports can be added for aerospace orders.
