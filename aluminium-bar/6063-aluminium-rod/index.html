6063 Aluminium Rod & Round Bar

6063 round bar is the rod to choose when surface finish, anodising quality and thermal or electrical conductivity matter more than raw strength. It is softer than 6061 (typical yield about 214 MPa in T6) but extrudes with a smoother skin and takes a cleaner, more even anodised colour. Metline supplies 6063 rod in T5, T52 and T6 from small diameters to about 150 mm, cut to length and certified, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is 6063 aluminium rod?

6063 is the extrusion alloy of the 6xxx family. Its lower magnesium and silicon content (about 0.45 – 0.9 % Mg and 0.2 – 0.6 % Si) lets it be pushed through a die fast and at lower pressure, and it can be quenched by air at the press exit. The result is a rod with a smooth, uniform surface, very few die lines and a fine grain that anodises evenly in clear, bronze or black.

The trade-off is strength. 6063-T6 has a specified minimum tensile strength of 207 MPa (30 ksi) under ASTM B221, against 260 MPa for extruded 6061-T6. For railings, balusters, furniture, handles, trim, lighting parts and electrical or thermal components that is usually enough, and the better finish saves polishing and rework.

Rod in 6063 is almost always extruded rather than drawn. If you need tight diameter tolerance or a bright drawn surface, ask for cold-finished or centreless-ground rod, or consider 6061 cold-finished bar for small precision parts.

Pick 6063 rod when

  • The rod is visible and will be anodised, painted or polished
  • Higher thermal or electrical conductivity than 6061 is useful
  • Loads are moderate and stiffness, not yield strength, governs the design
  • You want to match the alloy of 6063 profiles in the same assembly

T5 and T6 look identical on the rack, so state the temper clearly on the order and check it on the certificate. If a rod will be bent tightly or swaged, buy O or T4 and age it afterwards; bending T6 rod on a small radius risks cracking on the outside of the bend.

Tolerances and inspection

Tolerances and inspection

Diameter, ovality and straightness tolerances for extruded round bar follow ANSI H35.2 on ASTM orders and EN 755-3 on EN orders. Extruded tolerances widen with diameter, so if your part uses the as-supplied diameter as a fit, specify a ground or turned bar with a fit class such as h9.

Every lot is checked for diameter, straightness, length and surface condition, and for decorative rod we also look for die lines, pick-up and handling marks under good lighting. The EN 10204 3.1 certificate lists chemical analysis and tensile results against the ordered standard.

  • Diameter and ovality measured at several points along the bar
  • Straightness checked on a flat table or by roll test
  • Surface inspected for die lines, scratches and corrosion staining
  • Hardness or conductivity checks to confirm temper on request
Fabrication

Machining, bending, welding and finishing

Machining

6063 is softer than 6061 and tends to give longer, gummier chips, especially in T5. Use sharp, high-rake polished tools, high speed and good lubrication; T6 machines noticeably better than T5.

Bending

Bend in T4 or O for tight radii, then age. T5 and T6 rod bends on generous radii; use a mandrel or formed die to avoid flattening.

Welding

Readily TIG or MIG welded with ER4043 or ER5356 filler. Expect the heat-affected zone to lose much of its T6 strength.

Anodising

The main reason to choose 6063. It gives clear, uniform films and consistent colour; machine and polish before anodising.

Polishing

Mechanical and chemical brightening work well because of the fine grain and low iron.

Joining

Threads, cross-drilling and adhesive bonding are all practical; use thread inserts for repeated assembly.

Applications

Applications of 6063 rod

  • Architecture: balusters, handrail posts, glazing pins, door and window hardware, curtain rods
  • Furniture and interiors: chair and table legs, shelf supports, handles and display fittings
  • Lighting: lamp columns, fixture stems and heat-sink pins
  • Electrical: earthing and bus rods where good conductivity and moderate strength suit
  • Thermal: pin-fin heat sinks and thermal blocks machined from rod
  • General engineering: lightly loaded spacers, rollers and pins where finish matters

FAQ

6063 aluminium (aluminum) rod: frequently asked questions

Is 6063 rod weaker than 6061?

Yes. 6063-T6 has a specified minimum tensile strength of 207 MPa and yield of 172 MPa under ASTM B221, against 260 / 240 MPa for extruded 6061-T6. In return it has a smoother extruded surface, better anodising response and higher conductivity.

What is the difference between 6063-T5 and T6 rod?

T5 is cooled at the press and then aged; T6 is fully solution treated, quenched and aged. T6 is roughly a third stronger. T5 is cheaper and fine for decorative and lightly loaded parts.

What does T52 mean on 6063 bar?

T52 is cooled from the extrusion temperature, stress-relieved by a small amount of compression and then artificially aged. ASTM B221 gives it both minimum and maximum strength limits, so properties fall in a controlled band between T5 and T6.

How much does 6063 round bar weigh?

Weight per metre is about 0.002113 × d² kg with d in mm. A 20 mm rod weighs about 0.85 kg/m, a 50 mm rod about 5.28 kg/m and a 100 mm rod about 21.1 kg/m.

Can 6063 rod be anodised in colour?

Yes. 6063 is the standard alloy for decorative anodising and gives even clear, bronze, black and dyed finishes. Machine and polish before anodising, and order rod from one lot if colour match matters.

Is 6063 rod good for machining?

It machines acceptably, but it is softer than 6061 and can give long chips in T5. For heavily machined or precision parts, 6061-T6 or 2011 is easier; choose 6063 when finish or conductivity is the priority.
