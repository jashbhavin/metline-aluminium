2219 Aluminium Bar

2219 aluminium bar is a high-copper (about 6.3 %) heat-treatable alloy that keeps useful strength from cryogenic temperatures up to around 300 °C and, unusually for a 2xxx alloy, can be fusion welded. It is used for rocket propellant tanks, aerospace structures, high-temperature fittings and welded assemblies. Metline supplies 2219 round, square and flat bar in T351, T851 and related tempers, with full mill certification, to customers worldwide.

Overview

What is 2219 aluminium bar?

2219 was developed in the 1950s for parts that must stay strong when hot and survive welding. Its high copper content, combined with small additions of manganese, zirconium, vanadium and titanium, gives a fine, stable microstructure that resists softening at temperature and tolerates the heat of a weld.

Most high-strength 2xxx alloys, such as 2024 and 2014, crack when fusion welded. 2219 does not: with 2319 filler wire it can be TIG or MIG welded and then re-aged, which is why it was chosen for large cryogenic propellant tanks on launch vehicles. Its strength actually rises at cryogenic temperatures while toughness remains good, making it suitable for liquid oxygen and liquid hydrogen service.

At the other end of the range, 2219 in the artificially aged T8 tempers has among the best creep and elevated-temperature properties of commercial aluminium alloys, retaining useful strength up to around 300 °C. Bar in 2219 is used for machined fittings, supersonic airframe fittings, engine components and welded structures that see heat.

Where 2219 stands out

  • Fusion weldable with 2319 filler, unlike most 2xxx alloys
  • Strength and toughness retained at cryogenic temperatures
  • Good creep resistance and strength up to about 300 °C
  • High resistance to stress-corrosion cracking in T8 tempers

Tempers

Which 2219 temper should you order?

2219-T351

Solution treated, stretched and naturally aged. Moderate strength and good toughness; often bought as an intermediate temper and aged to T851 after machining or welding.

2219-T851

Solution treated, stretched and artificially aged. The main temper for rolled bar used in high-temperature or stress-corrosion-critical parts.

2219-T8511

Equivalent for extruded bar, with minor straightening allowed after stretching (AMS 4162).

2219-T62

Heat treated from O or F temper by the user and artificially aged; common for welded and re-aged assemblies.

2219-T87

More cold work before ageing; the highest strength temper, mainly for sheet and plate.

2219-O / F

Annealed or as-fabricated stock for forming, forging or later heat treatment.

Working with 2219

Welding, machining and heat treatment

Welding

TIG, MIG, electron-beam and friction-stir welding are all used. ER2319 filler is standard. Weld joint efficiency is typically well below parent strength unless the assembly is re-solution treated and aged.

Machining

Machines well in T8 tempers with short chips and good finish. Use carbide tools and coolant; T851 / T8511 limit distortion.

Heat treatment

Solution treat at about 535 °C, quench, then age at about 175 – 190 °C for T6 / T8 tempers. Follow AMS 2770 for aerospace parts.

High temperature

Retains strength and resists creep better than most aluminium alloys up to around 300 °C in T8 tempers.

Cryogenic service

Strength and toughness increase at low temperature, making 2219 suitable for liquid oxygen and hydrogen tanks.

Corrosion

Fair, typical of high-copper alloys; T8 tempers resist stress-corrosion cracking well. Protect by anodising or coating.

Applications

Where 2219 bar is used

  • Space launch: welded cryogenic propellant tank parts, rings, flanges and fittings
  • Aerospace: high-temperature structural fittings and supersonic airframe components
  • Engines: components that see sustained heat, such as compressor and mounting parts
  • Defence: welded armour and ordnance components
  • Cryogenic equipment: LNG and industrial gas fittings where toughness at low temperature matters
  • Welded structures: high-strength aluminium assemblies that must be fusion welded

FAQ

2219 aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

Can 2219 aluminium be welded?

Yes. 2219 is one of the few 2xxx alloys that can be fusion welded. TIG or MIG with ER2319 filler is standard, and strength can be partly restored by re-ageing or fully by re-solution treatment and ageing.

What temperature can 2219 withstand?

In T8 tempers it keeps useful strength and creep resistance up to about 300 °C, and it performs well down to cryogenic temperatures such as liquid hydrogen service.

What is the difference between 2219-T851 and T8511?

Both are solution treated, stretched and artificially aged. T851 applies to rolled bar and plate; T8511 applies to extruded bar and shapes and allows minor straightening after stretching.

Why is 2219 used for rocket fuel tanks?

It combines weldability, high strength and good toughness at cryogenic temperatures, and resists stress-corrosion cracking in T8 tempers.

Is 2219 stronger than 6061?

Yes. 2219-T851 typically reaches about 455 MPa tensile strength, compared with about 310 MPa for 6061-T6, and holds strength much better at elevated temperature.

Do you supply 2219 with aerospace certification?

Yes. 2219 bar and extrusions can be supplied certified to the relevant AMS specification with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate and ultrasonic test reports on request.
