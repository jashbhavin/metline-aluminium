Overview

What is 6110A aluminium bar?

6110A is a European-registered 6xxx alloy designed for parts that need more strength than 6082-T6 without moving to the cost and corrosion sensitivity of 2xxx or 7xxx grades. Higher magnesium, silicon and copper levels raise the amount of strengthening precipitate formed during ageing, while manganese, chromium and zirconium hold a fine, unrecrystallised grain structure in the extruded bar.

That grain control matters for bar. Many 6xxx alloys form a ring of coarse, recrystallised grains near the surface of an extruded rod (the peripheral coarse grain, or PCG, layer). PCG lowers fatigue strength and can cause orange-peel or cracking when the bar is later forged. 6110A, with its dispersoid-forming additions and careful extrusion practice, can be produced with a very thin PCG layer, which is why buyers of forging stock often specify a maximum coarse-grain ring depth, typically 0.5 mm or less, on the order.

The Aluminum Association registers a closely related alloy, 6110 (UNS A96110). The European variant 6110A has somewhat different limits, including a tighter iron maximum and an optional zirconium addition, so the two should not be treated as interchangeable without checking the governing specification.

Why engineers specify 6110A

  • T6 strength well above 6061 and 6082, close to some 2xxx grades
  • Fine-grained extruded bar with a controlled coarse-grain ring, suited to forging
  • Better general corrosion resistance than copper-rich 2xxx alloys
  • Weldable and anodisable, unlike most high-strength 7xxx grades

Tempers

Choosing a 6110A temper

T6

Separate solution heat treatment, quench and artificial ageing. Gives the highest strength and is the usual condition for machined parts and finished components.

T5

Press-quenched straight from the extrusion press and then aged. Lower cost than T6 and adequate where the full T6 strength is not needed.

T4

Solution treated and naturally aged. Softer and more ductile; used when parts are bent or formed before a final ageing step.

F / forging stock

As-extruded or as-cast bar bought for hot forging. The forging is then solution treated and aged to T6, so bar temper matters less than grain structure and PCG depth.

T651 / T6511

Stress-relieved by stretching after quench where straightness and low residual stress are critical for machining. Confirm availability by size.

Over-aged (T7x)

Occasionally specified to improve resistance to intergranular or stress corrosion at some cost to strength.

Working with 6110A

Forging, machining, welding and finishing

Hot forging

6110A forges well in the usual 6xxx temperature window (roughly 430 – 500 °C). A fine-grained, low-PCG bar reduces the risk of abnormal grain growth during solution treatment of the forging.

Machining

In T6 it machines cleanly with short chips, comparable to 6061-T6. Use sharp carbide tools with positive rake and flood coolant.

Welding

MIG and TIG weldable, typically with 4043 or 5356 filler. As with all 6xxx alloys, the heat-affected zone loses much of its T6 strength unless the part is re-heat-treated.

Heat treatment

Solution treat, quench rapidly and age artificially. Quench rate is important because the higher solute content makes 6110A more quench-sensitive than 6060 or 6063.

Corrosion

Good atmospheric resistance. Copper makes it slightly more sensitive to intergranular attack than copper-free 6xxx grades; painting, e-coating or anodising is normal for exposed chassis parts.

Surface finishing

Takes anodic and conversion coatings, e-coat and powder coat. Surface appearance after anodising is functional rather than decorative.

Applications

Where 6110A bar is used

  • Automotive chassis: forged control arms, knuckles, links and suspension brackets
  • Powertrain and mounts: engine and gearbox mounts, brackets and housings machined from bar
  • Body structure: high-strength crash and structural members where extrusion is followed by machining
  • Commercial vehicles: fittings, couplings and brake components
  • Machinery: shafts, conveyor parts and frames needing more strength than 6082
  • Sports and leisure: bicycle and motorcycle components machined or forged from bar

FAQ

6110A aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

Is 6110A the same as 6110?

No. They are closely related Al-Mg-Si-Cu alloys, but 6110 is registered by the Aluminum Association and 6110A is the European variant with different limits, including a lower iron maximum and an optional zirconium addition. Substitute only after checking both specifications.

How strong is 6110A-T6 compared with 6082-T6?

Typical T6 values for 6110A bar are roughly 400 – 470 MPa tensile and 360 – 430 MPa yield, against about 310 – 340 MPa and 260 – 300 MPa for 6082-T6. Design to the specified minimums for your size, not to typical values.

What is a coarse-grain ring and why does it matter?

It is a layer of large recrystallised grains near the surface of extruded bar. It lowers fatigue strength and can crack or roughen during forging, so forging-stock orders usually limit its depth, often to 0.5 mm or less.

Can 6110A be welded?

Yes, by MIG or TIG with 4043 or 5356 filler. The heat-affected zone softens significantly, so welded designs either use reduced allowable stresses or re-heat-treat the assembly.

Which standard covers 6110A bar?

Chemistry is in EN 573-3; extruded bar properties and tolerances are in EN 755-2 and EN 755-3; drawn bar is covered by EN 754. Many automotive buyers add their own specification on top.

Do you export 6110A bar?

Yes. Metline supplies 6110A bar worldwide from Mumbai with EN 10204 3.1 certificates, cut-to-length service and export packing.
