Overview

What makes 6061 round bar so widely used?

A turned part starts as a length of round bar, so the bar's diameter tolerance, straightness, internal stress and machinability decide how much work and scrap the part costs. 6061 performs well on all four: it is available in very tight cold-finished tolerances, it is straightened and often stress-relieved by stretching, and in T6 it cuts with short chips and a bright finish.

It is also a material that survives real service. 6061-T6 has a typical tensile strength of about 310 MPa, good resistance to atmospheric corrosion, and it anodises evenly, which matters for visible parts such as knobs, spacers, rollers and camera hardware. Free-machining alloys like 2011 cut faster, but they corrode more readily and cannot be welded; 7075 is stronger but costs more. 6061 sits in the middle and covers most jobs.

Round bar is produced three ways, and choosing the right one is the most useful decision a buyer can make: extruded bar for general machining and large diameters, cold-finished (drawn) bar for close-tolerance small diameters, and turned, peeled or precision-ground bar when the outside diameter is used as finished.

Choosing quickly

  • Under about 50 mm and the OD is a finished surface: cold-finished T6
  • Heavily machined or bored parts: extruded T6511 for low distortion
  • Shafts and guide rods: precision-ground bar for diameter and straightness
  • Forming, swaging or heading: T4 or O, aged to T6 afterwards

Machining

Turning and machining 6061 rod

Cutting speed

Aluminium tolerates very high surface speeds; with carbide, several hundred metres per minute is normal. Machine rigidity and chip evacuation are usually the limit, not the tool.

Tool geometry

Use sharp, polished inserts with high positive rake and a large relief angle to prevent built-up edge and smearing.

Chip control

T6 gives short, curled chips; O and T4 produce long stringy chips that need chip breakers.

Distortion

For long slender parts or deep bores, use T6511 or T651 bar and rough-machine before finishing to release remaining stress.

Coolant

Water-soluble coolant with good lubricity improves finish and tool life; mist or MQL works for light cuts.

Finishing

Anodise after machining. Hard anodising adds wear resistance for shafts and pistons; allow for coating growth on fits.

Applications

Applications of 6061 round bar

  • Shafts and rollers: conveyor rollers, idler shafts, light drive shafts and guide rods
  • Turned components: bushings, spacers, knobs, pulleys, pistons for pneumatic cylinders
  • Fasteners: pins, rivets, clevis pins and threaded studs
  • Optics and electronics: lens barrels, sensor housings and heat-sink pins
  • Transport and marine: fittings, hinge pins and brackets (anodised for marine use)
  • Sports and leisure: bicycle axles and components, fishing reel parts, climbing hardware

FAQ

6061 aluminium (aluminum) round bar: frequently asked questions

What is the weight of a 6061 aluminium round bar?

Weight per metre is 0.002121 × d² kg with d in mm. A 20 mm bar weighs about 0.85 kg/m, a 50 mm bar 5.30 kg/m and a 100 mm bar 21.2 kg/m. In inch units, a 1 in bar weighs about 0.92 lb/ft.

Should I buy cold-finished or extruded 6061 rod?

Cold-finished rod has a tighter diameter tolerance and brighter surface and suits parts where the bar OD is used as-is. Extruded rod is cheaper, available in larger diameters and, in T6511, stress-relieved for heavy machining.

Is 6061-T6 round bar strong enough for shafts?

For light and medium duty, yes: typical yield is about 276 MPa and fatigue strength about 96 MPa at 5×10⁸ cycles. For heavily loaded or high-cycle shafts, steel or 7075 may be needed; check deflection too, as aluminium is about one-third as stiff as steel.

Can 6061 round bar be anodised?

Yes. 6061 gives an even clear or coloured anodised finish and accepts hard anodising for wear resistance. Allow for coating build-up on toleranced diameters.

What is the largest 6061 round bar available?

Extruded bar is commonly available up to about 300 mm. Larger diameters are supplied as forged rounds or cut from forged blocks, with properties agreed per order.

Do you supply precision-ground 6061 rod?

Yes, on request. Tell us the diameter, the fit class or tolerance (for example h9) and the straightness you need, and we will confirm what can be supplied.
