2024 Aluminium Round Bar

2024 aluminium round bar is a high-strength, fatigue-resistant rod used for shafts, fasteners, fittings and machined aerospace parts. It is supplied cold-finished in small diameters, extruded or rolled in larger ones, most often in the stress-relieved T351 temper. Metline supplies 2024 round bar in metric and inch diameters, cut to length and certified to ASTM, AMS or EN, to buyers worldwide.

Overview

2024 round bar at a glance

Round bar is the most common way 2024 is bought for machining. Turned shafts, pins, bolts, bushings, hydraulic pistons and aircraft fittings all start as 2024 rod, because the alloy combines high strength with the best fatigue resistance of the common high-strength aluminium grades.

Unlike 6061 round bar, which is almost always extruded and artificially aged, 2024 rod is used mostly in the naturally aged T4 or T351 condition. Its strength comes from copper and magnesium precipitating at room temperature after quenching. For round bar that will be turned, the stress-relieved T351 temper (rolled bar) or T3511 (extruded bar) is preferred because stretching removes most of the quench stress that would otherwise make long parts bow during machining.

This page focuses on round bar: production routes, diameters, weights and tolerances. For a general overview of the alloy in all bar shapes, see our 2024 bar and rod page.

The route matters because tolerances and surface quality differ. Cold-finished rod has the tightest diameter tolerance and a bright surface; extruded bar has wider tolerances and may need a skim cut; rolled bar can show a slightly coarser surface layer. Tell us how the bar will be processed so we can recommend the most economical route.

Machining & finishing

Turning and finishing 2024 round bar

Turning

Use sharp carbide inserts with a positive rake and chip breaker. 2024 gives longer chips than 2011, so chip control matters on automatic lathes.

Drilling and threading

Drills and taps well; use cutting fluid to avoid built-up edge. Roll threading works well in T4.

Distortion control

Use T351 or T3511 and take balanced cuts on long slender parts; leave a finishing allowance for straightening if needed.

Surface protection

Anodise or apply conversion coating after machining. Hard anodising increases wear resistance on shafts and pistons.

Joining

Not suited to arc welding. Use threads, pins, interference fits or adhesive bonding.

Shot peening

Commonly used on fatigue-critical 2024 parts to add compressive surface stress.

Applications

Typical uses of 2024 round bar

  • Aerospace: bolts, pins, actuator rods, hinge fittings and turned structural parts
  • Fasteners: screws, rivets and studs from T4 rod
  • Hydraulics: pistons, rods and valve components
  • Automotive and motorsport: drive shafts, suspension and steering components
  • Machinery: gears, spindles, couplings and rollers
  • Medical and instruments: orthopaedic fixture parts and precision instrument components

FAQ

2024 aluminium (aluminum) round bar: frequently asked questions

Which temper of 2024 round bar is best for machining?

T351 for rolled bar or T3511 for extruded bar. Both are stretched to relieve quench stress, so long turned parts stay straighter. T4 is fine for small cold-finished rod.

How much does 2024 round bar weigh?

At 2.78 g/cm³, a 25 mm bar weighs about 1.36 kg/m and a 50 mm bar about 5.46 kg/m. In inch sizes, 1 in rod weighs about 0.94 lb/ft.

What diameters of 2024 round bar are available?

From small cold-drawn rod of a few millimetres up to large rolled or forged bar of several hundred millimetres. Common inch sizes run from 1/4 in to 8 in; confirm availability for your exact diameter and temper.

Is 2024 round bar suitable for shafts?

Yes. Its high fatigue strength makes it a good shaft material where weight matters; anodise or hard-anodise bearing surfaces to improve wear and corrosion resistance.

Can you cut 2024 round bar to length?

Yes. We supply random or fixed lengths and saw-cut pieces to your dimensions, with end marking for traceability.

What is the difference between 2024 and 2024A?

Not much in practice. In Europe, aerospace specifications sometimes use variants with tighter iron and silicon limits for better toughness. Follow the designation and specification on your drawing.
