6082 Aluminium Round Bar & Rod

6082-T6 round bar is the standard aluminium rod for turned parts, shafts, pins and structural fittings wherever drawings follow European or Indian standards. It combines the highest strength of the common 6xxx alloys with good machining and corrosion resistance. Metline supplies 6082 rod from about 6 to 300 mm diameter, extruded, drawn, peeled or ground, cut to length and certified to EN 755-2, worldwide.

Overview

What 6082 round bar is used for

A turned component is only as good as the rod it starts from. For 6082 round bar the things that decide cost and quality are the diameter tolerance (how much stock you must remove), straightness (whether the bar runs true in a bar feeder), internal stress (whether a bored part stays round) and the strength that is actually reached at the bar centre.

6082-T6 performs well on all of these. It reaches a minimum tensile strength of 310 MPa and proof stress of 260 MPa between 20 and 150 mm diameter under EN 755-2, it machines with short broken chips in T6, and stretched T6511 bar keeps residual stress low for parts that are heavily bored or slotted.

Round bar is the most common 6082 product after profiles. Typical parts include shafts and rollers for conveyors and packaging machines, hydraulic and pneumatic components, couplings, pulleys, pins and bolts, and machined structural nodes for trusses and towers.

Quick selection

  • General turning and milling: extruded 6082-T6 or T6511
  • Small diameters with a finished OD: cold-drawn T6 to EN 754-2
  • Bar-fed lathes and shafts: peeled or ground bar for size and straightness
  • Diameters over 300 mm: forged or cast-and-turned bar by enquiry

For round bar the size band is the diameter. Typical T6 rod of 20 – 100 mm usually tests at about 320 – 340 MPa tensile and 280 – 310 MPa proof stress, with hardness around 90 – 100 HB. Extruded tolerances widen as diameter increases, and ovality is limited to the same band. If the bar's outside diameter becomes a finished surface or a fit, specify drawn, peeled or ground bar with the fit class on the order. For bar feeders, also state straightness, for example in millimetres per metre, as extruded straightness can be too loose for high-speed feeding.

Tolerances and inspection

Inspection and certification

Each lot is checked for diameter and ovality at several points, straightness, length and surface condition before dispatch. Ultrasonic testing to EN 12680-2 or ASTM B594 can be arranged for large-diameter bar used in critical parts.

The EN 10204 3.1 certificate shows the chemical analysis and tensile test results (Rm, Rp0.2, A) against EN 755-2, plus the heat or lot number marked on the bar. Third-party inspection can be arranged at your cost.

  • Diameter, ovality and straightness to EN 755-3, EN 754-3 or your drawing
  • Chemistry and tensile results on the 3.1 certificate
  • Hardness or conductivity check to confirm temper on request
  • Ultrasonic inspection for large bar by agreement
Machining

Turning and machining 6082 rod

Speeds

Aluminium allows very high surface speeds with carbide; rigidity, clamping and chip evacuation are usually the limits.

Tooling

Sharp, polished, high-positive-rake inserts reduce built-up edge. Uncoated or DLC-coated carbide works well.

Chips

T6 forms short curled chips. O and T4 are gummy and produce long chips; avoid them for heavy machining.

Distortion

Use T6511 for long, slender or deeply bored parts. Rough, then finish after the part has relaxed.

Threads

Thread rolling and form tapping give strong threads in T6. Use inserts for threads that will be reassembled often.

Finishing

Anodise after machining for protection; hard anodising adds wear resistance on shafts. Allow for film growth on fits.

Applications

Applications of 6082 round bar

  • Machinery: conveyor rollers and shafts, packaging and printing machine parts, pulleys and couplings
  • Fluid power: cylinder end caps, pistons, valve bodies and fittings
  • Structures: truss connectors, scaffold spigots, pins and machined nodes
  • Transport: machined fittings for rail, truck, trailer and bus bodies
  • Marine: deck hardware, stanchion bases and fittings (anodised)
  • Forging stock: rod cut to slugs for closed-die forgings such as suspension arms

FAQ

6082 aluminium (aluminum) round bar: frequently asked questions

What is the strength of 6082-T6 round bar?

Under EN 755-2, extruded 6082-T6 round bar from 20 to 150 mm diameter has a minimum tensile strength of 310 MPa, proof stress of 260 MPa and elongation of 8 %. Bars up to 20 mm have 295 / 250 MPa, and bars over 150 mm have lower minimums.

How much does 6082 round bar weigh?

About 0.002121 × d² kg per metre with d in mm. A 25 mm bar is about 1.33 kg/m, a 50 mm bar 5.30 kg/m and a 100 mm bar 21.2 kg/m.

Is 6082 round bar good for shafts?

Yes, for light and medium-duty shafts, rollers and guide rods. Order ground bar for a fit class and good straightness, and consider hard anodising for wear surfaces. Heavily loaded shafts may need 7075 or steel.

What is the difference between 6082-T6 and T6511 round bar?

Both have the same minimum properties. T6511 bar is stretched after quenching to relieve residual stress, so it stays straighter and rounder when bored or slotted.

Can you supply 6082 round bar over 300 mm diameter?

Larger diameters are normally produced as forged or cast-and-turned bar rather than extrusions. Send your diameter, length and property requirements and we will confirm what can be supplied.

Can 6082 rod be welded?

Yes, by MIG or TIG with ER5356 or ER4043 filler. The heat-affected zone loses a large part of its T6 strength, which must be allowed for in design.
