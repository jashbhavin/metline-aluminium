7A04 T6 Aluminium Rod and Bar

7A04 is the Chinese GB designation for an Al-Zn-Mg-Cu "super-hard" alloy, formerly called LC4 and closely related to the Russian alloy V95. In the T6 condition, extruded 7A04 bar reaches strengths at or above 7075-T6, which makes it the standard high-strength bar on drawings that follow GB or GOST practice. Metline supplies 7A04 round, square and hexagonal bar in T6 to GB/T 3191, worldwide.

Overview

What is 7A04 aluminium?

7A04 is a 7xxx-series alloy that combines 5 – 7 % zinc with magnesium and copper. Ageing produces a dense population of fine MgZn2-type precipitates, which gives it some of the highest strengths available in wrought aluminium. In Chinese and former-Soviet engineering practice it fills the role 7075 plays elsewhere: highly loaded aircraft structure, landing-gear parts, fittings and high-strength machined components.

Compared with 7075, 7A04 allows a wider zinc range, adds manganese (0.20 – 0.60 %) and uses a lower chromium range. In practice the two are close in strength and behaviour, but they are separate alloys under separate standards. If a drawing specifies 7A04 under GB/T 3191, substituting 7075 to ASTM B221 (or the reverse) needs the approval of the design authority.

Like other peak-aged 7xxx alloys, 7A04-T6 has limited ductility, is notch-sensitive and is susceptible to stress-corrosion cracking when loaded in the short-transverse direction in a moist environment. Good detail design, generous radii and protective finishing are part of using it well.

Key characteristics of 7A04-T6

  • Very high tensile and yield strength for its weight
  • Good machinability after heat treatment; poor in the annealed state
  • Spot welding is possible; gas and conventional arc welding are not recommended
  • Needs corrosion protection (anodising, primer) in service

Working with 7A04

Machining, heat treatment and finishing

Machining

Machines well in T6 with short chips and a good finish. Avoid machining in the annealed state, where it is gummy. Leave allowance for distortion on long slender parts.

Heat treatment

Solution treat at about 465 – 475 °C, quench rapidly in water, then age artificially (for example around 120 – 140 °C). Follow the governing standard for exact cycles.

Joining

Spot welding is practical; fusion welding is not recommended because of hot cracking and strength loss. Use riveting, bolting or bonding.

Corrosion protection

Sulphuric or chromic anodising and primer are standard for aircraft parts. Avoid sustained short-transverse tensile stress in moist environments.

Notch sensitivity

Use generous radii, avoid sharp re-entrant corners and keep surface finish fine on fatigue-loaded parts.

Inspection

Ultrasonic testing of larger bars and conductivity or hardness checks are common acceptance tests.

Applications

Where 7A04 bar is used

  • Aircraft structure: frames, stringers, spar caps and heavily loaded fittings
  • Landing gear and actuators: machined brackets, links and housings
  • Fasteners and rivets: bar and wire for high-strength fasteners
  • Propeller and rotor components: hub parts and fittings
  • Heavy vehicles: wheel hubs and high-load brackets
  • Defence and tooling: high-strength mounts, jigs and dies

FAQ

7A04 aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

What is the equivalent of 7A04?

Its closest relatives are the Russian alloy V95 (В95) and, internationally, 7075. They are similar in strength but have different composition limits, so substitution needs approval.

What strength does 7A04-T6 bar have?

GB/T 3191 commonly specifies at least about 530 MPa tensile, 400 MPa yield and 5 % elongation for extruded T6 bar in the main size range. Limits vary with diameter.

Can 7A04 be welded?

Spot welding works; gas and arc welding are not recommended because the alloy cracks and loses strength in the weld zone.

What does "super-hard aluminium" mean?

It is a Chinese term for the high-zinc 7xxx alloys such as 7A04 and 7A09, which reach the highest strengths among wrought aluminium alloys.

Which shapes can you supply?

Round bar is most common; square and hexagonal bar and other sections can be supplied to order.

Do you ship 7A04 bar outside India?

Yes. Metline supplies 7A04 bar worldwide with EN 10204 3.1 certificates and export packing.
