Overview

What is 6061 aluminium bar?

6061 is a heat-treatable aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloy with small additions of copper and chromium. In the T6 condition it reaches a typical yield strength of about 276 MPa, roughly twice that of annealed 5083, while still machining cleanly, welding with standard procedures and resisting atmospheric corrosion well. That balance is why 6061 bar is stocked by almost every metals distributor in the world.

"Bar" covers several solid shapes: round rod, square, hexagon, flat and rectangular bar. Each comes from one of two production routes. Extruded bar is pushed hot through a die and then heat-treated; it is economical and available in large sizes. Cold-finished bar is extruded and then drawn through a second die; it has tighter tolerances, sharper corners on square and hex, a brighter surface and slightly higher guaranteed strength.

The right choice depends on what you will do with the bar. For heavy machining, order a stress-relieved temper (T651 or T6511) so parts stay straight. For screw-machine work on small diameters, cold-finished T6 with its close tolerance saves a turning pass. For welded frames and fabrications, extruded T6 is usually the most economical.

6061 bar in brief

  • Typical T6 strength: 310 MPa tensile, 276 MPa yield, about 95 HB
  • Machines well with short chips; anodises to a clean, even finish
  • Welds by MIG and TIG (the weld zone softens to roughly T4 strength)
  • Covered by ASTM B211, ASTM B221, AMS 4117, AMS 4150, AMS 4173 and EN 755-2 / 754-2

Tempers

6061 bar tempers: T6, T651, T6511, T4 and O

T6

Solution heat-treated and artificially aged, without stress relief. Standard for cold-finished rod and for bar that will not be heavily machined.

T651

T6 plus stretching (1 – 3 %) to relieve quench stresses; applies to rolled or cold-finished bar. Stays straight when machined.

T6511

The extruded-bar version of T651: stretched and then lightly straightened. The usual temper for extruded bar that will be machined.

T6510

Stretched, with no further straightening. Less common; tolerance on straightness is set by stretching alone.

T4

Solution treated and naturally aged. Softer and more formable; can be aged to T6 after forming.

O

Fully annealed for forming, swaging and upsetting. Re-heat-treat to T6 after forming for strength.

Tolerances & quality

Tolerances and inspection

Cold-finished bar is held to closer cross-section tolerances than extruded bar. For example, under ANSI H35.2 a cold-finished round between 1 and 1.5 in typically has a diameter tolerance of a few thousandths of an inch, while an extruded round of the same size is allowed several times more. If your part is turned all over, extruded bar is usually fine; if the bar surface is a finished surface, order cold-finished.

Working with 6061

Machining, welding and finishing 6061 bar

Machining

Good machinability in T6 with short chips. Use sharp carbide, high positive rake, high spindle speed and flood coolant. For heavy material removal, order T651 or T6511 to limit distortion.

Welding

MIG and TIG with ER4043 or ER5356 filler. Design welded joints to reduced strength (about 165 MPa tensile for welded 6061-T6 under the Aluminum Design Manual) or re-heat-treat.

Anodising

Takes clear, colour and hard anodising well. For the best colour match, keep all parts of an assembly from the same temper and route.

Applications

Where 6061 bar is used

  • Machine building: shafts, rollers, spacers, clamp bodies, manifold blocks and fixture components
  • Transport: brackets, fittings and machined parts for vehicles, trailers and rail
  • Marine: deck fittings and hardware above the waterline, usually anodised
  • Electronics and optics: housings, heat-sink blocks and camera or sensor mounts
  • Fluid power: hydraulic and pneumatic manifolds, valve bodies and fittings
  • Sports and consumer goods: bicycle parts, climbing hardware and tool bodies

FAQ

6061 aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 6061-T6 and T6511 bar?

Both have the same heat treatment and strength. T6511 extruded bar is also stretched to relieve internal stress and then lightly straightened, so it moves less when machined. Order T6511 (or T651 for cold-finished bar) for parts with heavy or asymmetric machining.

Is cold-finished 6061 bar stronger than extruded bar?

Its guaranteed minimum tensile is higher: 290 MPa under ASTM B211 against 260 MPa under ASTM B221, with the same 240 MPa minimum yield. In practice typical properties are similar; the bigger difference is tolerance and surface finish.

How much does 6061 bar weigh?

Multiply the cross-section in mm² by 0.0027 to get kg per metre. A 25 mm round bar weighs about 1.33 kg/m, a 25 mm square bar 1.69 kg/m and a 25 mm hex bar 1.46 kg/m.

Can 6061 bar be welded?

Yes, by MIG or TIG with ER4043 or ER5356 filler. The heat-affected zone softens, so welded strength is lower than T6 unless the part is re-heat-treated.

Which standard should I put on my order for 6061 bar?

ASTM B211 for cold-finished bar, ASTM B221 for extruded bar, AMS 4117 or AMS 4173 for aerospace, and EN 755-2 or EN 754-2 for European projects. Give the temper and the tolerance class as well.

Do you supply 6061 bar outside India?

Yes. Metline supplies 6061 bar to customers worldwide on EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP terms, with mill test certificates and export packing.
