6082 T6 / T6511 Aluminium Bar & Rod

6082 is the highest-strength common 6xxx alloy for bar and the standard structural choice in Europe, where it largely replaced 6061. In T6 it gives a minimum proof stress of 260 MPa in mid-size bar under EN 755-2, with good corrosion resistance, weldability and machinability. Metline supplies 6082 round, square, flat and hex bar in T6, T6511, T4 and O, cut to length and certified, worldwide.

Overview

Why 6082 bar is the European structural standard

6082 contains more silicon and magnesium than 6061 and, importantly, 0.4 – 1.0 % manganese. Manganese keeps the grain fine during extrusion and heat treatment, which gives 6082 slightly higher strength than 6061 in most bar sizes and good toughness. Copper is held to 0.10 % maximum, so its general corrosion resistance is very good.

Because 6082 was the alloy specified in British and German structural codes (as HE30 and AlMgSi1), it became the default "engineering aluminium" across Europe, the Middle East, India and much of Asia. It is used for machined parts, structural members, bridge and crane components, and transport fittings, and it is listed in Eurocode 9 (EN 1999-1-1) for structural design.

In bar form, 6082 is mostly extruded. Large diameters, flats and squares come as extruded T6 or T6511, while small precision diameters can be supplied cold-drawn to EN 754-2 for closer tolerances and a brighter surface.

6082 bar at a glance

  • Strongest common 6xxx bar alloy; T6 minimum Rm 310 MPa from 20 to 150 mm
  • Good machining in T6, with short chips; avoid O and T4 for heavy machining
  • Weldable by MIG and TIG, but the heat-affected zone loses roughly half its strength
  • Anodises well for protection, less evenly than 6063 for decorative work

6082 is quench sensitive: if a thick bar is cooled too slowly after solution treatment, its core will not reach full T6 strength. For bars over about 150 mm, ask for certified properties at the size you are buying, or for test pieces taken from the bar centre when the part is highly loaded.

6082 and 6061 are close but not identical. 6061 has more copper and chromium; 6082 has more silicon and manganese. Many drawings accept either, but you should not substitute one for the other without the designer's approval, especially where welding, anodising colour or a quoted standard is involved. See 6061 vs 6082.

Fabrication

Machining, welding and finishing 6082 bar

Machining

T6 cuts cleanly with sharp carbide or HSS tools at high speed. Chips break better than in 6063; for long runs of small turned parts, a free-cutting alloy such as 2011 or 6262 is faster.

Stress and distortion

Use T6511 or T651 bar for parts with pockets or deep bores, and rough before finishing to release remaining stress.

Welding

MIG and TIG weld well with ER5356 or ER4043 filler. Eurocode 9 gives reduced HAZ strength for 6082-T6, so design welded joints accordingly.

Bending

T6 bends only on generous radii. For tight bends, use T4 or O and age after forming.

Anodising

Gives good protective films. Colour can look slightly greyer and less uniform than 6063 because of the manganese content.

Corrosion

Very good in atmosphere and in many marine settings; insulate from steel and copper to prevent galvanic attack.

Applications

Applications of 6082 bar

  • Machined parts: housings, manifolds, brackets, couplings and fixture components
  • Structures: truss and scaffold fittings, bridge and walkway parts, crane and lifting components
  • Transport: rail, truck and trailer fittings, suspension brackets and machined automotive parts
  • Marine and offshore: deck fittings and gangway parts (class approval by agreement)
  • Electrical: busbar and conductor fittings where strength matters more than conductivity
  • Forging stock: extruded bar cut to slugs for closed-die forging

FAQ

6082 aluminium (aluminum) bar: frequently asked questions

Is 6082 stronger than 6061?

Slightly, in most bar sizes. EN 755-2 gives 6082-T6 extruded bar from 20 to 150 mm a minimum proof stress of 260 MPa, against 240 MPa for 6061-T6. The difference is small; the choice is usually set by which standard your drawing calls for.

Can 6061 be used instead of 6082?

Often, but only with the designer's approval. The two have different chemistry and slightly different minimums, welding behaviour and anodised colour. Certificates must show the alloy actually ordered.

What does T6511 mean on 6082 bar?

The bar was solution treated, quenched, stretched to relieve residual stress, allowed minor straightening, and then artificially aged. It distorts less when machined than plain T6.

What is the old British name for 6082?

HE30 for extrusions (H30 in general) under the withdrawn BS 1474 and BS 1470 standards. In German practice it was AlMgSi1, Werkstoff 3.2315.

Is 6082 bar suitable for marine use?

It has very good resistance to marine atmosphere and is used for deck fittings and gangways. For welded hull structure in constant seawater contact, 5083 or 5086 is usually preferred.

Why are properties lower for very large 6082 bars?

6082 is quench sensitive. The centre of a large bar cools more slowly, so it cannot reach full T6 strength. EN 755-2 therefore lowers the minimums above 150 mm diameter.
