What we offer

How we support your project

Material selection

We recommend the alloy, temper and form that meet your loads, environment and manufacturing route, and flag cheaper alternatives where they will do the job.

Specification review

We check your drawings and specifications against available standards so there are no surprises at inspection.

Cut-to-size supply

Plate, sheet, bar, tube and profiles supplied cut to your dimensions to reduce waste and handling. Availability confirmed per order.

Surface finishing

Anodising, powder coating, protective film and other finishes arranged on request.

Fabrication support

Guidance on machining, forming, welding and joining so the material performs in production.

Documentation

Mill test certificates and the inspection records your customers or auditors require.

Packing and logistics

Packing suited to the product and route, with delivery across India and shipment for export.

Programme supply

Scheduled deliveries for regular production requirements.

Technical resources

Alloy data, weight calculators and technical sheets to help you plan.
How it works

From enquiry to delivery

  1. Send your requirementAlloy, temper, dimensions, quantity and any drawings.
  2. Technical reviewWe confirm the specification or suggest alternatives.
  3. QuotationPrice, availability, lead time and documentation.
  4. Order confirmationFinal specification, finishing and packing agreed.
  5. PreparationMaterial sourced, cut, finished and inspected as agreed.
  6. DeliveryShipped with mill test certificates and agreed documents.
FAQ

Service questions

Can you supply aluminium cut to size?

Yes, for many products. Send the dimensions and quantity and we will confirm availability and lead time.

Do you arrange anodising or powder coating?

Finishing can be arranged on request. Tell us the finish, colour and specification you need.

Ready to discuss your requirement?

Send us the alloy, form, size and quantity. Our Mumbai team will reply with availability, lead time and a competitive quote.
