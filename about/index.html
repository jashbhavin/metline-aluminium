Who we are

Built around how buyers actually source aluminium

Most projects need more than one aluminium product. A trailer needs plate, extrusions and tread plate. A marine build needs 5083 plate, 6061 profiles and fittings. A factory fit-out needs sheet, tube and bar. Sourcing each from a different supplier means more purchase orders, more delays and more chances for something to go wrong.

Metline Industries exists to remove that friction. We supply plate, sheet, coil, foil, tube, bar, extrusions, forgings and specialist products across the full range of wrought alloys, and we take responsibility for getting the right material to you with the right documents.

What we stand for

  • Range: every grade, every form, every sector.
  • Accuracy: the right alloy, temper and specification, every time.
  • Accountability: one team that owns your order from enquiry to delivery.
How we work

How we work with you

  1. Understand the applicationWe start with what the part has to do, not just the alloy on an old drawing.
  2. Recommend the materialAlloy, temper, form and specification that suit the job and the budget.
  3. Quote clearlyAvailability, price, lead time and documentation in one reply.
  4. Supply and documentMaterial delivered with mill test certificates and the paperwork your project needs.
  5. Support after deliveryHelp with processing questions, repeat orders and changes in requirements.
  6. Grow with youFrom prototype quantities to regular programme supply.
